# T21.08 — Spacesuits, with a visor colour you choose (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T20.04**, **T20.12**, and **T21.07** for the mode that uses them
· Size: ~200 lines

Asked for: *"players are now in a spacesuit skin (make one with several colors and have a
different visor color selection)."*

## Read first
- `tasks/M20/T20.12-hats-and-sunglasses.md` — **the whole file.** This is the same problem with
  a different noun, and every trap there applies here.
- `client/src/render/tombstoneTextures.ts:1-14` — the procedural pattern and its design rule
- `assets/skins.json` — how a skin is declared today

## Two decisions this task must make, not inherit

**1. Is a spacesuit a skin, or an override?** The brief says players *are* in a spacesuit in
zero-g, which reads as a **mode override** — the game chooses it, not the player. But the
visor colour *is* a player choice. So it is both: a forced body with a chosen accent. Decide
where each lives, because a skin the player did not pick must not overwrite the skin they did.
**Their chosen skin must come back when the mode ends.**

**2. Where does the visor colour ride?** T20.12 ruled **new wire fields** over packing, on the
finding that `tombstone_skin_id` is carried through join → `player_join`/`lobby_state` and
**not** into the replay. A visor colour is the same kind of thing: cosmetic, no simulation
meaning. Follow that ruling rather than re-deriving it.

## Notes

- **There is no spacesuit art and none is available.** T20.12 established this: the atlas is 5
  characters × 10 poses, Kenney's pack has no accessories, and `../sprite_packs` is terrain and
  sky only. **Procedural, like the tombstones.**
- **Silhouette, not palette** (`tombstoneTextures.ts:11-14`): *"a skin picker whose options
  differ only by colour is a picker with one option."* Several spacesuit colours that differ
  only in hue is exactly the failure that rule names — and this brief asks for colours. **Give
  the suit a distinct silhouette from the normal characters**, and let colour distinguish
  players within it.
- **`PlayerView` is rebuilt mid-round** with `private readonly` appearance fields and no
  setter. A suit applied once vanishes on the next rebuild. Use whatever T20.04/T20.12 landed
  for re-reading; do not solve it a third time.
- `verify-assets` **rejects** borrowed decor frames (the 98 %-opaque-terrain-tile mistake).
  Procedural art sidesteps it; borrowed art meets it.

## Tests

- **Rendered pixels**, with a control: two players with different visor colours differ in the
  same frame, and a control region neither occupies is unchanged.
- The suit is a distinct silhouette from a normal character — assert on pixels, not on an id.
- **A player's chosen skin returns when the mode ends.** That is the regression this task is
  most likely to ship, and nothing else will catch it.
- Visor persistence round-trips through `readId`'s `String(n) === raw` check, junk refused.
