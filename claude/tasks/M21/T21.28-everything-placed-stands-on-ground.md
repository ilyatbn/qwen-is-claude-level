# T21.28 — Everything placed on the map stands on ground (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.21** (part A landed: `objects.rs::fill_under`) · Size: ~250 lines,
split if it grows

Reported from play, 2026-09-15, with a screenshot of a teleport gate on a slope: *"the teleport is
floating because a couple pixels are touching it in the center but the rest are in the air? make
sure that you properly add enough ground when creating the map to make it look seamless (fill the
gaps to the teleport sprite) — same for all other objects you place on the map"*.

## What is true today (read before you design)

- **Pads and gun platforms get no ground.** `map/meta.rs::choose_pads` and `choose_gun_platforms`
  take one surface point each from `map/gen/spawns.rs::choose_separated`, which prefers
  `walkable_both_ways` — a *player-sized* test. Nothing asks for ground under the whole footprint,
  and nothing fills it. `TeleportPad::rect` / `GunPlatform::rect` are read only by `map/carve.rs`
  to **refuse carving**; they never set a pixel.
- **Scenery already has a fill.** `map/gen/objects.rs::fill_under` (T21.21A) extends the ground up
  to an object's base, straight down, bounded by `OBJECT_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH`, and charges the pixels
  to the object budget. Reuse its rule — **share the function, or share the guard** — do not write
  a second copy with its own depth rule.
- **The drawn footprint is not the simulated footprint.** `assets/images/gate.png` is **64×71** and
  `client/src/render/pads.ts` draws it at native size, origin (0.5, 1), on a `PAD_W` **40** strip:
  12 px of stone base overhang each side even on flat ground. Find the gun platform's drawn width
  the same way (its art and where `GameScene`/the platform layer sizes it) — do not assume it is
  `GUN_PLATFORM_W` 48.

## Deliverable

1. **An inventory of everything placed on the map at generation**, in the task file before code:
   pads, gun platforms, scenery objects, decorations, spawn points, buried slots, lava vents,
   anything else `meta.rs` or `map/gen/*` places — and for each, whether it is *drawn standing on
   the ground* (needs ground under its drawn base) or not (buried slots, spawns: say why not).
2. **Ground under the whole drawn base** of every standing thing: for each column of the drawn
   footprint, fill straight down from the base row to the first solid pixel, within a bound
   (reuse `OBJECT_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH`'s rule or state why a pad needs its own constant, in
   `constants.rs`). Deeper than the bound is a cliff edge — prefer **refusing that placement** and
   picking the next candidate over growing a stalk.
3. **The footprint the fill uses is the drawn one.** Either put the drawn base width in
   `constants.rs` (e.g. `PAD_ART_BASE_W`) and read it on both sides, or make the art match
   `PAD_W`. **One number, read by both the generator and `pads.ts`** — two that can disagree is
   today's bug.
4. **Keep the sub-stream contracts.** Pads and platforms each draw from their own stream; the fill
   must not draw randomness at all, so adding it cannot move a spawn, a pad or an object.

(`cargo test -p game-core --release --test map_sweep -- --ignored`) before any change and after**,

`GOLDEN_UPDATE=1 cargo test -p game-core --release --test golden`, both generators. T21.21A is the

## Tests

- **A presence test with a control:** on several seeds and all scales, every pad and platform has
  solid ground under **every column of its drawn base**; the control is the same maps generated
  with the fill turned off, which must show hanging columns (or the test proves nothing).
- **Flat ground gains nothing:** a pad on level ground adds zero pixels.
- **Never upward, never into the sprite's own box above the base row.**
- **Sub-streams unmoved:** spawns, pads and objects chosen with and without the fill are identical.
- **Pixels (`docs/72` §C2):** a browser check that samples the rows just under a gate's drawn base
  in a real round and finds rock, with a control region of open air beside it. Falsify by turning
  the fill off at its live call site.

Everything placed at generation — `map/meta.rs::generate_full`, `map/gen/*` — and whether it is
drawn standing on the ground:

| Thing | Chosen by | Drawn standing? | Ground under its drawn base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teleport pads | `meta.rs::choose_pads` | **Yes** — `gate.png` 64×71, origin (0.5, 1) on `pos`; bottom 2 rows a 31 px foot, the stone body above 62 px | **T21.28** — 64 columns (`PAD_ART_W`) |
| Gun platforms | `meta.rs::choose_gun_platforms` | **Yes** — `platforms.ts` texture `GUN_PLATFORM_W` wide, full-width plinth on its bottom rows, origin (0.5, 1) | **T21.28** — 48 columns (`GUN_PLATFORM_W`, already one number on both sides) |

| Spawn points | `gen/spawns.rs::choose_spawns` | No — nothing is drawn; a body stands there | No |
| Buried slots | `meta.rs::choose_buried_slots` | No — inside sealed rock by definition | No |

| Birds, animals, tombstones, mines | runtime | Simulated | No |

**Measured at HEAD, 36 maps (6 seeds × 3 scales × both generators):** under the drawn 64 px gate,
7179 of 13824 base columns have air beneath them; 29 of 216 pads fully grounded; 63 with a column
past 71 px. Gun platforms at 48 px: 24 of 107 with a column past 71 px.

**The golden table was blind to all of this** (commit 1): its mask column is
`generate_terrain_with`'s, before pass 8, and `meta_digest` hashed neither pads, platforms nor the
finished mask. A pad planted 1 px right passed the old table and fails the new one.
