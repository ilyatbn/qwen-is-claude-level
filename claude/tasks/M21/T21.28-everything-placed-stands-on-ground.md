# T21.28 — Everything placed on the map stands on ground (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.21** (part A landed: `objects.rs::fill_under`) · Size: ~250 lines,
split if it grows

Reported from play, 2026-09-15, with a screenshot of a teleport gate on a slope: *"the teleport is
floating because a couple pixels are touching it in the center but the rest are in the air? make
sure that you properly add enough ground when creating the map to make it look seamless (fill the
gaps to the teleport sprite) — same for all other objects you place on the map"*.

## What is true today (read before you design)

- **Pads and gun platforms get no ground.** `map/meta.rs::choose_pads` and `choose_gun_platforms`
  take one surface point each from `map/gen/spawns.rs::choose_separated`, which prefers
  `walkable_both_ways` — a *player-sized* test. Nothing asks for ground under the whole footprint,
  and nothing fills it. `TeleportPad::rect` / `GunPlatform::rect` are read only by `map/carve.rs`
  to **refuse carving**; they never set a pixel.
- **Scenery already has a fill.** `map/gen/objects.rs::fill_under` (T21.21A) extends the ground up
  to an object's base, straight down, bounded by `OBJECT_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH`, and charges the pixels
  to the object budget. Reuse its rule — **share the function, or share the guard** — do not write
  a second copy with its own depth rule.
- **The drawn footprint is not the simulated footprint.** `assets/images/gate.png` is **64×71** and
  `client/src/render/pads.ts` draws it at native size, origin (0.5, 1), on a `PAD_W` **40** strip:
  12 px of stone base overhang each side even on flat ground. Find the gun platform's drawn width
  the same way (its art and where `GameScene`/the platform layer sizes it) — do not assume it is
  `GUN_PLATFORM_W` 48.

## Deliverable

1. **An inventory of everything placed on the map at generation**, in the task file before code:
   pads, gun platforms, scenery objects, decorations, spawn points, buried slots, lava vents,
   anything else `meta.rs` or `map/gen/*` places — and for each, whether it is *drawn standing on
   the ground* (needs ground under its drawn base) or not (buried slots, spawns: say why not).
2. **Ground under the whole drawn base** of every standing thing: for each column of the drawn
   footprint, fill straight down from the base row to the first solid pixel, within a bound
   (reuse `OBJECT_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH`'s rule or state why a pad needs its own constant, in
   `constants.rs`). Deeper than the bound is a cliff edge — prefer **refusing that placement** and
   picking the next candidate over growing a stalk.
3. **The footprint the fill uses is the drawn one.** Either put the drawn base width in
   `constants.rs` (e.g. `PAD_ART_BASE_W`) and read it on both sides, or make the art match
   `PAD_W`. **One number, read by both the generator and `pads.ts`** — two that can disagree is
   today's bug.
4. **Keep the sub-stream contracts.** Pads and platforms each draw from their own stream; the fill
   must not draw randomness at all, so adding it cannot move a spawn, a pad or an object.

## Tests

- **A presence test with a control:** on several seeds and all scales, every pad and platform has
  solid ground under **every column of its drawn base**; the control is the same maps generated
  with the fill turned off, which must show hanging columns (or the test proves nothing).
- **Flat ground gains nothing:** a pad on level ground adds zero pixels.
- **Never upward, never into the sprite's own box above the base row.**
- **Sub-streams unmoved:** spawns, pads and objects chosen with and without the fill are identical.
- **Pixels (`docs/72` §C2):** a browser check that samples the rows just under a gate's drawn base
  in a real round and finds rock, with a control region of open air beside it. Falsify by turning

## Outcome: the Rust fill (builder, commit 2 of 3)

- **Constants:** `PAD_ART_W` 64 (the gate's drawn width, one number for generator, build and
  `pads.ts`), and `STANDING_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH` = `PLAYER_H`: a px bound, because the generator does not
  know the gate's drawn height, so `OBJECT_GROUND_FILL_DEPTH`'s height-relative rule cannot apply.
- **Shared rule:** `objects.rs::column_gap` / `fill_column`, split out of `fill_under`; the pads use the
  same per-column code.
- **Choosing:** `meta.rs::seat_then_top_up`: candidates on ground under every drawn column and whose
  fill cannot bury a spawn (or a pad, for platforms), then spawn-safe, then anything. The sampler runs
  on the strictest tier and the **shortfall is topped up** by farthest-point at the sampler's relaxed
  separation floor; if even that cannot reach the count, the old whole draw from the loosest tier. No
  randomness drawn.
- **Fill:** after every choice (`generate_full_with`), then the surface is **re-derived**
  (`extract_surface` + `traversal::analyse`) because the fill both invalidates and creates standable
  points. First version only dropped points, and `surface_points_match_the_final_mask` caught it.
- **The golden table moves** (regenerated), attributable to this commit alone after commit 1.

**1000-seed sweep** (`map_sweep`, release):

| | attempts | fraction p05 | cave reachable | pads+platforms perched past the fill | maps that fell back |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HEAD | [0, 955, 42, 2, 0] | 0.872 | 81.8 % | not measured | not measured |
| fill, all-or-nothing fallback | same | 0.880 | 83.0 % | 835 / 8934 | 288 |
| **fill, top-up (committed)** | same | 0.880 | 83.1 % | **544 / 8916** | **284** |

The sweep now also asserts every surface point is standable and no pad or platform column hangs within
the fill's reach, and all 999 maps pass. The fallback rate (284 maps with at least one perched thing)
is the open number: the chooser seats pads on peaks, and seven in ten of those maps have a pad the fill
cannot reach.

**Owner-visible change beyond the fill (coordinator: record it).** A pad chosen on a peak used to have
almost no indestructible rock under it: seed `TEST_SEED` Small's pad at (1504, 496) had 3 of its 40
protected columns solid. Its protected strip is now real rock (40 of 40), so the ground under a gate
cannot be dug out, which is T21.12's stated intent made true. It broke
`checksum.rs::two_clients_agree_on_the_mask_after_a_hundred_carves` on every run (the shooter spawns on
that pad and fires at its feet: 120 carves became 35 against a floor of 50). The fixture now stands the
shooter on a spawn clear of every pad's and platform's drawn base, derived from the map. The floor is
unchanged; it is green with the fill on and still green with it off.

**Falsified:** turning the fill off at `generate_full`'s call site turns
`every_pad_and_platform_has_ground_under_its_whole_drawn_base` red ("2182 drawn base columns still hang
within the fill's reach"). The first version of that test passed the flag explicitly and stayed green
under the same plant, so the fill arm now goes through `generate_with`.

## Outcome: the client, decorations and the pixel check (builder, commit 3 of 3)

**One number for the gate.** `PAD_ART_W` crosses to the client (`constants_json`, `Constants`);
`pads.ts` draws the gate at that width (height keeps the art's aspect) and
`build-gate-sprite.mjs::targetSize` builds it that wide instead of `PAD_W × 1.6` (same 64, committed art
unchanged; `gateSprite.test.ts` pins the built width to `PAD_ART_W`).

**Decorations: coordinator's rulings, 2026-09-15.** No fill and no collision for decorations; the
client holds them to the standard instead, and the generator chooses where that standard can be met.
- *Client* (`decorations-math.ts`): drawn only when rock sits within `DECOR_GROUND_SLACK_PX` (2) under
  **every column of the art's opaque base**, measured once per frame from its alpha (`opaqueBase`,
  `decorations.ts` via `getPixelAlpha`), scaled and mirrored with the prop (`baseColumns`).
- *Anchor, a decision the ruling implies:* eight of eighteen frames have transparent rows under their art
  (`decor_3`'s lowest opaque row is 13 of 18), so anchoring at the frame edge drew them hovering on flat
  ground and the rule would have dropped them. The sprite origin now puts the base row on the feet line
  (`baseOriginY`).
- *Generator* (`meta.rs::choose_decorations`): candidates are surface points where the widest possible
  base, `DECOR_BASE_W` 23 (widest opaque base in the atlas, 18 px, × the 1.25 tier, rounded up), is
  seated within `DECOR_GROUND_SLACK` (2); the count still comes from the whole surface, and the
  unfiltered set is used only when nothing is seated (counted by the sweep).
- *Measured, 9 maps* (seeds 1, 4242, 31337 × three scales): the first version (client rule, old
  placement) drew **40 of 96**; now **97 of 97**, every map 100 % of what the old rule drew, every drawn
  prop re-checked seated column by column (`decorations-real.test.ts`, which also asserts the atlas
  never exceeds `DECOR_BASE_W` and the two slacks agree). Generator control: without the filter 61 of 97
  chosen unseated, with it 0.
- `meta_digest` does **not** cover decorations, so the golden table does not move for this; reported, not
  widened.
- *Falsified:* filter off in `choose_decorations` → "61 decorations were chosen unseated"; the client rule
  forced true → seven place/base tests red.

**Pixel check `gate-ground`** (in-page, sandbox seed 7): for every pad not perched past the fill, a patch
under each end of the drawn base must be nearer the rendered rock than the rendered air, both references
chosen from the live mask and sampled `2 × EDGE_BAND_PX` down (the first version picked the snow edge band
as its rock reference). Seed 7 because on 4242 no asserted pad had an end the fill changes at the sampled
rows (measured by a probe over 200 seeds); on 7 all six pads assert. Green: 6/6 pads. **Falsified** with the
fill off at `generate_full`'s call site: red on the rock-versus-air comparison itself, six ends.
