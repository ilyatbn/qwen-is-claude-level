# T21.03 — Unicorn wings (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T20.08**, **T20.09** (dropping is the only way off), **T20.04**
· Size: ~200 lines

Asked for: *"an item called 'unicorn wings'. When in the inventory you just fly constantly.
You cannot turn it off unless you drop them. You cannot jump or jetpack. It will have a custom
speed separate from jetpacking since I don't know how balanced this item is — might have to
reduce it to make it more fair."*

## Read first
- `crates/game-core/src/player/jetpack.rs` — `apply_thrust`, `gravity_scale`, the fuel model
- `crates/game-core/src/player/mod.rs:19-23` — `apply_input`'s purity contract
- `tasks/M21/T21.02-ironman-boots.md` — **the prediction problem is identical; read it once**

## This task hard-depends on T20.09

*"You cannot turn it off unless you drop them"* means **dropping is the only off switch**, and
there is no drop path in the tree until T20.09 lands. Until then this item is a trap the
player cannot escape. **Do not build this before T20.09.**

## Deliverable

- A new `ItemId`, appended, in T20.08's passive family.
- **Constant flight while held**, at its own speed constant — explicitly **not**
  `JETPACK_MAX_SPEED`, because the brief says it will need tuning independently.
- **Jump and jetpack are refused while held.** Refused, not ignored: a held JUMP that silently
  does nothing and a held JUMP that is documented as refused read the same on screen and
  differently in a bug report.
- No fuel cost — the brief does not mention one, and the jetpack's `JETPACK_MAX_FUEL` is what
  makes flight finite. **Say out loud that wings are unlimited**, because that is the balance
  decision hiding in "you just fly constantly".

## The interactions this task must settle, not discover

- **T21.02 boots.** Boots multiply jump height; wings forbid jumping. Holding both is
  reachable. Define the precedence or refuse the combination.
- **T20.11 fall damage.** If flight is constant, a player never falls — so wings are a
  blanket fall-damage immunity. That may be fine; it must be *stated*.
- **`gravity_scale`** (`jetpack.rs:161`) is how the jetpack already softens a descent. Wings
  are a third gravity regime alongside normal and jetpack-held. Do not add a fourth flag —
  derive it.
- **Zero-gravity mode (T21.07)**, if it lands, makes "constant flight" meaningless. Note the
  collision; do not solve it here.

## Notes

- **The prediction problem is T21.02's, verbatim** — `apply_input` runs on both sides, so
  "carries wings" must reach the client or every flying player rubber-bands. Prefer a bit
  derived at the encode site (T20.07's conclusion) over a stored, hashed field.
- Bots do not know about wings. A bot that picks them up and keeps trying to jump is the
  `bots/mod.rs:975` shape T20.08 found — **grep the bot for jump/jetpack decisions** and say
  what a winged bot does, even if the answer is "nothing, and here is why that is survivable".

## Tests

- A player holding wings rises without input and keeps rising; the control is that the same
  player without them falls.
- JUMP and jetpack are refused while held — asserted as *refused*, with a control that both
  work once the wings are dropped.
- **Dropping them ends the flight.** That is the whole off switch; test it directly.
- Client prediction agrees with the server for a flying player.
