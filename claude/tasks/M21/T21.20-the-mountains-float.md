# T21.20 — The background mountains are tiny and float in the air (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: nothing · Size: ~150 lines, one file

**Reported from play by the coordinator**: *"the background mountains are very
small and are in the air."*

Both halves are one bug.

## What was found

`render/parallax.ts` places the ridge like this:

```ts
ts.setPosition(view.left, view.top + view.h * c.MOUNTAIN_BASE_FRAC - bandH)
```

and sizes it from `view.h * MOUNTAIN_HEIGHT_FRAC`. **`view` is the camera's
visible rect**, so the ridge is positioned and scaled against *what the camera can
currently see* rather than against the world.

That produces exactly the two reported symptoms:

- **It floats.** The ridge sits at 86 % down the viewport, so it rides with the
  camera. The terrain does not. Climb a hill or jetpack and the skyline detaches
  from the ground and hangs in mid-air.
- **It is tiny.** `CAMERA_ZOOM` is 2, so the camera sees half as much world, and a
  ridge sized at 16–24 % of *that* is half the world-size the constants read as.
  The fractions look like they were chosen before the zoom was.

`MOUNTAIN_BASE_FRAC`'s own doc comment says it is *"below the sun and moon arc's
horizon (0.82) so the ridge line crosses the gradient rather than floating in the
middle of it"* — which is the intent, stated, and not what the code does once the
camera moves.

## Deliverable

A ridge anchored in **world** space: a horizon at a world height, at a world
size, so it stays put relative to the ground and does not change size with zoom.

## Watch for

- **Parallax must survive.** The ridge is meant to scroll *slower* than the world
  — that is the whole effect. Anchoring it to the world must not nail it to the
  world; it still moves at its own factor, just from a world-space origin.
- **The sun and moon arc uses the same horizon idea** (0.82 in `sky-math.ts`).
  If the ridge moves to world space and the arc does not, they will disagree
  about where the horizon is. Check them together.
- **`MOUNTAIN_HEIGHT_FRAC` and `MOUNTAIN_BASE_FRAC` change meaning** if they stop
  being viewport fractions. Either re-derive the values for their new basis and
  say so at the constant, or keep the names honest.
- **`living-sky` and `sky` both sample this**, and a ridge that changes size or
  position will move their numbers. Re-derive any threshold rather than nudging
  it green.
- `parallax.ts`'s header claims one bake per seed and a fixed sprite pool. Keep
  that true — this is a positioning fix, not a redraw-per-frame fix.

## Tests

- The ridge's world position is **independent of the camera**: move the camera up
  and the gap between the ridge base and a fixed world point does not change.
  **Control**: the same measurement without the fix moves. This is the assertion
  the bug is actually about.
- Its size is independent of zoom: the same ridge at two zoom levels covers the
  same world height.
- It still parallaxes — panning moves it, and by **less** than the terrain.
- **Rendered pixels** (`docs/72` §C2): a frame with the camera low and one with it
  high, the ridge in a sane place in both, with a control region of sky.

## Done when

```sh
npm --prefix client test -- --run && ./scripts/check.sh
```

## Resolution (builder, 2026-09-14)

**One rule, in `render/parallax-math.ts::ridgeLayout`, with its tests.** With a map, the ridge base is a
**world** row — `MOUNTAIN_BASE_FRAC` of the map height, now defined as `GROUND_BASE_FRAC` so the skyline
meets the land where the land usually is — and its height is `VIEWPORT_H × MOUNTAIN_HEIGHT_FRAC` **world
px** (115 / 173). Vertically it moves exactly with the terrain; horizontally it still parallaxes by
`MOUNTAIN_PARALLAX` (tile offset, unchanged). The sprites stay `scrollFactor(0)`, placed in camera space
from that one layout; the texture was already baked at the world height, so no re-bake.

**Constants: re-based, and split where one name would mean two things.** `MOUNTAIN_HEIGHT_FRAC` keeps its
values on a new basis; `MOUNTAIN_BASE_FRAC` changed value (0.86 of the screen → the ground's mean line); the
title, which has no map, keeps the old screen layout under a new `MOUNTAIN_TITLE_BASE_FRAC` 0.86; the sun
and moon's inline 0.82 is `SKY_HORIZON_FRAC` and stays a **screen** horizon (the bodies scroll at
`SKY_BODY_PARALLAX`), the world ridge covering them where the two meet.

**Found by looking, not in the brief: a skirt.** The first world-anchored frame showed the ridge ending in a
straight line with sky under it wherever the ground dips below the base. A solid rectangle in the near
ridge's tint now fills from its base to the bottom of the screen, at the near ridge's depth (no new layer;
title excluded).

**Tests.** Unit: base on one world row at every camera row, same world height at zoom 1 and 2, screen move
equal to the terrain's, title layout — each world claim with the replaced layout as its control. Browser,
`living-sky`: the ridge band is read from the layer instead of 0.64..0.85 (the fixed strip then sat in the
solid body and read "seed 999 drew the same ridge"); sprite = formula; base field **and drawn row** = the
constants' row; skirt at the base; camera moved 64 world px → base stayed at 983.0, screen row moved 128 px
(= 64 × zoom 2); 172.8 world px tall at zoom 1 and 2. `title`, `sky`, `terrain-render` green.

**Falsified at the live site** (`ParallaxLayer.layout` / `ridgeLayout`): ignoring the camera and ignoring the
zoom both red `living-sky` (at presence and at the cloud control); following the camera at 0.9 reds "it
rides with the camera again"; a height that does not scale with zoom reds "138.2 at zoom 2 (want 172.8)".

**Not covered:** no photograph at two camera heights — the sandbox has no freeze, so the camera claim is
asserted on the layer's report plus the one pixel frame; horizontal parallax is not re-asserted in a browser.

### Follow-up: the skirt was a wall; it is a fading foot now

The gate after `77a70e6` first went red on `game-server`'s lobby test (`a_lobby_room_has_no_bots`,
`AlreadyClosed` — T19.26's socket race; 5/5 green standalone, nothing here touches it), then on
`skins-ingame`, deterministically. Its frames showed why: wherever the camera sat below the ridge base, the
solid skirt filled the **whole sky** with mountain colour. A regression of this task, not of the check —
the ground difference behind two bodies went from 37.4 to 55–58 while the bodies stayed at 61.4.

The skirt is now a **foot**: a fade from the ridge's tint to nothing over `MOUNTAIN_FOOT_FADE` (90 world px)
below the base, so the valley edge is haze and the sky stays sky below it (looked at in `living-sky`'s
carved frame). Separately, `skins-ingame` now hides the parallax band while it samples — the same isolation
it already applies to the teleport gates (T21.12), through a new `setParallaxVisible` hook that reads the
layer's `hidden` back; its 1.5x bar is untouched and it reads 2.7x (bodies 61.0, ground 22.7).
`living-sky`, `title`, `sky`, `terrain-render`, `two-clients` green on the same build.
