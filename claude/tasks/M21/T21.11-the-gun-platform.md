# T21.11 — The gun platform (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: nothing hard · Size: **large — split it, see the end**

**Asked for by the coordinator**, in their words:

> *"A static platform spawning at a random location on the map (up to 3) and users can interact
> with them by standing on them for a second. When they are using this gun they can't move or do
> anything other than firing. They also cannot heal using Q. Right clicking 'exits' this mode.
> This gun fires a barrage of 4 bullets at a time at a high rate. It has 500 bullets."*

**Art reference:** `assets/T21.11-gun-platform-reference.webp` — a triple-barrelled turret on a
splayed tripod base. Silhouette guidance only; the sprite is a separate piece of work.

## The precedent is the teleport pad, and it is close enough to copy

Do not invent placement or activation. `TELEPORT_PADS` (6 of them) are already **static map
features chosen at generation and stored in `MapMeta`** — `map/meta.rs::choose_pads` picks them
from the mask, `carve.rs` protects them from being dug away, and a player **charges one by
standing on it** for `TELEPORT_CHARGE` (1.5 s). Every hard part of "a thing on the map you
activate by standing on it" is solved there.

**So `GUN_PLATFORMS` is `choose_pads`'s sibling, not a new mechanism.** Read both before
designing, and reuse the mask-eligibility rule — a platform on a slope or half inside rock is
the same defect a pad would be.

## What must be decided, not assumed

These are the questions the brief does not answer. **Answer them in the task, with reasons.**

- **Is 500 rounds per platform or per occupant?** Per platform is the reading that makes it a
  map resource worth fighting over; per occupant makes it a free weapon three times a round.
  Recommend per platform, stated at the constant.
- **What happens at zero?** Dead scenery, or does it despawn? Dead scenery is cheaper and keeps
  the cover.
- **One occupant at a time.** Say so explicitly and enforce it server-side; two players sharing
  a gun is a race nobody has thought about.
- **Can a mounted player be hurt?** They must be — a gun you cannot be punished for using is
  not a trade. **This is the whole balance of the feature**: enormous firepower, and you are a
  stationary target who cannot heal.
- **Does mounting drop what you were holding?** No, but say so; the inventory is untouched and
  you get it back on exit.

## The constraints, and the one that is hardest

- **No movement while mounted.** The player's own input must not move the body.
- **No item use, no `Q` heal, no `R` charge.** `GameScene`'s §C9 comment says both are slotless
  and both refuse in some states already — **find that refusal and add this state to it rather
  than writing a second gate.** *Share the guard, or share the function.*
- **Right-click exits.** ⚠️ **Right-click is already taken and this is the real integration
  risk.** `main.ts` suppresses `contextmenu` on the canvas, and `ui/inventory.ts` binds it on
  the inventory root *and* on individual tiles. So a world-level right-click handler has to not
  fire when the click was meant for the inventory. **Establish what right-click does today
  before adding to it** — the coordinator has also described right-click as opening the expanded
  inventory, so there may be three consumers, not two.

## Firing

- **A barrage of 4 at a high rate.** Decide whether the 4 are simultaneous (a spread) or a
  rapid burst of 4 in sequence — the reference art has three barrels, which argues for a spread
  or a rotating sequence. **This is a gameplay feel decision; make it and say which.**
- Every number goes in `constants.rs` mirroring the docs — barrage size, rate, ammo, mount time,
  platform count. **Never inline one, and never hardcode one in a test.**
- The bullets should be the existing projectile path, not a new one. A second bullet
  implementation is how two things that should look identical stop matching.

## Determinism and the wire

- **Platform positions come from the seed**, like pads, so every client agrees without being
  told. Assert that a client rebuilding the map derives the same platforms — `lava_vents` has
  this exact cross-check and `load_mask` once cleared the very field that made it work.
- **Mount state is server-authoritative and must reach the client**, or a mounted player will be
  predicted moving while the server holds them still. That is T20.19 and T20.21 exactly: the
  client's `apply_input` must know it is mounted, or it rubber-bands. **Add it to the state the
  mirror already receives; do not open a side channel.**
- Ammo is world state and belongs in the state hash, or a replay diverges the first time
  somebody fires.

## Tests

- Standing for the mount time mounts you; standing for less does not. **Both halves** — an
  absence needs a presence.
- A mounted player's movement input moves nothing. **Control**: the same input unmounted moves
  them, or the test passes against a game where nobody can move.
- `Q` refuses while mounted and works when not.
- Right-click exits, and — the control that matters — **right-click on the inventory still does
  what it did**.
- Firing consumes ammo; at zero it stops. Ammo is per platform: a second occupant finds it
  empty.
- A mounted player takes damage normally.
- **Rendered pixels** (`docs/72` §C2) for the platform and for the mounted state, with a control
  region and a control frame. A player who cannot tell they are mounted will think the game has
  frozen.

## Done when

**Split before starting.** Placement and rendering is one task; mounting and its input lockout
is another; the weapon is a third. One task covering all three is several files and well past
250 lines, which `CLAUDE.md` says to split rather than discover at 800.
