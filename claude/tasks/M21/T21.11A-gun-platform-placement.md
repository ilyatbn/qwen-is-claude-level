# T21.11A — The gun platform: placement, protection and art (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Parent: `T21.11-the-gun-platform.md` · Depends on: nothing hard
Size: ~250 lines across the generation → wire → render seam

**The parent file says to split before starting.** This is the first of three:

* **A (this file)** — the platform exists on the map, cannot be dug away, reaches
  the client, and is drawn.
* **B** — standing on one mounts you; mounted players cannot move or reach the
  inventory; holding jump dismounts.
* **C** — the gun: the 4-round barrage, the 500-round magazine, the empty state.

**A must not know anything about mounting or ammo.** It ships a static map feature
and its picture, and every one of its tests is about placement, indestructibility
and pixels.

## The precedent is the teleport pad, and it is close enough to copy

`map/meta.rs::TeleportPad` is *exactly* this shape already:

* `map/meta.rs::choose_pads` picks 6 from the surface through
  `map/gen/spawns.rs::choose_separated`, on its own named RNG sub-stream so that
  adding a consumer cannot move a spawn point.
* `TeleportPad::rect` is the protected rock — `PAD_H` rows below the feet line,
  `PAD_W` wide — and `map/carve.rs::carve_circle` subtracts those rects from every
  row it clears.
* `codec.rs::encode_map_init` writes `u16 count` then `count × (i16 x, i16 y)`;
  `client/src/net/codec.ts` reads it back.

**Do all four the same way.** A second placement mechanism, a second protection
rule or a second wire shape would each be a thing that can disagree with the pad.

### Share the footprint, do not copy it

`TeleportPad` has `rect`, `covers` and `underfoot`. `GunPlatform` needs all three
with different dimensions. **Extract them into free functions in `map/meta.rs`
taking `(pos, w, h)` and have both types delegate**, rather than writing a second
copy — *share the guard, or share the function*. The pad's own tests must keep
passing untouched; if one needs editing, the extraction changed behaviour and is
wrong.

## Rulings

* **Up to 3**, from the coordinator. `GUN_PLATFORMS: usize = 3`, in `constants.rs`.
* **`GUN_PLATFORM_W` / `GUN_PLATFORM_H`** — the footprint, and the protected rock
  under it. The coordinator's words for the sibling task: *"a couple pixels of
  ground that you cannot destroy under it (i dont care if it floats just make it
  look like it has some ground)"*. `PAD_W`/`PAD_H` are 40/8 and that is the model.
* **Its own RNG sub-stream**, named `"gun_platforms"`. Not `"pads"`, or adding
  platforms moves every pad on every seed and re-rolls the golden table.
* **Placement is `MapMeta`, ammo is `World`.** Position is generation output and
  never changes; ammo mutates and belongs in the state hash. **A only ships the
  `MapMeta` half** — do not add the ammo field yet, and do not move
  `REPLAY_VERSION`.
* **Drawn procedurally**, following `render/weaponTextures.ts` and
  `render/itemTextures.ts`: no vendored pack has a turret, and `docs/51` §5 makes
  the procedural path the shipping one for anything they do not cover. Silhouette
  first — the parent's reference art is a triple-barrelled turret on a splayed
  tripod. **Silhouette guidance only; do not try to reproduce the render.**

## Watch for

* **`carve.rs`'s per-row span array is fixed-size on purpose.** The comment there
  records why: an r=200 carve is 400 rows and a per-row `Vec` blew the 4 ms
  single-chunk rebake budget `docs/60` §6 asserts. The bound is currently
  `TELEPORT_PADS + 1`; protecting platforms too makes it
  `TELEPORT_PADS + GUN_PLATFORMS + 1`. **Keep it a fixed array.** `perf` in the
  gate is what notices if you do not.
* **Only a carve is refused, never a fill.** `carve_circle` takes `solid`;
  the pad protection is skipped when `solid` is true, because refusing to *add*
  rock inside the footprint would make it a hole nothing can ever fill.
* **`MapMeta` gaining a field touches everything that constructs one.** Grep for
  the struct literal, not just for `MapMeta`.
* **The client rebuilds the map from the wire.** `lava_vents` has a cross-check
  that a client-derived map agrees with the server's; the parent file records that
  `load_mask` once cleared the very field that made it work. Assert the platforms
  survive a map round-trip.
* Draw at `DEPTH.decorations - 1` beside `PadLayer`, or an object will sort in
  front of a platform depending on creation order — the reason that depth exists.

## Tests

* A generated map has `GUN_PLATFORMS` platforms, and they are standable —
  `is_standable` at each, the same property the pads assert. **Across several
  seeds**: a population claim needs more than one draw.
* Platforms and pads do not sit on top of each other.
* **A carve over a platform removes nothing inside its rect, and the control is a
  carve of the same radius just outside it, which removes plenty.** An absence
  needs a presence.
* **A fill inside the rect still adds rock** — the `solid` exemption, asserted.
* Adding platforms **does not move the teleport pads or the spawns** for a fixed
  seed. This is what the separate sub-stream buys, and nothing else tests it.
* The wire round-trips: encode a map, decode it, compare the platform list.
* **Rendered pixels** (`docs/72` §C2) — a platform is drawn where the server says
  one is, with a control region away from it and a control frame from a map with
  the layer disabled or a location with no platform. *"A thing you cannot see is a
  thing nobody uses"*, and this project has shipped that bug four times.
