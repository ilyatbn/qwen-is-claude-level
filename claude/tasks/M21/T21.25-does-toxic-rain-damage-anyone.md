# T21.25 — Does toxic rain actually damage anyone? (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: nothing · Size: medium · **Investigation first**

## The report

> *"its just that ive never seen any actual damage from toxic rains so i thought
> something odd is happening."*

## What is already known, before anyone starts

**The arithmetic says it should be very noticeable.** `TOXIC_SPLASH_R`'s doc
comment records a measured fix — expected hits per shower went from **0.26 to
2.6** when the splash radius was widened, *"measured as a count, not as a
feeling"*. At `TOXIC_POISON_DPS` 6.0 over `TOXIC_POISON_DURATION` 3.0 s, one hit
is 18 damage, so 2.6 hits is roughly **47 damage per shower** — about half a
player's health. Nobody could miss that.

**And nothing in this repository would notice if it stopped happening.** Checked:

- `scripts/checks/toxic-rain-game.mjs` is the only end-to-end rain check. Its
  assertions are that real drops exist, that the emitter's density is derived
  from them, and that droplets are drawn. **Not one of them reads a health bar.**
  Its own header lists the six hops it was written to cover and they are all
  about *getting the sheet on screen*.
- `effects/toxic.rs`'s tests are `a_roof_stops_the_poison_and_open_sky_does_not`
  and `a_player_beside_the_roof_is_still_poisoned` — both about `poison_lands`,
  which is geometry. Neither steps a world or reads a player's health.

So the hazard is verified as a *picture* end to end, and as *arithmetic* in
isolation, with nothing joining the two. That is this project's most expensive
recurring shape: **a claim reported through something other than the thing it
claims.** It is exactly how heavy fog's formula stayed correct for five
milestones while nothing carried the number to the screen.

## The work, in order

1. **Measure before changing.** Do not fix anything first. Stand a player in the
   open in a real round with `WEATHER=toxic` and count the damage actually taken
   over a full shower. The prediction is ~47. Report the number you get.
   - If it is near 47, the hazard works and the report is about *visibility* —
     the player could not tell what hit them. That is a real problem with a
     different fix (see below), and finding it is a valid outcome.
   - If it is near zero, find which hop drops it and fix that.
2. **Whatever the answer, add the missing assertion**: a player standing in the
   open under toxic rain, in the **game** scene and not the sandbox, loses
   health. With a **control**: a player under a roof does not (`poison_lands`
   already draws that line, so the control is cheap and it is what makes the
   claim mean something).
3. Consider a second control that costs nothing: the damage is attributed to
   `DamageSource::Weather(EffectKind::ToxicRain)`. A player killed by rain and
   credited to somebody else is a bug the kill feed would show.

## If the damage turns out to be fine

Then the finding is that **a player cannot tell they are being poisoned**, and
the fix is feedback rather than balance — the same damage, made legible. Check
what the screen does when poison is applied: is there a hit marker, a tint, a
sound, anything on the health bar distinguishing 6 dps of poison from a bullet?
Report what exists before proposing what to add.

## Watch for

- **`WEATHER=toxic` is how you make this testable** without waiting out
  `EFFECT_INTERVAL_MIN` and hoping the scheduler rolls rain — a one-in-four coin
  flip inside a gate.
- Bots off (`BOT_COUNT=0`), or something else may do the damage and the test will
  happily report success. An unattributed health drop proves nothing.
- Do not pin the expected damage to a literal. It is a function of the constants
  and a sampled process; assert a floor derived from them, and aggregate across
  seeds — one shower is one draw.

## Done when

```sh
cargo test -p game-core && npm --prefix client test -- --run && ./scripts/check.sh
```

and the journal records the measured damage-per-shower figure.

## Resolution (builder, 2026-09-14) — it damages; a player cannot tell

**Measured before changing anything**, networked game, `WEATHER=toxic`, `BOT_COUNT=0`, standing still,
one shower per seed (`debug().health` sampled every 100 ms of round time):

| seed | spawn | health lost | poisonings seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (run twice) | open | 84, 84 | 4, 4 |
| 7 | open | 73 | 3 |
| 42 | open | 65 | 4 |
| 3 | open | 97 | 3 |
| 12345 | open | 79 | 4 |
| 4242 | open | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | under rock | 0 | 0 |
| 99 | under rock | 0 | 0 |

**Mean 66.3 health per shower in the open over six seeds**, above the ~47 predicted. The prediction's
inputs moved: 54 drops (`ceil(TOXIC_DURATION / TOXIC_DROP_EVERY)`), a 2048 px small map rather than
1536, and a 72 px target (`PLAYER_W` 16 + 2 × `TOXIC_SPLASH_R`). With `PLAYER_HALF_BIAS` 0.7 that is
0.7·72/1024 + 0.3·72/2048 ≈ 0.060 per drop, **≈ 3.2 expected poisonings**, not 2.6.

**So the report is about legibility, and what exists explains it.** Poison is applied as a `Damage` event
every tick of `TOXIC_POISON_DPS × SIM_DT` = 0.1 (`World::step` stage 8a → `apply_damage_log`).
`DamageNumbers.add` drops anything under 1, so no number ever appears; `Vignette.hit` adds amount/50 =
0.002 against a decay of dt/0.55, so no red flash. What is left is the health bar shrinking in `TOXIC`
`#7cd44a` beside healthy `GREEN` `#3ec75a` — two greens — and only above half health. Proposed, **not
built** (the coordinator's call): accumulate poison per poisoning and show one number, and/or a green
edge while the wire's poisoned bit is set.

**Added**: `toxic-rain-game.mjs` now reads health. Seed 1 pinned (the check asserts hops, not the
population, which Rust owns); asserts the wire said poisoned and the shower cost `> 0` and
`≥ TOXIC_POISON_DPS × TOXIC_POISON_DURATION`, with an absence control (health unchanged until the first
drop) and a roof arm on seed 2 (rain falling, no health lost, never poisoned). **Falsified at live
sites**: stage 8a dealing 0 reds the open arm only; a splash poisoning everyone reds the roof arm (141
lost under rock); disabling `poison_lands` does **not** red it — drops land on the roof beyond the splash
radius, so that rule stays Rust's (`a_roof_stops_the_poison_and_open_sky_does_not`). Attribution
(`DamageSource::Weather`) is not asserted here: she does not die, so there is no death cause to read.
