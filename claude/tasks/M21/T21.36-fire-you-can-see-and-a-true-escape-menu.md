# T21.36 — Fire you can see, and a true escape menu

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: T21.18 (the flame shader, `fire-shader`) · Size: small

## Why

Two checks report something other than what is true.

### 1. With High Quality off, fire burns where it is not drawn

Found and measured by the T21.18 builder, verified here:

- `ordnance-state.ts` `LOOK.flame` had `r: 7`, under a comment saying a flame is
  "drawn a little larger than `FLAME_RADIUS` (10)". **False.** `ordnance.ts::render`
  draws three discs at `r × 1.15 × f`, `r × 0.8 × f` and `r × 0.38 × f`, and
  `flameFlicker` returns `f` in [0.75, 1.0]. So the outer, 45 %-alpha rim reaches
  6.0–8.05 px and the solid body 4.2–5.6 px, against a burn radius of 10.
- `fire-shader.mjs` samples 8 points just inside every on-camera flame's burn circle
  (`R − 1` screen px) against the same frozen frame with the layer hidden. With High
  Quality off it reported 85/192 and 97/192 painted — "reported, not asserted".
  (The task brief said `ordnance.mjs`; the report is in `fire-shader.mjs`.)
- **Also true with High Quality on, past the pool.** Flames beyond
  `FLAME_SHADER_POOL` (48) fall back to the same flat discs, and a flamethrower field
  holds up to `FLAME_MAX_LIVE`. Same code, same fix.
- **Every ground fire is one draw site.** `KIND_BY_WEAPON_KEY` maps only `flame` to
  the `flame` kind; molotov crowds, flamethrower presses and lava-vent afterburn all
  arrive as that weapon (`grep -rn "'flame'" client/src`). A molotov *bottle* in flight
  is `LOOK.molotov` and burns nothing. `ordnanceFx.ts` flame jets are particles, not a
  burn area.

### 2. `escape-menu` asserts something that stopped being true

It asserted that turning High Quality on leaves a patch of the playfield unchanged,
"because nothing reads it yet". Since T21.18 the cloud, beam, smoke, fire and
explosion shaders all read it, so the assertion is now a coin flip on whatever is in
that patch (red 6.5 in a gate, green 0.5 alone). Parked in `tasks/flaky-test.md`.

## Rulings

1. **The flat flame's body covers the burn circle at its smallest flicker.** The
   drawn radii come from `ordnance-state.ts::flameDiscs(FLAME_RADIUS, flicker)`:
   body = `FLAME_RADIUS × f / FLAME_FLICKER_MIN`, so body ≥ `FLAME_RADIUS` for every
   `f`; rim and heart keep the old 1.15 : 0.8 : 0.38 proportions, so it is the same
   flame, scaled. `LOOK.flame.r` is `0` and documented as unused, as a bullet's
   streak is: a second radius literal is the thing that drifted.
   *Reversible:* a smaller rim is one ratio.
2. **`fire-shader` asserts 192/192 (every sampled point) in both modes.**
3. **Found on the way: the flat flicker ran on the wall clock.** `ordnance.ts::render`
   ("draw without advancing") read `performance.now()`, so a High Quality flip on a
   frozen scene repainted the flat fire at a new flicker. Invisible with ~8 px discs;
   with covering discs `fire-shader`'s exact-restore read 2.5 and 1.1 against 1 in two
   of three runs. The flicker now runs on `OrdnanceLayer.flickerMs`, advanced by
   `update`: restore reads 0.0 in every run since.
4. **`fire-shader`'s "painted differently" counts pixels, not the mean colour.** Once
   the flat fire covers the ground the shader covers, the band's mean moved 4.0–4.7
   against a floor of 4 — a coin flip. It now asserts the fraction of fire-band pixels
   that differ flat vs painted (measured 33.6–35.2 %) over the same metric flat vs
   flat-restored (0.0 %), floor 5 %.
5. **`escape-menu` asserts what is true now:** High Quality defaults to Off; one click
   turns it On and it reaches storage (`deepcut.highQuality` = `1`); it survives closing
   and re-opening the panel; and **the renderer follows the panel live** — a laser fired
   after the panel set On paints ≥ 1 beam quad, and after the panel set Off paints 0
   (the control). No reload leg: `fps-counter` already reloads through `loadSettings`,
   which reads both flags. Whether High Quality changes the picture is `beams-shader`,
   `fire-shader`, `smoke-shader`, `explosion-shader` and `clouds-shader`'s claim, with
   control frames. The menu, Options, the round running behind it, the Esc stacking
   rule and quit-leaves-the-room are unchanged.
6. **Un-park `escape-menu` only after 3 consecutive green alone runs** — done, below.
