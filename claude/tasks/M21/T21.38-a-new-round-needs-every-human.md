# T21.38 — A new round needs every human (v1)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: T21.32 (`734932f`) · Size: medium

## The owner's ruling, 2026-09-15

> *"Regarding votes, as long as all human players vote yes, restart. If not, title screen."*

## What was true before this task

- `game-server/src/round.rs::RoundController::restart_wins` restarted on **a majority of the
  votes cast** (`yes * 2 > cast`); its `connected` parameter was deliberately unread and its doc
  comment argued for abstention over veto. Votes resolved only when the `ENDED_SECONDS` window
  closed.
- When the vote failed, `room.rs::return_to_lobby` announced `round_state {lobby}` and the client
  went to the title (`GameScene`'s `round_state` handler → `exitToTitle`).
- The results screen said "A majority of the players who vote starts a new round." and showed no
  tally, because no message carried one.

## Rulings (builder, reversible — put to the coordinator)

- **R1. The rule.** A new round starts only if **every human seated when the vote resolves has
  voted yes**, and there is at least one human. Bots never vote and never count
  (`Room::human_count`). A human who voted no, or did not vote, sends everyone to the title.
- **R2. A human who leaves during the window does not block.** They are no longer seated, so
  they are not in the count, and `RoundController::forget` already drops their vote on every
  seat-freeing path (`Leave`, `DropUnready`). "Exit to title" on the results screen closes the
  socket, so for a player it *is* the way to say no without holding the room — and it means the
  players who stay are not held hostage by one who walked away.
- **R3. Early resolution.** If every seated human has voted yes before the window closes, the
  round restarts on the next tick. No reason against it was found: the outcome cannot change
  (the client has no "no" button, and a yes cannot be un-cast), the resolution is a function of
  recorded commands so a replay reproduces it, and waiting out 20 s for a known answer is only
  cost. **Not** extended to "resolve to the title early on a no": the owner's ruling did not ask
  for it and a no cannot currently be cast from the client.
- **R4. The tally rides `round_state`.** `round_state` gains `votes: {yes, humans}` during
  `Ended` (omitted in every other phase, as `lobby_state` omits absent fields). The controller
  re-announces `round_state` whenever the tally changes — a vote, or a human leaving — so every
  client sees it, not only the voter. No new message; `vote_counted` stays the voter's own answer.
- **R5. The words.** The rule line: "A new round starts only if every player votes Play again —
  otherwise everyone goes back to the title." (Bots are not mentioned: they are not asked.) The
  tally: "N of M players want a rematch". The countdown: "Vote closes in N s" for everybody —
  "New round in N s" after a counted vote is no longer true, since one yes no longer carries it —
  and "New round starting…" once the tally shows everyone said yes.

- **R6. `REPLAY_VERSION` 9 → 10** (the coordinator asked for the decision, 2026-09-15). A
  recording file ends at `restart`, and the runner replays to that file's final tick, checking
  state hashes on the way. A v9 file in which every human voted yes before the window closed
  carries checkpoints and a footer from **after** the vote; the new code restarts on the next
  tick and replaces the world before reaching them. That is the silent-divergence case the
  version exists for. No layout change and no new tag. The new rule (a majority that is not
  unanimous now goes to the lobby) changes only what comes after the file's last tick, so the
  early restart is the break that forces the bump — the note in `replay.rs` names both.
- **R7. `rematch.mjs`'s `started` predicate is throw-safe.** Early resolution exposed a latent
  check bug, measured twice before the fix: `window.__game` on a torn-down scene throws from
  `debug()` (`cameras.main` is undefined), and a throw inside `waitForFunction` rejects on the
  first poll. It used to be masked by bo's restart taking the whole window.
- **R8. `rematch.mjs`'s round is derived, not 20 s.** Early resolution also moved bo's second
  round forward by the whole window, so it now has to outlast ana's exit, her lobby's timeout
  and warmup, and the frame check. Measured at the old 20 s: bo at **`playing 0:02`** during
  the frame check (control delta 0.9), and one run red with his frame frozen. `ROUND_S` is now
  `LOBBY_S + WARMUP_S + MOVE_WAIT_S + 30`, and the frame check prints both phases and timers
  whether it passes or fails.
