# T21.01 — Vampire fangs (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T20.08** (the passive-item pattern) · Size: ~150 lines

Asked for: *"an item called 'vampire fangs'. When you pick it up, you heal for 1 hp for every
10 damage you cause with projectile weapons. If the opponent has a shield generator, you gain
energy instead of health."*

**No doc section governs this.** Self-contained; flag to the coordinator that an amendment is
the durable home.

## Read first
- `tasks/M20/T20.08-the-shield-becomes-a-carried-thing.md` — **the pattern this copies.** A
  passive that acts from the inventory, with a derived predicate rather than a stored flag.
- `crates/game-core/src/player/state.rs:311-350` — `apply_damage`, the single damage path
- `crates/game-core/src/world/mod.rs:1924-1962` — `apply_damage_log`, the warmup gate
- `crates/game-core/src/weapons/explode.rs:29` — `DamageSource`

## The three questions this task must answer before it writes code

**1. Whose inventory is read, and where?** Healing the *attacker* from the *victim's* damage
means the effect needs both players at once. `apply_damage` is a method on the victim's
`PlayerState` and does not have the attacker. `apply_damage_log` (`world/mod.rs:1924`) **does**
have the world and therefore both — that is where this belongs. Putting it in `apply_damage`
means passing the attacker in, which changes a signature the wasm client mirror also calls.

**2. What counts as "projectile weapons"?** `Delivery` distinguishes `Projectile`, `Bullet`,
`Hitscan`, `Melee`, `Flames`. The brief says *projectile*; the natural reading is "a weapon
that leaves the muzzle", i.e. `Projectile | Bullet`, and **not** melee, flames, or weather.
`DamageSource::Weather(_)` must never heal — a player standing in toxic rain is not causing
damage. State the set explicitly and pin a test to each side of the boundary.

**3. "If the opponent has a shield generator, you gain energy instead."** After T20.08 that is
a derived predicate on the *victim*. Note the interaction: T20.08 makes a held generator
absorb 25 % and cost 1 energy per hit, so the victim's generator both **reduces the damage
you deal** and **changes what you get for it**. Decide whether the 1-hp-per-10 is computed on
damage *dealt* or damage *absorbed*, because those differ exactly when this clause fires.

## Deliverable

- A new `ItemId`, **appended** (§B16), `ItemKind` in the passive family T20.08 establishes.
- Held anywhere in the inventory. Not used, not consumed, not the active slot.
- **1 hp per 10 damage** dealt with a qualifying weapon, as a named constant in
  `constants.rs` — never inlined, and never a bare `/ 10.0` at the site.
- Energy instead of health when the victim holds a generator, capped at `BATTERY_MAX`.
- Health capped at `BASE_HEALTH`: a full-health attacker banks nothing.

## Notes

- **Do not add a "lifesteal pending" accumulator field.** 1 hp per 10 damage means fractional
  carry, and a fourth field is the rule this project has paid for. Derive it, or round at the
  site and say which — a test at 5 damage twice will tell you whether it matters.
- **Self-damage must not heal.** `DamageSource::SelfInflicted` exists; a rocket at your own
  feet healing you is the exploit this note exists to prevent.
- A dead attacker heals nothing. Check the ordering against `resolve_deaths`.
- Anything added to `PlayerState` is **hashed** (`world/mod.rs:3040-3095`) and must be added
  to the exhaustive destructure at `:3331`. If nothing is stored, nothing is hashed — prefer
  that.

## Tests

- 10 damage heals exactly 1; 9 heals what the rounding rule says; the boundary is asserted.
- **The control:** the same shot without the fangs heals nothing. Without it, "healing
  happened" is satisfied by any regeneration.
- Melee and weather damage heal **nothing**, with a projectile control in the same test — an
  absence needs a presence.
- Self-damage heals nothing.
- Against a victim holding a generator, energy rises and health does not — and the mirror:
  against one without, health rises and energy does not.
- At full health the heal is discarded rather than banked.
