# T21.18 — The rest of the shader effects (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.17** (the plumbing) · Size: **one commit per
effect — do not do these in one pass**

Asked for by the coordinator: clouds, smoke, lasers, fire, explosions, *"and
pretty much anything else"*.

Each effect below is a separate piece of work with its own tests and its own
commit. They are listed in the order to do them, easiest and safest first.

## The rule, restated because it is the whole risk

**The simulation stays; only the drawing changes.** Several of these look
decorative and are not:

| effect | the part that must not move |
|---|---|
| smoke | it blocks vision — that is a gameplay rule |
| fire | it is a damage area with a radius |
| explosions | they carve terrain and apply knockback |
| rain | the drops that hurt you are separate projectiles — the cosmetic sheet is not them |

## The queue

### 1. Clouds — **off by default, shader under High Quality**

The coordinator does not like the current ones. They are real sprite artwork
today; with High Quality off they should simply not draw, and with it on they are
a shader. `setParallaxVisible` already exists in the sandbox, so the hiding half
is partly plumbed.

### 2. Smoke grenades

A **flat grey circle** today, and tactically significant because it blocks
vision. Billowing, a soft eaten edge, slow curl as it disperses. **The vision
blocking is simulation and does not move.**

### 3. Laser beams — the easiest

Lasers are **not projectiles**: `Delivery::Hitscan` resolves instantly and the
client draws a fading line. There is nothing to make invisible — the shader
replaces the line. Glow, core brightness, a little bloom along its length.

### 4. Fire

Heat haze above flames is genuinely impossible today and is the win here.
**The flames are the damage area** — `ordnance.ts` already notes it draws them
slightly larger than the hurting radius deliberately. The shader must keep that
relationship or players get burned by fire they cannot see.

### 5. Explosions — missiles and grenades

A flash and particles today. A shader gives a proper blast front, heat distortion
and a lingering scorch. **The crater and the knockback are simulation.**

## Also flat, if wanted later

The shield bubble (a plain circle), the teleport gate's portal fill (a flat
disc), the world's void edges (a hard cut), the terrain surface, and the player
hit flash.

## Tests — for each effect, every time

- With High Quality **off**, the effect is unchanged from today.
- With it **on**, the region differs — control region outside it, control frame
  without the effect.
- It **animates**: two frames apart differ.
- The **simulation half is identical in both modes**. For smoke that is what a
  player can see; for fire and explosions it is where damage lands.

## Done when

Per effect:

```sh
npm --prefix client test -- --run && ./scripts/check.sh
```
