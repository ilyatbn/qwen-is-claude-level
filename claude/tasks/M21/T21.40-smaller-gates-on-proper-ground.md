# T21.40 — Smaller gates, on proper ground or not at all

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.28** (`3cf6fef`, `e7361d9`) · Size: ~250 lines

## The owner's words, 2026-09-15

After being told about 6 % of gates and platforms still sitting on peaks too steep to fill: *"Gates on
steep peaks? Too wide? Just place them somewhere else then like a floating island or just dont place any
more if there's no proper space.. also reduce its size by 20% its kinda large."*

## What is true today (read before changing)

- `map/meta.rs::generate_full_with` chooses pads and gun platforms through
  `seat_then_top_up` over three tiers: seated and body-safe, body-safe only, and anything clear of
  objects. If even the top-up cannot reach the count, it **re-draws the whole set from the loosest tier**.
  The perched ones come from those last two tiers: 544 of 8916 pads and platforms, on 284 of 999 sweep
  maps (T21.28's sweep). Then the ground fill runs.
- Candidates are drawn from `largest_component` only, so a floating island is never offered.
- The count is never relaxed. `choose_pads`' doc calls fewer pads "a generation failure worth seeing"
  and `six_teleport_pads_on_every_scale_separated_and_standable` pins six. **The owner's ruling
  overrides that.**
- The gate art `assets/images/gate.png` is 64×71, drawn at `PAD_ART_W` 64 on a `PAD_W` 40 × `PAD_H` 8
  protected strip. `build-gate-sprite.mjs::targetSize` reads `PAD_ART_W`.
- **How a pad picks its destination:** `world/teleport.rs::destination` picks uniformly among every pad
  *other than* the one you stand on, and returns `None` on a one-pad map. `World::step_teleports` does not
  even call it below two pads (`if n >= 2`). So pads are a network, not pairs: any count ≥ 2 works, and a
  lone pad is a gate that charges and then does nothing.
- **What a pad-less map does:** `player/respawn.rs::choose_respawn_pad` falls back to
  `state::choose_respawn` (the spawn-point scan) when no pad is available.
  `a_map_with_no_pads_falls_back_rather_than_panicking` already covers that path.

## Rulings (coordinator, reversible)

1. **Gates 20 % smaller.** `PAD_ART_W` 64 → **51** (height scales with the art, so it is not squashed);
   `PAD_W` 40 → **32**. Everything else follows from those two constants: the charge ring, portal fill,
   touch slack, `padUnderfoot`, `gate-ground`, `teleport`, the sprite build, and the derived
   `GUN_PLATFORM_PAD_CLEARANCE`. Gun platforms keep their size.
2. **No unseated placement, ever.** No fallback to an unseated tier for pads or platforms. Candidates are
   every surface point the sampler accepts, **floating islands included**, not only the main component.
3. **No proper spot means fewer placed.** If fewer than 2 pads can be seated, place **none**, so no lone
   pad leads nowhere. Platforms may be any number from 0 to `GUN_PLATFORMS`.
4. `choose_pads`' doc is rewritten to the new rule, citing the owner. `six_teleport_pads_on_every_scale…`
   is replaced by tests of the new contract.

## Builder's decisions (reversible, recorded at the code)

- **Main ground first, islands second.** The sampler runs on the seated points in the largest component,
  and any shortfall is topped up from seated points anywhere on the surface. Islands take part only when
  the main ground cannot seat the count, so maps that were already fine keep their pads where they were.

## For the coordinator: bases sunk into slopes (measured, not fixed)

"Seated" (`stands_on_ground`, T21.28's predicate, unchanged here) only asks for ground **below** each
drawn column. Nothing asks for air **at or above** the base line, so a gate or platform can stand
half-buried in a hillside. On 3 maps (4242 Small, 31337 Medium, 7 Medium), 15 of 26 pads and platforms
have rock at or above the feet line in some drawn column. 8 of those 15 reach 28 px (`PLAYER_H`, the cap)
in at least one column. The worst is 4242 Small's platform at (1456, 530): 30 of 48 columns. A player
standing on the bump beside such a platform is not on it and does not mount. Requiring clear footing
would need a tolerance (a new constant). A strict version would cost a large share of placements, since
bumps are common, so it is a design call. Not taken here.
