# T21.29 — Fall damage cut to a third (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small

Reported from play, 2026-09-15: *"fall damage is still way to high. im doing pretty basic landings
and it does quite a lot of damange. reduce it by 300%. its not fun."*

## Ruling (coordinator, reversible)

A 300 % reduction is not a number, so the reading taken is **today's damage is three times what it
should be: every landing does one third of what it does now.** The formula is
`player/state.rs::PlayerState::fall_damage` = `(impact − fall_safe_speed()) × FALL_DAMAGE_PER_SPEED`,
so the change is **`FALL_DAMAGE_PER_SPEED` 0.075 → 0.025**, with `FALL_SAFE_SPEED` 480 **unchanged**
— moving the safe speed would change *which* landings hurt, and the report is about *how much*.
If the owner meant something else (no damage on "basic landings" at all), that is the safe speed,
and it is one more constant.

## Deliverable

- The constant, in `constants.rs`, with a comment citing the report and this ruling.
- **Everything that derives from it moves with it, and is read, not assumed:** the 23 references
  to `FALL_DAMAGE_PER_SPEED` / `FALL_SAFE_SPEED` across `crates`, `client/src` and `scripts`
  (`grep -rnE "FALL_DAMAGE_PER|FALL_SAFE_SPEED"`), `constants.rs::boots_fall_safe_speed` and its
  three rulings, the "23.9 health cliff edge" note in `fall_damage`'s doc, and any browser check
  that expects a landing to hurt by an amount or to kill.
- **An assertion of a different kind** (CLAUDE.md, tunables whose value matters): a test that states
  the owner's claim in numbers — e.g. a landing from a measured "basic" height (a full jump, or a
  drop of N player heights) does no more than one third of what it did at 0.075 — computed from the
  physics, not from the constant, so planting 0.075 back turns it red.

## Done when

`cargo test -p game-core` green, the value test red with 0.075 planted and green at 0.025, and
`./scripts/check.sh --changed` run with its wall time recorded.
