# T21.31 — Real clouds, and rain that falls from them (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: T21.18 (landed), T21.26 (landed) · Size: large — **split into A (clouds)
and B (rain) before starting**; each ~250 lines

Reported from play, 2026-09-15, two messages a minute apart:
- *"the clouds you created are bad and buggy. they do not move and are honestly too large. should
  have smaller clouds in different shapes and sizes and colors."*
- *"it literally rains from the whole screen when the clouds are below me.. its just terrible."*

## What is true today (measured in the owner's window, then read in code)

- **There are no clouds in his game.** The sprite clouds — 12 small tinted sprites from a 120-frame
  atlas — were retired **in both modes** at `66f3514` ("the coordinator did not like the sprite
  clouds"), and T21.27 buried their code. What replaced them is `CLOUD_FRAGMENT`, a shader drawn
  only under High Quality. His browser has **High Quality off** and is on Phaser's **Canvas
  renderer** (no WebGL context), where no shader can run.
- **What he called clouds is the far mountain ridge.** `parallax.ts` tints it `MOUNTAIN_HAZE[0]`
  0.62 of the way to the sky's bottom colour, so it reads as a pale cloud bank; since T21.20 it is
  world-anchored at world size, so it is huge and only slides with the camera
  (`MOUNTAIN_PARALLAX` 0.10). Its flat bottom is the Canvas-renderer black bar (the mountain foot's
  `fillGradientStyle` is WebGL-only).
- **Rain is a screen-space sheet.** `weather.ts`'s `ambientGfx` (T21.26) and `rainGfx` (toxic) are
  `setScrollFactor(0)` Graphics at particle depth: they cover the viewport wherever the camera is —
  above the sky, under it, over rock and inside caves.

## Rulings (coordinator, reversible)

- **Clouds exist in every render mode, Canvas included.** High Quality may make them softer; it may
  not be the only way they exist. Whatever draws them must be checked on the Canvas renderer too.
- **Small, many, varied:** different silhouettes, sizes and tints per cloud, each **drifting** at its
  own speed with the wind (`MapMeta::wind` exists). All tunables in `constants.rs`. Seeded from the
  map seed, so two players in a round see the same sky.
- **In the sky, in the world.** Clouds are world-anchored (with parallax if wanted), in a band above
  the terrain surface — never inside rock, never visible from inside a cave.
- **Rain falls from clouds, in world space, and stops at the ground.** No rain above a cloud, none
  where there is no cloud overhead, none inside rock or caves. Ambient rain comes from ambient clouds.
- **Toxic rain's picture must keep agreeing with its damage.** Damage comes from the server's real
  drops (`toxic.rs`, `world/mod.rs` step 8a); T20.05 welded the toxic sheet to the live drop count
  because *"a player saw a downpour and was hit by a drizzle"*. Where the toxic picture comes from
  must still be where the damage comes from — draw the real drops in world space, do not invent a
  second population.
- **Out of scope, raised for the owner:** whether the far ridge should look less like clouds
  (darker, less haze). Do not change `MOUNTAIN_HAZE` in this task.

## Tests

- **Pixels, both renderers** (`docs/72` §C2): clouds drawn in the sky band and moving between two
  frames with the camera still, with a control patch of empty sky; the same on a page forced to the
  Canvas renderer.
- **Variety is asserted, not hoped:** across a seeded sky, cloud sizes and tints span a measured
  range — a sky of identical clouds fails.
- **Rain is where the clouds are:** a frame with the camera above the cloud band shows no drops
  above the clouds; a frame inside a cave shows none; the control is drops under a cloud.
- **Toxic rain:** a player standing in the drawn toxic drops is damaged; one standing where none are
  drawn is not.
