# T21.09 — The backpack rows hold the effect items (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.01, T21.02, T21.03** (the effect items must exist first),
and T20.08 (the passive pattern) · Size: ~150 lines

**Reported from play by the coordinator**: *"the expanded inventory has 2 extra rows (right
clicking). when the first row is full, the second row wont fit in new items. use that row to
fill in extra items which cannot be used, like flashlights, energy shield, wings, fangs,
boots. basically all 'effect' items that arent usable as weapons."*

**Do this after the M21 effect items land.** T21.01 (fangs), T21.02 (boots) and T21.03 (wings)
are three of the five items this rule is about, and a sorting rule written against two of five
will be re-written when the other three arrive.

## Why the split is the right shape, and not just tidier

`QUICK_SLOTS` is **8** and `BACKPACK_SLOTS` is **16**, and the constant says why the bar is
special: *"`1`–`8` and the wheel select from it, and **only** from it: firing and using act on
the selection, so a selection that could land in the backpack would mean shooting something
you cannot see."*

So the two rows are not overflow — they are **the slots you cannot select from**. That makes
the rule fall out of what already exists rather than being invented:

- **A weapon or a usable in the backpack is dead weight.** You cannot select it, so you cannot
  fire or use it. It has to be dragged to the bar first.
- **An effect item in the backpack works exactly as well as one on the bar.** It is passive:
  carrying it *is* using it. It never needs selecting.

**Therefore effect items belong in the backpack and weapons belong on the bar**, and a pickup
should place itself accordingly instead of taking the lowest free index.

## Deliverable

`items/inventory.rs::add` currently takes the first free slot — `add_takes_slot_zero_first`
pins that. Give it a placement rule instead:

- An item that is **passive/effect** prefers the backpack (`QUICK_SLOTS..INVENTORY_SLOTS`) and
  falls back to the bar if the backpack is full.
- Everything else prefers the bar (`0..QUICK_SLOTS`) and falls back to the backpack.
- **Derive "passive" from the item's own kind, do not add a list.** `ItemKind::Shield` and
  `ItemKind::Utility(UtilityId::Flashlight)` are already distinct from `ItemKind::Weapon`;
  the three M21 items must classify the same way by construction. `CLAUDE.md`: *derive, do not
  add a fourth flag* — a hand-kept list of "effect items" is the thing that goes stale the
  first time somebody adds a sixth.

**Do not change `QUICK_SLOTS`, `BACKPACK_SLOTS`, or which slots are selectable.** This is a
placement rule, not a capacity or UI change.

## Watch for

- **Dragging must still be free.** A player may put anything anywhere by hand; this changes
  where a *pickup* lands, not what a slot may hold. If the drag path shares the placement
  helper it will start refusing valid moves — *share the guard, or share the function*, and
  here they are different questions.
- **The bar filling up is the case that motivated this**, so it is the case to test: bar full
  of weapons, walk over a flashlight, and it must land in the backpack rather than being
  refused.
- `is_full_for_a_held_weapon_ignores_free_slots` already exists and encodes a related rule.
  Read it before changing `add`; a placement change that quietly redefines "full" would break
  pickup refusal in a way no test here names.
- The client mirrors the inventory. Check the UI orders and labels backpack slots the same way
  after this — a server that places an item somewhere the client draws differently is worse
  than either behaviour alone.

## Tests

- A pickup of each effect item, with the bar full, lands in the backpack. **Control**: the same
  pickup with the bar empty *also* lands in the backpack — the rule is preference, not
  overflow, and a test that only covers the full bar cannot tell the two apart.
- A weapon pickup with the bar empty lands on the bar; with the bar full it lands in the
  backpack rather than being refused.
- Every M21 effect item classifies as passive **without being named in the test** — iterate the
  registry and assert on the kind, so a sixth item added later is covered on the day it lands.
- Determinism: placement must not depend on iteration order of anything unordered.
