# T21.43 — The gun platform fires while held

Milestone: M21 · Supersedes the volley half of **T21.11C** · Size: ~250 lines

## The owner's words, 2026-09-15

*"About the gun platform. Make it click and hold to auto fire like a machine gun."*

## What is true today (verified at `dd2acc6`)

- `world/mod.rs::fire_platform` fires **`GUN_PLATFORM_BARRAGE` (4) rounds in one
  tick across a fixed `GUN_PLATFORM_FAN`**, through `Projectiles::spawn` and a
  `ProjectileSpawn` event, spends 4 of `GUN_PLATFORM_AMMO` (500), and gates on the
  platform's own `platform_ready_at` = `now + GUN_PLATFORM_COOLDOWN` (0.12 s).
  A short magazine fires what is left; an empty one refuses with `NoAmmo`. There
  is **no reload and no refill** — ammo is per platform, for the whole round.
- `World::fire` routes a mounted player to `fire_platform` (T21.22) after the
  T21.30 `accepts_input` guard, so `Ended` already refuses.
- **Held-auto is a flag, not a delivery**: `WeaponDef::is_auto` reads `auto` off
  `Delivery::Bullet`/`Hitscan`. The server holds no "held" state at all — `fire`
  is a command. The **client** repeats it: `input/autoFire.ts::RepeatFire` sends
  one `fire` per weapon `cooldown` while the left button is held, fed by
  `GameScene::stepRepeatFire` from the *selected bag slot's* registry profile.
  `ordnance.mjs` holds the smg (~6/s) against the laser pistol (exactly 1).
- `platform_gun`'s def says `auto: true` but `cooldown: 0`, and it has no item,
  so the client never repeats it: **holding the mouse while mounted fires one
  volley** (unless the bag's selected weapon happens to be automatic, in which
  case it repeats at *that* weapon's cadence — the wrong clock).
- Bots: `bots::should_fire` gates on the rider's own `fire_ready_at` and bag
  weapon; the room (and `AttractCore`) send `fire` every tick FIRE is pressed.
  Bots know nothing about platforms and mount only by standing still on one.
- `platform_ammo` and `platform_ready_at` are in `state_hash`; `REPLAY_VERSION` 8.

## Ruling (coordinator, reversible)

- While the trigger is held the platform fires a **steady stream of single
  bullets**, one per `GUN_PLATFORM_FIRE_INTERVAL`, each from the next of the art's
  three barrels (`GUN_PLATFORM_BARRELS`) with a small fixed per-barrel spread.
- One bullet spends one round. The magazine rules stay: 500 per platform, no
  reload, no refill, empty is dead scenery. One click fires at least one bullet.
- Balance: held DPS within ±20 % of today's spam-click DPS, recorded in the
  constant's comment and asserted against that measured basis.
- Cross-tick state (the timer and the barrel index) lives on the world beside
  `platform_ready_at`, is folded into `state_hash` with a coverage test, and
  `REPLAY_VERSION` moves because the replayed sequence changes.

## Decisions taken here (reversible)

- **The stream is paced by the client, as every automatic is.** The client repeats
  `fire` at `GUN_PLATFORM_FIRE_INTERVAL` while mounted and held; the server's
  per-platform clock is the authority. No new command, no "held" bit on the wire.
- **The server clock gives one tick of grace** so network jitter between two
  repeats does not eat a shot, without letting a tick-spammer out-fire the interval.

## Tests

- Held N ticks fires `N / interval` (± 1) cycling barrels; control: one click fires 1.
- A held stream stops at an empty magazine and does not resume (no reload rule).
- Dismounting mid-stream stops it; `Ended` stops it.
- Two identical worlds agree across a held stream; the barrel index is in the hash.
- Held DPS is within ±20 % of the recorded spam-click basis.
- Browser `platform-autofire.mjs`: hold ~1 s mounted in a real round; server spawns
  vs drawn vs interval; control a single click.
