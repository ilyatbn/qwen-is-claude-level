- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C8

## Deliverable

A bottom-left cluster: **health** (red→green, numeric overlay, and it can exceed
`BASE_HEALTH` up to `HEALTH_CAP` — show the overheal), **energy/battery** (blue),
**jetpack fuel** (yellow).

## Notes

- The battery bar is doing real work: §B5 makes every laser shot a shield you are not
  going to have, and that trade is only legible if you can see the number fall.
- Jetpack fuel drains at `JETPACK_DRAIN` and refills after `JETPACK_REFILL_DELAY` —
  the delay must be visible, or the bar looks broken for half a second.
- The shield is a *timer*, not a pool (`docs/21` §2) — show it as a countdown ring or
  a bar overlay, not as part of the health bar.

## Tests

- Each bar tracks its snapshot field across a range of values, including the
  endpoints (0, max, overheal).
- The jetpack bar shows the refill delay rather than filling immediately.
- Draining the battery to zero visibly ends the shield (§B5's payoff).
- Rendered pixels, with a control (§C2).
