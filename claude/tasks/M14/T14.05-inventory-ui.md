# T14.05 — Quick bar and backpack (v4)

Milestone: M14 · Depends on: T14.03 · Size: ~280 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C10
- `docs/30-items-inventory.md` §2–§3

## Deliverable

- **Quick bar**: `QUICK_SLOTS` (8) square tiles, centred at the bottom, always
  visible; `1`–`8` and the wheel select; firing and using act on the selection.
- **Right-click** expands **two more rows** — `BACKPACK_SLOTS` (16) — above the quick
  bar. `Esc` or another right-click closes it. It does **not** pause the round.
- **Drag** moves a stack between backpack and quick bar and reorders within either.
  A `move_item { from, to }` message joins the protocol; the client shows intent and
  the **server is the authority**.
- Pickups fill the quick bar first, then the backpack; both full → refused, as today.

## Notes

- Inventory grows from 8 slots to 24. The `inventory` event sends the whole array
  (`docs/30` §6) — it stays owner-only, and a joiner is sent the current value
  (§A39's pattern, fixed in T9.08).
- Merging rules are unchanged (`docs/30` §2): same item, up to `max_stack`, then the
  first free slot.
- Validate `move_item` server-side — indices in range, source non-empty, and merging
  onto a compatible stack. It parses attacker-controlled input; fuzz it.

## Tests

- Drag between the two regions moves the stack, and the server's inventory agrees —
  asserted at both ends (§A39).
- A `move_item` with out-of-range or equal indices is rejected without a panic.
- Merging a dragged stack onto a partial stack of the same item respects `max_stack`.
- Selection follows the quick bar only; a backpack slot cannot be selected.
- The panel opening does not pause the round — a control asserts the world ticked.
