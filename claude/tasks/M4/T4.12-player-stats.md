## Notes

**Overheal decays.** Above `BASE_HEALTH`, health drains at `OVERHEAL_DECAY` (2.0/s)
down to exactly 100 and then stops. A stacked medkit is ~25 seconds of extra buffer,
not a permanent upgrade. The "and then stops" is the part that is easy to get wrong —
do not let it decay past 100.

**The shield replaces, it does not stack.** Using a generator at 2 s remaining gives
a fresh 20 s, not 22. It is a flat 0.5 damage multiplier with no hit points and it
cannot be broken early.

`speed_multiplier` uses `clamp01(health / BASE_HEALTH)`, so overheal does **not**
make you faster — the clamp caps the ratio at 1.0. Multiply the *target* speed, not
the acceleration (T2.07 already expects this).

Include `item_speed_multiplier` as a field or a constant 1.0 with a comment: it is
the seam for a future boots item, and having it present means adding one touches a
single line (`docs/21-player-stats.md` §3).

Health below 0 is not clamped here — `apply_damage` (T4.13) detects `<= 0` and
handles death. Clamping to 0 in `heal` or `tick_stats` would hide overkill
information the damage path wants.

`tick_stats` does not handle death or respawn — those are T4.13. Keep the split.

## Tests

- A new player has `BASE_HEALTH`, no shield, full jetpack fuel, an empty inventory,
  `alive == true`, `score == 0`.
- `heal(50)` at 60 health gives 110.
- `heal(50)` at 130 health gives exactly `HEALTH_CAP` (150), not 180.
- `heal` never exceeds `HEALTH_CAP`.
- **Overheal decays from 150 to exactly 100 in exactly 25 s**, then stops — assert
  the value is still exactly 100 after another 10 s.
- Health at or below `BASE_HEALTH` does not decay.
- `shield_active` is true for exactly `SHIELD_DURATION` after `apply_shield`, false
  at `SHIELD_DURATION + ε`.
- Re-applying a shield at 2 s remaining gives a full fresh `SHIELD_DURATION`, not 22.
- `speed_multiplier` is exactly 1.0 at 100 health, exactly `HEALTH_SPEED_MIN` at 0,
  and the midpoint at 50.
- `speed_multiplier` at 150 health is exactly 1.0 (overheal gives no speed).
- `invulnerable` is true during `SPAWN_IFRAMES` and false after.
- `tick_stats` over 3600 ticks with no events leaves health bit-identical (no float
  drift).
