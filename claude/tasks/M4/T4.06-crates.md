## Deliverable

```rust
impl SpawnSchedule {
    /// Called once per tick during Playing. Returns the id of a crate if one
    /// spawned this tick.
    pub fn tick_crates(
        &mut self,
        world: &mut WorldItems,
        map: &Map,
        now: f32,
    ) -> Option<WorldItemId>;
}
```

Behaviour:
- Every `CRATE_INTERVAL` (35 s), spawn one crate.
- Random x, at least 200 px from either wall; `y = SKY_MARGIN / 2`.
- It falls as a physics body with a `CRATE_W × CRATE_H` AABB and `CRATE_DRAG`
  horizontal drag, colliding with terrain through the T2.05 resolver.
- **Contents are rolled at spawn time**, from the `Crate` weight column.
- On landing it becomes an ordinary grounded `WorldItem` with `source: Crate`.

## Notes

**Roll the contents when the crate spawns, not when it lands.** The doc is explicit
about this and the reason is replay fidelity: the spawn tick is deterministic, the
landing tick depends on the terrain under it, which depends on what players have
blown up. Rolling at spawn keeps the `"items"` sub-stream's consumption order fixed
regardless of what happens mid-flight.

A crate whose landing spot is carved out from under it simply keeps falling. If it
reaches bedrock it rests there. No special handling — the existing physics does the
right thing.

Crates are exempt from TTL and from `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` eviction (handled in T4.03;
verify it holds here).

Crates hold 1 item, at full stack for weapons — same `spawn_count` rule as
everything else.

The visual treatment (parachute, beacon, announcement) is client-side and belongs to
the event, not here. `game-core` only needs `source: Crate` on the item so the
client knows to render it differently.

Same `"items"` sub-stream. `next_crate_at` advances from the previous scheduled
time, not from `now` — same anti-drift rule as T4.05.

## Tests

- No crate before `CRATE_INTERVAL`; exactly one at it; the next at `2 ×` it.
- The spawn x is at least 200 px from both walls, over 100 spawns.
- The spawn y is `SKY_MARGIN / 2`.
- A crate falls and comes to rest grounded on a floor.
- A crate lands on the terrain surface, not inside it.
- A crate dropped over a pit falls into the pit.
- **Contents are fixed at spawn**: spawn a crate, carve the ground beneath it so it
  falls much further, and assert the item id is unchanged from what was rolled at
  spawn.
- Crate contents only come from items with a non-zero `crate_weight`.
- A crate is never despawned by TTL, even after `WORLD_ITEM_TTL * 2`.
- A crate is never evicted by `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS`, even with 60 items in the world.
- Determinism: same seed and timeline produce the same crates with the same contents.
- Cadence does not drift over 20 crates.
