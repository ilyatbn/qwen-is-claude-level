## Notes

Order matters: **carve first, then damage.** The carve's `revealed` slots are part
of the same result, so a rocket that exposes a buried item reports both in one
event (`docs/11-map-destruction.md` §5). Damaging first would work but would split
what is conceptually one moment into two.

Falloff, for both damage and impulse:
```
t   = clamp01(1 - dist / radius)
dmg = damage * t
imp = KNOCKBACK_MAX * t
```
Distance is to the player **centre**, not the nearest AABB point — simpler,
symmetric, and off by at most 8 px.

**Knockback is applied unconditionally** — through i-frames, through the shield,
even to a player who takes no damage. Being thrown is not damage. This is what makes
rocket-jumping work and what lets a player be launched into a hazard
(`docs/21-player-stats.md` §5).

Damage goes through the target's `apply_damage` callback, which owns the shield and
i-frame logic (T4.12). Do not re-implement either here.

`owner == victim` produces `SelfInflicted`, which is what makes a rocket-jump death
cost you a point without crediting anyone else (`docs/21-player-stats.md` §6).
`SELF_DAMAGE_MULT` is 1.0 — you take full damage from your own explosives.

**Explosions do not chain.** A grenade caught in another explosion is destroyed
silently. Chain reactions are fun and make the tick potentially non-terminating;
the seam for adding them with a depth limit is noted in
`docs/31-weapons-combat.md` §8.

## Tests

- An explosion at a player's exact centre deals full `damage`.
- At exactly `radius` away it deals ~0.
- Beyond `radius` it deals nothing and applies no impulse.
- Damage falls off linearly: at half the radius, exactly half the damage.
- Knockback direction points away from the epicentre — test from above, below, left
  and right.
- A player directly above takes a purely upward impulse (x component ~0).
- Knockback is applied to a player in i-frames who takes no damage.
- Knockback is applied at full strength to a shielded player (the shield reduces
  damage, not force).
- A dead player takes neither damage nor knockback.
- `owner == victim` produces `SelfInflicted`; `owner != victim` produces
  `Player { .. }`; `owner == None` produces `Weather`.
- Self damage is full (`SELF_DAMAGE_MULT` = 1.0).
- The carve happens: `count_solid` decreases and `ExplosionResult.carve` reports it.
- An explosion overlapping a buried slot reports it in `carve.revealed`.
- An explosion at the map edge clamps correctly and does not panic.
- An explosion with radius 0 damages only a player at the exact centre.
- Two players at the same distance take identical damage.
