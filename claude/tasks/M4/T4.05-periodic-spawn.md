## Notes

**The re-validation is the point of this task.** `map.meta.surface_points` is a
snapshot of the *pristine* map. Twenty seconds into a round, many of those points
are mid-air over a crater. Spawning there drops items into the void, where they
fall to bedrock in a heap — a bug that looks like "items stopped appearing" and is
tedious to trace.

So: sample a candidate from `meta.surface_points`, then call
`surface::is_standable(map, x, y)` (T1.09) on it. On failure, resample. Cap at 60
attempts; if none is valid, skip this spawn entirely rather than forcing it. Late in
a heavily-cratered round there genuinely may be nowhere good, and skipping is the
honest outcome.

Prefer points at least 200 px from any living player, so a spawn is something to
travel to rather than a gift. Treat this as a soft preference — try 20 attempts with
the distance requirement, then drop it — so it does not become an additional way to
fail.

Respect `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS`: if at cap, `WorldItems::cull` will evict the oldest
non-crate item. Call it before spawning, not after.

`next_item_at` advances by exactly `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` from the previous scheduled
time, not from `now`. Advancing from `now` lets drift accumulate and the cadence
slowly desynchronises from the round clock.

Uses the same `"items"` sub-stream, continuing after `place_initial`.

## Tests

- No spawns before the first interval elapses.
- Exactly one batch fires at `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL`, and the next at `2 ×` it.
- Batch size is always within `ITEM_SPAWN_BATCH` (1..=2).
- **The re-validation test**: carve away every surface point in a region, then force
  a spawn — the item lands on still-valid ground, never in mid-air. Build this
  explicitly; it is the reason the task exists.
- With *every* surface point carved away, the spawn is skipped and nothing is
  created (no panic, no infinite loop).
- Spawns prefer points far from players: with a player standing on the map, over 100
  spawns the mean distance is well above 200 px.
- Determinism: same seed and same tick timeline produce the same spawns, 20 times.
- Spawning at `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` evicts an old item first and does not exceed the cap.
- Cadence does not drift: after 100 intervals, `next_item_at` equals
  `round_start + 100 * ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` exactly.
- `tick_items` outside the `Playing` phase is never called (assert the caller
  contract in a comment; the phase gate lives in T6.01).
