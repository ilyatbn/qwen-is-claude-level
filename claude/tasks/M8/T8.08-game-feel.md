## Deliverable

- Trauma-based screen shake, decaying quadratically, scaled by explosion distance.
- Hit markers on damage dealt, damage numbers, a red vignette on damage taken.
- A kill feed in the top-left, 5 entries, 6 s each.
- Round timer, score table on `Tab`, phase banner on day/night flips and on
  weather telegraphs.

## Tests

- Trauma decays to 0 within the expected time and never exceeds 1.
- The kill feed evicts the oldest entry past 5 and expires entries at 6 s.

## Done when

```sh
npm --prefix client test -- --run feel && npm --prefix client test -- --run killfeed
```
