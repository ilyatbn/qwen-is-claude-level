## Notes

**Flip vertically, not horizontally, when aiming left.** Rotating a side-view weapon
sprite past ±90° puts it upside down; mirroring on the Y axis is the standard fix and
the only one that looks right. Getting this backwards is the most common bug in this
task.

The weapon rotates around its `pivot` (from the skin registry) and sits at a fixed
offset from the body centre. Draw it **behind** the body when facing away and in
front when facing toward the viewer — a simple depth swap inside the container based
on facing.

`muzzle` in the registry is **cosmetic** and deliberately independent of the server's
`MUZZLE_OFFSET` (`docs/31-weapons-combat.md` §2). Art can be adjusted without
touching gameplay. Do not unify them.

**The trajectory preview** (`docs/22-aiming-crosshair.md` §6) simulates the first
~0.4 s of flight using the same gravity and wind constants the server uses, drawn as
a short dotted arc. It is a **preview, not a guarantee** — it stops well before any
impact and does not show the full path. Enough to make arcing weapons learnable, not
enough to make them trivial. Hidden for hitscan weapons.

Simulate the preview through the WASM core if a helper exists, or with the same
constants read from WASM — not with numbers typed into TypeScript.

The muzzle flash spawns at `muzzleWorldPos()` on fire, immediately, before the
server confirms (`docs/42-netcode-prediction.md` §5).

## Tests

`client/src/render/playerView.test.ts` (extend), with Phaser stubbed:

- Aiming right: the weapon rotation equals the aim angle and there is no flip.
- Aiming left: the weapon is flipped **vertically** and the rotation is still the aim
  angle (not mirrored).
- Rotation and flip are correct in all four quadrants.
- The weapon is positioned at the registry's offset from the body centre.
- Depth ordering swaps with facing.
- `muzzleWorldPos` accounts for rotation and flip — assert against hand-computed
  values for four angles.
- `setWeapon(null)` hides the weapon sprite.
- An unknown weapon key falls back without throwing.

`trajectory.test.ts`:
- The preview arc for a horizontal shot with no wind is a parabola matching the
  analytic path within 1 px.
- Wind deflects the arc in the right direction.
- The arc length covers ~0.4 s of flight, not the full trajectory.
- The preview is hidden for the `smg` (hitscan).
- `setVisible(false)` removes it from rendering.
