## Deliverable

`BOT_COUNT` and `BOT_SKILL` read from the environment. The room seats
`min(BOT_COUNT, MAX_PLAYERS - human_capacity_reserved)` bots at warmup, names them
`Bot 1..n`, and drives them from the tick loop **before** `world.step`, pushing
their inputs through the same path a human input takes.

## Notes

- Bots occupy real `PlayerId`s and appear in `welcome`, `player_join`, snapshots and
  the scoreboard. Clients cannot tell them apart, which is correct — they are
  players.
- When a human joins and the room is at `MAX_PLAYERS`, **kick the newest bot** so a
  person is never refused a seat because of a bot.
- Bots must not be sent `inventory` events (there is no socket) — guard the
  per-owner emit path.
- Log bot seating once at `info` on `game::round`.

## Tests (integration, in `crates/game-server/tests/`)

- With `BOT_COUNT=2`, a joining client's `welcome` lists 3 players.
- Bots move: their snapshot positions change over 120 ticks.
- A 7th joiner with 6 bots seated is accepted and a bot is removed.
- `BOT_COUNT=0` seats none.
