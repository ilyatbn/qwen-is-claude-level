# T9.10 — Close the join-window carve gap (v2)

Milestone: M9 · Depends on: T9.09 · Size: ~120 lines

## The bug

`map_init` is stamped with `carve_seq = N` at **join**; events flush only to
**ready** sockets. Carves in that window are dropped, so the client's first
delivered carve is `M+1` against an expected `N+1`, and the gap triggers a full
`resync_map` after 2 s. Measured by the full-round check: **1–2 resyncs per client
per round**.

It has always been open, and was invisible until bots started firing and a round
went from ~1 carve to hundreds.

**Re-sending `map_init` on ready has already been tried and measured: resyncs went
1 → 4.** A second full transfer widens the window it was meant to close. Do not
repeat it.

## Deliverable

Separate readiness-for-events from readiness-for-snapshots (§A40). Carves between
`map_init` being sent and `ready` arriving are **queued per session** and flushed
in `seq` order at `ready`. Snapshot delivery stays gated on `ready`.

## Notes

- The client already buffers carves by `seq` and applies them in order, so early
  delivery is safe by construction. The gap is the unsafe part, not the earliness.
- Bound the queue. A client that never sends `ready` is dropped after 30 s
  (`docs/40` §1) — the queue must not grow without limit before that, and the
  behaviour when it is exceeded must be deliberate (a full resync is the correct
  fallback, and it is what happens today anyway).
- Mid-round join is a normal path (`docs/41` §4), and it is the case that matters:
  joining during a firefight is exactly when the window is widest.

## Tests

- A client that joins while carves are happening and delays `ready` receives
  **every** carve with `seq > N` in order, and its mask checksum matches the
  server's. Assert the checksum, not just the count.
- **The full-round check reports 0 resyncs per client.** That assertion already
  exists and currently fails; it is the acceptance test.
- Snapshots are still not delivered before `ready`.
- Falsify at the live binding site: dropping the queue flush must reproduce the
  gap.
- A control: the test must fail against a build that sends no carves at all.
