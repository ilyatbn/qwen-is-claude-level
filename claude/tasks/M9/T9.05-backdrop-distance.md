# T9.05 — Bound the backdrop by distance to rock (v2)

Milestone: M9 · Depends on: T9.04 · Size: ~120 lines

- `docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A37 (the rule and the measured evidence)

## Why this is a fourth round on the same subsystem

Three rounds tried to fix it by changing *which directions* count. §A37's evidence
says the missing constraint was never about direction: a hard-edged dark rectangle
sits in open sky, pixel-identical to the cave backdrop, a couple of hundred pixels
below a floating island — the exact case §A17's own table promised would read as
sky.

## Touch only
- `client/src/render/chunkBake-math.ts` (+ its tests)
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs` (`BACKDROP_MAX_DIST_TO_SOLID` = 160)
- `client/src/render/backdrop-real.test.ts`

## Deliverable

A third conjunct on the existing classifier: an air pixel more than
`BACKDROP_MAX_DIST_TO_SOLID` (160) from the nearest solid pixel is **sky**,
unconditionally, whatever enclosure and roofedness say.

Distance transform over the **coarse grid** — already maintained exactly by every
carve — bilinearly interpolated and thresholded at pixel resolution, exactly as
§A17 does for the hit count, so the boundary follows the rock and not the 8 px
lattice.

## Notes

- 160 is set by the widest hole the generator can make: a void at
  `VOID_RADIUS_MAX` (155) puts its centre 155 px from a wall. It is not taste, and
  lowering it starts drawing voids as sky — the failure §A18 ranked worst.
- The coarse grid is 8 px, so the transform is cheap; do it once per bake, not per
  pixel.
- Do not remove the enclosure or roofedness tests. This bounds them; it does not
  replace them.

## Tests

- **Measured on real generated terrain, all three scales, all three themes, from a
  fresh WASM build** (§A19, §A22): report enclosed-air-drawn-as-sky and
  open-sky-drawn-as-backdrop for each of the nine combinations. The residual should
  fall well below 1 %. **If it does not, the hypothesis is wrong — report the
  numbers rather than tuning the constant.**
- The specific regression: sample air 200 px below a floating island and assert it
  is sky. That case is what this task exists for.
- Voids stay interior: sample the centre of a `VOID_RADIUS_MAX` void and assert
  backdrop.
- Longest axis-aligned interior/exterior boundary run stays under 40 px — the bound
  already won in §A21, which must not regress.
- Falsify at the **live binding site**: removing the distance conjunct must fail the
  under-an-island case.
