# T9.06 — Play a full round (v2)

Milestone: M9 · Depends on: T9.05 · Size: ~250 lines

## Why this task exists

Every checkpoint in this project has been a **tour**: generate a map, fire a
rocket, screenshot, exit. Nothing in the repo has ever played a four-minute round
end to end. The builder who wrote the M9 checkpoint said so plainly — *"I can tell
you the parts feel right. I cannot tell you the round feels right."*

That is the last untested thing, and it is the one the whole design is for. Every
serious defect on this project was invisible to unit tests and obvious the moment
something drove the real thing: caves too small to enter, night that cost nothing,
an input rate that doubled your speed, a replay hash that certified almost
nothing, bots swept from every round after 30 seconds, a flashlight that could not
be equipped, world items drawn by nothing.

A four-minute round exercises the interactions none of those tests reach: the
scheduler firing weather **while** players fight, the day/night cycle turning
**during** a firefight, items spawning and being contested, deaths and respawns
against a map that has been getting destroyed for minutes, and the phase machine
running to `Ended`.

## Touch only
- `scripts/checks/full-round.mjs`
- `scripts/e2e.mjs` (register it)
- `client/src/scenes/GameScene.ts` (only if the debug handle lacks a reading)

## Deliverable

A headless check that runs **one complete round** on a real server with
`ROUND_SECONDS` shortened enough to be practical but long enough to contain the
interactions (a full day/night cycle is 120 s — do not shorten below that, or the
thing most worth testing never happens). Two browser clients plus bots.

It asserts, on the round it actually played:

- the phase machine reached `Ended` through `Warmup` → `Playing`, with
  `round_state` seen at each transition;
- **at least one weather effect ran start to finish** — telegraph, active, cleanup
  — while players were alive and moving;
- **the cycle crossed into night and back**, and terrain luminance moved with it;
- items spawned periodically and at least one was picked up;
- at least one death and one respawn occurred, and the respawn was **standable**;
- both clients' mask checksums agree at the end, after minutes of destruction;
- scores are consistent with the deaths observed;
- zero page errors, zero resyncs, and the client never fell behind the server tick.

Screenshots at: warmup, mid-round daylight, the weather effect at its peak, night,
and the scoreboard at `Ended`. **Look at them.**

## Notes

- Bots supply the pressure; `BOT_SKILL` may be raised so something actually
  happens in four minutes.
- This check is slow by nature. Put it behind a flag in the gate
  (`e2e.mjs full-round`) rather than in the default path, and say so in `RUNNING.md`.
- If it finds something, that finding is the point of the task — report it rather
  than adjusting the assertion.

## Done when

```sh
node scripts/e2e.mjs full-round
```
