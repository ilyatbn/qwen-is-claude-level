## Two carried items, both known and both deferred deliberately

**1. Frost's land does not read against frost's sky.** Measured: terrain fill
lum 116, daytime sky lum 106 — 10 apart. The backdrop-darker-than-sky assertion
landed in M7; its sibling did not. Add it:

> A theme's terrain **fill** must differ in luminance from its daytime sky by more
> than a stated margin, asserted across the whole theme table so it holds for
> themes added later.

Then separate frost's palette until it passes. Grassland and desert already do —
check and record their margins, so the constant is chosen from data rather than
picked.

**2. Two capsule rasterisers.** `shape::stamp_capsule` steps `max(r*0.5, 1)` in
floats; `Map::carve_capsule` steps 1 px in integers. This codebase has a stated
one-rasteriser invariant (§A24) and this is two. It cannot desync client and
server today — generated maps ship as RLE — but it is exactly the seam that
invariant exists to close.

Unifying **changes generated maps**, so this task owns the consequences:
regenerate the golden table, re-run the 999-seed sweep, and report the new
attempt distribution and safe-preset rate. If the sweep degrades, say so with the
numbers rather than adjusting a threshold.
