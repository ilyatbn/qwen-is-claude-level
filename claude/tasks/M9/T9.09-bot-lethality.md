# T9.09 — Bots that carry a round (v2)

Milestone: M9 · Depends on: T9.06 · Size: tuning + tests, ~150 lines

## The finding

From the first full round ever played on this project, at `BOT_SKILL 0.85`:
**two trial rounds produced 0 and 1 combat deaths.** The bots move, take items,
shoot and take terrain apart — and a human playing alone would not feel pressured.
The round is currently carried by weather and terrain, not by opponents.

`docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A5 says bots exist so that "a deathmatch with one human
is still a deathmatch". At this lethality they are scenery with a scoreboard entry.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/bots/mod.rs`
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs` (any new tunable, with its basis recorded)
- the bot tests

## What to find out first

**Measure before tuning.** Instrument a headless round and find where the kill
chain breaks. Candidates, and the measurement that distinguishes them:

- **They never engage** — count ticks with a live target selected vs total.
- **They engage and never fire** — count fire attempts vs rejections, by reason
  (out of range, no line of sight, cooldown, no ammo, blast-radius self-guard).
- **They fire and always miss** — count shots vs hits; compare the aim error the
  skill curve produces against the angular size of a 16×28 player at typical range.
- **They hit and it never adds up** — count total damage dealt vs `BASE_HEALTH`,
  and check whether overheal decay or medkits are outpacing it.

Report the numbers. The fix follows from which link is broken, and a tuning change
made without knowing which is guessing.

## Notes

- **Do not make bots cheat.** They go through `apply_input`, the same weapons and
  the same damage path as a human (§A5); that is what makes a bug in bots a bug in
  the game. Raising aim accuracy or reaction time is legitimate; reading state a
  player could not see is not.
- Determinism is non-negotiable: one `ChaCha8Rng` sub-stream per bot, same seed →
  same inputs, still asserted over 600 ticks.
- The self-guard against firing into your own blast radius should stay — a bot that
  suicides constantly is worse than a passive one.
- Consider that the arena is large and sightlines are broken by terrain: if the
  measurement says they rarely have a clear shot, the answer may be pathing or
  target selection rather than accuracy.

## Tests

- **The acceptance test is a round, not a unit**: over N headless rounds at default
  skill, the median combat deaths is at least a stated floor. Pick the floor from
  what a four-minute deathmatch should feel like, and write the reasoning down.
- A bot still never fires into its own blast radius.
- Determinism holds: same seed, byte-identical input sequence, 600 ticks.
- Bots at skill 0 are still meaningfully worse than bots at skill 1 — the curve has
  to do something, or the parameter is decoration.
