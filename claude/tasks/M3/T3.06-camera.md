## Deliverable

```ts
// backdrop.ts
export class Backdrop {
  constructor(scene: Phaser.Scene, theme: ThemeColors, mapW: number, mapH: number);
  update(camera: Phaser.Cameras.Scene2D.Camera): void;
  destroy(): void;
}

// cameraRig.ts
export class CameraRig {
  constructor(scene: Phaser.Scene, mapW: number, mapH: number);
  follow(target: { x: number; y: number }): void;
  update(dt: number): void;
  shake(intensity: number): void;      // trauma-based, decays over ~0.4 s
  readonly center: Phaser.Math.Vector2;
}
```

Layers (depths from `docs/12-map-render.md` §5):
- `-30` sky gradient, `scrollFactor` 0 — a vertical gradient from `theme.skyTop` to
  `theme.skyBottom`, drawn once into a texture and stretched to the viewport;
- `-20` parallax backdrop, `scrollFactor` `PARALLAX_FACTOR` (0.35) — distant hills
  or a starfield, tiled horizontally;
- `-10` cave backdrop, `scrollFactor` 1 — the theme's `back.png`, tiled, sitting
  behind the terrain so holes show dark rock rather than sky.

## Notes

The **cave backdrop is what makes destruction read correctly**. Without it, a
crater punched through a hillside shows the sky through it, and the map looks like
paper rather than rock. It is a full-map `TileSprite` at depth −10; the terrain
chunks at depth 0 cover it everywhere the mask is solid.

Camera bounds are clamped to the map, so the player never sees past the edges. Set
`setBounds(0, 0, mapW, mapH)` and let Phaser handle the clamp.

Use a manual lerp toward the target (`CAMERA_LERP` 0.12) rather than Phaser's
`startFollow` with lerp — the sandbox needs to move the camera freely, and mixing
the two produces a camera that fights itself.

**Trauma-based shake**, not a fixed duration: `shake()` adds to a trauma value
capped at 1.0, and the applied offset is `trauma²` scaled, decaying ~2.5/s.
Squaring makes small shakes subtle and large ones dramatic, and additive trauma
means five simultaneous explosions do not multiply into an unreadable screen.

`Scale.FIT` at the design resolution means every player sees the same amount of
world regardless of display — visible area is a competitive advantage, so this is a
fairness decision, not just a layout one.

## Tests

`client/src/render/cameraRig.test.ts`, with the camera stubbed:

- `follow` then repeated `update` converges the camera centre to the target.
- Convergence rate matches `CAMERA_LERP` — after one update the camera has moved
  exactly 12 % of the way.
- The camera never leaves the map bounds when following a target at a corner.
- On a map smaller than the viewport, the camera centres rather than clamping oddly.
- `shake` increases trauma; trauma is capped at 1.0 after many calls.
- Trauma decays to 0 within ~0.4 s of no further shakes.
- The shake offset is 0 when trauma is 0.
- `Backdrop` creates the three layers at the specified depths, with the specified
  scroll factors.
- `destroy` removes every object it created.
