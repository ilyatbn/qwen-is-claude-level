# T16.04 — The clouds pack belongs in the sky (v5)

Milestone: M16 · Depends on: T15.03 · Size: ~120 lines

## Read first
- `docs/73-amendments-v5.md` §D0
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C14

## Why

The `clouds` pack is 120 cloud sprites plus 5 lightning, mean opaque bounds 124 × 58
at fill **0.40** — wispy, and nothing is gained by stamping a cloud into terrain.
T15.03 draws procedural cloud blobs; real sprites are better, and they are already
here.

## Deliverable

T15.03's cloud layer draws from `Clouds_white`, `Clouds_gray` and `Clouds_black`,
picked by the current sky phase (§A4) — white by day, grey at dusk, black at night
and during a storm. Drift, wrap and parallax are unchanged.

## Notes

- Tint with the sky phase as §C14 requires, or you get bright daytime clouds at
  midnight — the §A32 mistake of judging a palette in one lighting condition.
- Three shape families exist per colour (`Shape1`, `Shape2`, …); pick per cloud from
  the map seed so a seed always looks the same.
- **`Lightning` (5 sprites) is not part of this task.** It is a natural fit for a
  future storm effect and belongs with the weather scheduler, not the cloud layer.
  Note it and leave it.

## Tests

- Cloud choice is seeded: same seed → same clouds.
- Cloud tint at night differs from noon — **sampled pixels**, with a control (§C2).
- Missing atlas falls back to the procedural blobs from T15.03 and logs once.

## Done when

```sh
npm --prefix client test -- --run clouds && node scripts/e2e.mjs living-sky
```
