# T11.09 — Balance the arsenal by measurement

Milestone: M11 · Depends on: T11.03–T11.08 · Size: measurement, then a tuning commit

## Read first
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B7 ("balance is measured, not asserted")
- `docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A19 (a threshold measured under narrower conditions than it ships under)

## Why

Twenty-two weapons cannot be balanced by inspection, and §B7's table is a starting
point, not a result. This project has already learned twice that a number chosen by
eye and validated on one case is wrong under the conditions it ships in.

## Touch only
- `scripts/checks/balance.mjs` or `crates/game-core/tests/balance.rs`
- `crates/game-core/src/weapons/defs.rs` (the tuning commit)
- `crates/game-core/src/items/registry.rs` (spawn weights)

## Deliverable

Headless bot rounds reporting, per weapon: **damage per second while held**, **kills
per pickup**, and **pick rate**. Any weapon more than 2× the median on any axis, or
below half, is reported **with its numbers**.

Then one tuning commit that moves the outliers, with the before/after table in the
commit message.

## Notes

- Run enough rounds that the numbers mean something, and **state the sample size**.
  A weapon picked three times has no measurable kill rate.
- Control for exposure: a weapon that spawns rarely will show few kills for reasons
  that have nothing to do with balance. Report per-pickup, not per-round.
- **Do not balance to make every weapon identical.** A hammer should be terrible at
  range and a laser SMG should be poor at digging. The check is for weapons that are
  never worth picking up, or always worth picking up over everything else.
- If the measurement says §B7's table is fine, say so — that is a valid result and
  it is what the sample size is for.

## Tests

- The measurement is reproducible: two runs agree within a stated tolerance.
- Every weapon in the registry appears in the report (a weapon that never spawns is
  itself a finding).
- The report fails loudly if a weapon is unobtainable — zero pickups across all
  rounds means its spawn weight is wrong, not that it is balanced.

## Done when

```sh
cargo test -p game-core --release --test balance -- --ignored --nocapture
```
