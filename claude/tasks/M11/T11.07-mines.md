## Deliverable

A placeable mine: arms after 1.0 s, triggers on a non-owner within 36 px, 60 damage
over a 48 px blast, despawns at 90 s, destructible by explosions.

## Notes

- **It ignores its owner**, or placing one is suicide. But it *does* damage the
  owner when it detonates — laying a mine and standing on it is your problem.
- Destructible by explosions is what stops a map filling with them, and it makes
  clearing a chokepoint a real play.
- A mine must be **visible** at close range and subtle at distance — a blinking
  light that reads at 40 px and not at 300. Invisible instant death is not fun; a
  trap you could have spotted is.
- It falls if the ground under it is carved (the §A39 lesson: items that hang in
  the air are a lie).

## Tests

- Arms only after `arm_time` — assert both sides of the boundary.
- Ignores the owner's approach; triggers on another player; triggers exactly at
  `trigger_radius` and not at `+1`.
- Detonating damages the owner if they are in the blast.
- An explosion nearby destroys it without detonating it twice.
- It falls when its support is carved away.
