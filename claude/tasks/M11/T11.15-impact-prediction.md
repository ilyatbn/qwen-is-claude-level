# T11.15 — Bots aim thrown weapons at where they land (v3)

Milestone: M11 · Depends on: T11.14 · Size: ~140 lines

## Read first
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B26
- `docs/22-aiming-crosshair.md` §6 (the client already does this maths)

## The finding

T11.14's throw guard tests distance to the **target**. A molotov is ballistic:
thrown uphill or into terrain it falls short, **onto the thrower**, and no
target-distance guard can see that. It is the residual cause of the only weapon
still failing §B24 — molotov at 1.54 self-harm per bot-second against a class
median of 0.68.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/bots/mod.rs`
- `crates/game-core/src/weapons/` if the arc simulation belongs there to be shared
- the bot tests and the balance harness

## Deliverable

Before throwing an arcing weapon, simulate its arc against the mask and guard on
**the predicted impact point**, not on the target's position. Refuse the throw when
the impact lands within the hazard's own reach of the thrower.

## Notes

- **The maths already exists**: the client's trajectory preview walks the same
  gravity and wind constants (`docs/22` §6). Share it rather than writing a second
  arc simulator — this codebase has paid twice for a re-derived `substeps`.
- Simulate at the same sub-step as the real projectile, or the prediction and the
  projectile will disagree exactly where terrain is tight, which is where it matters.
- Cap the simulated flight — a prediction that runs to `PROJECTILE_MAX_LIFETIME`
  every tick for every bot is a tick-budget problem.
- This is not cheating (§A5): a human sees the arc and the ground. Predicting your
  own throw is not privileged information.
- **It should also make bots able to use arcing weapons uphill at all**, which is a
  capability they do not currently have.

## Tests

- A bot on flat ground throws at a distant target; the predicted impact is within a
  stated tolerance of where the projectile actually lands. **This is the test that
  matters** — a predictor that disagrees with the simulation is worse than none.
- A bot facing a wall at 30 px does not throw a molotov.
- A bot on a slope below its target does not throw one that will roll back.
- **Acceptance: molotov self-harm falls below the §B24 class median (0.68)**,
  measured per **throw** as well as per bot-second (§B26 — a per-time metric
  conflates "dangerous per use" with "used more"), aggregated across ≥8 seeds.
- Determinism holds: same seed, byte-identical inputs over 600 ticks.
