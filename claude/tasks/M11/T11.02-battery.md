## Deliverable

`PlayerState.battery: f32` in `0..=BATTERY_MAX`. Energy weapons spend it per shot;
the shield drains `SHIELD_DRAIN` per second while up and **ends early at zero**;
energy hits on a shielded target use `LASER_SHIELD_MULT` instead of
`SHIELD_DAMAGE_MULT` and drain `LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN` from the victim.

## Notes

- One resource shared by shields and energy weapons: **every laser shot is a shield
  you are not going to have.** That tension is the whole mechanic and it needs no
  UI beyond a bar.
- Energy weapons have **no stack count** — a laser with no battery is a paperweight,
  and the fire-validation order in `docs/30` §4 must reject on battery the same way
  it rejects on ammo.
- `SHIELD_DURATION` stays the maximum; battery is what usually ends it first.
- Battery is **not** restored on respawn beyond the normal inventory clear — check
  `docs/21` §4 and be explicit about which it is, in a test.

## Tests

- Firing an energy weapon with insufficient battery is rejected and spawns nothing.
- A shield with a full battery lasts `SHIELD_DURATION`; with 10 battery it ends at
  5 s (`SHIELD_DRAIN` 2.0) — assert the early end, not just the duration.
- An energy hit on a shielded player deals `dmg * LASER_SHIELD_MULT` and reduces the
  victim's battery by `LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN`; a ballistic hit deals
  `dmg * SHIELD_DAMAGE_MULT` and does not.
- Draining a victim's battery to zero ends their shield — the mechanic's payoff,
  asserted end to end.
- Battery clamps at both ends; a pack at 80 gives `BATTERY_MAX`, not 130.
