## Deliverable

A `Cone` weapon: 14 dps over 150 px at 0.55 rad, leaving burning ground that uses
the existing `LAVA_BURN_*` hazard.

## Notes

- **Reuse the lava burn hazard**, do not write a second fire system — one damage
  path, one set of numbers, one set of tests (§A24).
- It carves nothing. Fire does not dig, and that is what stops it being strictly
  better than the arsenal it competes with.
- Area denial is the point: burning ground you leave behind shapes where people can
  walk for the next few seconds.
- At night, fire is a light source — feed it to the lightmap like every other
  emitter.

## Tests

- Damage scales with time in the cone; a target at `range + 1` takes none.
- A target behind a wall inside the cone takes none (control: same geometry without
  the wall).
- Burning ground appears, damages, and expires at `LAVA_BURN_DURATION`.
- The cone consumes ammo per tick of fire at the stated rate, and stops at zero.
