## Deliverable

`laser_pistol` and `laser_smg` per §B7, drawing ammo from the battery (§B5) rather
than a stack.

## Notes

- Energy tracers must **look different** from ballistic ones — a different colour
  and a brighter core — because their behaviour against shields is different and the
  player has to be able to tell what is hitting them.
- They light their own path like every tracer (§A3), and at night that is most of
  what makes them readable.
- Longer range and no gravity; they are the precision option against a turtle.

## Tests

- Firing spends exactly the table's battery per shot.
- Against a shielded target: `LASER_SHIELD_MULT` damage and `LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN`
  drain, versus a ballistic control on the same target proving the difference.
- Zero battery rejects the shot and spawns no tracer (assert the effect, not just
  the rejection).
