# T11.16 — Make players meet (v3)

Milestone: M11 · Depends on: T11.13 · Size: measurement, then a tuning commit

## Read first
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B27
- `docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A1 (why the map is large in the first place)

## The finding

**Five of eight seeds contain a fight at all.** Every per-weapon number in this
project was measured with 4 bots and a 320 px sight radius on maps up to
4096 × 2048, and that single fact is why the balance harness must arm every bot with
one weapon and sweep the ground flat.

It is a gameplay problem before it is a measurement one: a player alone with bots on
a large map spends most of a round not meeting anyone. §A5 says bots exist so one
human is still a deathmatch.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs`
- `crates/game-server/src/config.rs` (default bot count)
- the balance harness, for the measurement

## Measure first — this is the whole task

**Time-between-encounters** as a function of each lever, aggregated across ≥8 seeds
with the sample size stated. The current numbers cannot distinguish *"weapons are
balanced"* from *"nobody met anybody"*, and no tuning is meaningful until they can.

| lever | effect | risk |
|---|---|---|
| More bots per room | more encounters per unit time | tick cost — but §B2 measured 128 rooms at 0.12 % of half a budget, so there is room |
| Smaller default scale | shorter distances | undoes §A1's "discover the map as you go" |
| Denser item spawns | items pull players to the same places | §B17 — weights are a budget, and T11.13 just moved density |

## Notes

- **Do not simply shrink the map.** §A1 made it large deliberately, and the camera
  zoom, the minimap and the cave system all exist because of that decision. If the
  measurement says scale is the dominant term, say so with numbers and propose the
  trade explicitly rather than quietly reverting a design choice.
- More bots is the cheapest lever and the one with measured headroom. `MAX_PLAYERS`
  is 6; a human joining a full room already kicks the newest bot (T6.15), so raising
  the bot count costs a human nothing.
- Item density was just moved by T11.13 — re-measure rather than assuming its
  numbers still hold.

## Tests

- Time-between-encounters falls measurably against the baseline, across ≥8 seeds.
- **Rounds containing at least one fight rises from 5-of-8** toward all of them.
- Tick p99 stays inside the §B2 budget at the new bot count, measured, with the load
  conditions stated (§A38).
- The balance table is re-run afterwards: if encounters change, **every per-weapon
  number changes**, and the table in §B7 must be re-reported rather than assumed.
