## Deliverable

Four thrown weapons per §B7: `airburst` (bursts at apex or 1.2 s into 9 downward
energy pellets), `smoke` (radius 110 for 8 s, no damage, multiplies FoV by
`FOV_SMOKE_MULT`), `molotov` (6 fire patches), `toxic_grenade` (radioactive zone,
no terrain damage).

## Notes

- **Reuse the existing hazards**: molotov is `LAVA_BURN_*`, toxic is the toxic-rain
  puddle. One damage path per effect, already tested (§A24).
- Smoke is the only one with **no damage at all** — it is pure information denial,
  and it stacks with night the way fog does (`docs/14` §3).
- Airburst pellets are energy, so they interact with shields per §B5.
- Explosions do not chain (`docs/31` §5): a grenade caught in a blast is destroyed
  silently. That rule now covers four more things — test it for each.

## Tests

- Airburst bursts at apex when it never lands, and at 1.2 s when it does; exactly
  `AIRBURST_PELLETS` pellets in a downward fan; pellets do not spawn pellets.
- Smoke deals zero damage over its whole life (with a control proving the same
  harness detects damage from a molotov).
- Smoke multiplies FoV by exactly `FOV_SMOKE_MULT` inside it, and restores it after.
- Molotov leaves 6 patches that damage and expire.
- Toxic leaves the mask **byte-identical** — no terrain damage, the same assertion
  toxic rain already carries.
- None of the four chain-detonates another.
