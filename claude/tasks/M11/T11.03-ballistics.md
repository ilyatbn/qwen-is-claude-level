## Deliverable

`pistol`, `revolver`, `deagle`, `machinegun` exactly per §B7's table, each a
registry item with spawn/crate/buried weights, a sprite, a muzzle and a pivot.

## Notes

- Every one of these **carves** (§A3: every shot digs). The carve radius is in the
  table and is part of what distinguishes them — a deagle opens a hole a pistol
  does not.
- Recoil, muzzle flash and the tracer are already generic (T4.15); wire them, do not
  reimplement per weapon.
- `docs/30` §7's registry-integrity test must still pass with the larger table: ids
  unique, keys unique, every sprite non-empty, every `Weapon(id)` resolving.

## Tests

- Each weapon's damage, range, cooldown and carve radius match §B7 — a table-driven
  test, so adding a weapon without numbers fails.
- Sustained fire breaches a thin wall in a predictable number of hits, per weapon.
- Spread of 0 is exactly straight; nonzero spread stays within its bound over 1000
  shots, and is deterministic from the seed.
- Registry integrity over the whole arsenal.
