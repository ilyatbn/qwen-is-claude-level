# T11.14 — Bots walk into their own fire (v3)

Milestone: M11 · Depends on: T11.09 · Size: ~120 lines

## The finding

T11.09 measured molotov and toxic as **the two worst things in the game**: 0.46
damage dealt against **1.68** self-inflicted, and 0.60 against **1.79**. Three times
more harm to their user than to anyone else.

That is not a weapon-balance problem. The bot's blast guard checks a **blast
radius** and knows nothing about a hazard that *lingers* — so a bot throws a
molotov, walks into the fire it just made, and burns. The flamethrower has the same
root cause: T11.09 tried widening its range and measured it **worse** (0.37 → 0.30
dealt, self-damage 0.28 → 0.44), then reverted, because tuning the weapon treats the
symptom.

Three weapons currently look bad for a reason that has nothing to do with them.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/bots/mod.rs`
- the bot tests, and the balance harness for the after-numbers

## Deliverable

Bots avoid **standing hazards**, not just blast radii: burning ground, toxic zones,
lava burn. Two parts, and the second matters more:

- **Do not throw** a lingering-hazard weapon at a target so close that the hazard
  will cover your own position.
- **Do not walk into** an existing hazard. Bots path toward targets and items with
  no idea the ground is on fire.

## Notes

- The hazards already exist as queryable state (`BurnField`, the toxic zones). This
  is a bot **perception** gap, not a new mechanic — do not add a second hazard
  registry.
- **Bots must not cheat** (§A5). Knowing where a visible fire is, is not cheating; a
  human sees it. Knowing about a hazard outside their sight radius is.
- Keep the existing blast-radius guard. This is an additional check, and a bot that
  suicides constantly is worse than a passive one.
- Determinism is unchanged: one sub-stream per bot, same seed → byte-identical
  inputs over 600 ticks.

## Tests

- A bot standing in fire moves out of it — with a control proving the same bot in the
  same position with no fire does *not* move away, or the test passes for a bot that
  wanders.
- A bot does not throw a molotov at a target within the hazard's own radius.
- **The acceptance test is the balance table, on the criteria in §B24** — the
  original "self-damage below damage dealt" is **withdrawn**, because both sides now
  avoid the fire and a zone weapon that denies space successfully damages nobody.
  Measure instead: self-harm below the arsenal median's, **denied-ground-seconds**
  (hazard area × duration over *walkable* surface), and enemy path deflections.
  Aggregate across ≥8 seeds with the sample size stated.
- The flamethrower's self-damage falls without changing its range — that is what
  distinguishes fixing the cause from tuning the symptom.
- Determinism holds.
