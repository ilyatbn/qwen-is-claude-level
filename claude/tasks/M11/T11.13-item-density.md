# T11.13 — Make "tons of weapons" true of a single round (v3)

Milestone: M11 · Depends on: T11.09 · Size: measurement, then a tuning commit

## The finding

From T11.09's balance measurement: roughly **12–15 item spawns per round against 24
item types**, so a round shows you about **half** the arsenal. "Tons of weapons" is
true of the game and not yet true of any single round of it — and a weapon you never
meet is, from the player's seat, a weapon that does not exist.

`INITIAL_ITEMS` and `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` are the levers. Both sat outside T11.09's
scope, which is why this is its own task rather than a quiet edit.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs` (per-scale `INITIAL_ITEMS`, `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL`)
- `crates/game-core/src/items/spawn.rs` if the distribution needs work
- the balance harness, to report the new numbers

## What to measure before changing

- **Distinct item types seen per round**, aggregated across seeds — the number this
  task exists to move.
- **Items alive at once**, against `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` (40). Raising spawn rate into a
  cap that then evicts is churn, not density.
- **Time-to-first-weapon** from spawn. A round where you punch for forty seconds is
  a different complaint from a round that shows you only half the arsenal.

## Notes

- **Density is not the same as weight** (§B17). Weights decide *which* item; these
  constants decide *how many*. Raising a weight cannot fix this, and raising density
  does not need any weight to change.
- Watch the interaction with `WORLD_ITEM_TTL` (90 s) and `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` (40): more
  spawns with the same TTL means more simultaneous items, and the cap evicts the
  **oldest non-crate** first, so a too-high rate quietly deletes the thing that
  spawned two minutes ago rather than adding to it.
- Do not solve it by making every item spawn: scarcity is what makes a supply crate
  worth contesting. The target is that a round *shows* you the arsenal's variety, not
  that it hands you all of it.

## Tests

- Distinct types per round rises measurably against the T11.09 baseline, aggregated
  across at least 8 seeds with the sample size stated (§B17).
- Items alive at once stays clear of `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS`, so eviction is rare rather
  than routine — assert the eviction count, not just the live count.
- The initial placement invariants from `docs/32` §2 still hold: on valid surface
  points, 96 px apart, 64 px from any spawn.
