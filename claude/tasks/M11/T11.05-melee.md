## Deliverable

Five melee weapons per §B7, differentiated by reach, arc, cooldown, knockback and
carve — a whip reaches, a hammer throws you, an axe digs.

## Notes

- **No ammo.** They never run out, which is what makes them the floor of the
  arsenal rather than a novelty.
- Knockback is the interesting axis: a bat that launches someone off a ledge into a
  crater is a kill the damage number does not explain. Knockback applies through
  i-frames and shields (`docs/21` §5).
- The swing needs a visible arc with a short lifetime — a melee hit you cannot see
  reads as damage from nowhere.
- Axe and hammer **carve**, so melee is also a digging tool when you are out of
  everything else.

## Tests

- Reach and arc per the table, with the boundary asserted on both sides.
- Knockback direction is away from the attacker, and magnitude matches the table.
- A hammer hit on a shielded target still applies full knockback.
- The swing hits **every** player in the arc, not just the first.
- Cooldown gates the swing rate exactly.
