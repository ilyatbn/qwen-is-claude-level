## Deliverable

`Delivery` gains `Melee`, `Cone` and `Placed` per §B6. Each resolves through the
**existing** `explode`/`apply_damage` path — no new damage plumbing.

## Notes

- **Melee** sweeps an arc centred on the aim angle, hits every player whose AABB
  intersects it, and **carves** its `blast_radius`. No ammo, cooldown only. It is
  the answer to running out of everything and must never be worthless.
- **Cone** applies `dps` per tick inside the cone and leaves burning ground,
  reusing the lava-burn hazard (`docs/13` §5) rather than a second fire system. It
  carves nothing — fire does not dig.
- **Placed** arms after `arm_time`, detonates when a player **other than the owner**
  enters `trigger_radius`, despawns at `lifetime`, and is destructible by
  explosions — which is what stops a map filling with mines.
- Everything that moves uses `physics::substeps` (§A24) — this project has had the
  same tunnelling bug twice from a local re-derivation.
- `explode` takes its `BlastSource` from the caller (§A20), so a mine kill is
  attributed to whoever placed it, not to "meteor".

## Tests

- Melee hits a player inside the arc and misses one just outside it, on both sides;
  it carves exactly `blast_radius`; it works with an empty inventory.
- Melee does **not** hit through a wall (a control: the same geometry without the
  wall does hit).
- Cone damage scales with time in the cone; a player at `range + 1` takes none;
  the cone leaves burning ground that expires.
- A mine ignores its owner, triggers on another player, arms only after `arm_time`
  (assert both sides of the boundary), despawns at `lifetime`, and is destroyed by
  a nearby explosion without detonating twice.
- Determinism: same seed and inputs, byte-identical results over 600 ticks.
