# T23.26C — Bots keep moving: dodge meteors, wings hunt, the jetpack is a tank

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-01, after watching T23.26's bots · Supersedes T23.26 C's meteor cover · Pairs with T23.26B

> *"bots are now moving better but are sometimes randomly stuck not moving at all (i think its related to a meteor
> shower, if you taught them to hide i dont like it they should still be moving and attempting to dodge meteors (not
> always succeeding), in particular when they are near each other, it just looks like they are standing and
> constantly digging or something. bot 6 now got wings and hes just hovering doing nothing most of the time instead of
> looking for enemies. they also dont understand jetpack has a limit and then they get stuck when they dont have
> enough jetpack and keep trying to use it and they will never reach anything. they should know to wait to recharge it
> a bit or go a different direction or give up and maybe find a teleport to use."*

**Spec:** this narrows `docs/78` §A2's cover clause — the coordinator will amend it: meteors are **dodged on the
move**, not hidden from; a hurt bot may still break contact to cover.

## Do
1. **Meteors: dodge, don't hide.** Remove meteor cover-seeking (keep the hurt-bot cover). During a shower a bot keeps
   its goal (fighting, hunting, shopping) and **steers away from predicted impact points** of meteors in flight
   (`effects/meteor.rs` — positions and velocities are in the world; predict the landing x), imperfectly: a reaction
   lag scaled by skill and a horizon of a second or so, so some get hit. Two bots near each other keep fighting.
   No standing still, no digging in, because of a shower.
2. **"Standing and constantly digging."** Find every way a bot can stand still for > 2 s while it has a goal: a dig
   loop that never opens the cell (aim, reach, a protected cell, a rock regrowing?), a dig against an enemy's position,
   cover it never leaves. Instrument a watched match (counter: seconds still while goal ≠ none, per cause) and drive
   it to ~0. Each cause gets a test.
3. **Winged bots hunt.** A bot that picks up wings must use them to reach enemies and items (route through open air —
   wings are flight without fuel limits, so nav's air edges for a winged body are cheap), not hover. Test: a winged
   bot with an enemy 1000 px away across a gap reaches it.
4. **The jetpack is a tank.** Nav already prices fuel; the follower must obey it: never press jet below what the
   current edge needs; when the next jet edge needs more fuel than it has, **land and wait** for the refill (on a
   ledge, out of the void), or **re-plan** another way, or — if every route is out of reach — **give up the goal**
   (mark it unreachable for a while) and pick another. **Teleports** (gates) become nav edges: entering a gate is a
   cheap edge to its paired exit, so a bot uses one when it is the faster way. Tests: a ledge above the tank's reach
   with a gate route → uses the gate; without one → gives up within N s and does something else; a climb that needs
   one refuel → waits on a ledge and makes it.
5. **Learn from the owner's play.** Replays of the owner playing are in `.run/owner-replays/`, and screen frames + a
   4 Hz state log in `.run/owner-recording/` (`frames/`, `frames.jsonl`, `state.jsonl`). Extract how a human moves
   (jet duty cycle and refuel waits, how often and where he digs, routes over walls, teleport use, meteor response,
   distance kept in fights) — a `replay --player-stats <id>` mode or a small analysis in `tests/` — and tune toward
   it where the bots differ. Put the human-vs-bot table in the journal.

## Proof
Per item, a scenario test with its planted-red control (T23.26 D's style). Population, 8 seeds: **seconds still with a
goal** per bot-minute (new; must be near 0), stuck time, reach rate, kills/round, void deaths, meteor deaths (some, not
zero, not more than before), winged bots' kills. Then watch it on `make watch` (plain and frenzied) and look.
