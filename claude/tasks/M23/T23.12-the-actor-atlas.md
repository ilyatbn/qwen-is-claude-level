# T23.12 — The actor atlas: everything alive is drawn by code, per frame

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.03 · Size: ~250 lines · Rulings: R10, R12

## Do
1. Port `mockup-src/e_style.js`'s drawing functions to TS Canvas2D — `stick`, `beetle`, `spider`, `bird`, `turret`,
   `gate`, `crystals`, `rocket`, `smoke`, `glow` — keeping their geometry and the F4 proportions **exactly** (the
   figure is ≈ 35 px tall at zoom 1; `PLAYER_H` is 28 → a render scale of ≈ 1.2× the hitbox, a named render constant,
   not physics).
2. Draw into **three channels** (research § 3): R = ink coverage including scarf, G = accent/scarf, B = glowing parts
   (jet flame, laser tip, muzzle). One shared atlas canvas of 64×64 cells, one texture upload per frame; cells whose
   draw parameters did not change are not redrawn (cache key = the parameters).
3. Actors draw as quads in the world renderer sampling their cell, positioned from the sim like `PlayerView` today.
   The rim lighting is T23.13's; here they draw as flat ink.

## Tests

- Browser check `actor-atlas`: look-lab F4 with rim lighting off vs a mockup render of F4 with rim off (make it with
  the harness) — Level A on actor boxes within thresholds.
