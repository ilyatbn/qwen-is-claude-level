# T23.25B — What the review of T23.25 and T23.10B found

Review of `504343a` (T23.25) and `4611b9f` (T23.10B F1–F3), 2026-10-01. Code-only: read with `git show <sha>`, nothing
run (the box belonged to a builder). Not yet started.

**Verdict.** Both commits are mostly sound. The seeing rule follows docs/14 §5 ("outside your FoV **and not inside any
light**"). Gates and crystals revealing players matches the coordinator's own F1 wording. The server does no visibility
culling (`grep -in "fov\|visib" crates/game-server/src` → nothing; docs/14 §6 says that is v1), so there is no
authoritative rule for the client to disagree with. Excluding a body's own jet light is right: `effectLights.ts::jetFlames`
emits a jet only for a container that is already `visible`, so counting it would make visibility depend on itself. The
one-frame lag is the same for the pool and the player, because both read `effectLights.last`. Only the terrain's rock
light is a frame ahead, and nobody can see that. Touch-only was respected: T23.10B's `ui/minimap*.ts` sits outside the
list but the task named `visibleRemotes`, and the builder said so. T23.25's edits all fall inside its list.
What follows is the part that is not sound.

## Do

- **F1 (medium-high: a gate kept by testing a different picture). Daytime visibility of the parachute, the melee swing
  and bullets is no longer tested anywhere, and the check's own number says the parachute fails by day.**
  `crates.mjs`, `swing-mine-fx.mjs` and `bullets-visible.mjs` now open with `query: '&hour=1'`, which is night. Every
  round opens in moonlit day (T23.11). The builder measured the day and reported without gating it (T23.25 As-built):
  the parachute's rect moves **39.7–40.2 against the check's own visibility floor of 45**, and the swing tail reads
  23–50 against `VISIBLE` 24. So the check, run on the picture players see first, says the parachute is not visible. The
  fix moved the check to the hour where it passes. That is the "do not weaken an assertion to make it pass" case in the
  task's own Do section, even though it follows the practice of T23.11's eight checks.
  **The moving sky was not a reason to choose night.** `worldRenderer-math.ts::hourFromUrl` pins day as well:
  `&hour=0` (with `u` absent the moons stay at the picture's places). `debug-mode.mjs` uses exactly that in this same
  commit, and its control held still. So a still daytime leg was available.
  Nothing else covers these three things by day. `look-day-night*` tests the sky and the terrain grade, not
  ordnance or item sprites. Checked with `git grep -n "hour=" 504343a -- scripts/`.
  **Fix:** add a `&hour=0` leg to each of the three, with a control, and either (a) a floor for day that has a measured
  basis stated at the constant, or (b) a filed look task if the day value is a real "cannot see it" (40 < 45 suggests (b)
  for the parachute). The night legs stay as they are. **This is a decision for the owner or coordinator. The builder
  reported it but did not file it, and a report nobody filed is a finding that disappears.**
  *Proving check:* `crates` at `&hour=0` goes red today at 40 < 45. After the fix it is green, or red against a filed
  task.

- **F2 (medium: fairness, spec). The seeing rule has 7 slots, the terrain has 16, and combat takes a gate's slot first,
  so a player standing in a steady gate light blinks out while a fight goes on elsewhere on screen.**
  `GameScene.ts::renderRemotes` calls `sightLights(this.effectLights.last, view, NIGHT_CIRCLES - 1)`, which is 7 slots.
  `terrainLights.ts::pickLights` ranks every non-`fixed` light ahead of every `fixed` one. The terrain lights the rock
  with `TERRAIN_LIGHTS` = 16. With 7 or more combat lights in view (muzzle flashes, rockets, burning ground, all
  common at night in a 6-player fight at zoom 1), every gate and crystal drops out of the rule. A remote standing
  in a gate's pool becomes hidden while the rock around him is still visibly lit by the terrain's lights 8–16. That is
  the "not inside any light" breach in the other direction. He also flickers in and out as flashes come and go.
  **How verified:** read `sightLights` (the `pickLights(…, slots)` cap), the `NIGHT_CIRCLES` = 8 and `TERRAIN_LIGHTS` =
  16 constants, and the `pickLights` sort key. Not measured live.
  **Fix (a shape for the coordinator to choose):** judge `seenAt` against every in-view non-body light the terrain
  lights (`pickLights(…, TERRAIN_LIGHTS)`), and cap only what the shader draws. Alternatively, raise the night view's
  circles to `TERRAIN_LIGHTS + 1` so that "the pool drawn" and "the pool judged" stay one list. The second keeps F1's
  single list and costs uniforms.
  *Proving check:* a vitest in `worldRenderer-math.test.ts` with 7 blasts in view plus a gate and a remote at the gate
  expects `seenAt` to be true. It is red today. The control is the same case without the gate, which gives false.

- **F3 (low: a field that means two things). The minimap re-derives the verdict instead of reading it.**
  `GameScene.ts` builds `dots` from `sightSeen` positions, and `minimap.ts::draw` then filters them again with
  `visibleRemotes(others, this.sight)`. `sightSeen` already holds the screen's verdict (`visible`, which includes the
  `darkness <= 0.01` day bypass and `FLAG.alive`). The two disagree in both directions. A **dead** remote inside sight
  gets a dot the screen does not draw. By day, a remote on screen but outside the sight circles is drawn and gets no
  dot. `minimap-math.ts::visibleRemotes`'s doc comment ("the minimap can never show someone the screen is hiding")
  is false for the dead case. Part of this predates the commit, but F1 now has the verdict in hand ("derive, do not add").
  Separately, a remote missing from this frame's `sampled` keeps a stale `sightSeen` place.
  **Fix:** dots are `sightSeen` entries with `visible`. `visibleRemotes` becomes that filter or is deleted.
  *Proving check:* a vitest: a dead remote inside sight gives no dot. It is red today.
