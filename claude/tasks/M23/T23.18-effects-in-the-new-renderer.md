# T23.18 — Effects in the new renderer: tracers, beams, blasts, fire, smoke, plumes

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.08, T23.09 · Size: ~250 lines (split A/B if it grows) · Rulings: R10, R13, R15, R16

## Do
1. Port `mockup-src/kit.js`'s effect vocabulary — additive `ribbon` (tracers, laser beams, rocket trails), soft
   `sprite` (muzzle flash, impact glow, plume core), `explosion` (core, sparks, debris, smoke plume), `smoke`
   trails — with HDR intensities (the mockup's explosion core is 2.0, soft sprites up to 2.5: they must exceed 1.0 to
   bloom; that is why the post chain is half-float).
2. Drive them from what `ordnance.ts` / `ordnance-state.ts` / `ordnanceFx.ts` already track (bullets, beams,
   blasts, flame cones, mines, fire/toxic/smoke zones, melee swings), plus `thrusterPlume.ts` (T22.04),
   `radiationFx.ts` (T22.09), and **whatever M22 still lands in the old renderer** (solar flare, vortex, black hole —
   R16). **Simulation halves stay** (CLAUDE.md shader rule): smoke still blocks vision exactly as before, painted fire
   still covers every damage circle, a painted blast still covers its blast radius — the existing coverage assertions
   (`fire-shader`, `explosion-shader`, `smoke-shader`) move to the new renderer, they do not get dropped.
3. Retire the Phaser effect shaders (`shaders.ts` BEAM/SMOKE/FLAME/BLAST/THRUST/FOG fragments and their quads) once
   the new ones cover them; rewrite/retire `beams-shader`, `smoke-shader`, `fire-shader`, `explosion-shader`,
   `fog-shader`, `ordnance-visible`, `bullets-visible`, `fire-visible`, `thrusters*`, `radiation*` in the same commits.

## Tests
- Browser checks: each effect's coverage-of-damage assertion (the simulation half) against the new pictures; the
  look-lab F1/F3 effect boxes vs the references at Level A; bloom present (fraction above threshold) around an
  explosion and absent in a no-effect control frame.
