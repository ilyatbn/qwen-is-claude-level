# T23.26 — Bots that read the terrain: plan a route, jet over, dig through, take cover

Milestone: M23 · Owner ask 2026-09-30 (priority: after handoff items 1–3, before the HUD/space work) · Size: large —
**split into A–D, one commit each**; D may be its own session.

> *"they are too dumb. they lack terrain awareness. they get stuck on high walls and in caves. they need to be aware
> they can jetpack above them or through open space to reach open terrain. they also need to know that they can dig if
> there's an easier or faster way through a map, or to hide from incoming players or meteors."*

**Spec:** `docs/78` §A2 (overrides `docs/74` §E10's "not pathfinding" and its line-of-sight reachability).
**Read `SWEEP-T23.26-T23.27.md` (T23.26 half) before building** — it is the recommended design and order, and it
corrects this file: (0) run the full balance `--ignored` suite and µs/tick **first** for the before table; (1) extract
one shared `bots::drive` (the drive loop is copied ~12×) with no behaviour change; add a **hop edge** (without it every
kerb costs a jet); the stuck counter must measure displacement **over `BOT_STUCK_WINDOW`**, not the per-tick hop test
(which fires on every walking second); cover depth derived from `METEOR_CARVE_R`/`METEOR_FRAG_CARVE_R`; no dig within
`PLAYER_H` of the floor (no bedrock → void); tests that assert the opposite (`closed_pocket_gives_up`,
`item_behind_solid_rock_is_not_a_target`) are rewritten under §A2, and the flee test kept as the no-cover control;
unify `JETPACK_CLIMB_BUDGET` with `traversal.rs::JETPACK_RANGE`. Space flight is out of scope.

## Today (read before designing)
`bots/walk.rs::walk_buttons` steers **greedily** at `move_to` (left/right toward it), jumps when `rise > STEP_UP` or
after `BOT_STUCK_WINDOW` of no progress, and jets only when the target is more than `BOT_JETPACK_RISE` above with half
a tank. `bots/explore.rs` picks unseen coverage cells as wander targets, abandoned after `BOT_WANDER_GIVE_UP`. There is
no route: a wall taller than one hop, a cave whose exit is behind the bot, or a target on the far side of a ridge all
end in pressing into rock. Nothing ever digs on purpose — the shovel (`SHOVEL_*`, slot 0 for every player) and the
carving weapons are only swung at enemies.

## A — a route planner (`game-core/src/bots/nav.rs`, pure, deterministic)
A* over a coarse grid of the live mask (`map/coarse.rs::CoarseGrid` or a bot-owned grid at ~`PLAYER_H` cells), from
the bot's cell to the goal's cell, with **edge kinds and costs in seconds**:
- **walk** along a floor (cell free, cell below solid), steps up to `STEP_UP`;
- **fall** down open air;
- **jet** up/sideways through open air — priced from `JETPACK_*` (drain, refill, max speed, gravity scale;
  `JETPACK_CLIMB_BUDGET` is the existing budget) so a climb the tank cannot make is not a route;
- **dig** through solid cells — priced from `SHOVEL_COOLDOWN`/`SHOVEL_CARVE` (and cheaper when holding a carving
  weapon with ammo, never inside its own blast guard). A dig edge is how "a faster way through" and "out of a sealed
  cave" become ordinary routes rather than special cases.
Bounded work per tick (node budget; a partial search resumes next tick), a cached path re-planned when the goal moves
a cell, when the next waypoint is not reached in time, or when the terrain under the path changes. New tunables in
`constants.rs` with their basis stated.

## B — following it (`walk.rs`)
Steer to the next waypoint, not the goal: walk/jump for walk edges; **hold jet** for jet edges and let go to refuel on
a ledge when the tank runs low (never empty it hovering); **dig** for dig edges — select the shovel (or the carving
weapon), aim at the next solid cell on the path, swing on its cooldown (`wants_select`/aim/FIRE, the same channels
firing uses). Fighting still wins when an enemy is in `should_fire`'s reach; a route is resumed after.
The existing stuck hop stays as the last resort, and **counts** (see D) how often it is reached.

## C — taking cover
- **Meteors** (`effects/meteor.rs`): when a shower is announced or falling within reach of the bot, go under rock —
  the nearest cell with enough solid above (plan to it), or dig a pocket into the nearest wall/ground if none is close;
  come out when it ends.
- **Hurt and outgunned** (today's `Goal::Flee`): instead of only running, plan to cover out of the enemy's line of
  sight (or dig in), heal (`BOT_HEAL_BELOW`), then re-engage. Frenzy (`DEV_BOT_FRENZY`, T99.01) keeps "never flees".

## D — proof
Scenario tests in `bots/` (carved fixtures, `flat_shelf` style — never the generator's luck):
1. a bot at the foot of a wall **3× a hop** high, target beyond → over it by jetpack within N s;
2. a bot in a **sealed** cave, target outside → digs out;
3. target on the far side of a **thin** wall vs a long way round → digs through (and with a thick wall, goes round);
4. a meteor shower overhead → the bot ends under ≥ X px of rock before the first impact;
Each with its control (the same fixture with the new behaviour planted out is red).
Population, `tests/balance.rs`-style over 8 seeds × default skill: **seconds a bot spends pressing without moving**
(new `BotStats` counter), **goal reach rate**, kills per round. Before/after table in the journal; stuck time must fall
by a stated factor and kills per round must not fall. Server tick cost of 5 bots measured (µs/tick, before/after).

### Step 4 — B: following the route (builder3)
`bots/route.rs` (`Route`, `NavStep { buttons, aim, fire, select, unstick }`) wired in `Bot::think`: the route drives
when it has a step (walk/hop press; jet holds JUMP(+UP) and centres on the column by velocity for vertical steps; rest
stands; dig selects the shovel, aims at the node's middle — across — or the cell above/below, and swings), the greedy
walking model while a search runs / after "no route" / at the end / in fire. Fighting wins (`should_fire` first);
`choose_weapon` yields to a dig step. Re-plan on a new or moved goal, a carve under the remaining steps, or a step over
2× its price + `BOT_NAV_STEP_SLACK`. Items: §A2 reachability — no line-of-sight gate where routes run; a target is
dropped once its search returns no route (`Route::refused`, a list, `BOT_NAV_RETRY`). Wander gives a route its price
×2 before giving up, and a refused wander cell is given up at once. Enemies route only when `reachable` fails.
Not routed (unchanged): space, wings, a mount.

Found and fixed while making the scenarios pass (each measured):
- **A swing uses last tick's aim** (`World::fire` reads `p.aim`, which the queued input sets only in the step). The
  first swing slashed the floor toward the goal; a dig now swings only once its aim was held a tick.
- **Lips**: a cell may hold `BOT_NAV_AIR_PX` of rock; a sliver standing up blocks a body. A step with no movement for
  `BOT_STUCK_WINDOW` swings at it, hops and leans in (`lip_swings`, counted apart from `dig_swings`).
- **Falls hurt**: standard self-damage 16 → 86 a bot a round when routes first drove. A walking bot falling over
  `BOT_FALL_BRAKE` × `FALL_SAFE_SPEED` thrusts up (now ~31; see below).
- **The void**: planned jets keep `JETPACK_MIN_FUEL_TO_ENGAGE` in reserve; no step from ground into a column with no
  floor (a node already over the void may route out through it); the greedy step never walks into such a column
  (`nav::Grid::over_void`, shared); a bot in the air over the void climbs. No dig in the bottom `BOT_NAV_FLOOR_BAND`.
- **Empty platforms**: a mounted bot whose platform is empty holds JUMP (the dismount). One rode an empty gun for 30 s.

Tests: `scenarios.rs` D1 (a wall 3 × `JUMP_HEIGHT` tall, 8 thick: jetted, no dig), D2 (sealed cave: dug out), D3 (thin
dug / thick gone round, in play) — each with the greedy control (`Bot::without_routes`) red; the §E10 item test
rewritten as `an_item_sealed_in_rock_is_a_target_while_a_route_to_it_exists` (presence: with a shovel; absence: none).
`a_hurt_bot_breaks_contact…` and `a_winged_bot_in_a_closed_pocket…` unchanged and green (flee and wings are not routed).
