# T23.13 — Rim-lit silhouettes: `lit()` as one sprite shader

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.12, T23.09 · Size: ~180 lines · Rulings: R10

## Do
1. `mockup-src/f_kit.js::dominant(lights, x, y, moon)` ported verbatim to TS; runs per actor per frame on the CPU
   against T23.09's light list, and falls back to the moon (`P.moon`) when no light is near.
2. `f_kit.js::lit` draws four passes and then the ink: far rim at `L·o·1.7` (alpha 0.35a / 0.3a), rim at `L·o`, sky
   fill at `−0.7·L`, ink. Reproduce it in **one** sprite shader over T23.12's three-channel cell, sampling at those
   offsets and compositing in that order. **Rim passes recolour the accent to rim colour and drop marker and flame**,
   as the mockup does — so the scarf is never tinted by the rim (R10).
3. Contact shadow: one soft ellipse under grounded actors (`lit`'s `shadow`), none for flying ones.
4. The **halo** for actors in dark places (tunnels, cave backs): F's `halo` option, keyed on `back` under the actor's
   box (T23.05's field), so a figure is never ink on ink.

  the far side is the control); in a tunnel the halo lifts the luminance around the figure vs the same figure on open
  ground with the halo forced off.
