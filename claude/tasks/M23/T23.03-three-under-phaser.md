# T23.03 — three.js under Phaser: one world canvas, one camera

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.01 · Size: ~220 lines · Rulings: R1, R11, R14

## Do
1. A `WorldRenderer` (`client/src/look/worldRenderer.ts`) that owns a three.js `WebGLRenderer` on its own canvas,
   inserted **under** Phaser's canvas; Phaser's canvas becomes transparent (`transparent: true`, clear alpha 0).
   Both sized to the same element; device-pixel-ratio handled once. Post chain skeleton: half-float target with
   4× MSAA → `OutputPass` (ACES, exposure from `P`) — bloom and grade come in T23.08.
2. **Camera:** each frame, after Phaser's camera has updated (`preRender` — the `living-sky` trap: laying out in
   `update` trails the camera by a frame, measured at 3–9 px), copy `cameras.main.worldView` into an ortho camera in
   mask-pixel space (`mockup-src/kit.js::orthoCam`, y flipped as `wy`). **Phaser's camera stays the only source of
   truth.**
3. The renderer consumes the T23.01 scene description. For now it draws one test layer (a flat quad the colour of
   `P.bg.skyBottom`) so the plumbing is visible.
4. Wire it into `GameScene`, `SandboxScene` and the look-lab behind a single constructor; remove Phaser's
   `pixelArt: true` / `antialias: false` only if nothing else still needs it (check; say what you found).
5. Quality tier plumbing (R14): `isHighQuality()` becomes full/low; low halves the render target.

## Tests

  same screen distance in both canvases in the same drawn frame** (count frames, not wall clock).
