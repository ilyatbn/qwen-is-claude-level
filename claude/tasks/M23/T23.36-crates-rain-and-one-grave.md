# T23.36 — Crates every 2 s (up to 20), bots go for them; one tombstone per player, glowing in their colour

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-02 (watching a Hill match)

> *"should drop way more weapon crates. … barely anything drops and the bots are resorting to shovels again. increase
> it to drop like every 2 seconds as long as there are less than 20 crates on the map. bots should "see" the location
> of crates and they want to go pick them up to get more weapons. also change tombstone behavior to only show the last
> one for a player. and add a glow around the tombstone with the same color as the players."*

## Do
1. **Crates**: `CRATE_INTERVAL` 35 → 2 s, with a new cap `CRATE_MAX_ON_MAP` 20 (crates on the map — falling or landed,
   unopened; a crate opened/taken frees a slot). Basis at the constants: the owner's ask. Confirm crates carry weapons
   (ranged ones especially) and say what's in one; if a crate is mostly non-weapons, weight it toward weapons.
   Check server tick cost and wire traffic stay sane at 20 crates (measure).
2. **Bots see crates**: every crate's landing spot is known to every bot (map-wide, like a beacon — crates are visible
   events); a bot short of ranged weapons/ammo routes to the nearest reachable crate (nav, open ground) — ranked with
   T23.26F's "go for a gun before the shovel". Metric: melee share of kills and time unarmed fall; kills/round doesn't.
3. **One tombstone per player**: when a player dies, any earlier tombstone of theirs is removed (server and client —
   one place that owns graves); only their latest stays.
4. **Tombstone glow in the player's colour** (the scarf colour): a soft halo around the stone (the night halo exists —
   tint it; by day a subtle rim/glow so it reads too). Pixel check: two players' stones glow in their two colours
   (control: the stone with the glow layer hidden).

## Tests
Unit: crate spawner — never over the cap, spawns at the interval below it; one grave per player after N deaths.
Bot scenario: an unarmed bot with a crate 800 px away over open ground goes to it (control: no crate). Population row:
crates opened/round, melee share of kills, kills/round. Browser: graves (one per player after two deaths, colour glow).
