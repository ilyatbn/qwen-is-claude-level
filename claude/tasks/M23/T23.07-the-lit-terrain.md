## Handed on by T23.03B (2026-09-26)
- **Rebuild the `render_fields_ptr` view after every `render_fields_*` call.** A full pass resizes (can move) the
  buffer, and any allocation can grow wasm memory, which detaches every `Uint8Array` over the old `memory.buffer`.
- `render_fields_full_with_wall` (wrong length) and `render_fields_dirty` (before a full pass for this map) **throw**
  now, writing nothing — call the full pass at round start, then dirty per carve.
- Cost on the wasm32 release build (V8, `scripts/bench-render-fields.mjs`): r=60 crater 2.7 ms (all scales), full
  pass Small 194 / Medium 444 / Large 827 ms — the full pass belongs in the loading screen, not a frame.
