# T23.32 — Holding Space flies up, without W

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-02 · Size: small (game-core physics + tests)

> *"make pressing and holding space (jetpack) fly up (same as shift + W) natively without having to click w. this
> feels more natural."*

## Today
`player/jetpack.rs::thrust_delta` is the one author of "which way does the pack push": `UP` gives
`JETPACK_THRUST_UP`, `DOWN`/`LEFT`/`RIGHT` the others. Holding JUMP engages the pack (after `JETPACK_HOLD_DELAY`) but,
with no direction held, pushes nowhere — the player has to add W to climb.

## Do
- While the pack is **engaged by a held JUMP** and **neither UP nor DOWN is held**, the vertical push is UP's (exactly
  as if W were held). DOWN still wins its own direction (Space+S = today's Space+S); LEFT/RIGHT add sideways as today.
  Change it **in `thrust_delta`** (the one author — `apply_thrust`, the server, the client mirror/predictor and the
  plume all read it), not in the client's key mapping, so prediction and server agree.
- Standard and low gravity. In space decide at the code (follow `docs/77`'s space thrust rules; if Space-alone climbing
  fights the space control scheme, leave space as is and say so).
- The control hints (README/RUNNING's control table, any in-game help) say "hold Space to fly up".
- Bots press `JUMP|UP` for climbs today — confirm unchanged behaviour (or simplify) and that their tests hold.

## Tests
- Unit: JUMP held past `JETPACK_HOLD_DELAY` with no direction → vertical velocity change equals the UP case's (pinned
  to the constant); control: JUMP + DOWN → the DOWN case's; JUMP on the ground before the hold delay → a jump, no
  thrust. Planted (the rule removed) → red.
- Browser: `wasd` (or a new leg) — holding only Space in the air climbs (the body's y falls over N frames), both ends
  (predicted and server position).
- Golden/replay tables: replays recorded with JUMP-only inputs now move differently — state which tests move and why.
