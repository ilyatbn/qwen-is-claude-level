# T23.30 — Map shapes: random, hill, mostly flat, multilevel, islands

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-01 · Spec: `docs/78` §A5 · Reference silhouettes: `tasks/M23/map-shapes/{hill,flat,multilevel,islands}.png`
(black = rock, grey = open space) — the **shape** to aim at, generated per seed, not traced.

> *"the current map generator is fine but i want to make it more diverse and also simpler for bots to play … this will
> make bots behave better by playing on easier to understand maps (and also be fun changes for players too)"*

Split into parts, one commit each; a fresh session may take each part.

## A — the setting, and Hill + Mostly flat
- `MapShape { Random, Hill, Flat, Multilevel, Islands }` in game-core; a lobby setting beside gravity (server lobby
  state, `codec`, `lobby_state`, the client lobby UI — `MenuScene`/`net/lobby.ts`), into `MapMeta`, `map_init` and the
  replay header (version bump, old files readable). Hidden/ignored for space. Default Random.
- **Random = today's generator byte for byte**: `golden.rs` tables unchanged (the proof).
- **Hill**: a height profile with a few hills (varying width/height, smooth), ground band otherwise near-flat, plus
  some floating islands; everything else the generator does (objects, items, spawns, pads, crates, traversal checks)
  still runs. **Mostly flat**: near-flat ground (gentle undulation), islands as usual.
- Find the right seam in `map/gen/` (`v2` is the height-profile landscape — likely the base) and say why.

## B — Multilevel
- Two grounds: an upper rock band with sky above and an open gap below it; a lower ground under the gap. No islands.
  Spawns on both levels.
- **Pads paired across levels**: every bottom pad sends to a top pad and vice versa (today a pad sends to a random
  other pad) — a per-pad destination set, deterministic.
- **Meteors only on the top level** (impacts limited to the upper band's surface and above); **toxic clouds drift
  through the lower level** (reuse the toxic hazard — moving clouds that damage while inside — confined to the gap).
- Bots: nav must know pads (T23.26E prices them) — with pairing, a pad is a known edge to the other level.

## C — Islands
- Only floating islands (varied sizes, as in the picture), high in the clouds; falling below the lowest = death (the
  existing void rule). **No meteor showers** on this shape. Spawns, items and pads on islands; every island reachable
  from some other by jetpack (check with `traversal.rs`), or pads connect them.
- **The look**: a sea of cloud below the islands (the client's cloud/fog layers, in F's style), the sky visible
  beneath them — "high up in the clouds". GPU screenshot required.

## Tests
- Per shape over many seeds: the silhouette statistics that define it (ground-height variance for flat/hill; two
  separated bands for multilevel; no ground connected to the bottom edge for islands), spawns valid, every spawn can
  reach every other (walk/jet/pad), determinism (same seed → same mask). Random's golden unchanged.
- Server: the setting round-trips lobby → map_init → replay; multilevel pads always cross levels; meteors never on
  the lower level; islands schedule no meteors.
- Browser: a check per shape that loads it and photographs it (GPU screenshots of each shape for the owner).
