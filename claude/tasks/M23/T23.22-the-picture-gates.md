# T23.22 — The picture gates: F1–F7 reproduced, and the live game compared

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.02, T23.08, T23.11, T23.14, T23.16–T23.20 · Size: ~200 lines · Rulings: § Verification

## Do
1. **Level A**, one check per reference: `look-gate-f1` … `look-gate-f7` — the look-lab renders each reference scene
   through the game's renderer and `look-compare` holds every retained metric within `look-thresholds.json`, full tier.
   Each check also runs its must-fail control (F0 for F1; the single-knob controls) and fails if the control passes.
2. **Level B**, `look-live`: a staged sandbox frame — fixed seeds (at least 4), frozen sim time, scripted actors firing
   bazooka, laser and flamethrower, zoom 1 — night and moonlit day, compared by distribution (luminance histogram,
   p5/p50/p95, 8-colour palette ΔE, saturation, edge density, bloom fraction) against the F1/F5 references, bounds from
   the F1/F2/F3 spread, F0 the must-fail control.
3. Side-by-sides of every gate and every live seed in `shots/`, and **look at them** — a metric inside threshold on a
   picture that is visibly wrong is a threshold bug; say so and fix the threshold with a new control.
