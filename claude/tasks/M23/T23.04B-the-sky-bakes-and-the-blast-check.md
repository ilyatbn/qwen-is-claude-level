# T23.04B — The sky bakes; the blast check stops reading the sky

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.04 · Size: ~250 lines · Rulings: R21 (new), R13, R14

## Why
T23.04 shipped the sky as one full-screen shader re-shaded every frame the camera moves: measured 34–38 ms per
sky draw at 640×360 on the checks' SwiftShader, match fps 60 → 37 (21 in a continuous sweep) while panning.
And `explosion-shader` went red: the blast's dark interior now sits on F1's dark sky, and 4 of 12 points just
inside the blast radius read "unpainted" against a fixed threshold.

## Do
1. **R21 (coordinator): the sky is static in world space, so it is shaded once, not per frame.** Bake each
   band and the static sky (gradient, stars, moons, rays) once per map and tier into render targets at load;
   composite per frame with parallax offsets (a few texture reads). Output stays within `look-sky`'s thresholds —
   the Level A table re-run, F1 and F5 still ≈ 0 against the sky-only controls. Rebake on tier change and seed
   change only; the redraw skip still applies. Measure match fps panning and draw time per frame before/after
   (target ≈ 60) on SwiftShader, and state the bakes' memory at both tiers.
2. **explosion-shader — fix the instrument, not the threshold** (coordinator's ruling): a ring point is painted
   when it differs from the same camera's no-blast frame by ≥ `VISIBLE`, measured so the verdict does not
   depend on the background. Keep "the painted blast covers its blast radius". Plant: a blast drawn at half
   radius must fail.

## Tests
- vitest `skyLayout.test.ts`: `bakeExtents` covers every read of every band for cameras across all three map
  sizes (clamped ± slack), both texels and zooms; whole texels; the lab baked at exactly the frame;
  `snapOffsets`. Plants: extents without the zoom factor, without the slack → red.
- `look-sky`: Level A table as before; no rebake across the pans and band isolations; one rebake per seed
  change and per tier change; bake size printed. Plant: a bake key that includes the camera → red.
- `explosion-shader`: three backdrops, control (re-photographed blast-hidden frame paints 0), 3 runs green;
  half-radius plant → red.

## Builder notes (T23.04B done, 2026-09-26)
- **Split at the seams, exact algebra** (`skyMaterial.ts` header): screen-fixed `col·K + Bp` (sun, stars, moon
  discs fold to one scale + offset), `starK` for the stars' `ps`-dependent fade, rays as `(R+M) − occ·(0.75R+0.8M)`;
  gradient and glow/haze weight in horizon space (the latter one texel wide — it reads `ps.y` only); each band in
  its own `q`. Grain and `lin` stay per pixel (the grain baked moved F1 deltaE 0.00001 → 0.00012: the hash
  scatters an ulp; measured, reverted). Band tops cropped at `clearAbove` (clamp-to-edge repeats the clear row).
- **Float32, nearest, offsets snapped to whole texels** (`snapOffsets`): half float put F5 `deltaE_cave` at 0.011
  of 0.0125 and `paletteDE` 0.089; linear filtering cost ~0.7 ms on SwiftShader and half float was *slower*.
  Texture coordinates computed at the quad's corners (per-pixel arithmetic, not reads, was the cost).
- **Numbers** (SwiftShader, match seed 4242, low tier, panning 400 px/s): 20.6 → 59.0–59.6 fps; draw 38.4 →
  6.2–6.4 ms (a flat full-screen quad alone is ~4.5, no sky ~1.7). Still: 60.2 both. Memory: see R21.
- `look-sky`'s "renderer applied" tolerance is one texel (was 0.01) — the drawn offsets are snapped.
- explosion-shader: over the world canvas alone 10/12 on this pose; over {world, black, white} 12/12 ×3.
