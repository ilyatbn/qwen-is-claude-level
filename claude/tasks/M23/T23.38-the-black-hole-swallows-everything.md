# T23.38 — The black hole swallows everything

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-03

> *"black holes swallow tombstones, items, everything. there shouldnt be anything on top of it."*

## Do
- Anything that reaches the black hole's horizon (`world/black_hole.rs` — the radius that kills players) is
  **consumed**: ground items and pickups, supply crates (falling or landed), tombstones, mines, turrets/gun platforms
  if they can be there, thrown/fired projectiles, and loose objects; nothing is ever drawn on top of the hole.
  Items inside its pull drift toward it the way bodies do (if the pull already applies to them, keep it; if not, add
  it — server-side, deterministic) and vanish at the horizon with the same pull-in visual as a body (stretched,
  shrinking into the disc). Nothing spawns inside its reach while it is open (crate drops, item spawns, graves of
  players who die in it — those get no grave).
- Server authoritative; the client removes what the server removes (events), plus a short swallow animation.
## Tests
Unit: an item / crate / grave / projectile inside the horizon is removed within N ticks; one outside is kept (control);
no spawn lands inside the reach. Browser: a space match with the hole open — the drawn set over the hole is empty
(count both ends), a pickup dropped near it is pulled in and disappears. GPU screenshot.
