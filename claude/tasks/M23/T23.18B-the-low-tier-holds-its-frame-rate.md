## Outcome (builder, 2026-09-28)
- **Measured before changing.** The cost is the GPU process, not JS: the page's main thread waits on it 78 % of the
  time (Chrome trace), JS + GC < 1 %. Per layer (frozen sandbox, `__world.drawCost`, low tier): half-float scene target
  2.3 ms, terrain 3.6 (+2.8 with 16 lights), bloom 3.0 (13 passes — SwiftShader pays per pass), fog 0.9, the rest ~3.
- **Fixed at those costs** (13.5 → 10.6 ms a drawn frame): the low tier's scene target is R11F_G11F_B10F
  (`post.ts::buildPost`); the bloom's final blend is folded into the output pass and its 12 clears dropped (both tiers:
  the same sum); the terrain's light loop skips lights out of reach and is bounded at 10 on the low tier
  (`TERRAIN_LIGHTS_LOW`, the mockup's own MAX_PL); no depth buffers; the low tier's fog and fire use fewer noise
  octaves (`FOG_OCTAVES_LOW`, `FIRE_OCTAVES_LOW`). Busy fight: 40.6 → 50.9 fps mean of 3 (journal); full tier 59.6.
- **Square halos**: the low tier's bright pass takes four taps over the 4×4 block it stands for (`BRIGHT4_FS`).
- **Decided, reversible:** the low tier draws at most 10 terrain lights (combat lights first: `Light.fixed` ranks the
  map's standing lights last in `pickLights`), and a coarser fog/fire noise — R14's "costly passes dropped". The
  low-tier look-lab report (not gating) moves 7/11 → 8/11 metrics over. Reverse: the three `*_LOW` constants.
- **The arm-and-weapon quad (the task's candidate) was not built:** the atlas's uploads measured 0.3 ms and the whole
  cast's A/B was inside the fight's noise; the target was reached without it.
