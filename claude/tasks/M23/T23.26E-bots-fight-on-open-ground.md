# T23.26E — Bots fight on open ground: less hopping, less digging, more fighting

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-01, after watching T23.26C/D · **A regression to fix before anything else on bots**

> *"bots jump nonstop now and really like digging instead of engaging one another. looks worse than before honestly
> theyre just not fighting. they should always prefer open grounds. also they can use teleports."*

T23.26C/D raised airborne time to 73 % (the owner: 44 %), drove "seconds still" down partly by **hopping in place**
(step 6, `cc291ac`) and kept digging cheap. The metrics that went up were movement metrics; **nothing measured
fighting**, which is what the owner watches. Fix that first.

## Do
1. **Measure fighting, against the owner.** New per-bot report lines (and the same from the owner's replay via
   `replay --player-stats`): **share of time engaged** (an enemy within the held weapon's band with LOS, or firing at
   one), **shots fired per minute**, **damage dealt per minute**, **jumps per minute** (jump presses that leave the
   ground), **share of time digging**, **share of time inside rock** (cells with rock on both sides / above —
   tunnelling), **airborne share**. Before table at HEAD.
2. **Fighting first.** When an enemy is visible within the band of any weapon the bot can fire, the bot fights: picks
   that weapon, holds a position with LOS **on open ground**, strafes/steps (not hops) to dodge; routes and exploration
   wait. Losing LOS → move (walk/jet) to regain it, **not dig** toward the enemy, unless no open route exists.
3. **No hopping in place.** Revert/replace step 6's hold-ground hop: a bot holding its ground walks short strafes on
   the ground; jumping only for traversal (hop edges), dodging a predicted impact, or a jet climb. Target: jumps per
   minute and airborne share within ±25 % of the owner's.
4. **Prefer open ground.** Dig edges cost far more (a dig is worth it only when the open route is several times longer
   or doesn't exist — state the factor and its basis); route cost adds a penalty for enclosed cells so routes run over
   open terrain; exploration targets prefer open cells; never dig *toward* an enemy when an open route exists. Hurt
   bots run first (keep); digging in is the cornered fallback only. Target: digging and in-rock shares near the
   owner's.
5. **Teleports.** Bots use pads: when a pad is near and the goal is far (or the bot is stuck/cornered), taking a pad is
   a good gamble even though the exit is random — price it so bots take one on a 6-pad map in the situations a human
   would (state the rule). Test: a bot far from any enemy with a pad close by takes it within N s (control: no pad).
6. **Lasers** were 0 % of fires (T23.26D report) — find why and fix.

## Proof
Before/after table over 8 seeds: engaged share, shots/min, damage/min, kills/round, jumps/min, airborne share,
dig share, in-rock share, pad uses/round, seconds still with a goal — **with the owner's row beside it**. Engaged share
and kills/round must rise; jumps/min, dig share and in-rock share must fall toward the owner's. Each behaviour change
gets a scenario test with a planted-red control. Then the coordinator runs `make watch` and looks before the owner does.
