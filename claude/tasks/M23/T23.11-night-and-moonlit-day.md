# T23.11 — Night and moonlit day: two palettes, moons that move

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.04, T23.08 (and F5 in `reference/`) · Size: ~200 lines · Rulings: R7

## Do
1. Two palettes as data: `P_night` = F1's (`variant_F1.js`), `P_day` = F5's (`variant_F5*.js` — the moonlit day).
   One blend function over **every** numeric and colour field, in linear space, with
   `t = darkness / NIGHT_DARKNESS` from the server's darkness byte (`sky-math.ts::sceneDarkness`'s fallback clock
   stays for the sandbox). Layer *shapes* never blend.
2. 2–3 moons on arcs across the cycle (positions from `world/cycle.rs::cycle_u`), star alpha follows `t`.
3. The `phase_change` event and audio stay as they are; only the picture changes.

## Tests
- vitest: blend(0) == P_day and blend(1) == P_night field by field (count the fields at both ends — a field missing
  from the blend silently freezes); blend is continuous (no field jumps > its range/10 between adjacent `t` steps).
- Browser check `look-day-night`: look-lab F1 at `t = 1` vs F1 and at `t = 0` vs F5, Level A within thresholds; in a
  live sandbox advancing the cycle moves a moon across the sky (moon patch changes, a far layer patch with no moon is
  the control).
