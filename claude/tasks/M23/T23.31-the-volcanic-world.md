## Do
1. **An open list of looks.** `WorldLook { Classic, Volcanic }` (extensible), picked from the map seed on the server
   (deterministic — a replay and every client agree), sent in `MapMeta`/`map_init`, recorded in the replay header.
   One client registry: a look = its day/night palettes (T23.11's two-palette blend: give Volcanic both ends — its
   "day" a dusky red, its night F2), terrain albedo + crack emission, sky + background layers, fog/haze, ambient
   particles, fauna. Classic is today's, byte for byte (its look checks unchanged). A dev override (`?look=volcanic`,
   sandbox button) for checks and for the owner to compare.
2. **Volcanic terrain**: F2's rock — dark basalt plates with lava cracks that glow (emissive, lights the figures near
   them a little), orange-lit edges; matches F2 at Level A in the look-lab like F1 does (T23.02's method).
3. **Volcanic sky/background**: F2's dark sky and red haze; distant layers built from the `mapideas/` idea — a
   volcano silhouette with glowing lava rivers and a rising ash plume, ringed planets, red mist at the horizon line;
   drifting embers in the air. Parallax like today's background.
4. **Volcanic creatures** as animals (the animal system from T20.10/T23.19): a **tripod walker** (three long thin legs,
   small dome, stilted gait) and an **octopus crawler** (low body, 6–8 curled legs, scuttling), drawn by code as ink
   silhouettes in the actor atlas (T23.12's style), with simple animation, wandering like today's animals and fleeing
   gunfire. Classic keeps its animals.
5. Same gameplay on both: a check proves the mask, objects and simulation are identical for a seed under either look.
