# T23.26D — Bots use every weapon, at its range, with its arc

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-01 · Builder of T23.26C takes it in the same branch

> *"make sure bots also use all available weapons. ranged, lasers, flamethrowers. … they resort to fighting with
> shovels, which is funny and works but shovels are a last resort when no ranged weapons are available. they should be
> aware of the ranges of weapons, and the projectiles skews (like grenades and rockets shoot at an arc)"*

## Do
1. **Why the shovel wins today.** Measure it first: over 8 seeds, the share of a bot's fire ticks per weapon kind, and
   the share of ticks a bot holds the shovel **while carrying a firable ranged weapon**. Suspects: T23.26's dig steps
   select the shovel and nothing hands the weapon back; `arms.rs::choose_weapon`'s scoring (melee DPS at point blank
   beating a gun the bot then closes to shovel range for); the nav follower closing to `stop_within` of a melee reach;
   energy weapons refused at low battery. Fix at the cause.
2. **Range-aware engagement.** Each weapon's useful band (from `weapons/defs.rs` — `range`, `Delivery`, blast radius
   and `BOT_BLAST_GUARD`, flame reach `BOT_FLAME_REACH_SCALE`) decides both **which weapon** and **where to stand**: a
   bot with a rifle holds at range, with a flamethrower closes in, with a rocket keeps outside its own blast. The shovel
   is chosen only when no ranged weapon can fire (no ammo, no charge, out of every band).
3. **Arcs and lead.** Projectiles with gravity (grenades, rockets if they arc, molotovs, mines thrown) are aimed by
   solving the ballistic arc from the real projectile constants (speed, gravity scale — the same physics `World`
   steps), choosing the lower arc that clears terrain (probe the arc against the mask; else the high arc; else don't
   fire); fast straight rounds lead a moving target by its velocity. Skill keeps an error on top.
Tests: a bot holding a rifle and a shovel with an enemy at mid range fires the rifle (control: out of ammo → shovel);
a grenade at a target 200 px away on flat ground lands within N px (control: aiming straight at the target misses);
over a wall, picks the arc that clears it. Population: kills by weapon kind before/after (the shovel's share must fall),
kills/round must not fall.
