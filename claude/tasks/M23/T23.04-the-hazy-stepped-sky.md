## Do
1. Port `mockup-src/e_style.js`'s background material (`bgQuad`/`bgMaterial`: sky gradient, horizon glow, haze band,
   ≤6 stepped layers — pyramid/ziggurat/mesa/planet arc — with step, softness, fade and jitter, stars, grain, god rays)
   into the world renderer, driven by `P.bg`. **Extend the one sun to ≤3 moons** (position, radius, colour, ray

   are parallax layers: each layer's shape is placed in world space with a parallax factor (far layers move least),

3. Retire today's sky: `sky.ts`, `sky-math.ts` (keep only what `world/cycle.rs`'s client twin needs — `sceneDarkness`
   and whatever `cycle_matches_the_client` pins), `parallax.ts`, `parallax-math.ts`, `clouds-math.ts`, cloud rings.
   Clouds are gone: F has haze, not clouds. **Ambient rain** (T21.26/T21.31B) needs clouds to fall from — rule it
   out or re-home it and say which in the journal (default: retire it; F has no rain).
4. Rewrite or retire, **in this commit**, every check that photographs the old sky (R13): at least `sky`,
   `living-sky`, `clouds`, `clouds-canvas`, `cloud-rain`, `ambient-rain`, and the ridge half of `canvas-renderer`.
   List each with its fate in the journal.
