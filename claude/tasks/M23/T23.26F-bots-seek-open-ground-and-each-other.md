# T23.26F — Bots seek open ground and each other; the shovel is a last resort

Milestone: M23 · Owner, 2026-10-01, after watching T23.26E · Spec: `docs/78` §A6

> *"lets make them stop shoveling they are definitely not getting better at that. their top priority is to find as much
> open ground and each other. make them even more "open space" aware by calculating the best path to places outside of
> caves so they find each other better and battle more."*

## Do
1. **An openness field.** Per nav cell: is it open, sky-exposed, standable ground (a column of air above it to the
   sky, or a wide air volume around) vs enclosed (cave, tunnel, pocket). Computed once per map and updated cheaply on
   carves (`carve_seq`), deterministic. Expose "nearest open ground" and "route out of this cave" queries.
2. **Out of caves first.** A bot standing in an enclosed region (no sky exposure within N px) routes to the nearest
   open ground before exploring or shopping (unless an enemy is in reach). Exploration targets are open ground only;
   routes pay a cost for enclosed cells (T23.26E has one — make it decisive).
3. **Finding each other.** Bots converge: last-seen positions, heard gunfire (T23.26E), **plus** a slow drift toward
   where fights are likely — open ground near the map's middle/most-traversed areas — rather than uniform coverage.
   Metric: time from spawn to first engagement; share of time within weapon range of an enemy (T23.26E measured ~10 %).
4. **Stop shoveling.** Melee only when cornered at point blank or when no ranged weapon is reachable anywhere. An
   unarmed bot routes to the nearest reachable ranged weapon (over open ground) and avoids closing on armed enemies.
   Metric: melee share of shots and kills (T23.26D: 6 % / 24 %) → near zero when ranged weapons exist on the map.
5. Whatever the watch shows. Look at GPU screenshots of a `make watch` round before handing back.

## Proof
Before/after over 8 seeds: engaged share, time to first engagement, kills/round, melee share of kills, time in
enclosed cells, dig share, jumps/min. Scenario tests with planted-red controls: a bot in a cave with an exit routes out
to open ground; an unarmed bot with a gun 600 px away over open ground goes for it rather than shoveling an enemy 100 px
away (control: no gun anywhere → it may swing).
