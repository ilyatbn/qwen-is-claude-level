# T23.08B — The foreground leaves in the game

Milestone: M23 · Depends on: T23.08 · Size: ~200 lines · Rulings: R10, R11
Filed by T23.08 (its As built: "No leaves in the game"): the renderer side is built — `atmosphere.ts`'s
`kit.js::foregroundDoF` port with its player fade, `WorldRenderer.setOccluders` — and nothing in a match calls it.

## Do
1. **World-anchored clusters per map.** F1's two clusters are placed for its 1280×720 frame, not a map. A map gets
   its own, seeded by the map seed (every client of a round places the same leaves), anchored in world px so they
   move with the world like everything else in it: one per frame-wide strip of the map, at the terrain's surface in
   that strip (where F1's sit: low in the frame, over the ground's edge), with F1's sizes (`r`, `n`). Placement is
   cosmetic (no simulation reads it); it reads the mask through the scene's core, as the minimap does.
2. **The shader has six slots** (`foregroundDoF`'s `spots[6]`): each frame the renderer hands it the clusters that
   reach into the view, nearest the view's centre first.
3. **The scenes hand over their players' boxes** (`setOccluders`, mask px): every player drawn this frame — the local
   one and every remote the screen shows — in GameScene and SandboxScene, so a leaf never hides a player
   (`atmosphere.ts`: ≤ 0.2 alpha over a box, eased over 24 px). Space has no leaves (no foliage on an asteroid).
4. The tint is each end's (`F1`/`F5`, blended by the hour like the rest of the look).

## Tests
- `leaves` (new, sandbox): the map has clusters and the frame shows some (`atmosphere().fg`, and the leaves' own alpha
  over a visible cluster ≥ `FG_PRESENT`; control: `hideLayers(['fg'])`); the scene's occluders are the drawn players
  (counted at both ends); a cluster planted on the local player is faded to ≤ `FG_MAX_ALPHA` over its box, and the
  same cluster with no occluders covers it (the must-fail control).
- Unit: the placement is a function of (seed, map, mask) — same in, same out; a different seed moves it; every
  cluster sits on a surface; the visible pick is ≤ 6 and nearest-first.
