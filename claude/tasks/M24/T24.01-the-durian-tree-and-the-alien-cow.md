## Tasks (in this order when unparked)
1. **The durian tree** — a special spawn on non-volcanic maps (classic look; decide islands/multilevel), drawn from
   `durianbombtree.jpg` (the tree and its leaf clusters, in the game's ink/lit style so it sits in the world like the
   other furniture; collision as scenery decides), **hung with durian grenades** (the black spiky fruit, each with a
   pulsing green glow — a light, capped like crystals'). Seeded placement, deterministic, a few per map at most.
2. **Pick up from the tree** — a player passing through the tree's canopy picks up durian grenades (one or a few,
   respawning on the tree after a while; the existing pickup rules — `would_take`, stacks).
3. **The durian grenade** (item + weapon) — thrown like a grenade, **explodes mid-air** (on a fuse), **splits into 4
   small particles flying in random directions** (seeded), each then **bursting into a purple poison gas cloud** (the
   toxic hazard, purple). Constants with their basis; bots learn it (arcs, the zone guard so they don't gas themselves).
4. **The alien cow** — a new animal: a stick-figure alien in the ink style — short thick legs, a fat cow-like body, a
   long giraffe neck and head **with spikes all around the head**; idle wander, **sleeps** next to its tree, **chews**
   (jaw animation), and near a tree its **long tongue** reaches out and curls in a **random pattern** to eat. Spawns near
   a durian tree; moves a little; flees gunfire like other animals. Usable for promo shots (a promo hook to frame it).

the grenade's mid-air split into exactly 4 + 4 clouds (deterministic, damage only inside clouds, a control outside);
pixels for the tree, the glow, the cow (layer-hidden controls) and the tongue animating near a tree vs not.

## As-built — tasks 1–3 (builder K, 2026-10-04)

**Spec lines for the coordinator to amend docs** (proposed `docs/79` §B1; nothing in `docs/` was edited):
- **The durian tree (map).** A classic-look, non-space map grows up to `DURIAN_TREES` (2) trees, chosen at generation from the
  map's own `"durian_trees"` substream (so no other stream or golden mask moves): on walkable surface points, canopy box
  `DURIAN_TREE_W` × `DURIAN_TREE_H` (194 × 170) ≥ 97 % air, `DURIAN_TREE_CLEARANCE` (120) from spawns/gates/platforms and
  `DURIAN_TREE_SPACING` (640) from each other; volcanic and space maps grow none. Islands and multilevel grow them like any
  classic map. **Scenery**: nothing stamped in the mask. `MapMeta::durian_trees`; `map_init` appends them after the look
  byte (`u8` count, then `i16 x, i16 y, u8 flip` each — docs/40's map_init layout).
- **Fruit (world).** Each tree hangs `DURIAN_FRUIT` (3) durian grenades at fixed offsets (`map::durian::FRUIT_AT`,
  mirrored with the tree) as **world items** with `SpawnSource::Tree` ("Tree" on the wire): no physics, no TTL, outside
  the ground-item cap and eviction, taken by the ordinary pickup (`would_take`, stacks, `item_pickup`) within
  `DURIAN_PICKUP_REACH` (36) of a body's centre instead of `PICKUP_RADIUS`. A picked fruit grows back after
  `DURIAN_REGROW` (30 s). Fruit hang from the round's first step (every phase). Slots are in the state hash.
- **The durian grenade (item 28, weapons 27/28).** Thrown (`DURIAN_MUZZLE_SPEED` 600, `GRENADE_COOLDOWN`), it bursts on its
  fuse `DURIAN_FUSE` (0.5 s — mid-air above the hand for any throw ≥ 45°, and ≥ 150 px out for 30°–60°, past its own
  gas) into `DURIAN_PIECES` (4) `durian_piece` projectiles at seeded random angles (world RNG) at `DURIAN_PIECE_SPEED`
  (160); each bursts on contact or after `DURIAN_PIECE_FUSE` (0.4 s) into a purple gas cloud: the toxic burn (`BurnKind::Toxic`), `DURIAN_GAS_RADIUS` 50, `DURIAN_GAS_DPS` 6,
  `DURIAN_GAS_DURATION` 6 s, announced as `hazard_spawn` kind **`DurianGas`** (new `HazardKind`). The burst sends no `explosion`
  (the grenade's despawn and the pieces' `projectile_spawn`s are the event). Never spawned on the floor, in crates or buried (weights 0; not
  "retired"). `DEV_DURIAN=1` gives a spawning player two (dev only).
- **Bots**: the zone guard covers the cluster (reach = piece speed × fuse + cloud radius = 114 px); the arc solver may aim
  short of the target by that reach, and along a cluster's arc the burst point is guarded too.

## As-built — task 4, the alien cow (builder K, 2026-10-04)

**Spec lines for the coordinator** (proposed `docs/79` §B2):
- **The alien cow** (`AnimalKind::Cow`, wire kind 2): one per durian tree, hatched on the first `Playing` tick on the
  map's surface point nearest `COW_HOME_OFFSET` (56 px) from the trunk on the tree's own level; outside `ANIMAL_MAX`;
  never respawns in a round. Hit box `COW_W` × `COW_H` (30 × 18, the body — neck and head are drawn, not hit),
  `COW_HEALTH` 60. Every `COW_THINK_EVERY` (3 s) it grazes (stands facing its trunk, p `COW_GRAZE_CHANCE` 0.55) or ambles
  at `COW_SPEED` (16 px/s); past `COW_LEASH` (128 px) it heads home; it turns back at a drop deeper than `PLAYER_H`;
  at night (`DayPhase::Night`) it stands still (sleeps). Hurt, it flees from the nearest living player at
  `COW_FLEE_SPEED` (40 px/s) for `COW_FLEE_SECS` (3 s). Killed, it drops a durian grenade.
- **Drawn** (client only, cosmetic): an ink stick-figure grazer — fat body, short thick legs, long neck, spiked head,
  small horns, red eyes. Standing still it chews; still and within `COW_TONGUE_REACH` (96 px, `cow.ts`) of a tree's
  canopy it reaches a long pink tongue out to it once a `TONGUE_PERIOD_S` (2.4 s), each reach curling its own hashed
  way; still at night it lays its head down, eyes shut. `__game.cows()` lists them for a promo to frame.
