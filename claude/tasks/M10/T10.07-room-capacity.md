# T10.07 — Measure what a room costs

Milestone: M10 · Depends on: T10.01 · Size: measurement, then a constant with a basis

## Read first
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B2
- `docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A38 (a ceiling written for one part of a growing total)

## Why

`MAX_ROOMS` is provisionally 8 and that number is a guess. `docs/60` §6's 2 ms tick
budget was measured with 6 players and 20 projectiles — never with weather running,
the v3 arsenal in flight, late-round terrain, and several rooms competing for cores.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-server/benches/` or `crates/game-server/tests/capacity.rs`
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs` (`MAX_ROOMS`, with the numbers in its doc comment)
- `crates/game-server/src/metrics.rs`

## Deliverable

A repeatable measurement of a **full** room — 6 players, bots firing, weather
active, late-round terrain — reporting tick p50 and p99 at **1, 2, 4 and 8**
concurrent rooms. `MAX_ROOMS` is set from where p99 crosses **half** the 16.67 ms
budget, with the numbers and the machine recorded in the constant's doc comment.

`/metrics` gains `rooms_active`, `tick_p99_ms_max_over_rooms`, `rooms_over_budget`.

## Notes

- A room over budget does not merely run slow: `MissedTickBehavior::Burst` makes it
  catch up in a spike, which is worse than a steady lag. That is why the threshold
  is half the budget and not all of it.
- **Control for machine load** (§A38): the last performance investigation here
  concluded "the bake never got slower — the box was busy", proven by a control run
  under deliberate load where even pure-WASM work slowed. Do the same, and report
  the load conditions with every number.
- If the honest answer is that this machine supports fewer than 8, say so and set it
  lower. A cap that is too high is discovered by players, not by tests.

## Tests

- The measurement is reproducible: two runs agree within a stated tolerance.
- `MAX_ROOMS + 1` room creations refuse cleanly.
- `/metrics` reports the new fields and they move when rooms are added.

## Done when

```sh
cargo test -p game-server --release --test capacity -- --ignored --nocapture
```
