## Deliverable

Pick a **character skin** and a **tombstone skin**, previewed live. **Weapon skins
are shown greyed out with "Coming soon"** — visible, so the player knows it is
planned, and inert.

## Notes

- Selection persists in `localStorage` and is sent as `skin_id` and
  `tombstone_skin_id` in `join` (§B9).
- The server does not validate either against a list and never sends texture names
  (`docs/50` §1). An unknown id resolves to 0 client-side without throwing.
- Preview the character in its **walk** animation, not idle — a still frame hides
  the difference between skins that differ only by palette.
- The greyed-out weapon section must not be a dead rectangle: show the real weapon
  sprites at low opacity so it reads as content, not as a bug.

## Tests

- An unknown skin id resolves to 0 and logs once.
- Selection round-trips through `localStorage` and appears in the `join` payload.
- Every skin in `skins.json` resolves to frames that exist in the atlas manifest —
  the check that stops the registry and the art drifting apart.
- The weapon section is present, disabled, and not focusable by keyboard.

npm --prefix client test -- --run skins && node scripts/e2e.mjs skins
