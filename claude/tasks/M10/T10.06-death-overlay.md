## Deliverable

On death: an overlay with a **countdown**, who killed you and with what, and the
current scoreboard. At zero you respawn at a re-validated spawn point.

## Notes

- **It is an overlay, not a pause.** The round keeps running behind it and the
  camera stays where you died, so you watch the fight continue — consistent with
  the inventory panel in `docs/30` §3, which also does not pause anything.
- The countdown is driven by the **server's** `respawn_at`, not a local timer, or
  it will drift and disagree with when you actually respawn.
- Respawn re-validates the point against the **current damaged map**
  (`docs/21` §4) — that is not optional and is already implemented; do not bypass it.
- `RESPAWN_DELAY` becomes 5.0 per §B4. It is a felt number: note in the journal
  that it is the one constant here most likely to want playtesting.

## Tests

- The countdown reaches 0 within one tick of the server's `respawn_at`.
- The overlay clears exactly on respawn, and the player can act immediately after.
- The cause line renders for a player kill, a self-kill and a weather death — three
  different attribution paths (`docs/31` §6).
- Headless: die in a real round and assert the overlay appears, counts down, and
  clears — and that the world behind it kept ticking (assert a bot moved).
