# T15.04 — Birds (v4)

Milestone: M15 · Depends on: T14.03 · Size: ~220 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C16

## Deliverable

Birds spawn every `BIRD_INTERVAL` (18 s), up to `BIRD_MAX` (4) alive, flying across
the map on a gentle sine path and despawning at the far edge.

- A **normal bird** killed drops a **heal**; a **metal bird**
  (`BIRD_METAL_CHANCE` 0.25, visually distinct, slower, tougher) drops a **battery**.
- Server-simulated and seeded from `substream(seed, "birds")` — they drop items, and
  items are gameplay.
- They take damage from anything. The drop falls to the ground as a normal
  `WorldItem` and is refused if the finder is at `MAX_HEALS` / `MAX_BATTERIES` (§C9).

## Notes

- This is the first entity whose whole purpose is to be shot at for a reward, so the
  reward has to be legible: the drop must be visibly different for the two kinds, and
  visibly fall to a place you can reach.
- Keep them cheap — 4 alive, a sine path, no pathing, no collision with terrain.
- **They are not decoration**: because they drop items, a bird that never spawns is a
  supply line that never opens. Assert they actually appear in a real round, not just
  that the spawner ticks (§A15).
- Determinism: same seed → same birds at the same times.

## Tests

- Over a real round, birds spawn, cross, and despawn; the count never exceeds
  `BIRD_MAX` — asserted from the world, not from spawn events (§B25: a count of
  events misses what was created before the buffer existed).
- Shooting a normal bird drops exactly one heal; a metal bird drops exactly one
  battery; both fall and come to rest on ground.
- A metal bird survives a hit that kills a normal one.
- A drop is refused when the player is at max, and the item stays on the ground (§C9).
- Determinism over 600 ticks.
- **Rendered** (§C2): a bird is visible in flight, with a control frame before it
  spawns.
