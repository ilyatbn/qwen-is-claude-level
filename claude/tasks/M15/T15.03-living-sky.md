# T15.03 — Mountains and clouds (v4)

Milestone: M15 · Depends on: T13.01 · Size: ~200 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C14
- `docs/70-amendments-v2.md` §A4 (the sky phases these must tint with)

## Deliverable

- **Mountain silhouettes**: `MOUNTAIN_LAYERS` (2) parallax layers generated from the
  **map seed**, so a seed always looks the same, drawn in the theme's palette
  darkened toward the sky colour at distance. Scroll factors 0.10 and 0.20 — below
  `PARALLAX_FACTOR`.
- **Clouds**: `CLOUD_COUNT` (12) soft blobs drifting at `CLOUD_DRIFT` (6 px/s),
  tinted by the current sky phase and lit warm at dawn and dusk. They wrap.

This is the parallax layer at depth −20 that T3.06 deliberately left empty because
there was nothing to put in it. There is now.

## Notes

- Generate the mountain profile with the existing value noise, from
  `substream(seed, "mountains")` — not a new noise implementation (§A24).
- Both sit **behind** the terrain and **above** the gradient (`docs/12` §5).
- One draw each, no per-frame allocation. Clouds are a texture drawn at offsets, not
  twelve sprites rebuilt per frame.
- They must tint with the §A4 sky phase, or at night you get bright daytime clouds —
  which is exactly the §A32 mistake of judging a palette in one lighting condition.

## Tests

- Same seed → same mountain profile; different seeds differ.
- Clouds wrap without a visible seam, and their x advances at `CLOUD_DRIFT`.
- Cloud tint at night differs from cloud tint at noon — **sampled pixels** (§C2).
- The layers sit at the right depths: terrain draws over them.
