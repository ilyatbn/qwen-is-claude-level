# T15.01 — Teleport pads (v4)

Milestone: M15 · Depends on: T13.01 · Size: ~250 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C5
- `docs/21-player-stats.md` §4 (respawn re-validation)

## The bugs it fixes

Respawn puts you **in mid-air**, and **always in the same spot**. Pads replace ad-hoc
spawn selection and fix both permanently.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/map/gen/meta.rs` (pad selection at generation)
- `crates/game-core/src/map/carve.rs` (pads are indestructible)
- `crates/game-core/src/player/respawn.rs`, `world/teleport.rs`
- `client/src/render/pads.ts`

## Deliverable

`TELEPORT_PADS` (6) chosen at generation from valid, separated surface points by the
same farthest-point sampling as spawn points. A pad is a small **indestructible**
platform, `PAD_W` × `PAD_H`; `carve_circle` skips it exactly as it skips walls.

- **Death** → respawn on the pad furthest from the nearest living player.
- **Standing on a pad `TELEPORT_CHARGE` (2 s) while alive** → teleport to a different
  random pad.
- **Armed only after moving `TELEPORT_ARM_DISTANCE` (32 px) from where you spawned** —
  spawn and stand still and nothing happens.
- **`TELEPORT_COOLDOWN` (5 s)** after arriving.
- Client: a glowing ring and a charge indicator filling over the 2 s.

## Notes

- **Indestructibility is the point**, not a detail: it guarantees a valid respawn
  surface for the whole round however much of the map is destroyed, which is what
  makes §C15's diggable floor survivable. It also makes pads tactically real — the one
  ground nobody can dig away.
- Respawn still re-validates (`docs/21` §4). With pads that check should never fail;
  keep it anyway, and assert it never fires.
- The arming rule needs the spawn position remembered per life, not a global flag.

## Tests

- 6 pads exist on every generated map at all three scales, separated, and standable.
- A carve over a pad removes nothing — the §A19 lesson: measure at every scale.
- Respawn lands **standing on a pad**, never in mid-air, over 50 deaths on a map
  carved to pieces — the `docs/21` §7 test, strengthened.
- Two consecutive deaths do not always give the same pad.
- Standing still after spawn never teleports; moving 32 px then standing 2 s does.
- Teleporting twice in under 5 s is refused.
- Determinism: same seed, same pads.
