## Deliverable

```rust
pub struct Puddle {
    pub id: u32,
    pub pos: Vec2,
    pub radius: f32,
    pub expires_at: f32,
}

pub struct ToxicRain { rng: ChaCha8Rng, puddles: Vec<Puddle>, next_spawn_at: f32,
                       next_id: u32 }

impl ToxicRain {
    pub fn new(seed: u64, now: f32) -> Self;

    /// Spawn puddles on cadence, expire old ones, damage players inside.
    /// Returns newly spawned puddles for broadcasting.
    pub fn tick(&mut self, map: &Map, players: &mut [PlayerHitTarget],
                active: bool, now: f32, dt: f32) -> Vec<Puddle>;

    pub fn puddles(&self) -> &[Puddle];
}
```

Behaviour:
- While active, spawn one puddle every `TOXIC_PUDDLE_EVERY` (0.4 s).
- Position: a random point from `map.meta.surface_points`, weighted toward the half
  of the map where players currently are.
- Radius `TOXIC_PUDDLE_RADIUS` (40), lifetime `TOXIC_PUDDLE_LIFE` (3 s).
- `TOXIC_DPS` (6) per second to any player whose AABB overlaps a puddle.
- **No terrain damage.**

## Notes

Toxic rain denies space; it does not reshape the map. That is what distinguishes it
from the meteor shower, and it is why there is no carve here. Resist adding one.

The player-half weighting keeps the effect relevant. Uniform random placement on a
large map wastes most puddles on empty terrain and the effect becomes something
players never notice. Compute the mean player x, then bias sampling toward that
half — a simple 70/30 split is enough.

Damage is `TOXIC_DPS * dt` per tick, not a lump per puddle. A player crossing a
puddle takes proportionally less than one standing in it, which is the intended
incentive.

A player in **two** overlapping puddles takes double damage. That is intentional —
it makes dense clusters genuinely dangerous. Do not deduplicate.

Puddles do not need surface re-validation the way item spawns do: a puddle in
mid-air over a crater is harmless and invisible, not a bug. Skipping the check keeps
this cheap at 2.5 spawns a second.

Damage goes through `PlayerHitTarget::apply_damage` with
`DamageSource::Weather(EffectKind::ToxicRain)`, so shields and i-frames apply
without being re-implemented here.

## Tests

- Over `TOXIC_DURATION` (8 s), exactly `8 / 0.4` = 20 puddles spawn.
- No puddles spawn while `active` is false.
- Each puddle expires exactly `TOXIC_PUDDLE_LIFE` after spawning.
- A player standing in a puddle for its full life loses exactly
  `TOXIC_DPS * TOXIC_PUDDLE_LIFE` (18) health, within one tick's tolerance.
- A player outside the radius takes nothing.
- A player at exactly the radius boundary: assert whichever behaviour you implement,
  and state it in a comment.
- A player in two overlapping puddles takes double damage.
- A shielded player takes half.
- A player in i-frames takes none.
- **The mask is unchanged** after a full toxic rain — assert `count_solid` is
  identical before and after.
- Puddles are biased toward the players' half: with all players on the left, over
  200 puddles the mean x is well left of centre.
- Determinism: the same seed produces the same puddle positions and times.
