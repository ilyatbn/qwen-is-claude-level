## Beats (≈35 s; tune by eye, keep it moving)
1. 0:00–0:05 descent + touchdown (keep today's art). **Top-left caption typed letter by letter: `PLANET DROXILON 7`**
   (monospace/terminal feel, a blinking cursor; on screen 0:00–0:05, then gone).
2. The door: the hinged hatch swings down **until it faces the ground** (flat, outward), then a **ramp extends** from it
   (slides out) **until it touches the ground** — only then the researchers walk out and down it.
3. Crew out (4 researchers in white suits, helmets on).
4. **The tablet**: over-the-shoulder close shot of a researcher holding a **semi-transparent holographic tablet**
   (glassy panel, cyan UI) — readings fill in one by one: `OXYGEN 21.4%`, `HYDROGEN 0.6 ppm`, `CO2 0.04%`,
   `TOXINS: NONE DETECTED` (each with a green ✓). No caption text on screen besides the tablet.
5. **The breath** (longer than before): another researcher takes his helmet off, a long breath in (spores drawn in),

7. **The alarm**: back on the tablet — it flips red and beeps: `⚠ UNKNOWN SUBSTANCE DETECTED`, flashing.
8. **Twitching**: the researchers start twitching / jerking (glitchy, small, escalating).
