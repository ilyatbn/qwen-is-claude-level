# T99.02 — The SHRED trailer (~59 s)

Milestone: M99 (promotional) · Owner ask 2026-09-30.

Brief: a prequel — an expedition ship lands on an alien planet, white stick figures step out, one takes off his
helmet and breathes the clean air, exhales and turns into the game's black figure (the virus makes each think the
rest are enemies). Then fast trailer cuts of real fights and explosions from the game, "SHRED", "COMING SOON", fast
industrial metal. The intro may be 3D as long as the game is shown after.

## What it is (all under `promo/`, outputs in `promo/build/`, gitignored)
| step | script | out |
|---|---|---|
| clock | `timeline.mjs` — 150 BPM; every section and tagline on the beat grid | — |
| footage | `capture-match.mjs <take> <s> <zoom>` — headed GPU Chrome, a director camera chasing the busiest fight, CDP screencast at 60 fps, HUD/minimap/names/crosshair hidden | `raw/<take>/` |
| prequel | `render-page.mjs intro/index.html intro 14.4` — three.js, a pure function of `t`, stepped frame by frame | `intro/` |
| score | `music.mjs` — synthesised industrial metal, no samples | `music.wav` |
| cut | `edit.mjs` — scores footage from the director's light log, best moments to the key beat slots | — |
| finish | `assemble.mjs` — compositor page (`comp/`) draws every frame: grade, punch-ins on the snare, taglines, SHRED, COMING SOON; ffmpeg encodes with the score | `SHRED-trailer.mp4` |

Client: two e2e-only handles on `__game` — `setZoom(z)` (the live camera zoom the world renderer reads) and
`setCrosshairVisible(on)`. Nothing in a real round calls them.

## Done when
`promo/build/SHRED-trailer.mp4` exists, ~59 s 1920×1080 60 fps with audio, and the owner has watched it.
