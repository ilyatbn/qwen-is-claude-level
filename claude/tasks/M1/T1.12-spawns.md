## Deliverable

```rust
/// Choose well-separated spawn points from the traversable component.
/// Returns at least SPAWN_COUNT_MIN points when the map allows it.
pub fn choose_spawns(
    surface: &[Point],
    component: &[usize],
    seed: u64,
    count: usize,
) -> Vec<Point>;
```

Algorithm — **farthest-point sampling**:

1. Candidates are `component`'s points only (never a stranded ledge).
2. Pick the first candidate at random from `substream(seed, "spawns")`.
3. Repeatedly add the candidate whose distance to its **nearest already-chosen
   point** is greatest.
4. Stop at `count`, or when the best remaining candidate is closer than
   `SPAWN_MIN_SEPARATION` to something already chosen.
5. If fewer than `count` were found, relax the separation by 25 % and try again,
   up to 3 relaxations. Report the final separation used.

## Notes

Farthest-point sampling rather than random rejection sampling: random placement
clusters, and clustered spawns mean two players start in each other's faces while a
third of the map is empty. Farthest-point gives a deterministic, well-spread set for
free.

Step 3 is O(n·k) with n candidates and k=6 — a few thousand times six, which is
nothing. Do not over-engineer it.

The relaxation in step 5 matters on small maps and on maps whose traversable
component is a narrow strip. Returning four well-separated spawns is better than
returning six by ignoring separation entirely, but returning fewer than
`SPAWN_COUNT_MIN` is worse than a slightly tighter map — hence relax, do not give
up.

Return points in a deterministic order (selection order), not sorted — the
selection order is itself meaningful and reproducible.

## Tests

- Determinism: same inputs produce the same spawn list, 20 times.
- On a long flat floor, 6 requested spawns are all at least
  `SPAWN_MIN_SEPARATION` apart.
- Every returned point is in the supplied component.
- On a tiny component (3 points, all close together), it returns what it can
  without panicking and without infinite looping.
- Requesting 0 spawns returns an empty vec.
- An empty candidate list returns an empty vec, no panic.
- Spread quality: on a long flat floor, the minimum pairwise distance among 6
  chosen points is at least 60 % of the theoretical maximum (`floor_length / 5`).
  This is the assertion that actually proves farthest-point sampling was
  implemented, rather than random rejection.
- On 20 real generated medium maps, at least `SPAWN_COUNT_MIN` spawns are found
  every time.
