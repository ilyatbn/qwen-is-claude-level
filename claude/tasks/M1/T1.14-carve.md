## Notes

Rasterisation, exactly as `docs/11-map-destruction.md` §2 specifies:

```
for dy in -r ..= r:
    y = cy + dy
    if y < 0 || y >= h - BEDROCK_H: continue        // bedrock and top clamp
    dx = isqrt(r*r - dy*dy)
    x0 = max(cx - dx, WALL_W)
    x1 = min(cx + dx, w - WALL_W - 1)
    removed += mask.clear_run(y, x0, x1)
```

**Integer arithmetic only.** `isqrt` from `math.rs`, not `f32::sqrt`. This is what
makes the result bit-identical on the server and in the browser, which is the whole
basis of terrain replication (`docs/11-map-destruction.md` §6). A float version
will diverge by a pixel somewhere and the divergence will surface weeks later as
"I got shot through a wall".

**Bedrock and walls are excluded by clamping the span**, not by testing per pixel.
A rocket at the base of a wall correctly digs a half-crater.

**Coarse grid maintenance** — do not recount. Within `clear_run`, accumulate the
bits removed per 8-px-aligned segment and `subtract` from the corresponding cell.
You will need `clear_run` to report per-segment counts, or to do the segmentation
here and call `clear_run` once per coarse cell. Either is fine; the second is
simpler and the cost is negligible.

**Dirty chunks** are every chunk the circle's bounding box overlaps, clamped to the
map. Do not compute exact per-chunk intersection — the bounding box is close enough
and much cheaper. Keep a `FixedBitSet` (or a `Vec<bool>`) over chunk indices plus a
`Vec<ChunkId>` for iteration order; `drain_dirty` clears both.

**Buried reveal**: after the spans, iterate `meta.buried_slots` (at most 16) and for
each unrevealed slot whose position is now air *and* within the carve circle, set
`revealed = true` and push its id.

## Tests

- Carving entirely outside the map bounds is a no-op and does not panic.
- Negative coordinates and radii do not panic.
- Radius 0 clears exactly one pixel; radius 1 clears a plus-shape (5 pixels).
- **Idempotence**: carving the same circle twice — the second returns
  `pixels_removed == 0` and an empty `dirty_chunks`.
- A carve overlapping bedrock removes nothing at or below `h - BEDROCK_H`.
- A carve overlapping the left wall removes nothing at `x < WALL_W`; same on the right.
- Pixel count for radius 40 on a full mask is within ±2 % of `πr²`.
- **Coarse grid exactness**: after 500 random carves, `coarse.verify(&mask)` is `Ok`.
  This is the most important test in the task — a drifting coarse grid produces
  invisible walls and phantom holes in collision.
- `dirty_chunks` exactly covers the set of chunks whose bits actually changed
  (compare against a brute-force diff of before/after masks).
- `drain_dirty` returns each chunk once and leaves the set empty.
- A carve covering a buried slot reveals it and returns its id; a carve whose edge
  is one pixel short does not.
- An already-revealed slot is not returned twice.
- `fill_circle` is the inverse: carve then fill restores the original `count_solid`
  for a circle carved from a full mask.
