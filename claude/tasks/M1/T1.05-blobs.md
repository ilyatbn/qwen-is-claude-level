## Notes

Each blob is a **cluster**, not a single circle: pick a centre, then stamp 3–6
overlapping circles with radii in `BLOB_RADIUS_MIN..=BLOB_RADIUS_MAX`, jittered
around that centre by up to `BLOB_RADIUS_MAX / 2`. A single circle reads as a
cartoon planet; a cluster reads as an island.

Centres go in the **upper two thirds** of the map (`y` in
`SKY_MARGIN .. h * 2 / 3`) — that is where the silhouette has left airspace, so
that is where islands are visible rather than absorbed into the ground.

Enforce a minimum centre separation of `2 * BLOB_RADIUS_MAX`. Use rejection
sampling capped at 50 attempts per blob; if a blob cannot be placed, skip it
silently rather than looping forever. On small maps some blobs will not fit, and
that is fine.

Use `substream(seed, "blobs")` — its own stream, so tuning blob counts never
disturbs the terrain field (`docs/10-map-generation.md` §2).

`stamp_circle` uses the same integer span fill as the carve in T1.14: `isqrt` per
row, then `set_run`/`clear_run`. Do not write a float-distance version here and an
integer version there — they will disagree at the edges, and the carve one is the
one that must be bit-exact.

Call `force_borders` at the end: a blob near an edge must not eat the wall.

## Tests

- Determinism: same seed produces the same mask hash, 20 times.
- `add_blobs` strictly increases `count_solid` (blobs only add).
- With `blob_count = 0` the mask is unchanged.
- Borders still hold afterwards.
- All stamped centres are within the upper two thirds.
- Minimum separation holds for all pairs of placed centres (expose the chosen
  centres from a test-only helper, or assert via a returned `Vec<Point>`).
- `stamp_circle` with `solid = true` sets exactly the expected pixel count for a
  known radius (compare against a brute-force float-distance count, tolerance ±1
  per row).
- `stamp_circle` at radius 0 sets exactly one pixel.
- `stamp_circle` overhanging the map edge does not panic and does not wrap onto the
  neighbouring row.
