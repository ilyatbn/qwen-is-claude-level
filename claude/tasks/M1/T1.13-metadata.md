## Notes

**Buried slot placement** — the interesting part. A candidate must:
- be solid, with at least 24 px of solid in all four cardinal directions (so it is
  genuinely buried, not just under the skin);
- be at least 128 px from every other chosen slot.

Bias candidate sampling toward the neighbourhood of `tunnels` and `pockets`: sample
a random point from a tunnel path or pocket centroid, then offset it by 30–80 px in
a random direction and test that. This makes slots reachable by a single
well-placed rocket rather than requiring someone to mine out half the map, which is
what uniform random placement produces.

Cap attempts (200 per slot) and accept fewer slots rather than looping. Slot ids
are the index in the returned vec, assigned in order.

**Decorations** are cosmetic. Pick roughly one per 10 surface points, at most 200
total, each with a random kind from the theme's set, a random flip and a scale
tier. They carry no gameplay meaning and a client may ignore them entirely.

`theme` is `substream(seed, "theme")` mod 3. `wind` is
`range_f32(substream(seed, "wind"), -WIND_MAX, WIND_MAX)`.

`generate` builds the `CoarseGrid` from the final mask exactly once, at the end.

## Tests

- Determinism: `generate(seed, Medium)` produces identical mask hash, spawn list,
  buried slots and decorations, 20 times.
- **Sub-stream isolation**: generating with the same seed but after draining an
  unrelated stream produces the same map (this is the property from
  `docs/10-map-generation.md` §2 — test it here, at the top level, where it matters).
- Every buried slot is inside solid rock, with ≥ 24 px of solid in all four
  directions.
- Buried slots are at least 128 px apart.
- Buried slot count is close to the scale's `BURIED_SLOTS` (allow shortfall on
  awkward maps, but assert at least half were placed on 20 real maps).
- Every buried slot starts with `revealed == false`.
- Every decoration sits on or just above a surface point.
- `wind` is within `±WIND_MAX`.
- `theme` is in 0..3.
- `meta.surface_points` is non-empty and matches what `extract_surface` returns for
  the final mask.
- `coarse.verify(&mask)` returns `Ok` on the generated map.
- All three scales produce a valid `Map`.
