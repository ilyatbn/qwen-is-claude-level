## What A left, and what this owes

A landed everything that decides: the arrival (`world/black_hole.rs`), the pull through the one
`env_at`, the horizon kill and `DeathCause::BlackHole`, the eaten asteroid (list, mask, well), the
`Ended` freeze (`black_hole::pulling`, called by both sides), the respawn/vortex-destination
clearance, the `black_hole` event + join catch-up, `GameCore::set_black_hole`, the client mirror
(`WorldMirror.blackHole`, `pushBlackHole` drops the eaten rock from the core's list by centre),
the kill-feed/overlay sentences, and the dev hook `debug_black_hole {dist?}` (`DEV_PROBE=1`).

**Nothing draws it**, and nothing asserts the networked behaviour. This task:

1. **`client/src/render/blackHoleFx.ts`** (+ `blackHoleFx-math.ts` and its vitest): a black disc
   at `BLACK_HOLE_HORIZON_R`, a solid accretion ring just outside it in one known colour, a faint
   ring at `BLACK_HOLE_CAPTURE_R` (the no-escape line), a glow to `BLACK_HOLE_REACH`. **Both
   render paths** (`webgl && isHighQuality()` shader quad; `Graphics` otherwise), both painting
   the disc and the ring solid. Drawn from `WorldMirror.blackHole`, so it stays on the results
   screen (R8.4). Wired in `GameScene` beside `vortexFx`; `__game.showBlackHole(on)`,
   `__game.debugBlackHole(dist)`, `debug().blackHole { hole, fx, lastProbe, asteroids }`.
2. **`scripts/checks/black-hole.mjs`** (registered in `scripts/lib/e2e-checks.mjs`), one human in
   a private Space room on a `DEV_PROBE=1` server — `breach-vortex.mjs`'s route:
   - **arrival agrees**: the client's hole is where the server put it; the eaten rock is gone
     from the client core's asteroid list; the mask checksum still agrees (terrain + list +
     mirror, counted at both ends);
   - **no rubber-band while pulled**: placed at rest `0.9 × BLACK_HOLE_REACH` out, idle,
     until the horizon takes the player — worst `lastAckErrorPx` and correction `lastJumpPx`
     ≤ `RECONCILE_EPSILON_PX`, with the no-pull floor measured first and a travel control;
   - **cannot escape** (the inverse assertion, on the wire): after the respawn, placed at
     `0.9 × BLACK_HOLE_CAPTURE_R` holding the thrust that points away — dies anyway, cause
     `black_hole`, nobody credited; the overlay reads the black-hole sentence and the kill feed
     line has no `?` (rendered, R20); the respawn is outside `BLACK_HOLE_REACH`;
   - **coverage in both render paths**: accretion-ring probes in the ring's own colour and the
     disc centre black, against the same frozen instant with the layer hidden, plus a control
     point clear of the glow (§C2); screenshots under the shots dir; **look at them**;
   - **frozen at `Ended`**: after the bell it is still drawn, and a body placed in its pull is
     not pulled (drift against the playing-phase control).
   The arrival *timing* across seeds is Rust's (`it_arrives_inside_the_last_minute_…`); a
   browser round long enough to see a natural arrival costs a minute per run for no new claim.
