# T22.04B — The plume in a real match, and never outside space

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.04** (`2346676`, `d5122de`) · Size: ~150 lines, mostly one
browser check · Origin: the harsh review of T22.04, 2026-09-23 (findings F1–F6 below, verbatim
in substance; the review's plant logs are `gate-review2204-*.txt`, gitignored)

T22.04 shipped and is green, and the review **saw** the plume on all three render paths — by
screenshot and by three plants (direction flip, invisible shader quad, round-end gate removed),
each of which went red. What it did not find is a guard for the two places the sandbox cannot
reach.

## Do these

### F1 — a plume under normal gravity is invisible to the suite (player-visible risk)
Planted: `plumeOn(flags.alive, flags.jetpack, true)` at the live call in `PlayerView.setState`
→ vitest 978/978 and e2e `wasd,hud-bars,platform-autofire` 3/3 **all green**. `plumeOn`'s unit
test covers the pure function, not the three scenes that pass `space`.
**Do:** a standard-gravity arm (`?gravity=standard`, hold the jetpack until `moveState===2`)
asserting the plume is not drawn — `debug().plume.drawn === false` **and** the pixel strip
unchanged against the hidden-plume control. The space arms are its presence control. **Re-run
the plant above and watch it go red.**

### F2 — GameScene's wiring is exercised by nothing (guard that cannot fail)
Only `thrusters` and `asteroid-gravity` reach space, both sandbox. Unguarded: `this.gravity`
from `lobby_state`, `space:` at both `setState` calls (local, remote), and
`jetpack: moveState===2 && this.meAlive`. **The As-built line saying a networked space round
"needs the lobby UI (R22)" is wrong** — `window.__menu.step('gravity', 1)` already exists in
`lobby.mjs`; correct that line.
**Do:** a networked check (two clients): step gravity to space, start the round, hold DOWN on
one, assert the **other** client's GameScene `debug()` reports that remote's plume drawn and
pointing up; add the `debug()` field GameScene lacks (derive it from the `PlayerView`, do not
keep a second copy). Then a round in standard gravity: no plume. Plant each of the three
wiring sites in turn; each must go red.

### F3 — "no plume while Ended" is asserted only at the wire
Fold into F2's check: after the round-over bell, the local plume is not drawn, with the
pre-bell frame as the presence control.

### F4 — the journal says the direction flip "reds all arms"
The check aborts at the first failure, so the plant stopped at "DOWN held, HQ off". Reword the
T22.04 journal line to what was measured, in a new journal entry (do not rewrite history).

### F5 — the braking limit exists only in prose
File it as a row, not a sentence: **T22.04C — the local player's plume follows the thrust
input, not velocity**. It covers more than braking: bots brake on purpose (R5), and a player
thrusting out of an asteroid well while still falling toward it shows the plume on the wrong
side — T22.11's core situation. Remote players stay on velocity (the input is not on the
wire). Write the task file (brief, ~30 lines) and a TASKS.md row; do **not** build it here.

### F6 — optional, cosmetic
The HQ shader plume's painted sheath is about half the quad's width, visibly narrower than the
flat plume. The doc comment's "the same box" is true of the quad, not the pixels. Either widen
the sheath or narrow the comment's claim. Screenshot both paths and look.
