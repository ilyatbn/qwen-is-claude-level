## What the review found
- **R97 (ruling).** Outside a vortex's **capture** radius escape is always possible: the asteroid wells **and every
  vortex's pull** are summed and clamped together to `SPACE_WELL_ACCEL_MAX` (R46/R96's basis) before the black hole is
  added. Inside the capture radius the vortex captures (unchanged). The black hole stays uncapped (R90/R91). One
  summation (`attractors::env_at`, `field_from` ordering), identical on server and mirror. At `c3f7861` the capped
  wells (675) plus a vortex's outer pull (up to 900 at `VORTEX_REACH / 2`) reach 1575 px/s² against DOWN 900.
- **F1 (HIGH).** T22.03H put `hold_off` on **wandering** bots too — the walking model's `stop_within` served
  `Goal::Enemy` and `Goal::Wander` — and standard void deaths rose 17 % (424 vs 363 over four draws; 369 of 424
  shovel-selected, not engaged). Enemy only: 342, kills held. Needs a standard void-death bound, red with the Wander
  arm on `hold_off`. The deeper cause — wander cells whose middle is over a pit — is pre-existing: filed.
- **F2 (MEDIUM).** The "beside a live vortex" pinned-run exclusion is the whole `VORTEX_REACH` disc (508 px, up to 3
  live) and hides real pins (planted at `REACH / 2` → 3/1/3/1/1 runs, to 79 s). Re-measure after R97; exclude only
  inside the capture radius (or none) and bound the rest; trace what remains.
- **F4 (MEDIUM, player-visible).** Winged "stuck" counts per-tick movement < `STUCK_PX` (6 px) while wings fly
  3.3 px/tick → 74 % of winged "stuck" ticks had |vx| > 100 px/s; `still_for` never resets; bots zig-zag in growing
  legs across open air; no give-up; the sweep's DOWN/UP legs ignore the void/black-hole keep-outs.
- **F5 (LOW).** `would_take` duplicates the pickup's counter routing.
- **F6 (LOW).** `hold_off`'s doc comment was inserted inside `stand_off`'s; the wasm test
  `field_accel_at_reports_the_summation_the_server_runs` compares against the raw `field_at`, not the capped sum. The
  walking model's "enemy a body height above/below" gap lives only in T22.03H's file.
