## The shape is decided: an **ellipse**, inset — CORRECTED 2026-09-20, see `R13`

`T21.07` said *"pick one, build one, and say why the others were not"*, and this file
originally said "circle". **That was written on a wrong premise and `R13` revises it.**

**Every map is 2:1** — `MAP_SMALL_W/H = 2048/1024`, `MEDIUM 3072/1536`, `LARGE 4096/2048`. A
true circle is limited by the short axis, so it leaves **w − h px of dead map: 2048 px on
Large, half the arena.** This file's later claim that a circle leaves `WALL_W` strips of 8 px
is out by a factor of 64.

**Build an ellipse inscribed in the map rect, inset clear of `SKY_MARGIN` (96) at the top and
`FLOOR_CRUST` (16) at the bottom.** And the reason it is better than the circle rather than a
compromise: **`MINIMAP_W` × `MINIMAP_H` is 200 × 100 — also 2:1 — so an ellipse inscribed in
the map draws as a true circle on the minimap**, which is the only place the arena's shape is
ever visible. The camera viewport is a small fraction of the map; from inside you see an arc.

**Inset, not full-height**, because `silhouette::force_borders` forces the sky band empty and
the floor-crust band solid full-width, and `borders_hold` is asserted by
`gen/mod.rs::every_scale_produces_the_right_dimensions_with_borders_intact`, the identically
named test in `gen/v2/mod.rs`, and `tests/map_sweep.rs::thousand_seed_playability_sweep`.
Insetting keeps all three green and untouched.

## The golden hazard, correctly identified

**It is not shared RNG.** The substreams are isolated — `rng.rs` seeds each pass as
`seed ^ fnv1a64(tag)` and **two tests pin it**:
`meta.rs::sub_stream_isolation_at_the_top_level` burns 10 000 draws off `"items"` and
`"weather"` and asserts the mask hash is unchanged, and
`objects.rs::draining_another_substream_does_not_move_an_object`.

**The real mechanism is the retry loop** — and note **the default generator is `V2`**
(`DEFAULT_MAP_GENERATOR`), whose identical loop lives in `map/gen/v2/mod.rs::generate_terrain`.
A coder who reads only `gen/mod.rs` is editing the path production does not take. `gen/mod.rs`
runs
`for attempt in 0..MAX_GEN_ATTEMPTS { let seed = requested_seed.wrapping_add(attempt); … }`
and accepts on `report.passed`, which comes from `traversal::analyse`, which reads
**`JUMP_REACH` and `JUMP_HEIGHT` — compile-time consts of `JUMP_VELOCITY`, `WALK_SPEED` and
`GRAVITY`.** So anything that flips a traversability verdict changes which attempt first
passes, which re-derives every substream from `requested_seed + attempt`, which moves the whole
map. **Golden hashes move through the accept/reject gate, not through RNG sharing.**

`crates/game-core/tests/golden_hashes.txt` holds **24 data rows** — 4 seeds × 3 scales × 2
generators, plus two comment lines (counted 2026-09-18, not remembered). A third generator
makes it 36. Regenerate with
`GOLDEN_UPDATE=1 cargo test -p game-core --release --test golden`; it is the only pinned-digest
fixture in the tree.

**Traversability needs its own verdict here — and the framing below was too confident.**
`R17` rules the shape, and corrects two things this file assumed. The existing predicate is
**not** walk-only: `can_jetpack` and the `NavRegions` flood edges are in the disjunction, so a
scatter of asteroids within one jetpack budget of each other **could score surprisingly
well**. And `surface_points` will **not** be empty — asteroid tops are standable and
`extract_surface` finds them. **Measure what the existing predicate says about a space map
before asserting it is meaningless.**

**The failure mode if nothing replaces the verdict** is silent: all 12 attempts
(`MAX_GEN_ATTEMPTS`) fail, the safe preset runs, and the map that ships is the **safe-preset
map** with `used_safe_preset = true` — and `map_sweep` is `#[ignore]`d, so nothing routine
reports it.

## The rim is ordinary destructible rock — and there is **no** bedrock to reach for

**`BEDROCK_H` is `0`** (§C15 removed it), so `Map::circle`'s bottom clamp `h - BEDROCK_H` is
inert and `carve_circle`'s doc sentence *"Bedrock and the side walls are never touched"* is
half-stale. **Report that stale doc comment; do not fix it in this task.** The only
indestructible border is the 8 px `WALL_W` side band below `SKY_MARGIN`. `FLOOR_CRUST` is
generated solid but `force_borders`' own comment already says it is not indestructible.

**And `clamp_to_world` has no bottom clamp** — x to the `WALL_W` bands, `y >= half_h` at the
top, nothing downward. What stops a player leaving the world downward is
`world/mod.rs::step_void`. `R16` rules that it stays, and puts the consequence on `T22.10`.

## The original section, with its `WALL_W` warning still correct

`Map::circle`, the one function every carve goes through, already clamps to
`(WALL_W, w - WALL_W - 1)` and `h - BEDROCK_H`, and `carve_circle`'s doc says *"Bedrock and the
side walls are never touched."* **There is already an indestructible border, and building the
rim out of it would be the obvious move and the wrong one**: the owner has said the rim is
breakable — *"you can still destroy it obviously"* — and `T22.10` is a whole task about what
happens when it breaks.

Two consequences:

- The rim is **ordinary mask**, carved and destroyed like any other rock.
- A circle inscribed in a rectangular map leaves `WALL_W` strips at the far left and right
  **outside** the circle, still indestructible and **not** the rim. Say what is in that space —
  void the players never reach, or something. It is dead area either way and it should be a
  decision rather than an artefact.

## Deliverable

- A generator, on its own substream tag, as **`MapGenerator::Space` — a third variant in
  `MapGenerator::ALL`, derived from the gravity mode and never selected beside it** (`R15`).
  **`MapScale` branches nothing**; it is a size/parameter table. The one branching site is
  `map/gen/mod.rs::generate_terrain_with`'s `match generator`.
  The variant is what makes `tests/golden.rs::cases()` grow **24 → 36 automatically** — it
  iterates `MapGenerator::ALL`. A `GravityMode` branch inside the existing generator gains
  `cases()` nothing and would ship the space map with **zero** golden coverage.
- **Gravity must reach the map path, and today it does not.** `room.rs::generate_world_task`
  builds the world and assigns `world.gravity` *afterwards*; `World::with_generator` →
  `World::build` → `map::generate_full` has no gravity parameter at all. **Make it a
  constructor input** — that signature change is part of this task and was not in its original
  estimate. `client/src/scenes/PreviewScene.ts` calls `core.generate` directly too, so **the
  lobby preview needs the mode** or a host who picks space is shown a normal map.
- A **closed** circular boundary of thin land. Closed is the assertion; "land exists at the
  edges" is not the same claim and a gap is a map players leave.
- Many small **asteroids** inside it, with float-through space between them, at every
  `MapScale`. *"Asteroid", not "island"* — the owner renamed them on 2026-09-18 and the word is
  load-bearing, because they are no longer inert scenery.
- **A gravity level, 1–5, on each asteroid** — generated here, part of the hashed map, and on
  the wire, because the client predicts against it (`T22.11` owns the field itself).
  **Cost it: this is a wire-format change** — `game-server/src/codec.rs::encode_map_init_at`
  writes magic, w, h, seed, scale, theme, wind, carve_seq, spawns, pads, platforms, so a
  per-asteroid list means the codec on **both** sides. **But NOT a `REPLAY_VERSION` bump** —
  corrected 2026-09-21, this file was wrong: the replay carries **no `map_init` at all**
  (`grep -c "map_init\|MapInit\|MAP_MAGIC\|encode_map" crates/game-server/src/replay.rs`
  → **0**). It stores seed, scale, generator and gravity and **regenerates**, so a space
  replay reproduces from the derivation. `REPLAY_VERSION` stays at 14. Whether
  the level correlates with size is a design choice this task makes: a big rock with a weak
  pull reads as wrong, so the straight answer is that it does, and it should be said either way.
- Its own acceptance predicate, replacing walk-traversal, stated in the file.

## Tests

- The boundary is **closed** — walk it, do not sample it. **This is a claim about
  *generation*, and `T22.10` deliberately makes holes at runtime.** Both are true and neither
  is a bug: the map is born closed, and a breach is a vortex rather than an exit. Write that
  distinction into the test, or the next reader finds a closure assertion and a
  hole-making feature and assumes one of them is wrong.
- Asteroids exist, are separated, and have space between them: a **population claim over many
  seeds**, not one draw.
- Every level 1–5 appears across a sweep, and the distribution is stated. A generator that
  technically can emit level 5 and does so once in 900 maps has a feature nobody meets.
- **Existing golden hashes are unchanged when the mode is off.** The most important test here,
  and the one that catches a shared-RNG perturbation if the analysis above is ever wrong.
- Density and gap distributions reported across a 999-seed sweep, the way T21.40 reported its
  placement change.
