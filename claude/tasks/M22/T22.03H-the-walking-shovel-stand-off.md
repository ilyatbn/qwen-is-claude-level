# T22.03H — The walking model's shovel stand-off (40 px vs 28 px reach)

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.03D · Size: small · Origin: T22.03D (filed), T22.03E F7. **Not built.**

T22.03D found, in flight, two shovel bots parked 32 px apart refusing every swing as out of range: `stand_off`'s 40 px
floor against the shovel's 28 px `effective_reach`. It fixed flight only (`BOT_SPACE_IN_RANGE`). The walking model's
stand-off is `dx`-only with the same 40 px floor, so on flat ground a shovel bot may hold 40 px off and never swing —
**not measured**. Measure first (standard `bots_report`: `rej_range` share with a shovel selected, and the distance held),
then hold the enemy inside the selected weapon's reach, as flight does.
