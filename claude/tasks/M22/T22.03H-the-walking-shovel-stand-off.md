# T22.03H — The walking model's shovel stand-off (40 px vs 28 px reach)

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.03D · Size: small · Origin: T22.03D (filed), T22.03E F7. **Built** (below).

T22.03D found, in flight, two shovel bots parked 32 px apart refusing every swing as out of range: `stand_off`'s 40 px
floor against the shovel's 28 px `effective_reach`. It fixed flight only (`BOT_SPACE_IN_RANGE`). The walking model's
stand-off is `dx`-only with the same 40 px floor, so on flat ground a shovel bot may hold 40 px off and never swing —
**not measured**. Measure first (standard `bots_report`: `rej_range` share with a shovel selected, and the distance held),
then hold the enemy inside the selected weapon's reach, as flight does.

## Done when
A unit: a walking bot with only a shovel closes to within its reach and swings (red at the parent commit); standard
kills not reduced; `cargo test -p game-core && ./scripts/check.sh --changed <base> --fast`.

## As built (builder, 2026-09-24)
- **Measured first** (scratch probe over standard `World`s, 16 seeds × 6 bots × 240 s, not kept): with the shovel in
  hand, **47 896** swings refused as out of range against **73** swings taken; at the refusal, |dx| 24–40 px on
  21 664 ticks (the 40 px floor against the 28 px reach). After: 143 swings; the refusals left are mostly **|dx| < 16
  with the distance 40–48 px** (17 806) — an enemy a body-height above or below, which a `dx`-only walk cannot close.
  Filed here, not fixed: the walking model's vertical miss with melee.

  `effective_reach`, a gun's `range`) — **one function for both models**: flight's T22.03D inline copy moved into it,
  and the walking model's `stop_within` calls it. Guns are unaffected where `range × 0.75 > stand_off`.
