## The findings
- **F1 — `GameScene`'s bit-7 path cannot fail** (player-visible). Planting
  `this.irradiated = flag(…) && false` **and** `FLAG.irradiated: 1 << 6` (poisoned's bit) left
  tsc, vitest 987/987 and `e2e radiation`/`radiation-standard` green. (a) `codec.test.ts`: a
  bit-7 row decoded from the **wire byte**, not the constant, with the existing all-false row as
  its control. (b) `DEV_START_BATTERY` beside `DEV_START_HEALTH`/`DEV_POISONED`, applied **after**
  `issue_suit` and §F7's kit, at join **and** respawn. (c) a networked arm: `radiation-match`,
  a flat suit irradiated in `GameScene`, a default suit sealed as control, and the edge strip on
  pixels. Re-run both plants; each must go red.
- **F2** (hygiene) — `radiation.mjs`: at both photos, the body's screen box intersects neither
  the centre control nor the HUD line box, so a layout change fails by name.
- **F3** (hygiene) — an explicit `assertUnchanged` on the centre at its own `maxDelta`, so
  "centre untinted" stops riding on the subject's threshold of 40 (a 37.5 tint passed it).
- **F4** (guard that cannot fail) — `affected.mjs`'s `byFile` keeps one check per file, so a
  scripts-only change to `radiation.mjs`, `thrusters.mjs` or `clouds.mjs` skips every entry but
  the last. `Map<file, check[]>`; pinned in `affected.test.mjs`, red first.
- **F8** (player-visible, minor) — the HUD line is `nowrap` at a fixed size and clips below
  ~550 px; wrap it inside `calc(100vw - 20px)`. Hide the sealed line outside `Playing` — it says
  "uses energy" while nothing drains.
- **F5** (nit) — "`p.alive && !p.is_dying()`: `is_dying` includes `alive`, simplify." Measure it:
  `!is_dying()` is *true* for the dead, so the simplification would irradiate corpses.

## As built (2026-09-23)

**F1.** (a) `codec.test.ts` decodes the wire byte `0b1000_0000` and `0b0100_0000`, each against the
other flag. (b) `Config::dev_start_battery: Option<f32>` — an `Option` because `0` is the value the
knob exists for; `Room::apply_dev_battery` runs after `grant_start_kit` at all three join sites and in
the respawn loop. (c) `radiation-match`, standalone, two servers with one human each. **Its pixel
control is stack A's frame, not a warmup frame** — warmup→Playing on one page moved the centre 89
(camera unsettled, sky shade follows the phase). Edge asserted on **green gain ≥ 20** over the sealed
frame (measured 45.6 at opacity 0.72; the red hit flash cannot supply green); centre moved 1.4.
**F8 went one step further than asked:** `suitState` takes `live`, and *both* pictures are off outside
`Playing` — the RADIATION glow in warmup or after the bell would be as false as "uses energy".
Bit 7 is not phase-gated; the gate is on the picture. The sandbox passes `true`.
**F5 refuted:** `!is_dying()` is true for the dead. Planting the simplification left every radiation
test green, so `the_dead_are_neither_drained_nor_irradiated` now guards `p.alive` (red under it:
*"a dead sealed suit drained, left: 96.99936"*).
