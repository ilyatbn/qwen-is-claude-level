# T22.21 — Sturdier, bigger asteroids with a tough core

Milestone: M22 (owner round 3) · Asked 2026-09-26, verbatim: *"also asteroids should be harder to destroy. i was
playing a game, and 2 minutes in, the map was mostly empty. make them larger again, and sturdier so they somehow take
less damage. the core should be even stronger so it takes a few hits to destroy the center of gravity."*

## The owner's metric, measured first
Asteroid rock remaining (pixels, % of round-start) at **t = 2 min** and at the **end of a round**, in a bots round on
the space map (≥ 8 seeds, Small and Medium), broken down by who carved it: meteors, the black hole, players/bots, cores.
Coordinator's suspect: R99 aims every space meteor at an asteroid (152/160 hit one in T22.14A's measurement).

## Rulings (coordinator, reversible by the named constant)
- **R110 — bigger again:** the asteroid base radius range grows by about a quarter (`SPACE_ASTEROID_R_MIN/MAX` × ~1.25,
  mass draw R103 unchanged on top); asteroid count may drop so the free-float share (T22.15's measure) stays ≥ its
  current value. Goldens (space rows) regenerated with the sweep.
- **R111 — sturdier rock:** a carve that bites asteroid rock in space removes rock with its radius × `ASTEROID_HARDNESS`
  = 0.5 (the rim is NOT hardened — a player's rocket must still breach it; standard maps unchanged). One place: the
  carve path, keyed on the map (space) and on whether the carve centre/target is asteroid rock (define precisely).
- **R112 — a tough core:** the core ignores carves until it has taken `CORE_HITS` = 3 hits (a hit = a carve whose
  disc overlaps the core); core pixels are uncarvable until then; the third hit destroys it (T22.16's destruction:
  well off, crumble, battery). Hits are hashed state, on the wire as needed for drawing (show damage: the core's glow
  dims per hit — both render paths, pixel-asserted).
- **R99 amended:** space meteors aim at a random open point inside the rim, not at an asteroid (they still despawn
  at the rim without carving).

## Added 2026-09-26 — iron asteroids (owner, verbatim: *"lets also have a couple asteroids be even larger, different darker color (lets say made of iron), and be indestructible."*)
- **R113 — iron asteroids:** each space map places **2** iron asteroids (`SPACE_IRON_COUNT`), radius 1.5–2× the largest
  ordinary asteroid (named constants), on their own generator sub-stream (so ordinary rocks don't move when the count
  changes); **indestructible** — every carve refuses their pixels (the same mechanism that protects pad ground, one
  predicate); drawn in a darker iron palette on both render paths (pixel-asserted against ordinary rock); gravity at
  the top level (5); **no core** (nothing to destroy, no battery); the black hole never picks an iron asteroid to eat;
  spawns/placement/vortex-trip clearance treat them as rock. They count as asteroids for "is this space" and the minimap
  (drawn darker there too). Include them in the owner's metric (they should stay at 100 %).

  A destroyed core's disc is soft, so the crumble takes it whole.

  `CarveResult::core_hit` + `changed()`: the three emitters that skipped empty carves now publish a hit that moved no
  pixel (the mirror counts hits off the carve stream). A hit marks the core's chunks dirty (the glow rebakes).
- **Wire:** asteroid record 27 → **29 bytes** (`u8 iron, u8 core_hits`); `set_asteroids` keeps the higher of held and
  given hits (the hole's re-install passes stale ones), `load_mask` zeroes them. **`black_hole` is now pushed before
  its eating carve** — the mirror drops the rock on the event, and a listed rock now guards its pixels.
- **R99 amended:** target = `random_open_space` on the shower's stream (160/160 rays through a rock centre → 2/160).
- **R113:** 2 iron rocks, r = 120..160 (1.5–2 × base 80), level 5, no core, **seated first** (`place_iron`,
