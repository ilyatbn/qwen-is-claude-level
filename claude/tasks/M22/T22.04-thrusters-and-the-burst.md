# T22.04 — Thrusters: the energy cost and the burst you can see

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.03** · Size: ~200 lines · Origin: new, 2026-09-18

Asked for: *"moving in space requires using energy, from the spacesuits thrusters, so add a
cool animation to it so that if i move down, you can see a burst of energy coming from above
the player."*

## The one rule the animation has to get right

**The plume fires from the side opposite the direction you travel.** Move down, the burst is
*above* you. That is Newton and it is also what the owner asked for in as many words. Getting
it backwards is the kind of thing that looks fine in a still and wrong in motion, so this is
asserted on rendered pixels with the player's velocity as the control.

## Read first
- `client/src/render/ordnanceFx.ts` and `client/src/render/shaders.ts` — how T21.17 plumbed
  effects, and what a High-Quality-off fallback has to look like
- `client/src/render/playerView.ts` — `setState`, and how wings and boots are toggled per frame
  off snapshot bits rather than rebuilt
- `client/src/ui/jetpackReadout-math.ts` — the fuel readout this shares a resource with
- T21.36 — *"fire you can see"*: flames covered 108 of 192 burn-circle points with High Quality
  off. The lesson is that the two render paths need the same assertion, not the same code.

## Deliverable

- **The cost**, in `T22.03`'s fuel economy: thrusting consumes, idling does not. One constant,
  in `constants.rs`, and the readout already exists.
- **The plume**, drawn opposite the thrust vector, in **both** render paths. High Quality gets
  the shader; Canvas gets something that is unmistakably the same event. `T21.33` is the
  reminder that the owner's browser has run Canvas and that no check had.
- **A wire bit for the thrust direction**, derived at the encode site. It is cosmetic, so it
  does not belong in the hashed simulation state — but it must be *derived*, not stored, or it
  is a third place that can disagree with `MoveMods`.

## Hazards

- **Do not rebuild `PlayerView` to add it.** T20.12's `sameAppearance` rebuild guard exists
  because appearance fields are `private readonly` with no setter; a plume added at construction
  vanishes on the next rebuild. Toggle per frame, the way boots and wings do.
- **A player thrusting in two axes at once** has one plume or two — decide, and say which.
- **Nobody is thrusting when the round is over.** T21.30 froze input in `Ended`; the plume must
  freeze with it or a corpse fires its jets.

## Tests

- **Rendered pixels, with a control region and a control frame** (§C2). Thrust down, assert the
  bright region is *above* the player's centre and that a region below is unchanged.
- The same assertion **with High Quality off**, which is the half T21.36 caught.
- Fuel falls while thrusting and does not while idle — both ends, against the readout.
- No plume while `Ended`.
