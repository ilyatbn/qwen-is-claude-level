# T22.16 — Asteroid cores and the battery

Milestone: M22 (owner round 2) · Rulings: R102 in [M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md](M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md) — read it first; the
owner's words are there verbatim. Plus the coordinator's two refinements from the review of T22.15 (A: a hollowed
centre traps a body in an undamped bounce; B: reach from the core, "a few pixels").

> *"when an 'asterodid' is destroyed (make like a round core at the center) its gravity pull disappears, a battery is
> spawned when its destroyed."*

## Re-verified against the tree (`8e6f59a`)

- The well is read in one place: `attractors::asteroid_attractors(map)` over `map.meta.asteroids`, summed by `env_at`
  on both sides (server `World::apply_inputs`, mirror `GameCore::apply_input`). The hole removes its rock from that
  list (`World::arrive_black_hole`) and the mirror drops it by centre (`WorldMirror.pushBlackHole`).
- Attractor events carry `tick`, mapped to the first seq it changed by `seqClock.ts::firstSeqAfter`
  (`WorldMirror.anchorSeqs`); the core takes seq spans (`GameCore::set_vortices`, `SeqSpan`).
- The terrain is one baked canvas texture per chunk (`render/chunkBake.ts::bakeChunk`) for **both** renderers; the
  client draws no asteroid overlay today.
- Loot drops go through `World::drop_wildlife_loot` (make room, `items.spawn`, `ItemSpawn`); in space an item floats
  where it is put (R14). Bots shop for any `world.items` entry they can see (`bots/mod.rs`, battery first when the suit
  is low); **no bot code reads a well** (`grep -rn "env_at\|wells_at\|field_at\|well_reach" crates/game-core/src/bots`
  → none), so a vanished well leaves no stale model in a bot.

## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **Two radii, named apart.** The *core* (R102) is a new disc, `SPACE_CORE_FRAC` × `r` = **0.3 r** (7..21 px over the
  grown range 24..70) — small and central, the thing drawn and destroyed. The generator's `SPACE_ASTEROID_CORE_FRAC`
  (0.75) stays the rock's round *body*, and **refinement B's reach is measured from that body, not from the R102 core**:
  the inequality B asks for, reach ≥ the farthest a touching body can be, is
  `f·r + PLAYER_H/2 + WELL_SURFACE_BAND ≥ r + ½·hypot(PLAYER_W, PLAYER_H)` ⇔ `(1 − f)·r ≤ 28 − 2.12`, which at r = 70
  needs f ≥ 0.63: **0.75 holds it (8.4 px spare at r = 70), 0.3 does not (fails from r = 37)**. Reported, not
  hidden: "reach from the core radius" read literally would leave a body on a big rock's outer lump unpulled.
- **Destroyed = at least `SPACE_CORE_DESTROYED_FRAC` (0.2) of the core disc's pixels are air.** Refinement A's
  arithmetic: a body whose box contains the rock's centre covers at least the part of the core in a 16 × 28 box with
  the centre at its corner — a quarter disc while c ≤ `PLAYER_W`, 310 of 1373 px (22.6 %) at c = 21 — so the minimum
  over every r in 24..70 and every pixel placement is **22.58 %** (brute-forced in
  `cores::tests::a_body_that_can_hold_the_centre_has_already_destroyed_the_core`). 0.2 is under it: **no body-sized
  cavity can hold the centre while the well is on.**
- **On destruction the rest of the core crumbles** (one `Carve`, radius = core, `CarveKind::Meteor` — the hole's
  kind): the drawn core disappears whole rather than standing 80 % intact and dead, and the centre the battery floats
  at is air. The flag is sticky (the round), derived once from the mask by the server.
- **The battery**: one `BATTERY_PACK` at the core's centre through `drop_wildlife_loot` (now taking the drop's
  velocity — zero here: it floats where the core was), `SpawnSource::Periodic` like a bird's.
- **The flag lives on the rock**: `Asteroid::core_intact` (true at generation, so no golden row moves; hashed in
  `World::state_hash`). `asteroid_attractors` skips a rock whose core is gone. The black hole removes its rock from the
  list **before** carving it, so an eaten rock is never counted as a core destruction.
- **The wire**: `core_destroyed {tick, x, y}` (Everyone; in the join catch-up). The mirror keeps the list for the
  round (a resync keeps it, like the vortices), hands the core each dead core's `firstSeqAfter(tick)`, and
  `GameCore::apply_input` sets each rock's flag for the seq it steps — so a replay of a seq before the destruction
  still pulls. `map_init` does not carry the flag (joins are refused mid-match; the catch-up and the event cover it).

## Tests

`cores.rs`: the threshold under every centre-holding box (brute force, the arithmetic above; control: a threshold
over the minimum is caught); a carved centre switches the well off, crumbles the core, spawns exactly one battery
(presence) and a shallow crater does not (absence + control); the hole eating a rock is no destruction; deterministic
(two worlds, same hash). `attractors.rs`: **the reviewer's hollow** — a body at rest 20 px from a carved centre, 10 s:
it settles or feels nothing (red at `8e6f59a`: ±20 px at ~54 px/s); the reach covers a body on the outermost lump for
every r (the inequality); the air-gap report before/after. `game-wasm`: a core destroyed and heard late switches the
well off at the server's seq (side by side, exact; control: switched off when heard). Bots: a low-suit bot shops for
the core's battery. Vitest: the mirror keys the switch to the seq and keeps it across a resync.
