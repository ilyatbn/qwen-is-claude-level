# T22.10H — Positions to an eighth of a pixel

Milestone: M22 · Coordinator brief (2026-09-24), from `DOCS-77-OWED.md` point 6 and T22.12B's √2 slack ·
Size: small

## The defect
Snapshot position and velocity were `as i16` — whole px and whole px/s, **truncated toward zero**. So every
re-anchor was up to 1 px (and 1 px/s) off per axis, always toward the origin: a free-flying zero-g body re-anchored
on it drifted past `RECONCILE_EPSILON_PX` within ~1.5 s (T22.10F), `thrusters-match` needed 2 × ε after the bell,
and `black-hole`'s pull arm needed √2 px of slack (T22.12B). The coordinator ruled the wire change (T22.10G had
ruled it out of scope).

## Brief
- Position and velocity at **1/8 px (1/8 px/s), rounded**, in whatever fixed point fits the largest map and the
  fastest body — **state the range arithmetic at the constant**.
- **One wire-quantum constant** in `constants.rs`; the codec (both sides) and every check tolerance that charges
  for the wire's rounding derive from it. No literals.
- Health: rounded, or finer if cheap — **first check what `speed_multiplier()` reads** (T20.21).
- Codec tests round-trip at the extremes, both sides; `SNAPSHOT_PLAYER_BYTES` and the stale "102 bytes" comment.
- `REPLAY_VERSION` only if replays are affected — say.
- Measure `lastAckErrorPx` before/after on `radiation-match` and `breach-vortex`; update `ARCHITECTURE-SURVEY.md` §2
  and `DOCS-77-OWED.md` point 6.

## As built (2026-09-24)
- **`SNAPSHOT_QUANTUM` = 1/8** (`constants.rs`), one constant for position (px) and velocity (px/s); the range
  arithmetic is at it: an `i16` of eighths stops at ±4095.875 — short of `MAP_LARGE_W` — and upward velocity is
  unclamped, so all four are **`i32`**, `round(v / q)` (half away from zero; `as` saturates, NaN → 0). Two const
  asserts pin the range claim. `SNAPSHOT_PLAYER_BYTES` 20 → 28 (six players 132 → 180 B before base64); the stale
  "8 + 15n + 4, so 102" comment now points at the constant. Decoders: `codec.rs::dequantize` (`SnapshotPlayer.x…`
  are `f32` px) and `codec.ts::decodeSnapshot` (`Reader.i32 × C().SNAPSHOT_QUANTUM`, exported by `constants_json`).
- **Health stays the truncated `u8`, deliberately**: `PlayerState::speed_multiplier` reads `health.floor()`, so the
  floor is exactly what a re-installed client must walk with; rounding would send 50 for 49.6 (the T20.19/T20.21
  shape). Tested (`health_goes_out_as_the_floor_speed_multiplier_reads`).
- **`REPLAY_VERSION` unchanged**: replays record commands (inputs, joins, readies), never snapshots.
- **Tolerances derived from it**: `black-hole` ε + √2·q (was ε + √2); `thrusters-match` after the bell
  ε + √2·q·(1 + `AFTER_BELL_S`)/2 (was 2ε, whose basis was truncated whole px/s).
- **`black-hole`'s pull sampler starts before the probe** and reads the placement in-page: it started after three
  Playwright round-trips, so on a loaded box the pull had carried the body past `near` — *"the prediction never
  reached the placed body"* 1/2 at the base (4-way parallel), 3/3 after (two 4-way runs and the `--changed` gate), 0/1 alone; 2/2 green
  in 4-way runs after the fix. Not the wire's doing; found because the Done-when ran it.
- **Plants, each red**: truncation (`as i32` without `round`) → *"vx 0.2 came back 0.125: more than half a quantum
  (0.0625) off"* and the rounding test; rounded health → *"health 49.6 left: 50.0 right: 49.0"*; TS `q = 1` →
  *"decodes every field"*, *"…i32 quanta at the extremes"* red.
- **Measured** (worst `lastAckErrorPx` per client, `gate-t2210h-{before,after}.txt`, 2 × 4-way runs each):
  `radiation-match` 2.12–2.61 → 1.40–2.24 px; `breach-vortex` 2.02–2.25 → 2.08–2.23 px (whole-match maxima are
  not the wire's — its pull arm 1.00–1.09 → 0.10–0.13 px); `black-hole` pull arm 1.26 → 0.19–0.34 px;
  `thrusters-match` after the bell: base run 1 **red** (4.34 px vs 2ε), after 0 corrections in 4 runs.
