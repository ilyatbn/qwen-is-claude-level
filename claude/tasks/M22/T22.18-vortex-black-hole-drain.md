## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **R105 — `VORTEX_CAPTURE_R` = `SPACE_RIM_THICKNESS` (32; was 127), `VORTEX_REACH` = 4 × = 128 (was 508),** drawn ring
  32 and swirl 64 (were 127 / 254) — a quarter each. Basis: the vortex sits on the rim's centreline, so a disc one
  thickness across spans the hole's depth and reaches half a thickness into the arena; a body at the hole's mouth
  (centre `PLAYER_H / 2` inside the inner edge, 30 px from the centreline) is taken. `VORTEX_ACCEL_MAX` unchanged
  (smaller, not weaker).
- **R16 restated (a hole never kills):** the old 127 was sized so the disc covered every way out of a one-carve hole.
  At 32 it does not (a meteor hole is ~94 px wide), so **`vortex::captor` gains an outside arm: a body past the rim's
  outer edge (`SpaceGeometry::past_outer_edge`) is taken by the nearest vortex, live or spent, at any distance.** Only a
  hole puts a body there; `SPACE_VOID_GRACE` guarantees two samples in the band; `step_vortices` runs before the void.
  It also covers a hole widened by carves that R87 reported as no new breach. With no vortex at all the void still kills.
- **R97 unchanged in form**: outside the capture radius the capped sum (675) is under every thrust; re-tested from one
  pixel outside the new ring.
- **R106 — `BLACK_HOLE_REACH` = 8 × horizon = 512 (was 256).** `BLACK_HOLE_ACCEL_MAX` =
  `EDGE_PULL / (1 − HORIZON/REACH)` = 810 / 0.875 ≈ 925.7 (was 1080): the linear law falls outward, so the pull outside
  the horizon is ≤ 810 = 0.9 × DOWN at any reach — R90 holds. R91 now mutes the wells over 4× the area (most of a Small
  arena in the last minute) — the ruling's cost, stated at the constant.
- **`BLACK_HOLE_HORIZON_R` kept at 64**, basis corrected: it is the largest *base* radius, not the largest rock (70 since
  R103). R106 says the horizon and the kill stay; the ring is the rule and the crater (r + 2) is only terrain.
- **The escape flights move to Large maps** (`black_hole.rs::ESCAPE_SCALE`, and `bots/space.rs`'s flying-bot escape):
  the clearing (`REACH + 8 + PLAYER_H` = 548) is taller than a Small arena's half-height (400), so no Small map
  qualified. On Large the first 13 seeds all qualify, 0 skipped.
- **R108 — `RADIATION_SHIELD_COST` = 0.5 × `RADIATION_DPS`** (was equal): one energy holds off two damage; a full suit
  200 s, a pack 100 s. Jetpack fuel unchanged.
- **The core's battery (T22.16's review):** a bigger crumble does not make the battery reachable — a body-sized pocket
  behind a 6 px tunnel is just as sealed — so `cores::battery_site` asks the question itself: a flood over body centres
  (body-fitting one-pixel moves, a summed-area table of the window) from open space round the rock; the battery stays at
  the centre (R102) when a body can get within `PICKUP_RADIUS` of it, else goes to the reachable centre nearest it.
