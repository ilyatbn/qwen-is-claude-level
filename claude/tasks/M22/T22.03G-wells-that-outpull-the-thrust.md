# T22.03G — Pockets where the summed wells out-pull the thrust (and a vortex against rock)

trace. **Not built.**

## What the trace found (T22.03E, `space_bots_report` offset 32, 32 seeds)
- **The summed asteroid field can exceed the weakest thrust.** Seed 451383, a body at (2194, 1235) under a rock ceiling:
  `attractors::field_at` = **(18, −919) px/s²** against `JETPACK_THRUST_DOWN` **900**. Thrust in each of the 8 directions
  for 30 ticks on a full tank moved it **0 px** (1.8 px sideways, into a 1-px bump — airborne bodies do not step up). A
  bot there refills and burns for the rest of the round; **a human would be held the same way** (same physics). Each
  well is clamped under the thrust (`well_strength`'s "escape ceiling"; `BOT_SPACE_BRAKE` is "the weakest thrust less the
  strongest well" — "the one brake a bot can count on next to any rock"), but **the sum of two or three is not**. T22.03D
  already met it once ("two wells beating DOWN thrust in a crevice") and treated it as a detour problem.
- **A vortex against rock** (T22.03C): a bot 190 / 202 px from two vortices — inside the no-escape disc
  (`VORTEX_REACH / 2`) — pressed on rock, burns every refill escaping and stays (runs to 60 s).
- **An item never picked up** (seed 261327): a bot on `Goal::Item` 12 px from its item, inside `PICKUP_RADIUS / 2`, full
  tank, still for 40 s — the destination is "arrived" and the pickup never happens. Unexplained; trace it.

## To rule (coordinator)
The physics claim first: may the **summed** field exceed the weakest thrust? If not, clamp the sum (both sides use
`attractors::env`, so it stays one function) and assert it over every map's worst point; if so, the bots need a way out
(dig with the shovel, walk along the face) and the report a trap count.

## Done when (once ruled)
