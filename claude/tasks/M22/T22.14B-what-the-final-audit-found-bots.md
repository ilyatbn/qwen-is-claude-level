# T22.14B — What the final audit found: the bots

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.03I, T22.14A · Size: large (split at compiling boundaries — one commit per finding
group) · Origin: the final M22 audit of the bots (read-only reviewers, findings from code reading). Every finding is
measured or red-first before its fix.

## What the audit found
- **M1 (MED, player-visible).** `space::wanted` refuses a destination inside a keep-out
  (`dest.filter(|d| !forbidden(world, d.at))`) and returns zero velocity — for `Goal::Enemy` that is an enemy within
  ~320 px of a live vortex or inside the black hole's reach, and the bot freezes pressing nothing (fits T22.03G/I's
  "idles on rock with fuel, enemy behind the rock", seed 79190). **Fix:** clamp the destination to the nearest point
  outside the disc along the line; trace 79190 before/after.
- **M5 (MED).** Vortex trips rose 1.67 → 2.40 → 2.79 a bot a round unnoticed; suspected: `space::keep_out` skips
  `world.spent_vortices`, which still capture (R88). Measure trips at spent vs live vortices; include the spent ones;
  a trips bound with a stated basis.
- **M2.** `STANDARD_VOID_MAX` (0.53) gates nothing on the 8 default seeds (0.23 vs plant 0.42) and sits 0.02 from the
  worst 32-seed draw — bound the pooled 128 seeds with a basis, or print it and name the unit test as the guard.
- **M3.** `PINNED_ANY_RUNS_MAX` 0.01 and `PINNED_WINGS_RUNS_MAX` 0.015 are one event from red on the default run —
  restate as "≤ N runs" with a basis, or measure over more seeds.
- **M4.** Winged bots use the walking model in space with no black-hole/vortex/flare escape (`leg_barred` covers only
  the sweep's legs) and their environment deaths are never counted — count them; give winged bots in space the same
  keep-out/escape (shared functions); trace the remaining 27–35 % "stuck while moving" winged share (file if not fixed).
- **LOW.** The bots copy the hazards' geometry — replace with world-owned point predicates called from both
  (`black_hole::clearance`, `vortex::clearance`/capture, `SolarFlare::touches`, the void check: feet vs `head_y`);
  move `bots/mod.rs`'s tunables into `constants.rs` with basis comments; two misplaced doc comments; `think`'s stale
  25-line "wings change nothing here" note.
- **Structure.** Split `bots/mod.rs` (4156 lines) into `walk.rs`, `arms.rs`, `explore.rs` — pure moves, state hashes
  identical before/after on 8 seeds standard + space.

- **M5 — measured, and the suspect was the smaller half.** At `e454373` the trips were **54 on the 8 seeds (1.12 a bot
  a round; 0.65/0.80/0.79/0.49 on the draws), of them at spent vortices 5 / 20 / 3 / 15 / 0** — about 8 %. (R99 had
  already taken the rise back from 2.79: showers no longer open vortices.) None at a vortex under 1 s old. Traced
  (125 trips, offset 0): **69 were a bot with a dry tank a second before, 41 at the reserve**: it hovered at the
  keep-out's edge, `VORTEX_REACH / 2` + margin = 318 px, **where a live vortex's pull is already the cap** (1800 × (1 −
  318/508) ≈ 673 px/s²) — escaping in and out of the margin on thrust until the tank ran dry, then taken. So:
  `KeepOut::Live` is the **whole pull** (`vortex::pull_clearance`, `VORTEX_REACH`), `KeepOut::Spent` the capture radius
  (`vortex::capture_clearance` — it catches, R88, and pulls nothing). Spent only (the audit's fix alone,
  `gate-t2214b-v1-spent-only.txt`): 1.02 / 0.50 / 0.56 / 0.82 / 0.52 — spent trips gone, live ones not. Whole pull
  (`gate-t2214b-after.txt`): **0.27 / 0.17 / 0.21 / 0.23 / 0.17**; of the 13 left on the 8 seeds, **8 are a respawn
  beside a hole** (respawns and joins are held clear of the black hole only — filed, a world placement). Bound
