# T22.08 — Solar flares

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.01** · Size: ~250 lines, likely a split · Origin: new,
2026-09-18

Asked for: *"solar flares (make it a cool shader) burns players touching it for N seconds
(default to 4). should look like a magnetic solar prominence loop or fiery ribbon strand moving
at random on the map."*

## `BurnField` is the wrong home, and its own first line says so

`weapons/burn.rs` opens: *"Damaging ground zones"*. §F12's recorded reasoning is that a fire
which drifted off the ground *"would be a second fire"*. A flare is a **ribbon moving through
open space with no ground under it** — the exact thing that comment rules out.

**So: either this is a new hazard shape, or `BurnField` is generalised deliberately and its doc
comment is rewritten to say so.** Do not quietly widen a type whose comment argues against the
widening. Whichever is chosen, say why in the file.

What `BurnField` *does* have that is worth reusing is the burn **duration** model — the owner's
*"burns players touching it for N seconds"* is the same shape as the flamethrower's lingering
damage, and `FLAME`/molotov/lava already share it.

## Read first
- `crates/game-core/src/weapons/burn.rs` — the ground-zone model and §F12's reasoning
- `crates/game-core/src/effects/scheduler.rs` — `KINDS`, `WEIGHTS`, and the `enabled` table
- `crates/game-core/src/effects/meteor.rs` — the closest existing hazard: it moves, it arrives,
  it damages
- `client/src/render/shaders.ts` — T21.17's plumbing, and the fallback contract

## The scheduler seam is already the right shape

On 2026-09-16 `EffectScheduler`'s `toxic_enabled` bool became `enabled: [bool; KINDS.len()]` —
a per-instance field, **already hashed**, injected through `with_enabled`, with
`enabled_from_constants()` as the only thing tying it to the constants. A hazard that exists
**only in space** is that array taking its value from the match setting.

**Two consequences to plan for, not discover:**
- Adding a `KINDS` entry changes the hash shape and moves `REPLAY_VERSION`.
- `two_live_kinds_alternate` pins that two live kinds under never-repeat produce a strict
  alternation. Space's table is a different set; the same arithmetic applies to it and the test
  should be read before the table is chosen.

## Deliverable

- A hazard that **moves at random across the map** and burns on contact for `N` seconds,
  `N = 4` by default, in `constants.rs` and nowhere else.
- A shader that reads as a prominence loop or a fiery ribbon, **with a Canvas fallback that is
  unmistakably the same event** (T21.33, T21.36).
- Scheduled only in space.

## Hazards

- **Assert on effects, not intentions.** T21.25 is the precedent: toxic rain's arithmetic said
  ~47 damage a shower while `toxic-rain-game.mjs` asserted drops exist and are drawn and **never
  read a health bar**. A flare check must read health.
- **T21.36's coverage lesson**: with High Quality off, flames covered 108 of 192 burn-circle
  points. Painted fire must cover every damage point in **both** modes, asserted.
- **A moving hazard is a prediction question.** If it damages on contact, both sides must agree
  where it is on the same tick, or it kills people who dodged it on their screen.

## Tests

- A player touching a flare **loses health**, and one beside it does not — the control.
- The burn lasts `N` seconds, pinned to the constant, and the constant's *value* is justified
  against a measured basis (`ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL`'s lesson: a suite pinned to a constant cannot
  detect the constant moving).
- The painted flare covers every damaging point, in both render modes.
- It appears in space and **never** in the other modes — with the control that it does appear
  when it should, or "never" is satisfied by a hazard that was never built.
