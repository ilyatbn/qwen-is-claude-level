# T22.22 — A strong brake in space, and less iron on Small maps

Milestone: M22 (owner round 3) · Filed 2026-09-26 by the builder from the coordinator's rulings, after T22.20 (R109,
R109b) and T22.21 (R113).

## Rulings (coordinator; each reversible by the named constant)
- **R109c — gentle to accelerate, strong to stop.** In space, the component of thrust that **opposes** the body's
  current velocity is applied at full strength (`SPACE_BRAKE_SCALE` = 1.0); the rest stays at `SPACE_THRUST_SCALE` 0.5.
  One place (`jetpack::thrust_delta`), server == mirror exactly (side-by-side test). The escape caps keep deriving from
  the **unboosted** weakest thrust (`SPACE_THRUST_DOWN`), so every guarantee stays conservative. Target (R109b): stop
  from top speed (450) in < 0.5 s and < 100 px — report measured; if missed, state the arithmetic and the smallest
  change that meets it (do not raise the max speed). Near-zero velocity must not blow up ("opposes" with a dead-band);
  the plume (T22.04C `thrust_at`) must still point against the applied thrust. Bots brake by counter-thrust: re-run
  `space_bots_report` and `space_flight_report`. `REPLAY_VERSION` bump.
- **Iron on Small maps.** T22.21's two iron rocks are 56 % of all asteroid rock on Small. Scale the iron radius range
  per map scale so iron is ≤ ~35 % of rock on Small, keeping **2** per map (the owner: *"a couple"*); Medium/Large
  unchanged unless also above 35 %. Space golden rows regenerated with the sweep; iron share per scale before/after.
- Also: attribute `thrusters-match`'s death-arm precondition failure (red 2/2 at `2e4c815`) — R109b or T22.21's map —
  and fix the check's setup if the check is at fault.

## As built
- **`jetpack::brake_axis(push, v)`**, called from `thrust_delta(input, gravity, vel, dt)` in space only (standard/low
  never read `vel`: bit-identical, asserted). **Per axis, not a projection** — the buttons are axes and the clamp in
  `apply_thrust` is per axis; a projection would turn LEFT while drifting diagonally into a push with a vertical part
  nobody pressed. *Decision for the coordinator; reversible in the one function.* An axis whose push opposes that
  axis's velocity pushes at `SPACE_BRAKE_SCALE / SPACE_THRUST_SCALE` × the gentle push **only until the velocity
  reaches zero**; the rest of the tick is at the gentle share. So the boost never accelerates, never overshoots, and is
  continuous at `v = 0` — the dead-band in its exact form (no threshold to chatter across; the only quotient is of two
  constants). The sign of each axis never changes, so `space::engaging` reads the same.
- Callers: `apply_thrust` (`body.vel`, before the push), `space::engaging` (`body.vel`), wasm `thrust_at` (the body's
  velocity *after* the tick, asked at `SIM_DT` and returned ÷ `SIM_DT`: while braking the axis still opposes, so the
  plume's per-axis direction is the applied push's; only a zero-crossing tick reads the gentle share).
- Escape caps untouched: `SPACE_WELL_ACCEL_MAX`, `VORTEX_ACCEL_MAX`, `BLACK_HOLE_EDGE_PULL` still derive from
  `SPACE_THRUST_DOWN` (450). A body at rest against a pull gets no boost, so the guarantees are as before; the boost
  only ever adds to a body already moving into the pull.
- **Iron:** `ScaleParams::iron_r_frac` (per scale) → `SpaceParams::iron_r_frac` → `space::iron_radii` (one spelling for
  `place_iron` and its tests). Small **1.125–1.25 × 80 = 90..100 px** (`SPACE_IRON_R_{MIN,MAX}_FRAC_SMALL`); Medium and
  Large keep 1.5–2× (120..160). The floor is the smallest round share over the largest grown ordinary rock (88), so
  iron is still bigger than every ordinary rock. `SPACE_IRON_SHARE_MAX` 0.35. `ScaleParams` lost `Eq` (f32 field; no
  caller used it). `REPLAY_VERSION` 34 → 35.
