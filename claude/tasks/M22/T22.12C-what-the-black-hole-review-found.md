# T22.12C — What the black-hole review found

Milestone: M22 · From the measured review of T22.12A/B (`5a2496e`, `d131c86`) · Coordinator rulings R90–R93,

## Findings (the review measured each)
- **F1 → R90 — the sizes were ruled against the wrong thrust.** `BLACK_HOLE_THRUST_BOUND` = UP + SIDE, but the
  weakest thrust is **DOWN** (`JETPACK_THRUST_DOWN` 900), and the anisotropy put the no-escape line at a different
  radius on every side — the faint "capture" ring promised a rule the physics did not keep. Ruled: the drawn ring
  is the horizon and the horizon is the rule. `BLACK_HOLE_ESCAPE_MARGIN` 0.9, `BLACK_HOLE_EDGE_PULL` =
  `JETPACK_THRUST_DOWN` × margin (the pull at the horizon), `BLACK_HOLE_REACH` = 4 × `BLACK_HOLE_HORIZON_R`,
  `BLACK_HOLE_ACCEL_MAX` = `EDGE_PULL / (1 − HORIZON_R / REACH)`. Delete `BLACK_HOLE_THRUST_BOUND`,
  `BLACK_HOLE_CAPTURE_R`, the capture ring in `blackHoleFx`, and the capture-based check arm.
- **R91 — rocks near the hole trapped 20/208 flights.** Neighbouring wells summed to 745 px/s² at the horizon.
  Within the hole's `REACH` only the hole pulls — in the one `env_at` summation (not a second loop), identically on
  both sides. Test: from `HORIZON_R + ε` on every open side over ≥ 10 seeds, full thrust away escapes past `REACH`
  (0/208 escaped at the review's base); keep kill-inside-horizon; a presence control that wells still pull outside it.
- **R92 — a black-hole death drops nothing** (the hole swallows the inventory): no floating loot bait at the horizon.
- **R93 — a 2 s telegraph at the arrival spot**, from the same deterministic schedule (sent if the client cannot
  know the arrival time), and **the hole on the minimap**.
- **F2 — bots die in the hole** (6 and 5 deaths on two of six seeds). Not flight here (T22.03B's): a "must also"
  line in T22.03B, and a one-line avoidance in item picking if trivial.
- **F3 — two clearances untested**: `World::spawn_for` (mid-round join) and the vortex-trip destination, each with
  the hole on the site the unfiltered picker would choose; plant each red.
- **F5 — the bell**: the client pulls until it *hears* `ended`; the server stopped on its Ended tick. Gate the
  client's pull on the bell **tick** (derivable from the round clock); measure the bell correction for a body in
  the pull, with a test — do not hide it as settled.
- **F6** — T22.12B's As-built misattributes the 12.8 px jump (a one-tick off-by-one on a stalled page during the
  coverage arm, not the no-escape arm).
- **F7** — re-run the "client not told" plant after R90 (pull ~6× weaker) — the rubber-band gate must still
  separate; its slack from the wire quantum (T22.10H).
- **F8** — the arrival window on short (dev) rounds: scale it, or document it at the constant.
- **F9** — check arm 6 asserts "not pulled after the bell"; `DeathCause::BlackHole` only when the kill was gated
  (`Playing`).
- DOCS-77 points 7–8 rewritten.
