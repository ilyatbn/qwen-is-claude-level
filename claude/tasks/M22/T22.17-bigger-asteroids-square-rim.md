## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **Inset `SPACE_RIM_INSET` = `SKY_MARGIN` (96) on all four sides.** The top cannot be higher
  (`force_borders`). The sides and bottom do **not** hug the `WALL_W` bands / `FLOOR_CRUST`: a rim
  flush against solid border has no air outside it, so `breach_in` (which floods from air past the
  outer edge) could never see a breach there and the void would not exist on that side. 88 px of
  air outside each side, 80 px above the crust; the grace band is 45.
- **Outside the rim the distance is Chebyshev**, so the outer edge, the breach flood's seed band
  and the void line are all square-cornered like the rock. Inside it is exact Euclidean.
- **The band is judged at pixel centres** (`in_rim_band`), so it is exactly 32 px on every side,
  symmetric: first rock at 96 from left/top, last at 96+1 from right/bottom.
- **Mass (R103)**: `m ~ U[0, SPACE_ASTEROID_MASS_MAX]` on sub-stream `"asteroid_mass"`, one draw per
  candidate; `r = round(base · sqrt(1 + m))` (`grown_radius`, 64 → 70 max). Rim clearance,
  spacing, level, stamp and wire all use the grown radius. **The level is read off the grown
  radius** (bigger rock, stronger pull); level 5 rises from 10.9 % to 16.7 % on Large.
  `SPACE_ASTEROID_R_MIN..=R_MAX` stays the *base* band; `BLACK_HOLE_HORIZON_R` (= `R_MAX`, "the
  size of the largest asteroid") is **left for T22.18**, which owns the hole's sizes (R106).
- **Asteroid counts unchanged**: the bigger arena makes the field sparser (coverage 6.5/6.3/6.3 %).
