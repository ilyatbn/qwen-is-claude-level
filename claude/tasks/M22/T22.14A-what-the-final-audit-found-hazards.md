# T22.14A — What the final audit found: the hazards

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.03I (R97), T22.10I (R98), T22.12E, T22.13 · Size: large (split at compiling
boundaries — one commit per finding) · Origin: the final M22 audit (read-only reviewers, findings from code reading),
and **R99** (coordinator ruling on the shower in space). Every finding is measured or red-first before its fix.

## What the audit found
- **H1 (HIGH).** Inside `BLACK_HOLE_REACH`, `attractors::env_at` mutes the wells (R91) but still passes the live
  vortices to `capped_at` — up to 675 px/s² after the cap, plus the hole's 810 at the horizon: ~1485 against DOWN 900 /
  SIDE 1100, so a player **outside** the horizon can be dragged in. The escape test drained the carve's breaches
  (`take_breaches()`) to dodge exactly this. **Fix:** inside the hole's reach no vortex pulls either (capture there is by
  radius, so the rim never becomes an exit); a vortex arm in the escape test, red before. Mirror identical (one `env_at`).
- **H2 (MED).** Respawns and vortex-trip destinations built their clearance from `self.black_hole.pos()`, `None` while
  `BlackHole::Warned` — a player could be put where the hole opens 2 s later and die with no warning. **Fix:**
  `World::black_hole_site()` (warned or here) at every placement site.
- **H3 (MED).** Meteors and fragments in flight at the bell kept dealing damage, deaths and kill credit in `Ended`
  (`step_projectiles` is not phase-gated; `apply_damage_log` refused only `Warmup`; `active_duration(MeteorShower)` had
  no flight time). **Ruled:** weather-sourced damage is refused in `Ended` (one rule in `apply_damage_log`, like
  Warmup), **and** the shower's `active_duration` includes the fall time, so a shower cannot end at the bell with
  meteors airborne.
- **R99 (ruling).** In space, meteors spawn just inside the rim (random angle) and fly toward a random asteroid; a
  meteor or fragment reaching the rim despawns **without carving** — the rim breaks only from player weapons (the
  owner's *"if you make a hole"* secret). Standard unchanged (its golden weather table must not move); a SPACE block in
  the weather golden (T22.08C F2's argument: a comparison of the table against itself cannot fail).
- **B3 (MED).** "Is this space?" was `!meta.asteroids.is_empty()` while `arrive_black_hole` removes asteroids at
  runtime — only its `n >= 2` branch kept space from switching off mid-round. **Fix:** a `MapMeta` generator field set
  at generation and carried by `map_init`; the predicate reads it; stale docs fixed.
- **B4 (LOW).** `World::darkness` keyed on `self.gravity == Space` while the phase change used the map. **Fix:** key
  it on the map (B3's field); `space_has_no_night` built with `with_gravity(Space)`.
- **L (LOW).** The black-hole disc grew 0→1 over 900 ms while the server kills at the full horizon on arrival; and
  `vortexFx-math::vortexRadii` still faded the swirl at `REACH / 2`; `vortex::clearance` still said "no-escape disc".
