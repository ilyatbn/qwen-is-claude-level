## The findings
- **F1 (player-visible, HIGH) — the void took players the vortex had caught.** Two causes. The capture
  read the teleport cooldown, so a player who had just used a pad or just been taken was *declined*
  and pulled through the hole; and `random_body_site` could put a caught player inside another
  vortex's pull. 2–5 of 12 seeds died. **R86**: the destination is chosen outside `VORTEX_REACH / 2`
  of every hole, by a clearance parameter on the **shared** picker (`Map::random_body_site_where`,
  which `random_body_site` now calls with an accept-everything clearance — no copy); the capture
  ignores the cooldown; a picker that finds nothing clear falls back to the clearest open point
  over the draws and the whole `open_space_grid` — never `None` while there is open space.
- **F2 (player-visible) — a phantom vortex over solid rim.** `note_breach` asked whether air crosses the
  rim *anywhere* in the carve's padded box, and the box reaches an existing hole. A meteor-sized carve
  biting half-way into the rim 128 px from a hole (just past `VORTEX_CAPTURE_R`) opened a new vortex;
  with three live it evicted a real one. **R87**: `breach_in` on the same box **before and after** the
  carve; a breach is closed → open only (`Map::breach_probe` / `Map::note_breach`).
- **F3 (spec gap) — an evicted vortex's hole was an exit to the void.** **R88**: the evicted one stops
  pulling and fades, and **keeps catching** for as long as its hole is open (the round). Only the pull
  is capped at three. `World::spent_vortices`, hashed; `vortex::captor` asks both lists.
- **F4 (guard that cannot fail)** — the only mirror test read `field_accel_at`, a readback; planting
  `env_at(map, gravity, &[], …)` in the wasm `apply_input` left game-wasm green.
- **F6** — `game-server/tests/bots.rs::config` inherited `fixed_seed: None` (the shared-cause lesson).
- **F8 hygiene** — `no_carve_on_a_landscape_map_is_a_breach` never asserted its carves removed rock;
  `CROSSHAIR_HALF` in `solar-flare-match.mjs` was a hand copy of `Crosshair`'s arm; the pull test's
  0.8–1.3× band had no basis.

## Known limit, reported not fixed
R87's box test is per box, not per hole: a *new* hole cut by a carve whose padded box also contains an
old hole is not reported. For a circle that is harmless — the old hole's air inside a `r + 34` px box
puts its vortex inside `VORTEX_CAPTURE_R` of the new one, so it is the same hole anyway. A **long**
capsule along the rim could reach both; the production capsules are a shovel swing (`SHOVEL_REACH`)
and lava (off in space, R43), so nothing reaches it today.
