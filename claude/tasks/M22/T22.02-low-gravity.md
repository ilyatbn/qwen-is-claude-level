## "Everything a bit slower, including projectiles" is not one number

The original file worked this out and the reasoning still holds — read it from git
(`git log --diff-filter=D -- tasks/parking-lot/T21.05-low-gravity.md`). In short:

- Less gravity makes a lobbed grenade travel **further**, not slower. "Slower" and "less
  gravity" are different axes.
- Bullets carry `gravity_scale = 0.0`, so a gravity change does **nothing** to them.
- A **time** scale would need `dt` threaded through every system. Nothing is shaped for it.
- Gravity *magnitude* is not injectable: `apply_gravity` reads the `GRAVITY` constant directly
  and only the **scale** is a parameter.

**So "everything slower" can only mean a gravity scale.** Things arc further and hang longer,
which reads as slower without touching a clock.

## The two copies of the projectile gravity term

`projectile.rs::integrate` is the extracted function, **but the hot loop inlines
`p.vel.y += GRAVITY * w.gravity_scale * dt` and bypasses it.** A scale applied only to the
extracted version silently misses **every projectile in flight**. Same expression, two places,
and the second copy is already written. Verify both before claiming either.

## Why the constant is off limits

`JUMP_HEIGHT` and `JUMP_REACH` are compile-time consts of `JUMP_VELOCITY`, `WALK_SPEED` and
`GRAVITY`, and they drive the generator's traversability verdict. Changing the constant changes
**which maps the generator accepts**, which moves the golden table. A runtime scale leaves them
alone — the generator keeps standard-gravity reachability while the player moves under low
gravity. **That asymmetry is the trade and it is the right one**; the alternative regenerates
every map in the game.

## Deliverable

- One named multiplier in `constants.rs`. Not a second `GRAVITY`.
- Applied at the scale seam, both copies.
- **Fall damage "much lower"** — note it is *already* lower for free: impact falls out of
  gravity as `v = √(2gh)`, so half gravity is ×0.71 impact at the same height. Ask whether an
  extra multiplier is wanted **on top**, or whether the free reduction is the feature. T20.11
  owns the formula it would multiply, and the 2026-09-16 retune moved both its numbers.

## Tests

- Jump height and fall speed scale with the multiplier, pinned to it, with a standard-gravity
  control in the same test.
- A lobbed weapon's range changes and a **bullet's does not** — the `gravity_scale == 0.0`
  boundary asserted on both sides.
- Prediction agrees with the server under low gravity.
- **`balance.rs` re-measured over 8 seeds and reported**, the way §C20's repeal did. Bots
  jetpack when a target is `JETPACK_RISE` above them; halve gravity and their reach, their
  self-damage and their encounter rate all change.
- Interactions with T21.02 (boots, ×2.25 jump height on top of this) and T21.03 (wings make
  gravity irrelevant) **stated, not discovered**.
