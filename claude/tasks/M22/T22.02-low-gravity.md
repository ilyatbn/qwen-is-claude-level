## "Everything a bit slower, including projectiles" is not one number

The original file worked this out and the reasoning still holds — read it from git
(`git log --diff-filter=D -- tasks/parking-lot/T21.05-low-gravity.md`). In short:

- Less gravity makes a lobbed grenade travel **further**, not slower. "Slower" and "less
  gravity" are different axes.
- Bullets carry `gravity_scale = 0.0`, so a gravity change does **nothing** to them.
- A **time** scale would need `dt` threaded through every system. Nothing is shaped for it.
- Gravity *magnitude* is not injectable: `apply_gravity` reads the `GRAVITY` constant directly
  and only the **scale** is a parameter.

**So "everything slower" can only mean a gravity scale.** Things arc further and hang longer,
which reads as slower without touching a clock.

## The two copies of the projectile gravity term — CORRECTED 2026-09-20

**The sentence that was here was wrong and the correction inverts the consequence.** It
claimed the hot loop inlines the term and bypasses the extracted function. It does not:
`weapons/projectile.rs::Projectiles::step` calls `projectile.rs::integrate` explicitly, twice
— once for gravity, once for wind.

There *are* two copies, and a scale applied to one does miss the other. The second is:

| # | symbol | expression |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `weapons/projectile.rs::integrate` | `vel.y + GRAVITY * gravity_scale * dt` |
| 2 | `weapons/projectile.rs::predict_impact` | `p.vel.y += GRAVITY * w.gravity_scale * dt` |

`predict_impact` is **the bot's throw-safety arc walker**, not the flight path. So the failure
you get from scaling only `integrate` is not "every projectile in flight is wrong" — it is
**every projectile in flight is right and the bots' throw guard is wrong**: bots under-throw,
or walk into their own molotovs, and `balance.rs`'s encounter numbers move for a reason nobody
attributes correctly.

`crates/game-core/tests/thrown.rs::prediction_agrees_with_the_simulation` ties the two
together and goes red if you scale one and not the other. It is **not** in this task's
Done-when's `--test balance` clause; plain `cargo test -p game-core` does run it.

**And there is a fourth production reader of `GRAVITY` that the original file missed entirely:**
`bots/mod.rs::zone_reach` derives a flame stand-off radius as a ballistic range,
`speed * speed / (GRAVITY * FLAME_GRAVITY_SCALE) + FLAME_RADIUS`. Under half gravity the real
spread doubles and this number does not. Its own neighbouring comment records that getting
this class of number wrong by 2.7× made bots refuse 120 throws out of 120. **Scale it or say
why not.**

Exhaustively: three production sites integrate velocity with `GRAVITY` — `apply_gravity`,
`projectile::integrate`, `projectile::predict_impact` — plus `zone_reach`, which reads it
without integrating. Verified 2026-09-20 by reading all 35 `GRAVITY` lines under `crates/`.

## Why the constant is off limits

`JUMP_HEIGHT` and `JUMP_REACH` are compile-time consts of `JUMP_VELOCITY`, `WALK_SPEED` and
`GRAVITY` — **and they live in `map/gen/traversal.rs`, not `constants.rs`**, so a reader who
greps `constants.rs` for them finds nothing. They drive the generator's traversability verdict. Changing the constant changes
**which maps the generator accepts**, which moves the golden table. A runtime scale leaves them
alone — the generator keeps standard-gravity reachability while the player moves under low
gravity. **That asymmetry is the trade and it is the right one**; the alternative regenerates
every map in the game.

## Deliverable

- One named multiplier in `constants.rs`. Not a second `GRAVITY`.
- Applied at the scale seam, both copies.
- **Fall damage "much lower" is already free, and by far more than ×0.71.** Impact falls out
  of gravity as `v = √(2gh)`, which is the ×0.71 the original file quoted — **but that
  understates it, because `player/state.rs::PlayerState::fall_damage` subtracts a *fixed*
  `fall_safe_speed()` before scaling**: `((impact - safe) * FALL_DAMAGE_PER_SPEED).max(0.0)`.
  Take 29 % off the impact and a great many falls land *below* the safe speed and do literally
  **zero** damage, not 71 % of their old damage. **So an extra multiplier on top is very
  probably the wrong answer**; the free reduction is the feature. If you add one anyway, say
  what fall it is meant to change and show the arithmetic. T20.11 owns the formula and the
  2026-09-16 retune moved both its numbers.
