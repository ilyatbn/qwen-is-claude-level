## What T22.00B established, which makes this task cheap and necessary

`smoke-shader` failed twice in M22's gates. The cause was **not** load — measured, under load
it read *higher* — but that a wall-clock gap between two frames is a **marginal** measurement
of a slowly-drifting shader. On an idle box the old form went **4 of 15 trials at or under its
floor**, twice reading exactly 0.0 %.

**Three checks have the identical instrument and have simply not been unlucky in a gate yet:**

| check | the gap | note |
|---|---|---|
| `fire-shader.mjs::pair` | `sleep(300)`, *"frozen fire, 300 ms apart"* | direct twin, same floor |
| `explosion-shader.mjs::pair` | `sleep(300)`, *"held blast, 300 ms apart"* | direct twin; wider observed margin (60.3 %), identical instrument |
| `beams-shader.mjs` (the beam-ripple pair) | `sleep(200)` | **shorter gap, so a tighter margin than smoke's was** |
| `fog-shader.mjs` | `page.waitForTimeout(700)` between two `sampleSpots()`, asserting `drift >= 3` | **a `sleep(` grep cannot see this one** |

**`beams-shader` is the one to worry about**, because its window is shorter than the one that
already failed twice.

## Do it as one shared helper, not four edits

`T22.00B` wrote the working instrument inside `scripts/checks/smoke-shader.mjs`: advance a
counted number of **drawn** frames, repeat for N steps, and let the **largest** change decide.
Its numbers and reasoning are in the comment above `STEP_FRAMES`.

**Lift that into `scripts/lib/harness.mjs` and have all four call it.** *Share the guard, or
share the function* — a second copy is a second place to get the frame budget, the timeout and
the two distinguishable failure messages wrong, and this task exists because the first copy
was got wrong four times over.

The helper must keep both of `T22.00B`'s hard requirements:

- **a generous per-step timeout**, so a genuinely frozen shader fails rather than hangs;
- **failure messages that distinguish *"it does not animate"* from *"the box never
  rendered"***, reporting frames drawn and elapsed time. That distinction is half the value
  and it is what neither the old form nor two sightings could express.

## The separate class, listed so nobody "fixes" it

These use a wall-clock gap as a **noise floor** — under-rendering *shrinks* it and loosens
whatever threshold derives from it, so they fail **safe** but report a false floor:
`living-sky.mjs` (`waitForTimeout(300)`), `weather-visible.mjs` (`waitForTimeout(700)`),
`void.mjs` (`sleep(900)`). **Leave them.** Note in your report whether converting them would
tighten or loosen their thresholds; do not find out by changing them.

And these wait for a **commanded state change**, where polling is the fix and it is a
different bug class entirely: `terrain-render`, `clouds`, `skins`, `teleport`, `fps-counter`,
`hud-bars`, `objects`, `ordnance-visible`, `platform-autofire`, `hud-timer`, `birds`.

## A third one, and it is a different shape (`R37`)

`scripts/checks/objects.mjs` asserts `median single-chunk rebake ≤ 4 ms over 40 samples`. It
**failed a `--changed` run at 4.20 ms** and read **0.80 ms median / 2.30 max** alone on the same
tree minutes later. The six gate logs already in the tree show it was always marginal: **four of
six prior runs exceeded the 4 ms budget on their *max*** — and the check gates only the median,
so under `--jobs` the median walks into the max's old range.

**This is a performance budget, not a sampling window**, so frames do not fix it. **Move the
assertion into `scripts/checks/perf.mjs`**, which is already `serial: true` and `flaky: true` —
the check that exists for wall-clock budgets.

**Do not put `flaky: true` on `objects`.** That disables eight real pixel assertions, including
*"carve took the art with it"* and the seam test, to silence one timing line.

## Tests

- **Measure each of the four before you change it**, the way T22.00B did: 15 trials of the
  existing form on an idle box, and report how many land at or under its floor. **If one of
  them is genuinely not marginal, say so and leave it** — converting a check that was never a
  coin flip buys nothing and costs a diff.
- **Falsify each conversion at its live binding site**: freeze that shader and confirm the
  check goes red naming it. A frame-counting wait that passes for a frozen shader is worse
  than the clock, because it looks rigorous while asserting nothing.
- **Falsify the second arm too**: stop the page drawing (`requestAnimationFrame = () => 0`)
  and confirm you get the *other* message, not the first one.
- `smoke-shader` must still pass after being moved onto the shared helper — it is the one with
  a known-good baseline.

## A pre-existing defect to fix here, because it is in your way

`scripts/checks/`'s `frame()` helper — `new Promise(r => rAF(rAF(r)))`, six callers in
`smoke-shader` alone and the same idiom across the suite — **hangs when rAF stops**. T22.00B
hit it: a plant died with Playwright's *"Resulting promise was garbage collected"* instead of
reaching the branch under test. It fails rather than hanging forever, but the message says
nothing about the cause. Give it the same timeout-and-say-why treatment.
