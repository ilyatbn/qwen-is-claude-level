# T22.19B — What the standing review found

Milestone: M22 (owner round 2) · Rulings: R107 in [M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md](M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md) (and its T22.19B
addendum there). Source: the review of `93c7517` (`gate-review2219-*.txt`) and the coordinator's rulings below.

## As ruled (coordinator, 2026-09-25)

- **The figure tilts toward asteroid wells only** — not the black hole's pull, not a vortex's. At T22.19
  `stand_pull_at` was the full `env_at`, so anyone within the hole's 512 px reach was drawn feet-toward-the-hole with no
  rock under them. A wells-only query in Rust — the capped wells sum the prediction steps with, no TS copy; winged still
  zero. **Stated consequence: on a rock inside the hole's reach the figure stands upright too**, because R91 mutes the
  wells there (nothing it stands on pulls it).
- **F3: pivot at the feet contact point** — where the body's box meets the rock — not the box centre, so the feet do not
  sink into a rock's flank at a quarter turn. The drawing still overhangs the axis-aligned hitbox sideways (the figure is
  `PLAYER_H` long across a box `PLAYER_W` wide): the accepted cost of "visual only". 180° unchanged.

## Findings

- **F1 (guard that cannot fail).** `stand-on-asteroid`'s 180° reference faced the wrong way: the upright pose facing the
  screen aim, turned 180°, faces *away*. A vertical-flip plant (`setRotation(0).setScale(1,-1)`) scored 1.00 against it
  vs the real drawing's 0.77, and passed.
- **F2 (unguarded).** No sideways or in-between angle was checked: a snap-to-0/π plant passed every arm, because the ease
  arm read `debug().stand.drawn`, not pixels.
- **F5.** Remotes skipped `stepTilt` while culled (`renderRemotes` `continue`d before it) and turned from a stale angle when
  they reappeared; relocations (pads, vortex trips, respawns, dev placements) turned ~0.25 s from the old angle.
- **F6 (player-visible, pre-existing since T3.08).** `PlayerView.setName` never had a caller: no player name has ever shown
  in a match, though `docs/50` lists a name label.

## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **Wells only, through the one composition**: `GameCore::stand_pull_at` = `env_at(map, gravity, flying, &[], hole,
  false, pos)` — no vortices, the hole *present but not pulling*. That leaves `capped_at` with no vortex (= `wells_at`)
  and keeps every rule of that sum: R91's mute inside the reach, R100's wings, the standard/low zero, a dead core's well.
- **F3**: `standTilt-math.ts::feetOffset(θ, w, h)` — the figure's down cast from the box centre to the box outline
  (upright `(0, h/2)`, feet-up `(0, −h/2)`, a quarter turn `(∓w/2, 0)`; continuous). The tag's placement
  (`uprightLocal`) takes the feet offset, so it stays above the box on screen.
- **F5**: `trackTilt` (pure) holds `{θ, x, y}` per body: first sight and a jump of more than `STAND_SNAP_PX` (64, the
  predictor's `SNAP_PX` basis) beyond what the velocity carried **snap** to the target; otherwise `stepTilt`. The scene
  steps every sampled remote **before** the visibility cull.
- **F6**: `GameScene` calls `setName` every frame (local and each remote) from `scores` (lobby names; Phaser's `setText`
  is a no-op when unchanged). The tag is a container child, so it hides with the body (the dark's cull, death). Style
  unchanged (the constructor's 9 px `#dfe6ee`). The sandbox still sets no name (not a match).
- **F1/F2 in the check**: references are posed upright with the aim in the figure's frame (`aim − tilt`, and `+ π` for
  the facing-away control), and a drawn pixel is looked up through the *drawn* pivot → the reference's pivot, turned by
  the drawn tilt. New arms: the side of a rock (a quarter turn), one in-between ease frame (frozen in the page's own
  frame callback) matched at its tilt against upright and feet-up, and the name tag on both paths.
