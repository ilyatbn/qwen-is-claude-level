## What is parked, and what they share

Three in `crates/game-server/tests/lobby.rs`, one in `crates/game-server/tests/integration.rs`.
Every one fails at a **websocket handshake** — `IncompleteResponseFromEngineIo(WebsocketError(
AlreadyClosed))`, or `waited N s for 1 'welcome', saw 0 (inbox: empty)` — every one passes
standalone in under a second, and **a different member fails each time**.

`CLAUDE.md` names that pattern and names the trap in reading it:

> *"'The failure moves' is evidence against **one broken test**, not evidence **for** load…
> Before concluding 'load', ask what the failing tests share."*

They share the lobby harness. **And the last time that reasoning ran here it was still wrong**
— the plausible shared cause (a timeout budget) was inert under measurement, and the real one
was `test_config`'s inherited `fixed_seed: None`. So the rule that matters is the one after it:
**a plausible shared cause is still a hypothesis; instrument the suspect before you fix it.**

## The coverage that is currently gone

At least one of the four is the **only** test asserting a seventh client is refused *over the
wire* — `tests/room.rs` asserts the cap on the `Room`, which is a different claim. Enumerate
what each park removed before you start, so the value of unparking is a number rather than a
feeling.

## What to do, in order

1. **Enumerate the four** by symbol, from `scripts/ignored.sh`'s manifest and
   `tasks/flaky-test.md`, and say what each one uniquely covers.
2. **Instrument the harness**, do not guess. What does it share — a port, a runtime, a client
   pool, a teardown that races the next setup? Measure across many runs with the suspect
   quantity recorded, the way the 277-wait instrumentation was done.
3. **Only then** propose a fix, and falsify the remedy: show the harness change makes the
   parked tests pass repeatedly under the same conditions that failed them.
4. **Unpark what the fix covers**, and remove its `ignored.sh` rows and `flaky-test.md` rows in
   the same commit.

**Do not park a fifth.** Each park removes real coverage and four is already the evidence that
the harness, not the tests, is the thing to change.
