# T22.19 — Standing on asteroids

Milestone: M22 (owner round 2) · Rulings: R107 in [M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md](M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md) — read it first; the
owner's words are there verbatim.

> *"rotate the character to the center of gravity when being pulled towards it so they apear to be standing even if
> they are on the bottom. when not pulled by gravity they go back to being vertical."*

## Re-verified against the tree (`093333a`)

- Every player is drawn by `render/playerView.ts::PlayerView` — one `Container` whose origin is the **feet**
  (`setPosition(x, y + PLAYER_H/2)`), holding the body sprite, boots, wings, hat, glasses, shield bubble, weapon and
  the name label; the thruster plume draws into the same container (`ThrusterPlume`). Callers: `GameScene` (local:
  `rp` the predictor's render position; remotes: `RemoteInterpolator` samples), `SandboxScene` (its player; a skin
  line-up), `SkinsScene` (the picker preview).
- The field both sides step with is `attractors::env_at` (survey § 3); the client reaches it through wasm. Its only
  readback was `GameCore::field_accel_at`, which assumes an unwinged body (R100 says a winged one feels none).
- `grep -rn setName client/src/scenes` → none: **no scene sets a name tag today**, so the label is empty in every
  match (pre-existing; recorded, not this task's).

## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **The pull is asked of Rust**: `GameCore::stand_pull_at(x, y, move_mod_bits)` — `env_at` with the core's live
  attractors, `flying` derived through the mirror's own `set_move_mod_bits` → `move_mods` (no TS copy of R100).
  Anything that pulls turns the figure: a rock's band, a vortex's pull and the black hole's (the owner's "center of
  gravity"); exactly zero in open space (R101's step), so there the figure eases upright.
- **Smoothing** (`render/standTilt-math.ts`): exponential, the short way round; `STAND_TURN_RATE` 12 /s toward a pull,
  `STAND_UPRIGHT_RATE` 5 /s back to upright (slower, so a hop out of the band does not spin twice). Drawing only.
- **What turns**: the figure turns about the **body's centre** (the feet go half a body along the pull), with its
  boots, wings, hat, glasses and shield bubble. **The weapon and the plume ride with the body but point in screen
  space** — the weapon at `aim − tilt` inside the turned container, the plume's thrust and velocity carried into the
  turned frame — because the aim and the thrust are the controls' directions and R107 keeps the controls
  screen-relative. Facing is judged in the figure's frame (`facingLeft(aim − tilt)`). **The name tag stays upright**
  above the body (counter-rotated, `uprightLocal`).
- Local: the pull at the drawn render position, the local byte; remotes: at the interpolated position drawn, their
  snapshot byte; dead → upright. The sandbox player turns too (pivot at its sprite centre, which the sandbox has always
  hung half a body above the body).
- **`debug_place {x, y}`** (DEV_PROBE only, `World::dev_relocate`) is the check's way to put a body in a band.
