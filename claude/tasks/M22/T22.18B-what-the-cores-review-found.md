# T22.18B — What the cores review found

Milestone: M22 (owner round 2) · Rulings: R101, R102, R106 in [M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md](M22-OWNER-ROUND-2.md). Source: the
review of T22.16/T22.18 (`gate-review2216-*.txt`) and the coordinator's rulings on F1 and F4.

## Findings

- **F1 (HIGH, player-visible; breaks R101's promise).** T22.16 started the band at the round body (`0.75 r`), so over a
  lump reaching `r` only `WELL_SURFACE_BAND − 0.25 r` of band was left; on level 1 a two-tick hop from a lump top never
  came back on r 48 and r 70. **Ruling:** measure the band from the asteroid's *generated outline* on the body's bearing —
  `reach(θ) = outline_r(θ) + PLAYER_H/2 + WELL_SURFACE_BAND`, `outline_r` a pure function of the asteroid, not a mask read;
  server == mirror exactly.
- **F2.** Nothing asserted R106's "≈ 2× today's": `BLACK_HOLE_REACH` planted back to 256 left 1218 tests green.
- **F3.** The escape guarantee flew only on Large maps; add a Small-map arm asserting *never dies* (not "passes the reach").
- **F4 (ruling).** R91 mutes the wells inside the hole's reach, and nothing showed where that is: draw a faint ring at
  `BLACK_HOLE_REACH` (both paths, pixel-asserted with a control) and give the minimap marker the reach's radius.
- **F5.** Stale comments: `blackHoleFx-math.ts::warnClosingRadius` ("the reach at 0") and `blackHoleFx.ts`'s header.

## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **The lumps are carried on the rock, not re-derived.** They are drawn on the map's shared `"asteroid_shape"` stream in
  placement order, so a lump is a function of the seed *and every earlier rock*, not of one asteroid. The smallest honest
  pure function is therefore the generator's own rounded lump list stored on the asteroid: `Asteroid::lumps`
  (`ASTEROID_LUMP_SLOTS` = `SPACE_LUMPS_MAX`), filled by `space::draw_lumps` (the same draws, same order — no golden row
  moves) and **stamped from** by `space::stamp_asteroid`, so the list and the pixels cannot disagree. `Asteroid::outline_radius(dir)`
  is the farthest point of the body-and-lumps union on the ray; it takes a direction, not an angle, so the per-tick sum adds no
  `atan2`/`cos`. The wire carries the list (`map_init`, 27 bytes a rock); the hash covers it; `REPLAY_VERSION` 31.
- **`well_reach(a, at)` / `well_contact(a, at)` take the bearing** (the body's position): every caller now says which
  direction it means. `well_reach_max` is the bound `Attractor::asteroid` rejects by.
- **Carving does not shrink the outline** (the band never followed carves; "carving never adds reach" still holds). A
  rock whose lumps are blown off keeps its lump-height band there: carved max gap 40.5 → 51.8 px (below).
- **F4**: the reach ring is `Graphics` on both paths (the shader quad ends at the glow), `BLACK_HOLE_RING_COLOR` at alpha
  0.35, 3 px; the minimap circle is 1 px at 0.6. Not a line of the death rule — the doc comments say so.
