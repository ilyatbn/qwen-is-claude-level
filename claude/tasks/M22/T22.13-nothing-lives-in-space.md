## **Gate on the generator, not on the gravity field** (`R58`)

The one hazard, and it is the whole reason this file says anything about how to do it. `R15`
derives `MapGenerator::Space` **from the mode**, and that derivation is the single source of truth

## Outcome (builder, 2026-09-22, commit `bd89ec4`)

**The birds question, answered: they are two rulings wearing one row at the module
level, and one ruling one layer up.** `Animals::tick` takes a `&Map` and could gate
itself; `Birds::tick` takes only `map_w: f32` and could not without a signature
change. So there is no suppression point *inside* the two modules. What they do share
is the `active: bool` each is handed by `World`, which is the layer that owns the map —
so the guard is one function there, `world/mod.rs::World::wildlife_allowed`, folded
into both `step_birds` and `step_animals`. `R14`'s sentence is therefore true, just not
where it points.

**Birds stayed a simulation rule and did not move to `T22.06`'s backdrop.** A bird
drops a heal or a battery (`birds.rs`' module header calls it a supply line), so
whether one exists is a balance decision. A renderer declining to draw one would leave
the drops in the game and only the bird invisible, which is the worse of the two bugs.

**Gated on the generator** — `Map::space_geometry()`'s `Some` — never on
`self.gravity`. Both are documented at `wildlife_allowed`, along with which side the
call runs on: every live caller of `World::step` is server-side (`game-server::room`,
`game-server::round`); the only other is `game-wasm::AttractCore`, which generates its
own map locally and has been dormant since T18.01. Nothing networked steps a `World`,
so `F6`/`R49`'s stale-`meta.asteroids` landmine is not reachable from here.

**Nothing alive is stranded, and the mode cannot change under the guard.**
`World::gravity` is a public field that callers do assign after construction;
`World::map` is written exactly once, in `from_map`, and `grep -n 'self\.map = '` over
`world/mod.rs` returns nothing. The guard reads the map, so its subject is immutable
for the round — asserted by
`nothing_lives_in_space::flipping_the_gravity_field_under_a_landscape_map_does_not_suppress_wildlife`,
which is also `R58`'s hazard as a test.

**Tests** are in `world::nothing_lives_in_space`: four seeds, **peak** population
rather than the end count (an end count reads a spawn-then-cull build as a clean zero),
and the control on the same seeds — a standard round still grows both, measured at 2
and 2 on each of the four.
