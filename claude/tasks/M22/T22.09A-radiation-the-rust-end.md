## Do these
- **Constants** with their basis in the doc comment (`R24`): `RADIATION_DPS` 1.0,
  `RADIATION_SHIELD_COST` 1.0 energy/s, `BATTERY_PACK_SPACE_WEIGHT_MULT` 2, and the log interval
  (`R25`, one second).
- **`shield_active(now, suit: bool)`** (`R26`): generator **or** suit, while the battery has
  charge. Production callers pass the suit bit derived from the mode, in one place. Codec bit 3
  passes `suit = false` — the bubble stays the generator's. `apply_damage` gains `suit`.
- **`DamageSource::Radiation`** and **`DeathCause::Radiation`** (`R20`), every Rust match site the
  sweep lists, and `events.rs::cause_name` → `"radiation"`.
- **World stage 8c**, after 8a's poison, in `Playing`: sealed → battery pays
  `RADIATION_SHIELD_COST · dt`; unsealed → the hashed accumulator (`R74`) emits one
  `DamageSource::Radiation` entry per whole second through `apply_damage_log`. The death cause
  rides the transient per-tick list (`R75`), never hashed and never surviving a step.
- **The suit battery full** at join and at respawn, in space (`R24`).
- **`BATTERY_PACK` doubled on the natural Spawn column in space only** (`R76`); crates unchanged.
- **Snapshot flags bit 7 = irradiated** (space && alive && unsealed), one predicate shared with a
  new `GameCore::irradiated(id)` for the sandbox.
- **`REPLAY_VERSION` 15 → 16** (`R77`).

## Tests (each falsified at its live binding site, red pasted in the journal)
- Unsealed loses `RADIATION_DPS` a second over a measured interval, **with a sealed control in
  the same world that loses none**.
- The battery drains at `RADIATION_SHIELD_COST` while sealed, and a battery pack restores it.
- At zero battery the seal fails and damage starts — the transition, not the two states.
- No radiation outside space, with the space presence control.
- Exactly one `Damage` event a second, not sixty.
- A radiation death is `DeathCause::Radiation`, and the transient list is empty after a step.
- The pack's space weight is doubled on Spawn and unchanged on Crate and outside space.
- Codec bit 7 set when irradiated, clear when sealed, clear outside space; bit 3 not set by the suit.
- `#[ignore]` space balance report over 8 seeds (`R24` owes it) — numbers in the journal.
