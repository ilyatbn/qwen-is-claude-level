# T22.05B — The space map: spawns, objects, and everything that assumes "up"

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.05A** · Size: **large** · Origin: the parked

Asked for: *"You can generate objects anywhere since there's no 'up'."*

## Your red-before-green, measured: every spawn ships **outside the arena** today

**`R35`.** `T22.05A` landed, and measured through the real pipeline, **all 6 spawn points and
all 6 teleport pads sit at `y = h − FLOOR_CRUST − 1`** — on the full-width floor crust,
**outside the rim ellipse**, in the band `generate_once`'s own comment calls the void. A space
match is **currently unplayable**, and that is expected: spawns are your task, not `T22.05A`'s.

Why it happens: `Small surface 148 = crust 84, asteroid 14, rim 37, other 13`. **The crust
outnumbers the asteroids 6:1**, so `choose_spawns` finds it first.

**Two things you owe because of it:**

1. **Move `analyse_space`'s spawn clause onto `MapMeta.spawn_points`.** Today it counts
   `open_space_candidates(...)` and **throws the list away**, so the verdict names a property
   of the shipped map and measures something else. Pointed at the real field it is **red today
   on all three scales** — which is your red-before-green, and what a Done-when that can report
   its own violation looks like.
2. **`asteroid_tops_are_standable` asserts `!o.surface.is_empty()`**, which the floor crust
   satisfies 6:1 on its own — **it would pass for a generator that stamped no asteroids at
   all.** Its name states a claim its body does not test. Fix the assertion, not the name.

**And `R32`'s inert guard is yours to fire.** `gen::reanalyse` is correct and runs on **0 of
900** space maps, because pass 8's ground fill never triggers when a pad already sits on rock.
The moment you reseat pads it may. Write the test that forces `filled > 0` and asserts the
space verdict survives — a fixture is fine.

**One more field nobody has named** (`S5` of the review): `components::cleanup` has a **third**
job beyond deleting specks and filling pockets — it computes the `SealedPocket` list that
`choose_buried_slots` consumes. Space skips that pass and returns empty, so `choose_buried_slots`
takes its `anchors.is_empty()` branch: **uniform random placement over the whole map**, which
is the branch its own doc exists to reject (*"Uniform random placement buries things where
nobody will ever dig"*). Measured: buried slots 1 / 7 / 10.

## Spawn points are the blocking problem, not a detail

The existing generator derives spawns from a **surface**. There is a surface here — asteroid
tops are standable, so `extract_surface` finds them — but it is a **scatter of rock tops, not a
ground line**, and `map/gen/spawns.rs::choose_spawns` picks from the traversal component, which
does not exist here.

**The original list of `surface_points` readers in this file named three and got two wrong.**
Corrected 2026-09-20, by grep:

| reads `surface_points` | what it does with it |
|---|---|
| `effects/lava.rs::LavaBurst::new` | picks vent positions; **nothing else** — the T19.24 finding, correctly attributed |
| `effects/toxic.rs::ToxicRain::pick_column` | takes only the **x** of a random point |
| `items/spawning.rs::place_initial` | round-start item positions; returns 0 if empty |
| `items/spawning.rs::resample_surface` | periodic item spawns; re-checks `is_standable` |
| `player/state.rs::choose_respawn` (via `choose_surface_point`) | **the respawn fallback** when every listed spawn is destroyed |
| `map/dump.rs::dump_surface` | PNG tool |
| `game-wasm/src/lib.rs::Core::load_mask` | **writes** it — re-extracts from the arriving mask |
| `game-server/src/room.rs::Room::generate_world_task`, `::debug_dump` | `.len()` in a log line |
| `client/src/render/debugOverlay.ts`, `PreviewScene.ts`, `SandboxScene.ts` | debug readouts |
| `tests/golden.rs::meta_digest` | hashes `surface_points.len()` |

**`world/birds.rs::SkyBand::of` does not read it** — it walks `map.mask` column by column.
**`map/gen/objects.rs::stamp_objects` does not read it either** — it runs *before*
`extract_surface` and anchors with its own `surface_anchor`. Both were wrong in the original.

**A mode where players spawn inside rock or outside the boundary is unplayable.** This task owns
that.

## The wider blast radius nobody had costed

**`map/meta.rs::generate_full_with` is generator-agnostic and runs unconditionally** after
`generate_terrain_with` — spawns, pads, platforms, buried slots, decorations, the T21.28 ground
fill and the surface re-derivation. A third generator plugged in at `generate_terrain_with`
**inherits all of it.** That, not the spawn picker alone, is this task. Go through `MapMeta`
field by field and rule on each; the ones that are wrong in space are `spawn_points`,
`teleport_pads`, `gun_platforms`, `buried_slots`, `decorations`, `wind` (meaningless in a
vacuum), `traversable_fraction` and `largest_component` (`R17` rules those last two).

## Deliverable

- Spawn points that are in open space, inside the boundary, and clear of each other, over many
  seeds.
- Objects placed without an up-vector, or a stated reason why they keep one.
- A ruling, in this file, for every row of the table above.

## Hazards

- **T21.40's placement rules are a live guard, not scenery.** Pads and platforms are seated or
  not placed, and fewer than two pads means none. Whatever this does must leave that rule
  intact in the modes it governs.
- **`T22.03`'s collision rule is what makes islands playable or lethal.** They are the same
  feature; do not tune them apart.

## Tests

- Every spawn point is in open space and inside the boundary, **over many seeds**.
- No object intersects the boundary or a spawn.
- The minimap draws the mode's world — **on rendered pixels**, because "the data is right" is
  what four shipped "I cannot see it" bugs also said.
- Existing golden hashes still unchanged with the mode off.

**`node scripts/e2e.mjs minimap` cannot fail for this task as it stands, and that is a
deliverable.** The check drives `window.__game.regenerate('12345','medium')`; `SandboxScene`'s
`regenerate(seed?, scale?)` has **no mode parameter**, and `e2e-checks.mjs` registers `minimap`
at `?sandbox=1&seed=12345`. So it can never see a space map. **Extend `regenerate` to take the
mode and add a space arm to the check (or a new check), then run it and watch it fail.**
