# T22.05B — The space map: spawns, objects, and everything that assumes "up"

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.05A** · Size: **large** · Origin: the parked
`T21.07-zero-g-map.md`, second half of the split

Asked for: *"You can generate objects anywhere since there's no 'up'."*

## Spawn points are the blocking problem, not a detail

The existing generator derives spawns from a **surface**. There is no surface here — there is a
rim and a scatter of islands. `surface_points` is read by more than spawning:
`effects/lava.rs::LavaBurst::new` reads `map.meta.surface_points` **and nothing else** to choose
where the ground opens (T19.24 records that a networked client derived zero vents because the
list was empty), `birds.rs` derives flight altitude from the median column top, and
`objects.rs` seats scenery on it.

**A mode where players spawn inside rock or outside the boundary is unplayable.** This task owns
that.

## The list of things that assume up, from the original plus this session

| assumes | why it breaks | 
|---|---|
| `birds.rs` | flight altitude from the median column top — meaningless with land at the edges |
| tombstones | they fall |
| crates | `is_falling_crate` |
| buried items | there is no "under" |
| the minimap | assumes a wide, short world with ground at the bottom |
| the camera | same |
| `objects.rs::seat` | T21.21/T21.28 made scenery stand *on ground*; "anywhere" contradicts it |
| teleport pads and gun platforms | T21.40 places them only on seated ground, and refuses rather than perching |

**Each one is a decision: off in this mode, or given its own answer here.** Do not discover
them at play time — the owner has now reported floating scenery twice (T21.21, T21.28) and
perched gates once (T21.40), and each was one of these assumptions surfacing late.

## Deliverable

- Spawn points that are in open space, inside the boundary, and clear of each other, over many
  seeds.
- Objects placed without an up-vector, or a stated reason why they keep one.
- A ruling, in this file, for every row of the table above.

## Hazards

- **T21.40's placement rules are a live guard, not scenery.** Pads and platforms are seated or
  not placed, and fewer than two pads means none. Whatever this does must leave that rule
  intact in the modes it governs.
- **`T22.03`'s collision rule is what makes islands playable or lethal.** They are the same
  feature; do not tune them apart.

## Tests

- Every spawn point is in open space and inside the boundary, **over many seeds**.
- No object intersects the boundary or a spawn.
- The minimap draws the mode's world — **on rendered pixels**, because "the data is right" is
  what four shipped "I cannot see it" bugs also said.
- Existing golden hashes still unchanged with the mode off.
