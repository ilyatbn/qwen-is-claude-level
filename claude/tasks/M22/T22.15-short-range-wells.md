## Decisions (reversible; at the code)

- **How the band is measured**: from the rock's **bounding circle** `r` (the grown radius — it bounds every lump, is
  on the wire and hashed), not the carved surface (T22.16's concern). `well_contact(a)` = `r + PLAYER_H / 2` (the
  farthest a body touching the rock can be); `well_reach(a)` = `well_contact + WELL_SURFACE_BAND`, body centre to
  rock centre. So the pull reaches one body height of **air** past the rock's outermost point, the same at every level.
- **`WELL_SURFACE_BAND` = `PLAYER_H`** (28), the ruling's default, with its basis at the constant.
  `SPACE_WELL_REACH_MAX` is deleted (nothing else read it).
- **A step, not R47's linear taper** (builder's call; reverse by `floor: well_contact(a)` in `Attractor::asteroid`):
  full strength inside the reach, exactly zero at and beyond it. The band's return speed is `√(2·a·BAND)` as a step,
  `√(a·BAND)` tapered: on a level-1 rock 87 vs 61 px/s, against the ~70 px/s of the smallest hop (UP until airborne,
  two ticks). Tapered, the stamped level-1 hop came back only after 1.83 s, nearly stalled at the edge; as a step, 1.02 s.
  The vortex and the hole keep the linear law, bit-identical (`floor` 0; `d − 0.0`, `reach − 0.0` exact).
- **Strengths unchanged**: `well_strength` = `SPACE_WELL_ACCEL_MAX × level / 5` (135..675 px/s²), now felt everywhere
  on the rock; a level-5 body feels exactly 675 < `JETPACK_THRUST_DOWN` 900 (R46 binds at every radius now, not the
  smallest rock). R96/R97 caps unchanged; on generated maps no two wells stack past the cap (worst raw sum over 396 271 sampled points: 675.0 — the 659 "over" it are a lone level-5 well an ulp over on a diagonal).
- **A jump always leaves** (430 px/s would need a 137 px band at level 5). That is the push-off (`SPACE_JUMP_FUEL`'s
  "pushes off a rock: 10"), not a defect; "fall back after a hop" is the hop, measured both ways below.
- **`SPACE_MAX_SPEED` left at 1350.** Its basis test now integrates the well (247.8 px/s), a vortex under the cap and the
  hole to its horizon, and asserts the clamp is above 1.5× the fastest; the 2.5× upper bound is retired (no chain of
  wells to run away along). Doc updated.
- **Bots: not retuned** — nothing broke (below).
