# T22.08F — The flare-clock check measures the box, not the clock

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.08E · Origin: coordinator's batch gate at `54b2b88`, 2026-09-24

`solar-flare-match` asserts the client's flare clock against the server's by sending `debug_effects` probes and
requiring ≥ 4 of 12 round-trip brackets narrower than 50 ms. Under the gate's `--jobs 4` load it read
`[115, 37, 77, 74, 71, 92, 98, 45, 130, 46, 84, 93]` — 3 narrow — and failed; alone on an idle box it passed.
T22.08E measured 48–85 ms brackets on 1–2 probes in a third of idle runs. **A round-trip width is a property of the
box's load, so the assertion is a coin flip under load** — CLAUDE.md: *a gate that fails on a coin flip gates
nothing; remove the assertion or fix its cause, do not weaken it.* Parked (`flaky: true`, row in `flaky-test.md`).

## Do

(e.g. in the snapshot or an event the client already receives), and the client compares its own flare elapsed **for
that same tick** — both sides then sample one instant, and the wall clock drops out. Keep the +0.5 s origin plant
red. Show the check green at `--jobs 4` beside three other heavy checks (the load that failed it), then un-park.
