# T22.03C — Bots throw what burns (part B of T22.03B: combat)

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.03B (`41000cf`), T22.03D (`cb63610`) · Size: small–medium · **Ruled: R95** (below)

## What T22.03B found (measured at `41000cf`, `balance.rs::space_bots_report`, 8 seeds × 6 bots × 240 s)
- **Bots in space fight**: 1.52–1.82 player kills a bot a round against standard's 0.27–0.47, 31–37 shots. They fire
  what they select — every trigger pull `fire` took, by weapon, over the four arms (natural and molotov-held, both
  modes): space pistol 779, smg 738, machinegun 576, shovel 414, laser smg 164, **bazooka 99**, deagle 91, laser
  pistol 65, revolver 55, **grenade 45**, **mine 24**; standard shovel 1130, machinegun 468, pistol 234, smg 60,
  deagle 32, grenade 23, bazooka 17, mine 12, revolver 10. **Thrown and launched weapons are used in both modes.**
- **The molotov and the toxic grenade are never thrown, in any mode** — not because of the 1110 px space reach.
  `Bot::choose_weapon` scores `damage / cooldown`, and both have `damage: 0.0` (their harm is the flames' `FLAME_DPS`
  and the cloud's `TOXIC_GRENADE_DPS`), so the shovel (54.5 dps, ×0.25 out of reach = 13.6) always outranks them.
  Zone weapons are selected **0.0 %** of alive time in both modes **even when every bot starts holding a molotov**:
  the selection moves off it on the first tick. So `zone_reach`'s space value never binds anything today.

## Why it is not in T22.03B
`M22-RULINGS` R5: *"Target selection, aiming and firing are untouched — only the part that turns 'I want to be over
there' into inputs changes."* Making a zone weapon selectable is a **selection** change, and it moves **standard**
balance too — the mode with T11.09's history of molotovs hurting their thrower three times more than anyone else.

## To rule (coordinator)
1. **Selection:** score a zone weapon by its hazard's rate (`FLAME_DPS` 12 / `TOXIC_GRENADE_DPS` 6 — still under the
   out-of-reach shovel's 13.6, so this alone changes little), or by area denial (§B24), or leave them unselected in
   every mode and say so.
2. **The space reach, once one can be held:** `zone_reach(molotov, Space)` = 1110 px (the lifetime bound) refuses
   every throw inside `FOV_DAY` (320). And `should_fire`'s impact walk (`predict_impact`) returns `None` for a straight
   zero-g flight past a floating target, which is also a refusal. A proposal with a physical basis and no new tunable:
   a flame crowd of `count` leaves gaps a body fits through at `count · (2·FLAME_RADIUS + PLAYER_W) / τ` =
   **137.5 px** from the burst — past it the thrower is more likely missed than hit, and a space bot dodges visible
   flames (`bots::space::escape`). As `min` with the other two bounds it would also bind under **Low** gravity
   (207.55 → 137.5), so either it is space-only (a branch `zone_reach`'s comment argues against) or Low moves too.
   And a `None` impact in zero-g could be read as "reaches the target it is aimed at".

## As ruled — R95 (coordinator, 2026-09-24, recorded by the builder)
Bots **may select zone weapons** (molotov, toxic grenade; smoke checked) with a scoring that values **area damage
over time**, not `damage / cooldown` (0 for them). The space throw reach is **not** the 137.5 px ring-gap distance:
pick the stand-off at which the thrower's **own-hit rate is no higher than in standard mode, measured**. `predict_impact`
returning `None` is **not** "reaches". Done-when: self-kills / self-damage a bot a round in space and standard ≤
standard's pre-change value (measured first); zone-weapon usage > 0 in both modes; standard kills not reduced by more
than a stated margin.

## As built (2026-09-24)
- **Instrument** (`balance.rs`): self-damage a bot a round (`DeathCause::SelfInflicted` — **falls included**, the event
  cannot tell them apart; before/after compare like with like), and of it the part taken **inside the bot's own fire
  or cloud** (an own flame or own burn patch within `PLAYER_H`), own-fire self-kills, molotov throws apart from toxic;
  a toxic-held arm; `BOTS_NATURAL=1` runs the two natural arms only.
- **Score** (`bots/mod.rs::zone_rate`): the hazard's damage to one target over its life, per cooldown — molotov
  `FLAME_DPS × FLAME_LIFE / 0.9` = 66.7, toxic `6 × 8 / 0.9` = 53.3 (pistol 50, smg 80). **Smoke: `None`** — no damage.
- **One guard, called twice** (`zone_refusal`): `should_fire` refuses on it and `choose_weapon` scores ×0.1 by it. Scored
  on distance alone at first, bots held molotovs they could not throw (4.7 % of alive time) and standard kills fell 14 %.
  In **zero-g** a throw must also **reach the target** (lands within the reach of it, or a contact weapon's straight
  line passes within `PLAYER_W`): toxic grenades otherwise flew their 960 px fuse past everyone. `None` stays a refusal.
- **Space reach `BOT_SPACE_ZONE_REACH` = 100 px**, swept at 100/150/200/300 (32 seeds, molotov-held arms): own fire a
  throw in space **2.49 / 2.54 / 2.59 / 0.38** hp against standard's **4.74** — the smallest measured value meeting
  R95 (`gate-t2203c-sweep.txt`). **Standard's flame stand-off doubled** (`BOT_FLAME_REACH_SCALE` 2.0): at 1× the
  thrower took **4.2 hp of its own fire a throw** (natural arm; traced: bots in pits under their own flames) and
  standard self-damage rose past its pre-change value; at 2× it is 1.8. **This moves standard, and it is the
  coordinator's to reverse** (the constant).
- Found and **not** fixed: a bot pinned by a vortex against rock burns its tank to zero and stays (the 48–66 s any-fuel
  runs at 96 seeds — **present at `cb63610` too**). T22.03D's "longest any-fuel run < 20 s" was one draw of that tail
  (13 s at 8/32 seeds, 48 s at 96, before this task); it is now a **rate** — runs ≥ 10 s a bot a round ≤ 0.1 (0.02–0.04
  measured, 2.4–2.5 at `d741b3d`, 1.25 with the no-detour plant).
