# T22.14C — What the final audit found: the netcode

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: T22.10G, T22.12D, T22.12E, T22.14A, T22.14B · Size: large (split at compiling boundaries —
one commit per finding group) · Origin: the final M22 audit, area A (read-only reviewers, findings from code reading), the

## What the audit found
- **HIGH-1 (player-visible, predates M22).** A correction replays from stale state. `GameCore::set_player_state` writes
  pos/vel/grounded/fuel/health/alive/mods only; `prev_input`, `JumpState`, `JetpackState` (except fuel) and
  `body.airborne_ticks` are never reinstalled, so the first replayed input compares against the **newest** applied input
  (a jump pressed inside the pending window loses its edge). And the mirror returns early for a dead player, skipping
  `prev_input = input`, and never resets jump/jet on respawn (the server's `PlayerState::respawn` does) → a phantom jump
  or thrust after a respawn. **Fix:** the core keeps each seq's full movement state and previous input (as `predicted`
  keeps kinematics); a correction restores the **acked** seq's copy, then overwrites the wire fields; alive false → true
  resets jump/jet/airborne ticks as respawn does.
- **MED-2.** Two copies of "past the bell": the mirror gates the hole's pull by seq (`bell_seq`) and the buttons by the
  heard phase, so inputs between the server's bell tick and hearing `ended` are predicted with buttons held (~15 px
  walking snap at the bell). **Fix:** one `past_bell(seq)` drives both.
- **MED-3.** The round clock is sent truncated to 0.1 s and `GameScene` resets `roundTime` on every snapshot: the death
  countdown reads "5.1s" (> `RESPAWN_DELAY`) and the clock steps back. **Fix:** send it exactly; the countdown never
  exceeds `RESPAWN_DELAY`.
- **LOW-4.** `vortex_open`/`vortex_close` and the black hole's arrival switch the mirror's pull when the **event** is
  heard, for replays of earlier seqs too. Events carry `tick`: map to a seq like the bell.
- **LOW-5.** Three copies of the seq ↔ tick mapping in TS (the bell's, `enterNeutral`, `predictionAt`); `MAX_FRAME_TICKS`
  re-derived twice in TS.
- **LOW-6/7.** `Predictor.relocate`'s doc and a vitest name say the core predicts vortex trips (it never does);
  GameScene's "server keeps accepting input in Ended … queues a burst" comment is false since T21.30.
- **R100 (ruling).** A winged (flying) player in space is not pulled by the asteroid wells or a vortex's outer pull —
  wings are the flying regime (gravity scale 0), extended to the fields. Capture inside a vortex's capture radius and
  the black hole's horizon still apply. Whether the hole's outer pull applies to wings: builder decides and states it,
  measured against R90's escape guarantee. Server == mirror exactly. Closes T22.14B's "wings do not beat the capped
  pull" row.
- **T22.14B's filed row.** Respawns and joins are held clear of the black hole only; 8 of 13 remaining trips were
  respawns inside a vortex mouth. Shared placement clearance.
- **UI.** The shower's banner counts 26 s (`METEOR_DURATION + METEOR_FALL_TIME`, T22.14A H3): show the dropping window,
  then "clearing" once the last meteor has dropped — from the effect's own schedule.
- **Checks.** `thrusters`' braking arm waits for a condition and freezes in a separate evaluate (a round trip later).
- **ARCHITECTURE-SURVEY.md** is stale in the places the audit listed.

## As ruled (coordinator, 2026-09-25)
- **R100.** A winged (flying) player in space is not pulled by the asteroid wells or by a vortex's outer pull — wings
  are the flying regime (gravity scale 0), extended to the fields. Capture inside a vortex's capture radius and the
  black hole's horizon still apply. Whether the hole's outer pull applies to wings is the builder's call, stated and
  measured against R90's escape guarantee (below: it does not). Same summation, server == mirror exactly. Closes
  T22.14B's "wings do not beat the capped pull" row. *Reverse it by:* the `flying` arm in `attractors::env_at`.
