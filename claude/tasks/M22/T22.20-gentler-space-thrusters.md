# T22.20 — Gentler space thrusters

Milestone: M22 (owner round 3) · Asked 2026-09-26, verbatim: *"can you make jetpacks in space less powerful? it creates
too much inertia."*

## Ruling R109 (coordinator; reversible by the one constant)
- A space-only thrust scale, `SPACE_THRUST_SCALE` = **0.5**, applies to all thrust directions in space (standard and
  low gravity unchanged). The space top speed (`SPACE_MAX_SPEED`) is re-derived from the scaled thrust with its basis
  restated (it must still exceed what flight needs — measure crossing times). **No drag** — the owner's original M22 ask
  was that motion continues until you hit something.
- **Every escape guarantee keeps holding**, because they are measured against the thrust: R46/R96/R97 (wells and
  vortex outer pull capped under the weakest thrust × margin) and R90 (black-hole pull outside the horizon ≤ margin ×
  weakest thrust). The caps must **derive** from the scaled space thrust (no literals) so a future change to the scale
  moves them together; re-run each escape test and the Small-map black-hole arm.
- Fuel: thrust cost per second unchanged (you get the same flight time, just gentler) unless measurement shows fuel now
  runs out before a crossing — then state it.

## Done when
Escape tests (wells, vortex, black hole incl. the Small arm) green; a side-by-side server == mirror test; the owner's
words asserted against a basis (space peak acceleration ≤ 0.5 × the pre-change value; stopping distance from top speed
reported before/after); bots report re-run (space kills, pinned, trips, env deaths) with numbers here; thrusters,
thrusters-match (parked — run by name), asteroid-gravity, black-hole, breach-vortex browser checks run.
