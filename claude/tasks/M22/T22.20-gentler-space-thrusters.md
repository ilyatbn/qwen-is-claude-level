# T22.20 — Gentler space thrusters

Milestone: M22 (owner round 3) · Asked 2026-09-26, verbatim: *"can you make jetpacks in space less powerful? it creates
too much inertia."*

## Ruling R109 (coordinator; reversible by the one constant)
- A space-only thrust scale, `SPACE_THRUST_SCALE` = **0.5**, applies to all thrust directions in space (standard and
  low gravity unchanged). The space top speed (`SPACE_MAX_SPEED`) is re-derived from the scaled thrust with its basis
  restated (it must still exceed what flight needs — measure crossing times). **No drag** — the owner's original M22 ask
  was that motion continues until you hit something.
- **Every escape guarantee keeps holding**, because they are measured against the thrust: R46/R96/R97 (wells and
  vortex outer pull capped under the weakest thrust × margin) and R90 (black-hole pull outside the horizon ≤ margin ×
  weakest thrust). The caps must **derive** from the scaled space thrust (no literals) so a future change to the scale
  moves them together; re-run each escape test and the Small-map black-hole arm.
- Fuel: thrust cost per second unchanged (you get the same flight time, just gentler) unless measurement shows fuel now
  runs out before a crossing — then state it.

## Where space thrust is applied, and what derives from it (step 1, grepped)
- **Applied:** one author, `jetpack::thrust_delta` — called by `jetpack::apply_thrust` (← `player::apply_input`, which
  both `World::step` and `GameCore::apply_input` run), `space::engaging` (sign only) and the plume's
  `GameCore::thrust_at`. No other thrust path (bots emit inputs; wings are `apply_flight`, R100 untouched).
- **Derived from the weakest thrust (now `SPACE_THRUST_DOWN`, no literals):** `SPACE_WELL_ACCEL_MAX` (R46/R96, 675 →
  337.5), `VORTEX_ACCEL_MAX` (R97, 1800 → 900; the cap still binds at 80 px), `BLACK_HOLE_EDGE_PULL` (R90, 810 → 405) and
  through it `BLACK_HOLE_ACCEL_MAX` (925.7 → 462.9), `BOT_SPACE_BRAKE` (225 → 112.5; `bots::space` stop distance and
  arrival speed read it — shared, no copy).
- **Not thrust-derived:** `SPACE_MAX_SPEED` (basis: dives, the 260 governor's diagonal, the sub-stepper),
  `SPACE_VOID_GRACE` (from it), `JUMP_VELOCITY` (a velocity), `JETPACK_DRAIN`/fuel. No projectile inherits body velocity
  by thrust; `zone_reach` does not read thrust.

## For the coordinator
- **Flag, measured: halving the push doubles the stopping distance.** From rest, flight is still governed at 260/axis,
  so crossing barely changes (11.57 → 11.68 s); but a push-off with a direction held still runs away to 1350 (the thrust
  stacks unclamped on a body already past 260 — `jetpack::apply_thrust`'s "never brake a rocket jump" policy), only
  slower (2.50 → 2.78 s), and stopping from there now takes 1646 px / 2.47 s instead of 817 px / 1.23 s. If the owner's
  "inertia" means *hard to stop*, R109 makes that half worse; the levers are a lower `SPACE_MAX_SPEED` or governing
  thrust along the velocity in space. Not done — outside R109.
- **Feel changes that ride on "caps derive":** rock wells pull half as hard (level 5: 337.5 px/s², ~¼ g), the hole
  pulls half as hard (405 at the horizon), bots close ~20 % slower and kill ~22 % less.
