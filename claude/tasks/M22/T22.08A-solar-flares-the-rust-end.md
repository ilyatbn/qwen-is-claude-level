## Do these
- **`EffectKind::SolarFlare`**, appended (`KINDS`, `WEIGHTS`, `active_duration`,
  `enabled_from_constants`, every match site). The wire name is the `Debug` spelling, `SolarFlare`.
- **The table at roll time, keyed on the map** (`R78`): `EffectScheduler::tick` takes a
  `WeatherTable` derived from `map.space_geometry()`; space rolls meteor + flare, the ground everything
  but the flare. `enabled` stays constants-only. The ground schedule does not move (a zero weight at the
  end of the table draws identically). Space arms for `two_live_kinds_alternate`,
  `a_draw_is_always_possible` and the live-distribution test (`R28`), and a never/always pair: space
  never rolls fog/toxic/lava, the ground never rolls a flare, each beside its presence.
- **`effects/flare.rs`**: the ribbon as a pure function of (seed, elapsed, map size) — a prominence
  loop that wanders the map — sampled at `SOLAR_FLARE_SAMPLES` points, with a test that neighbouring
  samples are never farther apart than the ribbon's radius (so a body cannot slip between two).
- **Contact** through `flame`'s circle-against-box test, given a radius and made `pub(crate)`.
- **`PlayerState::burning_until`** (`R79`), `burn()` writes the deadline, cleared at die and respawn,

  `a_hazard_death_on_the_radiation_tick_is_not_named_radiation`.

- **Forcing** (`R83`): `WeatherMode::Always(SolarFlare)` and wasm `force_effect` refuse a non-space

- **Load guard** (`R84`): a meteor-in-space peak guard, ceiling from `METEOR_SPEED`/`METEOR_EVERY`
  plus fragments; the toxic guard says why Space is not in its loop.
- **`REPLAY_VERSION` 16 → 17** (`R85`). `docs/13` §7's "never a hazard position" is contradicted by
  lava and the flare — reported for `docs/77`, not edited.

## As built (2026-09-23)

- **Shape** (`effects/flare.rs`): the loop's centre rides an arc of `SOLAR_FLARE_ORBIT` (or what fits)
  at exactly `SOLAR_FLARE_SPEED`, about a seeded point; the axis turns at `SOLAR_FLARE_TURN`; the arch
  breathes and ripples inside the ribbon's width. 48 samples, neighbours ≤ `SOLAR_FLARE_RIBBON_R` apart
  on every seed tested. Its clock starts at the telegraph (`effect_start`), contact only while `Active`.
- **Decision — the burn stage is not gated on `Playing`**, like poison (8a) and unlike radiation (8c): a
  burn caught in the last seconds finishes after the bell. Contact itself is `Playing`-only (stage 5).
- **R82's arithmetic, corrected, not the ruling**: sealed, the flare costs **24 health + 4 energy** (one
  `SHIELD_HIT_COST` per logged second); the "8" is those 4 plus the 4 the seal drains over the same 4 s.
  Both measured in `a_full_burn_costs_what_the_basis_says`. Also found: `GameEvent::Damage.amount` is the
  **rolled** amount, not what landed through the suit — the test reads health for the sealed total.
- **Timing trap, recorded at `PlayerState::burn_tick`**: burning is judged at the tick's middle so a touch
  and its first burn tick sharing one `now` give exactly 4 logs; a burn stamped at the previous tick's
  clock measured 3.
- **R84**: meteors in space, Medium, one shower: peak **66** airborne against a derived ceiling of **108**
  (1320 `ProjectileMove`/s).
- **Balance** (`space_radiation_report` rerun, 8 seeds): radiation deaths 0.69 → **0.71**, other deaths
  1.27 → **1.08**, packs 0.73 → **0.60**, picked 0.27 → 0.27, unsealed 25.1 % → **23.0 %** per player per
  round. Space now alternates meteor/flare where it rolled fog.
- **Reported for `docs/77`, not edited**: `docs/13` §7 ("never a hazard position") — lava and the flare
  both derive positions client-side from the seed. And `a_draw_is_always_possible`'s old "panic" doc was
  wrong (R28): `pick_weighted` returns 0 on an empty table; the test now also asserts nothing non-live ran.
- Size: ~460 lines of non-test code with comments, past the ~400 guide; one coherent change.
