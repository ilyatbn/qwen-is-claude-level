## The golden hazard, correctly identified

**It is not shared RNG.** The substreams are isolated — `rng.rs` seeds each pass as
`seed ^ fnv1a64(tag)` and **two tests pin it**:
`meta.rs::sub_stream_isolation_at_the_top_level` burns 10 000 draws off `"items"` and
`"weather"` and asserts the mask hash is unchanged, and
`objects.rs::draining_another_substream_does_not_move_an_object`.

**The real mechanism is the retry loop.** `gen/mod.rs` runs
`for attempt in 0..MAX_GEN_ATTEMPTS { let seed = requested_seed.wrapping_add(attempt); … }`
and accepts on `report.passed`, which comes from `traversal::analyse`, which reads
**`JUMP_REACH` and `JUMP_HEIGHT` — compile-time consts of `JUMP_VELOCITY`, `WALK_SPEED` and
`GRAVITY`.** So anything that flips a traversability verdict changes which attempt first
passes, which re-derives every substream from `requested_seed + attempt`, which moves the whole
map. **Golden hashes move through the accept/reject gate, not through RNG sharing.**

`crates/game-core/tests/golden_hashes.txt` holds **24 data rows** — 4 seeds × 3 scales × 2
generators, plus two comment lines (counted 2026-09-18, not remembered). A third generator
makes it 36. Regenerate with
`GOLDEN_UPDATE=1 cargo test -p game-core --release --test golden`; it is the only pinned-digest
fixture in the tree.

**Traversability is meaningless here anyway.** A map with no continuous ground cannot pass a
walk-reachability check, so this generator needs its own acceptance predicate — and that
predicate is a deliverable, not a detail.

## Deliverable

- A generator, on its own substream tag and reached the way `MapScale` already branches the
  existing one — **not a second entry point.**
- A **closed** circular boundary of thin land. Closed is the assertion; "land exists at the
  edges" is not the same claim and a gap is a map players leave.
- Many small islands inside it, with float-through space between them, at every `MapScale`.
- Its own acceptance predicate, replacing walk-traversal, stated in the file.

## Tests

- The boundary is **closed** — walk it, do not sample it.
- Islands exist, are separated, and have space between them: a **population claim over many
  seeds**, not one draw.
- **Existing golden hashes are unchanged when the mode is off.** The most important test here,
  and the one that catches a shared-RNG perturbation if the analysis above is ever wrong.
- Density and gap distributions reported across a 999-seed sweep, the way T21.40 reported its
  placement change.
