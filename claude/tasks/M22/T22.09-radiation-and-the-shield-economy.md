# T22.09 — Radiation, and what a shield means now

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.01** · Size: ~200 lines · Origin: new, 2026-09-18

Asked for: *"radiation. players who dont have a shield are hit by radiation taking 1 damage per
second. all players have a shield by default in a spacesuit, but energy is still a thing so
they need to make sure to constantly replenish it with battery packs."*

## The blocking question: is this the shield that already exists?

`PlayerState::shield_active(now)` ships today, driven by the shield-generator **item**, with
`SHIELD_DAMAGE_MULT` and `SHIELD_HIT_COST` charged against the battery. The owner says *"all
players have a shield by default in a spacesuit"*.

So either:

- **(a) the suit is a shield source** — one predicate, two ways to satisfy it, and
  `shield_active` keeps meaning exactly one thing; or
- **(b) the suit shield is a second thing**, and `shield_active` now answers two questions —
  *"does incoming damage get multiplied"* and *"am I safe from radiation"* — which may not have
  the same answer.

**(a) is the straight reading and the one this file assumes.** It is also the only one that
does not create the field-means-two-things shape, which this project has paid for before. **But
it is the owner's call — milestone open question 2** — because (a) means a shield generator
picked up in a *normal* match also grants radiation immunity in a mode that has no radiation,
which is harmless, and that the suit's shield multiplies incoming weapon damage too, which is a
balance change nobody asked for.

## Battery packs already exist

`BATTERY_PACK` is item id 6 with `BATTERY_PACK_AMOUNT`. The owner's *"replenish it with battery
packs"* needs **no new item** — it needs a reason to want one, which is what constant radiation
supplies. Check the spawn weighting before adding anything: a mode that drains energy constantly
and spawns batteries at the standard rate is a mode where everyone dies at the same time.

## Deliverable

- Radiation: **1 damage per second** to an unshielded player, the number in `constants.rs`.
- The suit grants the shield, by whichever of (a)/(b) the owner rules.
- Energy drains, so the shield is not free, and battery packs are the answer.
- **A player can tell it is happening.** T21.25's finding was that the real defect in toxic rain
  might not be the damage at all: *"if the damage is fine, the bug is that a player cannot tell
  they are being poisoned."* One damage a second with no feedback is a health bar that drifts
  down for no visible reason.

## Hazards

- **Ambient or zonal** — milestone open question 6. This file assumes ambient and constant,
  which is the straight reading of *"players who dont have a shield are hit by radiation"*.
  Zonal is a different task.
- **1 dps against `BASE_HEALTH` 100 is a 100-second clock.** State the intended pacing against
  the round length and the battery economy, and measure it, rather than shipping a number.
- **Death by radiation needs an attributable cause.** The kill feed, the death overlay and the
  score all key on a cause; "died of nothing" is what a missing arm produces.
- **`docs/` does not describe radiation.** Report the gap; do not edit `docs/`.

## Tests

- An unshielded player loses health at the rate, pinned to the constant, over a measured
  interval — **with a shielded control in the same test that loses none.**
- Energy drains while shielded, and a battery pack restores it — both ends.
- At zero energy the shield stops and the damage starts: the transition asserted, not just the
  two states.
- The player can see it: **rendered pixels** for whatever the feedback is.
- Radiation is absent outside space, with the presence control.
