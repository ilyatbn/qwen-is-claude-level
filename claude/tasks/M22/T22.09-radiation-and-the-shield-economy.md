# T22.09 — Radiation, and what a shield means now

Milestone: M22 · Depends on: **T22.01** · Size: ~200 lines · Origin: new, 2026-09-18

Asked for: *"radiation. players who dont have a shield are hit by radiation taking 1 damage per
second. all players have a shield by default in a spacesuit, but energy is still a thing so
they need to make sure to constantly replenish it with battery packs."*

## `shield_active` cannot see the mode, and the suit must not draw the bubble

**`R26`, and it re-prices `R2`.** `shield_active(&self, now)` is
`self.battery > 0.0 && self.holds_shield_generator()` — `PlayerState` has **no route to the
game mode**, and `World.gravity` is read by nothing in production. `R2`'s *"reverse it by
`shield_active`"* priced this as one place; it is one place **plus a way to know you are in
space**, which does not exist. Adding a `PlayerState` field is the exact shape that struct's
own comment rejects (*"**No `shield_until`** … A field beside them would be a third answer that
can disagree"*).

**Ruled: `shield_active(&self, now, suit: bool)`** — the mode bit comes from the three
production call sites that know the world (`net/codec.rs`'s encode, `state.rs::apply_damage`,
`game-wasm/src/lib.rs::shield_active`). One predicate, two sources, no fourth flag.

**And the suit does not draw the shield generator's bubble.** `codec.rs` sets flags bit 3 from
`shield_active` and `playerView.ts` shows `shieldBubble` from it — so under `R2` as written
**every player in space wears a bubble all round**, and a bubble that means *"I am carrying a
shield generator"* would come to mean nothing. The suit gets its own quieter feedback, which
is what this task's *"a player can tell it is happening"* requirement wants anyway.

**Fix the lie while you are there:** `game-wasm/src/lib.rs::shield_active` passes a hardcoded
`0.0` for `now`.

## Radiation logs once a second, never once a tick

**`R25`.** `SIM_HZ` is 60. One `DamageLog` entry per tick per player is **360 events a second
at `MAX_PLAYERS`, all round** — and on the client each drives a floating damage number reading
`0.0167`, a red hit vignette retriggered 60×/s, and **`audio.spatial('hit', …)` sixty times a
second, forever**. Accumulate and emit on whole seconds, still through `apply_damage_log`,
because that is where the warmup gate lives.

**The right precedent is `World::step`'s step 8a (toxic poison)**, not lava and not
`BurnField`: it is the only existing damage source that is a per-player standing predicate
rather than a placed object.

## The blocking question: is this the shield that already exists?

`PlayerState::shield_active(now)` ships today, driven by the shield-generator **item**, with
`SHIELD_DAMAGE_MULT` and `SHIELD_HIT_COST` charged against the battery. The owner says *"all
players have a shield by default in a spacesuit"*.

So either:

- **(a) the suit is a shield source** — one predicate, two ways to satisfy it, and
  `shield_active` keeps meaning exactly one thing; or
- **(b) the suit shield is a second thing**, and `shield_active` now answers two questions —
  *"does incoming damage get multiplied"* and *"am I safe from radiation"* — which may not have
  the same answer.

**(a) is the straight reading and the one this file assumes.** It is also the only one that
does not create the field-means-two-things shape, which this project has paid for before. **But
it is the owner's call — milestone open question 2** — because (a) means a shield generator
picked up in a *normal* match also grants radiation immunity in a mode that has no radiation,
which is harmless, and that the suit's shield multiplies incoming weapon damage too, which is a
balance change nobody asked for.

## Battery packs already exist — and the economy around them does not

`BATTERY_PACK` is item id 6 with `BATTERY_PACK_AMOUNT` = 50. The owner's *"replenish it with
battery packs"* needs **no new item**. But the file's own caution — *"a mode that drains energy
constantly and spawns batteries at the standard rate is a mode where everyone dies at the same
time"* — turned out to be **correct and understated**. Measured 2026-09-20:

- **Nobody spawns with a battery.** `PlayerState::new` sets `battery: 0.0` and `STARTING_KIT`
  is `[SHOVEL]`, so under `R2` every player in space is **unshielded from the first tick** and
  dies of radiation in 100 seconds.
- **There is no per-second shield drain and there must not be one.** T20.08 **deleted**
  `SHIELD_DURATION` and `SHIELD_DRAIN`; `SHIELD_HIT_COST` is 1.0 **per hit taken and nothing
  per second**, a pool whose doc says *"`BATTERY_MAX` of 100 buys a hundred absorptions"*.
  **This file's deliverable *"energy drains, so the shield is not free"* would reverse a
  shipped, documented decision — `R24` rules that it does not.**
- **`BATTERY_PACK`'s weight is 14/241 on the ground (5.81 %) and 18/271 in crates (6.64 %).**
  A 240 s round on Medium yields **2.69 packs for the whole lobby — 22.4 energy per player** —
  and **43 % of rounds start with no pack on the map at all**. Against a 1 dps clock that is
  short by roughly **17×**.

**`R24` is the ruling and it is a design, not a patch**: `RADIATION_DPS` 1.0 to health when
unsealed, **`RADIATION_SHIELD_COST` 1.0 energy per second to battery when sealed**, the suit
battery **full at spawn and restored on respawn** in space, and `BATTERY_PACK`'s space spawn
weight **doubled**. One energy buys one damage avoided and `BATTERY_MAX` equals `BASE_HEALTH`,
so **the suit battery is literally a second health bar and radiation eats it first** — one
sentence a player learns by dying once. **Those four numbers are a stated starting point, not
a measurement: this task owes a `balance.rs`-shaped run over 8 seeds and must report it, and
must move them if it disagrees.**

## Deliverable

- Radiation: **1 damage per second** to an unshielded player, the number in `constants.rs`.
- The suit grants the shield, by whichever of (a)/(b) the owner rules.
- Energy drains, so the shield is not free, and battery packs are the answer.
- **A player can tell it is happening.** T21.25's finding was that the real defect in toxic rain
  might not be the damage at all: *"if the damage is fine, the bug is that a player cannot tell
  they are being poisoned."* One damage a second with no feedback is a health bar that drifts
  down for no visible reason.

## Hazards

- **Ambient or zonal** — milestone open question 6. This file assumes ambient and constant,
  which is the straight reading of *"players who dont have a shield are hit by radiation"*.
  Zonal is a different task.
- **1 dps against `BASE_HEALTH` 100 is a 100-second clock.** State the intended pacing against
  the round length and the battery economy, and measure it, rather than shipping a number.
- **Death by radiation needs an attributable cause — and this is `R20`'s problem arriving one
  task early.** Radiation is not scheduled, so it is not an `EffectKind`; falling back to
  `DamageSource::Weather` makes the kill feed say **"died of the weather" in a mode that has no
  weather**. A `DamageSource::Radiation` arm touches five production match sites
  (`state.rs::apply_damage`'s energy-pierce match, `state.rs`'s attacker-recording match,
  `world/mod.rs::lifesteal_attacker`, `world/mod.rs::apply_damage_log`, `bots/mod.rs`). And
  **the client end fails silently**: `GameScene`'s death allowlist turns anything unrecognised
  into `'player'`, so `"radiation"` renders as *"? → ana (radiation)"*. Read `R20` before you
  start; it counts both ends.
- **`docs/` does not describe radiation.** Report the gap; do not edit `docs/`.

## Tests

- An unshielded player loses health at the rate, pinned to the constant, over a measured
  interval — **with a shielded control in the same test that loses none.**
- Energy drains while shielded, and a battery pack restores it — both ends.
- At zero energy the shield stops and the damage starts: the transition asserted, not just the
  two states.
- The player can see it: **rendered pixels** for whatever the feedback is.
- Radiation is absent outside space, with the presence control.
