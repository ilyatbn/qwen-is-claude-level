## What the review found
- **F1 (HIGH, player-visible).** Space bots spend **~30 % of alive time pinned motionless against rock**
  (runs up to 91 s, bots stacking on one rock, no engagement; `gate-review2203b-pinned.txt`,
  `gate-review2203b-stuck.txt`). Two causes in `bots/space.rs::steer`:
  (a) `funded = urgent || fuel > BOT_SPACE_FUEL_RESERVE` has **no hysteresis** — at 1.5 s the bot burns
  ~1 tick, drops under, waits `JETPACK_REFILL_DELAY`, and the well pulls it back onto the rock;
  (b) **no "blocked by rock" handling** — a bot pushes toward a destination behind the rock it is
  pressed against; `clear_heading` was used only when escaping a hazard.
  Reviewer's plant for (a): `fuel > RESERVE && (jetpack.active || fuel > RESERVE + 1.0)` → pinned at the
  reserve 29.6 → 3.3 %, runs ≥ 10 s 84 → 0, kills 159 → 190; against rock at any fuel still 29.3 %, 49 runs,
  longest 73 s — that part is (b).
- **F2.** `space_bots_report`'s fight floor (space kills ≥ standard's 0.27 a bot a round) is weak: space
  measures 1.5–1.8. Replace with a floor derived from the measured space number with a stated margin, and say
  which plant it catches.
- **F3.** The arrival test must assert the **maximum overshoot past the destination** along the approach
  ≤ stop distance + `PLAYER_H` across the whole run; removing the `√(2·brake·d)` profile must turn it red.
- **F4.** `breach-vortex.mjs`'s skip: skip only the first snapshot at/after the relocate event's tick, and
  report hitch skips separately (no blanket `max(1, acked/3)` allowance).

  refill at 0.3 and sat 35 s at an empty tank.

  **swept** at 2 px (it passed "up" under a thin overhang and thrust into it) and also tries ±135°/back; fire and
  flare escapes use it too.
