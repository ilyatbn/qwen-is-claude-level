# T13.06.7 — A weapon occupies one slot, ever (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.06.1 · Size: ~110 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C24
- `docs/30-items-inventory.md` §2

## Deliverable

A given weapon id appears **at most once** in the inventory. Picking up a weapon you
already hold tops its ammo to `max_stack`; if already full, the pickup is refused and
the item stays on the ground.

## Notes

- With 20 weapons and 24 slots (§C10) this is the difference between a loadout and a
  hoard.
- Consumables are unaffected — heals and batteries are counters, not slots (§C9).
- Non-weapon stackables keep the existing merge rule.
- Death drops: a refilled weapon drops one stack, not two.

## Tests

- Picking up a held weapon refills it and creates no second slot.
- Picking up a held weapon at full ammo is refused and the world item remains — with a
  control that a *different* weapon is accepted.
- The inventory never contains two slots with the same weapon id, over a long random
  pickup sequence.
- Death drops one stack per weapon held.

## Done when

cargo test -p game-core --lib inventory
