## The bug

`crates/game-server/src/app.rs:109` creates a room at **server startup**,
unconditionally. It seats 5 bots and starts ticking — map, timers, scoring, items,
weather. Every player who connects lands in a battle already in progress.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-server/src/{app,registry,room,session}.rs`
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs`
- `client/src/scenes/LobbyScene.ts`, `client/src/net/lobby.ts`

## Deliverable

- **No bootstrap room.** The server starts with an empty registry; `/healthz`
  reporting `rooms 0, players 0` on a fresh server is correct.
- Rooms are created **on demand** by `create_room` or quick match, and generate their
  map immediately — that is the *only* work a `Lobby` room does.
- **A `Lobby` room does not tick**: no timers, no scoring, no item spawns, no weather,
  no bots.
- `MIN_PLAYERS_TO_START` = **2**, counting **humans**. With 2+ present the round
  starts after a visible `LOBBY_COUNTDOWN` (5 s).
- **"Start with bots"** in the lobby seats bots and starts the round — the solo path,
  now a choice rather than the default that caused the bug.
- Bots are seated **when a round starts**, never in a `Lobby` room.
- A room that empties of humans returns to `Lobby`, not to a fresh round, and is
  reaped after `ROOM_EMPTY_TTL`.

## Notes

- The lobby screen already exists (T10.04). It needs the roster, the countdown and
  the button.
- Check every place that assumed a room is always ticking — `/metrics`, the reaper,
  the checksum broadcaster, `sweep_unready`. A `Lobby` room must not trip any of them.
- This changes what the e2e suite must do to get into a game. Expect to update the
  harness; `full-round`, `two-clients` and `m10-checkpoint` all assume a running
  battle on join.

## Tests

- A fresh server has **zero** rooms and zero players; nothing ticks. Assert the tick
  count is zero after 2 s, with a control that it is non-zero once a round starts.
- Creating a room generates a map and still does not tick.
- One human alone never starts a round — assert over 10 s, with a control that
  "Start with bots" does start it.
- Two humans start after `LOBBY_COUNTDOWN`, and both see the same countdown.
- No bot exists in a `Lobby` room.
- A room emptied of humans returns to `Lobby` and is reaped.
- **End to end**: connect, and assert you are in a lobby rather than a battle —
  the bug as reported.
