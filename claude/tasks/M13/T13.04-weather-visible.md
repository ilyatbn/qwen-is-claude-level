## The bug

The weather simulation is correct and heavily tested — and none of it reaches the
screen. This is the same shape as §B21, where heavy fog ran correctly for five
milestones and changed nothing visible.

## Touch only
- `client/src/render/weather.ts` (+ test)
- `client/src/render/worldView.ts`

## Deliverable

| effect | what it looks like |
|---|---|
| Toxic rain | green droplets falling across the map during the active phase, a sickly green vignette, a bubbling glowing puddle at each hazard |
| Meteors | bright head, long tapering tail — a falling star; impact flash, shake by distance, glowing fragments |
| Lava | fire spewing **upward** from the vent, spreading and falling back; burning ground that flickers; additive blending |
| Fog / smoke | a drifting layer, driven by the per-player `vision` byte (§B21) |

All are light sources at night.

## Notes

- Droplets and embers are **particle emitters**, not entities. `TOXIC_DURATION` is
  8 s of continuous rain over a whole map; per-drop objects will not do.
- The telegraph phase is spatial for meteors (`docs/13` §4) — shadows where the first
  will land. That is the warning, and three seconds is meant to be enough to move.
- Hazard positions come from `hazard_spawn`; clients never roll their own
  (`docs/13` §7).

## Tests

- **Pixel acceptance per effect** (§C2): each changes pixels in its own region
  during its active phase, with a control region and a control frame (effect off).
- A transient hazard cannot always be photographed — assert the effect **ran** from
  its lifecycle events, and the pixels from a sustained phase. Name the file for what
  it shows (§A22).
- Toxic leaves the mask byte-identical — it denies space and does not dig.
