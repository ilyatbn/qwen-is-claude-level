# T13.06.9 — Show the jetpack number, and check the refill (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.06.1 · Size: ~90 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C26
- `docs/20-player-movement.md` §5 (the fuel rules)

## Deliverable

A numeric readout beside the jetpack bar showing current fuel to one decimal, so the
refill curve is legible.

**And verify the refill against the spec while you are there**: `JETPACK_DRAIN` 1.0/s
while thrusting, `JETPACK_REFILL_DELAY` 0.5 s of no thrust, then `JETPACK_REFILL`
0.5/s — a full 5 s burn takes 10 s to recover.

## Notes

- The reported symptom is "refills weirdly", which is an observation, not a diagnosis.
  **Measure the curve** — burn to empty, then sample fuel every tick for 12 s — and
  compare against those three constants before changing anything.
- If the observed curve disagrees, that is a bug and the measurement is the evidence.
  If it agrees, the readout is the fix and the feel is a tuning question — say which.
- The refill runs whether or not you are grounded (`docs/20` §5); a common wrong
  implementation gates it on landing.

## Tests

- The readout matches the snapshot's fuel value.
- Measured: 5.0 s of held thrust drains to exactly 0; 10.0 s idle refills to exactly
  5.0; no refill during the first 0.5 s. All three already have unit tests — confirm
  they still pass and that the *rendered* number agrees with them.
- **Rendered** (§C2): the number changes between frames while draining.
