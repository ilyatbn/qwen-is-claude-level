## The bugs

Crates appear **in mid-air**, and cannot be picked up. Two separate failures that
present as one.

## Touch only
- `crates/game-core/src/items/crate.rs` (or wherever the crate step lives)
- `client/src/render/items.ts`
- the crate tests

## Deliverable

A crate spawns at `y = SKY_MARGIN / 2` and **falls** through the same sub-stepped
resolver everything else uses, landing on the first solid ground below. On landing it
becomes a normal `WorldItem`, picked up on contact like anything else.

Falling crates draw a parachute and a beacon (`docs/32` §4) — they are meant to be
visible across the map and to pull players together.

## Notes

- Mid-air almost certainly means the client draws it at its spawn position and never
  follows it, **or** the crate never falls. Determine which before changing anything
  — they are different bugs (§A32: report what you sampled).
- "Cannot pick up" most likely means the landing transition to `WorldItem` never
  happens. Assert the **pickup**, end to end, not the landing.
- Crates are exempt from TTL and from `MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` eviction (`docs/32` §4).

## Tests

- A crate's y decreases over successive frames, then stops — sampled from the
  rendered frame (§C2), not only from the sim.
- A crate that lands on ground later carved away **keeps falling** (`docs/32` §4).
- A player walking into a landed crate picks it up: the item enters their inventory
  and the crate disappears from the world — asserted at both ends (§A39).
- A crate reaching the void despawns (§C15).
