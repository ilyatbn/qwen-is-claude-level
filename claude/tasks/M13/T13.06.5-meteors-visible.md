# T13.06.5 — Meteors you can see and dodge (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.04 · Size: ~120 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C22
- `docs/13-weather-effects.md` §4

## The bug

Only meteor *explosions* are visible. `docs/13` §4 already says meteors are ordinary
projectiles — so this is the §C4 render gap, not a simulation change.

## Deliverable

The falling star is drawn **from spawn**, not from impact: a bright head with a long
tapering tail, visible for its whole flight. At `METEOR_SPEED` 700 it crosses a
1536 px map in about two seconds, which is the dodge window.

The telegraph shadows (`docs/13` §4) mark where the first ones will land.

## Notes

- If meteors are not reaching the client's projectile layer, that is the same defect
  class as §C23 — check both together and fix the path, not the instance.
- They are light sources at night (§A3).
- Fragments too: `METEOR_FRAGMENTS` (6) per impact, and they must not spawn fragments.

## Tests

- **Rendered**: a frame during descent shows the meteor above the ground, with a
  control frame before the shower starts (§C2).
- Time from spawn to impact is ≥1.5 s on a medium map — the dodge window, asserted.
- Both ends: meteors the server has alive vs sprites drawn.
- Fragments do not recurse.
