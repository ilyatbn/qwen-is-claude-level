# T13.06.2 — Melee hits what is in front of you (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.06.1 · Size: ~120 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C19
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B7 (the table), and T11.09's balance finding

## The bug

Reach is measured from the player **centre**, so `axe` at 40 connects with someone
2.5 player-widths away who is visibly not adjacent.

## Deliverable

Reach becomes **`PLAYER_W / 2 + weapon_reach`** — from the body edge, so the number
means "how far in front of me". New values: `knife` 12, `bat` 16, `whip` 44, `axe` 16,
`hammer` 14.

## Notes

- **T11.09 raised these reaches deliberately** (knife 26→36, axe 30→40, hammer 28→38)
  because melee sat below half the arsenal's median damage and hit rate tracked reach
  almost exactly. This is a real trade.
- **Compensate with arc and cooldown, not reach.** A wider sweep and a faster swing
  keep melee viable without letting it connect at a distance.
- **Re-run T11.09's balance harness and report** what this did to melee's damage
  share, aggregated across ≥8 seeds. If melee falls back below half the median, say
  so with the numbers rather than quietly restoring reach.

## Tests

- A target at the stated reach is hit; one a pixel beyond is not — both sides.
- The whip still out-reaches the knife by its table difference.
- Reach is measured from the body edge: a test that would pass under the old
  centre-based rule must fail.
- Balance numbers reported with their sample size.
