# T13.06.3 — You cannot fire while moving (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.06.2 · Size: ~130 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C20

## Deliverable

Firing, throwing and swinging are refused while the player is moving under their own
power: `|vel.x| > FIRE_MOVE_MAX_SPEED` (8) **or** a movement key held this tick.

## Notes

- **Check the input, not only the velocity.** Without the key check you can fire in
  the single tick between releasing a key and friction taking effect.
- **Being knocked around does not stop you firing.** This is about your own movement —
  a player thrown by a blast must still be able to shoot, or knockback becomes a stun.
- **It applies to bots.** They fire while walking today; if they keep doing so they
  become strictly better than a human at the same skill and every §B7 number shifts.
  Re-run the balance harness and report.
- Rejection is silent and logged at `debug` like every other fire rejection
  (`docs/30` §4) — it is normal, not an error.

## Tests

- Firing while walking is refused and spawns nothing (assert the effect).
- Firing while standing succeeds — the control, or the test passes for a build that
  never fires.
- Firing one tick after releasing a key is still refused.
- Firing while being knocked back **succeeds**.
- Bots respect it; balance numbers re-reported with sample size.
