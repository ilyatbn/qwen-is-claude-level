# T13.06.4 — Toxic rain falls from the sky (v4)

Milestone: M13 · Depends on: T13.04 · Size: ~160 lines

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C21
- `docs/13-weather-effects.md` §3

## The bug

Puddles spawn directly on surface points, so they appear **underground inside caves**
— rain that fell through a roof.

## Deliverable

A toxic drop is a **projectile**: spawned from a cloud at `y = SKY_MARGIN`, falling
under gravity, forming its puddle **where it lands**.

## Notes

- This fixes the underground bug **by construction** — a drop cannot reach a cave
  floor without an opening — rather than by filtering surface points.
- Drops are drawn by the same layer as every other projectile (§C4), so the rain
  becomes visible for free.
- 20 drops over `TOXIC_DURATION` is not a load concern.
- Puddle damage, radius and lifetime are unchanged; only where they appear changes.
- The cloud arriving is the telegraph.

## Tests

- Every puddle's centre is reachable from the sky — assert no puddle forms under a
  roof, using a map with a known cave, and a control that puddles *do* form in the
  open on the same map.
- Drop count still equals `TOXIC_DURATION / TOXIC_PUDDLE_EVERY`.
- Toxic still leaves the mask **byte-identical** — it denies space, it does not dig.
- A player standing in a puddle its full life loses `TOXIC_DPS * TOXIC_PUDDLE_LIFE`.
- **Rendered**: drops are visible falling (§C2), with a control frame before the
  effect starts.
