# T17.05 — A live match is closed (v6)

Milestone: M17 · Depends on: T17.03, T17.04 · Size: ~120 lines

## Read first
- `docs/74-amendments-v6.md` §E4

## Deliverable

A join against a room whose phase is past `Lobby` is refused with
`join_error { reason: "in_progress" }`. Quick match skips such rooms rather than seating
into them.

## Notes

- **`docs/41` §4 is overridden, not deleted.** The damaged-map catch-up encoder stays: a
  player who *drops* and reconnects to a match they were already seated in still needs
  it, and §E4 names that as a deliberately open seam. Do not remove
  `encode_map_init_at`.
- Refusing a join is not the same as freeing the seat. A disconnected player's seat and
  the rules around it are out of scope here.
- The existing `full` rejection stays; `in_progress` is a distinct reason, because the
  client says different things about them.

## Tests

- A join into a `Warmup`, `Playing` or `Ended` room is refused with `in_progress`, and a
  join into `Lobby` succeeds — the control without which the refusal proves nothing.
- The refusal does not seat, does not allocate a seat id, and does not emit
  `player_join`. Count seats at both ends.
- Quick match with only started rooms available creates a new lobby rather than failing.
- `encode_map_init_at` still exists and still has a test.
