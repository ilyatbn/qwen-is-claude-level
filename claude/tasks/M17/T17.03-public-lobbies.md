# T17.03 — Public lobbies: fill to five, or bots after ten seconds (v6)

Milestone: M17 · Depends on: T17.02 · Size: ~200 lines

## Read first
- `docs/74-amendments-v6.md` §E2, §E5
- `docs/71-amendments-v3.md` §B10 (there is no queue — still true, different rule)

## Deliverable

Quick match seats into the **fullest public lobby in `Lobby` phase with a free seat**,
lowest room id on a tie, else creates one. Never a private lobby, never a started match.

- Seating the `LOBBY_CAPACITY`th player starts the match immediately.
- A `LOBBY_BOT_TIMEOUT` clock starts when the **first** player is seated and **does not
  reset** when others join. On expiry, empty seats fill with bots and the match starts.
- Quick match randomises its settings (§E7), seeded, not `thread_rng`.

`LOBBY_COUNTDOWN` and `MIN_PLAYERS_TO_START` are retired.

## Notes

- **The timeout does not reset on join** — a player who has waited ten seconds is not
  made to wait twenty because someone else arrived. That is the whole rule; a resetting
  timer is the bug this is written to prevent.
- `MAX_PLAYERS` (6) stays the seat cap; `LOBBY_CAPACITY` (5) is the fill target. A dev
  `BOT_COUNT=5` alongside one human is a legal six-seat game (§E5).
- Bots are still seated only at match start (`docs/72` §C18) — the timeout does not seat
  them into a lobby, it starts a match that seats them.
- Reuse `start_with_bots` as the manual form of the timeout rather than adding a verb.

## Tests

- Five players seat and the match starts on the fifth, with no wait — assert the phase
  changed and the elapsed lobby time is under the timeout.
- One player waits the timeout, gets bots, and the match starts. Control: before the
  timeout it has not started.
- The timeout does not reset: seat a player at t=0 and another at t=8, assert the match
  starts at t=10 and not t=18.
- Quick match never returns a private lobby or a started match — falsify by starting one
  and asserting it is skipped.
- Randomised settings are seeded: same seed, same scale, twice.

## A note on the Done-when

`cargo test -p game-server --lib <pattern>` **matches zero tests and exits 0** when the
pattern names nothing, and `crates/game-server/tests/` is where the lobby tests already
live — `--lib` never runs an integration target. Five of these six Done-whens were
written in that vacuous form and are corrected here to name a `--test` target. **Create
that target.** If your Done-when passes before you have written anything, it is not a
Done-when; say so rather than reporting it green.
