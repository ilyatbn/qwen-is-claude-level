## Deliverable

The reaper counts **humans only**. A room with bots and no humans is empty and is reaped
after `ROOM_EMPTY_TTL`, whether it is a lobby or a running match.

## Notes

- **This is what makes bot-only testing work**, not what breaks it: an e2e check drives a
  real client, and that client is the human holding the room open. Five bots fighting
  survive exactly as long as the tester is connected.
- The reaper already exists and already runs (`ROOM_REAP_INTERVAL`). This task is the
  counting rule, not a new mechanism — confirm it still has its caller.
- `return_to_lobby` on the last human leaving mid-round is now redundant with reaping and
  may be wrong: decide, and say which, rather than leaving both.

## Tests

- A room with five bots and zero humans is reaped after the TTL. Control: the same room
  with one human is not.
- A lobby with no players is reaped.
- The reaper's caller is asserted to exist — a test calling `reap` is not a caller.
- Reaping a running match stops its tick task and frees its room id and code.

## A note on the Done-when

`cargo test -p game-server --lib <pattern>` **matches zero tests and exits 0** when the
pattern names nothing, and `crates/game-server/tests/` is where the lobby tests already
live — `--lib` never runs an integration target. Five of these six Done-whens were
written in that vacuous form and are corrected here to name a `--test` target. **Create
that target.** If your Done-when passes before you have written anything, it is not a
Done-when; say so rather than reporting it green.

## Done when
