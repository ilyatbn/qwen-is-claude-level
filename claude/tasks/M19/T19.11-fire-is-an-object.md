## Deliverable

The flame itself, and nothing that lights one yet. A flame is a projectile with four
differences from a bullet:

- **It does not die on contact**: it bounces at `FLAME_RESTITUTION` / `FLAME_FRICTION`
  and settles. Its only end is `FLAME_LIFE`.
- **It falls slowly**: `FLAME_GRAVITY_SCALE`.
- **It burns players**: `FLAME_DPS` to anyone within `FLAME_RADIUS`, for as long as they
  are inside it, **including whoever lit it** once the owner grace has passed.
- **It burns terrain**: a `FLAME_SCORCH_R` bite every `FLAME_SCORCH_EVERY`.

`FLAME_MAX_LIVE` caps the field globally, oldest first. The flame carries who lit it so a
burn kill is attributed (§A20).

## Notes

- Damage goes through the same `apply_damage_log` path as everything else. A flame that
  subtracts health directly is the one damage source that skips the warmup gate (§E13's
  lesson, one milestone old).
- **Continuous, not per-contact.** `FLAME_DPS × dt` per tick per overlapping player, and
  two overlapping flames damage twice — that is what makes a crowd of them dangerous and
  it is a decision, so assert it rather than discovering it.
- Do not write a second flight loop. `projectile.rs` already bounces (grenades) and
  already rests (`GRENADE_REST_SPEED`); a flame is those rules with a different end
  condition. **Share the function** (`CLAUDE.md`).
- The scorch is on a **timer per flame**, not per tick: a flame that carves every tick
  would eat a crater in a second and this is meant to be a slow burn. It scorches while
  resting on ground; a flame in mid-air scorches nothing.
- `game-core` is pure — the jitter is seeded `ChaCha8Rng`, and 160 live flames must not
  make the state hash order-dependent.
- Bandwidth: flames ride the existing per-projectile broadcast at `SNAPSHOT_HZ`. Measure
  bytes/s with `FLAME_MAX_LIVE` alight and report it. If the cap is the wrong number, say
  so with the measurement.

## Tests

- A flame lives exactly `FLAME_LIFE` and then despawns — pinned to the constant, with a
  control one tick earlier.
- A player standing in one loses `FLAME_DPS × FLAME_LIFE` over its life. Control: a
  player `FLAME_RADIUS + 1` away loses nothing.
- Two overlapping flames deal double. The control is one flame in the same place.
- The **owner** is burned by their own flame once the grace has passed, and not before.
- A flame thrown at a wall bounces and comes to rest rather than vanishing.
- A resting flame carves `FLAME_SCORCH_R` every `FLAME_SCORCH_EVERY` and no more; a
  flame in the air carves nothing.
- Spawning `FLAME_MAX_LIVE + 10` leaves exactly `FLAME_MAX_LIVE`, and the ten that went
  are the **oldest**.
- Attribution: a burn kill credits the player who lit it, not the map.
- Determinism: same seed, same 600 ticks with flames alight, same state hash, twenty runs.
