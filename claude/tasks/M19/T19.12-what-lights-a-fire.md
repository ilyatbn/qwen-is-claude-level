## Deliverable

Everything that made fire now makes flames:

- **Flamethrower**: `FLAMETHROWER_FLAMES_PER_SHOT` per shot at `FLAME_MUZZLE_SPEED` with
  `FLAME_SPREAD` jitter, on its existing cooldown and ammo. `Delivery::Cone` and
  `cone.rs` are **deleted**, with their three private trail constants.
- **Molotov**: bursts into `MOLOTOV_FLAMES` at `MOLOTOV_FLAME_SPEED`, outward and up from
  the impact. `Burst::Zone { Fire }` is retired.
- **Lava vent afterburn**: `LAVA_FLAMES_PER_SECOND` for `LAVA_BURN_DURATION`. The
  telegraph and the jet are unchanged.
- `BurnKind::Fire` is retired. `BurnField` survives holding **toxic zones only** — the
  toxic grenade is deliberately not a flame.

## Notes

- Three emitters, one spawn function. A second "spawn a fan of flames" written for the
  molotov is the §A24 mistake this codebase has paid for twice, and `burn.rs`'s own header
  says so about the system being replaced.
- The flamethrower is **not** `auto` (§F3): its cooldown is 0.05 s and a per-press weapon
  at 20 presses/s is what it already was. Do not give it a hold-repeat here; if it should
  have one, that is a report, not a decision to take mid-task.
- **Reach is now emergent** — speed × life, not `FLAMETHROWER_RANGE`. Measure the actual
  reach in px and report it against the retired 150. If it is wildly different the weapon
  has been rebalanced by accident, which is a finding.
- Re-run T11.09's balance harness. The flamethrower and molotov both change delivery; a
  damage share that moves is expected and unreported is not.
- Grep the **production** callers before deleting: `bots/mod.rs` lights burn patches
  directly in two places, and `thrown.rs` and `delivery.rs` assert on `BurnKind::Fire`.
- The client will break on the retired events. That is T19.13's half — leave it compiling,
  not working, and say so.
### What a pre-flight sweep found waiting for you

- **`crates/game-core/src/bots/mod.rs:657` `hazard_at()` reads `world.burn.patches()`.**
  Take fire out of `BurnField` and **bots silently stop avoiding fire** while still
  avoiding toxic. No test names this behaviour, so nothing will go red. It is the exact
  shape of "a mechanism wired to nothing" — point it at the flame field and give it a test.
- **`scripts/checks/ordnance.mjs:348` asserts `d.jets > 0`** — a cone-only counter — and
  `:364` asserts a **drawn hazard** from a molotov. Both die with `Delivery::Cone` and
  `Burst::Zone { Fire }`. They belong to T19.13's repair; name them there so they are not
  found by the gate.
- **Retiring `LAVA_BURN_RADIUS` breaks the typecheck in three client places**:
  `client/src/render/lightmap-math.ts:85` types hazards as `'lava'|'burn'|'meteor'`,
  `SandboxScene.ts:1091` draws `C().LAVA_BURN_RADIUS`, and `client/src/core/index.ts:220`
  declares it. Retire across both languages in one go.
- `crates/game-core/tests/thrown.rs` asserts `BurnKind::Fire` and `MOLOTOV_PATCHES`;
  `tests/delivery.rs` constructs a `BurnField` directly. Both are yours to rewrite.

## Tests

- One flamethrower shot spawns exactly `FLAMETHROWER_FLAMES_PER_SHOT` flames, inside
  `FLAME_SPREAD` of the aim angle, at `FLAME_MUZZLE_SPEED`.
- A molotov landing on ground spawns exactly `MOLOTOV_FLAMES`, and they end up **spread
  along the ground** rather than stacked at the impact point — assert the x-spread against
  a control of one flame.
- A molotov that lands on a player still bursts (it is a contact weapon), and the flames
  burn them.
- A lava vent's afterburn emits `LAVA_FLAMES_PER_SECOND × LAVA_BURN_DURATION` flames and
  none outside its window. Control: the jet phase emits none.
- A toxic grenade still leaves a toxic zone and no flames.
- `grep` proves `Delivery::Cone`, `cone.rs`, `BurnKind::Fire` and the retired constants
  are gone in both languages.
- The flamethrower's measured reach is asserted against `FLAME_MUZZLE_SPEED × FLAME_LIFE`
  as an upper bound, so a future speed change cannot silently double it.
