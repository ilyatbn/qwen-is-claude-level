# T19.26 — `reap.rs`'s control arm races its own socket (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small — but read the family below first

**Found during T19.24's gate run.** `reap.rs::a_socket_that_drops_before_it_is_seated_does_not_burn_the_room`
panicked inside a full suite:

```
emit: IncompleteResponseFromEngineIo(WebsocketError(AlreadyClosed))   reap.rs:393
```

**3/3 standalone on an idle box.** Fails under a loaded gate.

## Where it is, and why that matters

`:393` is the **control arm** — a `.expect("emit")` on a freshly connected socket, in the half
of the test that proves a *normal* join still works so the defect arm means something. So the
failure is not in the behaviour under test; it is the control losing a race with its own
connection. `docs/70` §A28's window: an emit issued before the socket is ready to carry it.

**Ruled out, by the finder rather than assumed:** `reap.rs` spreads `Config::default()`, so
`weather_mode` is `Auto` and none of T19.24's `WeatherMode::Always` path runs; `effect_seed`
yields the value `force_effect` already computed. Nothing in that change can reach this test.

## This is the fourth member of one family, and that is the point

Four checks now fail this way — **an assertion that races the state it names, wins on an idle
box, loses under a full gate**:

- `rematch`'s premise — read `debug().players` after waiting on `debug().ready`, two streams,
  nothing ordering them. **Fixed** by waiting for the thing actually asserted on.
- `rematch`'s closing window — two browsers rendering at once (**T19.25**).
- `night-combat` — ten bare sleeps against three condition polls (**T19.22**).
- this one — an emit against a socket that may not be open yet.

**Fix the cause, do not widen a timeout.** The `rematch` premise fix is the shape to copy: it
did not sleep longer, it waited on the exact condition the next line asserts, and it was
falsified by making that condition unreachable so the wait could not mask a real failure.
`rooms.rs::emit_until` already exists for precisely this window and this file never used it.

## Tests

- The test passes **5 consecutive times inside a full suite**, not standalone. Standalone is the
  condition under which it already passes.
- **The control arm still proves what it was there to prove** — a normal join succeeding. A
  control that was made robust by asserting less is not a control.
- Falsify: break the seating path and the defect arm must still fail. A retry that swallows a
  genuine refusal has replaced one race with a blind spot.
