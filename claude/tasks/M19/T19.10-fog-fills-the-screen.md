## Deliverable

A grey screen-space veil, drawn over the world and under the HUD:

- Full-screen fill of `FOG_SCREEN_COLOUR` at `FOG_SCREEN_ALPHA × strength(now)`.
- Scroll factor 0 — it is a veil on the camera, not an object in the world.
- Above terrain, players, ordnance, sky and weather particles; **below** the HUD, the
  death overlay and the escape menu.
- The FoV multiplier stays. At night the two compound.

## Notes

- The simulation has been right since M5 and nothing carried the number to the screen —
  the same shape `weather-visible`'s own header describes for `fogMult`. The fix is a
  drawing, and the acceptance is therefore pixels or it is nothing.
- `strength()` already ramps over `FOG_RAMP`; do not re-implement a fade. The client
  computes it locally from the effect's start time, which is what `fog.rs`'s header says
  it is for.
- Depth: `weather.ts` already documents that two layers created at the same depth order by
  creation. Pick the depth deliberately and say why in a comment.
- 0.8 is a heavy veil by design (80 % of visibility gone). If it makes the game
  unplayable rather than merely hard, that is a finding with a measured screenshot, not a
  number to quietly halve.

## Tests

- Unit: the veil's alpha is `FOG_SCREEN_ALPHA × strength`, sampled across the ramp and
  pinned to the constants; zero before the effect starts and after it ends.
- **Pixel acceptance** (extends `weather-visible`): the same seed and the same camera,
  one frame at full fog and a control frame before it starts. Mean luminance moves toward
  the grey by the alpha the constant names, in a world patch **and** in a sky patch.
- Pixel control: a HUD patch is unchanged between the two frames — the veil is under it.
- A deliberate `FOG_SCREEN_ALPHA = 0` fails the acceptance. Falsify at the live binding
  site, not at a default.
