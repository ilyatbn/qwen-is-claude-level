# T19.28 — `e2e.mjs` reports a killed check as a failed one (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: **one line, plus the test that proves it**

**Found during T19.22/25/26's five-gate loop**, by a builder who had to diagnose its own
`FAILED (151.5s)` on `fog-visible` and prove it was a signal rather than a defect.

## The defect

`scripts/e2e.mjs`:

```js
p.on('exit', (c) => res(c ?? 1))
```

A child **killed by a signal** delivers `code === null`, and `?? 1` turns that into exit 1 —
**identical to a genuine failure**. So a check that was SIGKILLed mid-teardown is reported the
same way as a check whose assertions failed.

The evidence that it was a signal, and why nobody should have to reconstruct this again:

- the check printed its own `fog-visible ok`, which it reaches **only** when
  `failures.length === 0`;
- no stack trace and no `FAIL:` line, with `stdio: 'inherit'` so either would have shown.

**This is the recurring shape in the runner itself**: a claim reported through something other
than the thing it claims. `1` stands in for "this check failed" when what happened was "this
check was killed", and the two want opposite responses — one is a bug, the other is a re-run.

## Deliverable

```js
p.on('exit', (c, sig) => ...)
```

Report a signalled child **distinguishably** — as its own outcome, not as a failure and not as
a pass. It is neither: the check made no claim.

Two things to get right:

- **A signalled check must not go green.** The gate's exit code should still be non-zero, or a
  killed suite reads as a passing one, which is worse than what it does today.
- **Name the signal in the output.** `killed by SIGKILL` is what turns a twenty-minute
  diagnosis into a one-line read, which is the whole value here.

## Tests

- Spawn a child that prints its `ok` line and is then killed with `SIGKILL`; assert the runner
  reports it as signalled and **not** as a failure.
- **Control**: a child that genuinely exits 1 is still reported as a failure. Without it, a
  change that reports everything as "signalled" passes.
- The gate's overall exit stays non-zero in both cases.

## Done when

```sh
node scripts/e2e.mjs --help >/dev/null && ./scripts/check.sh
```
