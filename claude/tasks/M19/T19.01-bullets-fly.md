## Deliverable

A new delivery, and the five ballistic guns move to it:

```rust
Delivery::Bullet { spread: f32, auto: bool }
```

- Flies **straight**: `gravity_scale` and `wind_scale` are 0.0, enforced in the step,
  not merely set in the table.
- Speed is the def's `muzzle_speed` (five new constants), lifetime is its `range` — a
  bullet that has flown `range` px despawns with no blast.
- Stops at the first terrain pixel or player body, through the **existing** sub-stepped
  loop in `projectile.rs`. Damage, carve (`blast_radius`), shields and attribution go
  through `detonate` unchanged.
- Pistol, revolver, deagle, machinegun, SMG become `Bullet`. `auto: true` for machinegun
  and SMG only.
- `Delivery::Hitscan` **stays** and keeps the two laser weapons. Nothing else.

## Notes

- `Delivery` is matched exhaustively in several places (`projectile.rs`, `world/mod.rs`
  `fire_from_slot`, `bots/mod.rs`, `game-wasm`). Adding a variant is a compile error at
  each — that is the design (`defs.rs` says so) and each site is a decision, not a
  `_ =>`.
- The muzzle offset and owner grace already exist for projectiles; a bullet spawned
  inside the shooter's own AABB and killed by it is the failure mode. Check
  `PROJECTILE_OWNER_GRACE_TICKS` is enough at 1050 px/s — one tick is 17 px.
- Ammo is spent at fire, not at impact. `spends_stack()` already answers this; do not
  add a branch.
- `SMG_SHOTS`, `SMG_GRAVITY_SCALE` and `SMG_WIND_SCALE` are retired (§F10). Delete them;
  do not leave constants nothing reads.
- Do **not** touch the client in this task. T19.02 is the picture.

## Tests

- A bullet fired horizontally over a chasm has the **same y** after 30 ticks — pinned to
  the constant, not to a literal. Control: a bazooka rocket over the same gap drops.
- It arrives: time-to-target over a known distance matches `distance / muzzle_speed`
  within a tick.
- It stops at a wall, carves `blast_radius`, and the pixels behind the wall are
  untouched. Control: the same shot with the wall removed reaches the far side.
- It stops on a player and deals `damage` once — not once per sub-step.
- It despawns at `range` with **no** carve and no damage: assert the mask is unchanged.
- It cannot tunnel: a 2 px wall at the fastest muzzle speed still stops it.
- Determinism: same seed, same spread draw, twenty times.
- `grep` proves no ballistic weapon is `Hitscan` and both lasers still are.
