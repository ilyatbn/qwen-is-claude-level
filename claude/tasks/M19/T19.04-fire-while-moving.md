# T19.04 — §C20 repealed: you fire while moving (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: ~150 lines, mostly deletions

## Read first
- `docs/75-amendments-v7.md` §F4
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md` §C20 — the design being repealed
- `crates/game-core/src/world/mod.rs` — `moving_under_own_power`, `fire_from_slot`
- `crates/game-core/src/bots/mod.rs` — the two sites that mirror the gate

## Deliverable

Firing, throwing and swinging are allowed **always**. Cooldown and ammo are the only
gates.

- `moving_under_own_power` deleted.
- `UseError::Moving` deleted from the enum, its wire mapping, and every match arm.
- `FIRE_MOVE_MAX_SPEED` deleted from `constants.rs` and from `game-wasm`'s constants
  table.
- The bot mirror in `bots/mod.rs` — two blocks whose comments say they copy the gate
  "term for term" — deleted, along with the stand-still-to-shoot behaviour built on it.

## Notes

- **Delete, do not disable.** A gate behind a `if false` or a config flag is a gate
  somebody re-enables, and this one has been reported three times.
- The client may also be suppressing or annotating a refused shot. Grep for `Moving` in
  `client/src` and remove what is left dangling — an error string nothing can produce is
  the same debt in TypeScript.
- Removing the gate changes bot behaviour and therefore balance. **Re-run T11.09's
  balance harness and report the numbers**, the way §C20 required when it added the rule.
  A shift is expected; hiding it is not.
- Bots fired while walking before §C20 and will again. Check nothing else grew a
  dependency on "a bot standing still is about to shoot".

## Tests

- A player at full run fires successfully; a player mid-jump fires; a player under
  jetpack thrust fires. **These replace** the tests that asserted `Err(UseError::Moving)`
  — an absence needs a presence, and this is the presence.
- Firing at a run still respects the cooldown: two shots inside `PISTOL_COOLDOWN` yield
  one shot.
- A moving player's shot goes where they aimed — the shot's initial velocity does not
  inherit the body's, which would be a new behaviour nobody asked for.
- `grep` proves `UseError::Moving`, `moving_under_own_power` and `FIRE_MOVE_MAX_SPEED`
  appear nowhere, in either language.
- Determinism unchanged: the state hash of a fixed-seed 600-tick run is stable across
  twenty runs.
