# T19.05 — The shovel, and the end of the melee cabinet (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: ~250 lines

## Deliverable

- A **shovel**: a `Melee` weapon that hits and digs, on the six `SHOVEL_*` constants.
- **Every player spawns holding it**, in the first quick-bar slot, on join and on every
  respawn. It has no ammo, is not dropped on death, and cannot leave the inventory.
- **Knife, bat, whip, axe and hammer are retired** — registry, weapon table, spawn/crate/
  buried tables, constants, client art, and the pinned key list.
- The shovel is **not** a ground, crate or buried spawn. Everyone already has one.

## Notes

- **Ids are positional and must not renumber.** `WEAPONS[i].id == WeaponId(i)` and the
  client mirrors the order in `WEAPON_KEYS`; §B16 is the bug where that assumption was
  made silently and a laser resolved as a bazooka. Retire the five ids, give the shovel
  the next free one, and if the table cannot hold holes then keep an explicit retired
  placeholder — the pinned test decides.
- Old replays reference the retired ids. A replay that no longer loads is acceptable; a
  replay that loads and resolves a knife as something else is not. Say which happens.
- The five weapons carried real spawn weight. Removing them re-weights every remaining
  item — **re-run the item-density sweep and report the counts**, before and after.
- Art at both ends through the §E14 path: a world sprite and an inventory tile from one
  sprite key. `itemTextures.ts` is where the procedural fallback lives.
- Digging: the melee carve already works (axe, hammer). Confirm the shovel's carve is
  reachable from a real swing at the aim angle, not only from the unit seam.

## Tests

- A fresh player has exactly one item: the shovel, in slot 0. Control: after death and
  respawn, still exactly one.
- A swing at a wall removes `SHOVEL_CARVE`-sized pixels; a swing at open air removes
  none.
- A swing at an opponent in reach deals `SHOVEL_DAMAGE`; the same opponent one pixel
  beyond `SHOVEL_REACH` takes nothing; an opponent behind a wall takes nothing.
- The shovel is never consumed: swing until well past any `max_stack` and it is still
  there.
- 200-seed sweep: no map spawns a knife, bat, whip, axe, hammer **or** a shovel.
- The pinned `WEAPON_KEYS` test passes, and a deliberate off-by-one in the list fails it.
- **Pixel acceptance**: the shovel's inventory tile draws its art, with the empty tile as
  the control.
