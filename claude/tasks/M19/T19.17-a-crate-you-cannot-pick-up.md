# T19.17 — A crate you cannot pick up (found by T19.05)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: unknown until it is diagnosed

## What was seen

**Booked from a measurement, not a theory.** While re-seeding `crates` after §F5 moved the
`"items"` sub-stream, seed **555** produced a crate the player stood **on** and could not
take. Over the check's full 70 s budget, sampled every 160 ms:

- crate `{id: 11, item: 0, source: "Crate", grounded: true}` at (306, 871.87) — item 0 is
  `MEDKIT`
- player oscillating 281..323 on x, y ≈ 875, **closest approach 0 px** against a
  `PICKUP_RADIUS` of 20
- `heals=0 batteries=0`, so `bump` (`items/world.rs:107-118`) cannot refuse it
- one shovel and one molotov in 24 slots, so `Inventory::add` cannot return `Full`
- alive, 100 health, and **ground pickups working in the same run** — the molotov appeared
  in slot 1 mid-walk, so the pickup path itself is live

By `items/world.rs:341-381` that pickup should have happened. It did not, ~430 times.

## What was ruled out

- **Not the counter cap.** A repair that spent heals and batteries (`Q`/`R`) whenever the
  player was within 40 px was written, run, and **reverted**: it changed nothing, and
  CLAUDE.md says revert what you cannot explain. `heals=0` was then confirmed directly.
- **Not the inventory being full**, not the item being locked (`pickup_locked_until` is 0
  for a crate), not the player being dead.
- **Not a load flake.** It reproduced on an idle box, alone, with identical coordinates.

## Where to start

Nothing in `debug()` exposes the **server's** copy of the local player, so the 0 px is the
client's number and only one end of the comparison. `resolve_pickups`
(`world/mod.rs:2062`) measures from `p.body.pos`. Print both, for one run, before
theorising further — a distance asserted from one end is the shape §A39 is about.

Reproduce with:

```sh
CRATE_SEED=555 node scripts/e2e.mjs crates
```

**Do not re-seed `crates` onto 555** while this stands; it is on 31337 for the reason
recorded in its own header.

## Done when

The refusal is explained. Either a fix with a test that fails without it, or a written
finding that says why the pickup is correct to refuse — which would make the *check* the
thing to change.
