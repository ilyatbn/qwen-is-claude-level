# T19.29 — `teleport`'s pixel sample arrives on a schedule the renderer does not keep (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small · **Read the family note before starting**

**Found in gate 4 of T19.22/25/26's five-gate loop.** Passed gates 1–3, failed gate 4:

```
the pad's pixels changed by only 4.2
```

**Its simulation half was entirely correct in the failing run** — charge climbed 0 → 98 %, the
pad fired, the player moved 1192 px and arrived at a different pad. Only the pixel assertion
missed. So this is not a teleport defect; it is a sampler arriving when the renderer has not
caught up.

## The family, and why this is the fifth

Five checks now fail the same way: **an assertion that races the state it names — right on an
idle box, wrong under a full gate.**

| | status |
|---|---|
| `rematch`'s premise — read `players` after waiting on `ready` | **fixed** (wait on the asserted condition) |

**The three that are fixed share one remedy and it is the one to copy: do not sleep longer —
wait on the exact condition the next line asserts, and falsify by making that condition
unreachable so the wait cannot mask a real failure.**

**T19.23 is the exception and this task may be one too.** `bullets-visible` cannot wait for its
subject, because the subject is a round that has already gone — its timing is inherent to the
measurement. **Decide which kind this is before writing anything**: if the pad's redraw is
observable, wait for it; if the sample is inherently racing a transient, it belongs with T19.23
and wants that shape of answer rather than a wait.

## Deliverable

Establish first, and say which: **is there a condition that means "the pad has redrawn"?** A
frame counter, a render-state flag, a digest that changes — anything the page can be asked
about. If yes, wait for it and this is a ten-line fix.

If there is nothing to wait for, **do not add a sleep**. Report that, and book it with T19.23
as the second inherent-timing check, so the two get one answer instead of two guesses.

## Tests

- Passes 5 consecutive times **inside a full suite**, not standalone. Standalone is the
  condition under which it already passes.
- Falsify: stop the pad redrawing and the check must still go red.
