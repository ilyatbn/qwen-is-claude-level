## Deliverable

§E13's design, with numbers that reach a player:

- **A drop splashes.** Everyone within `TOXIC_SPLASH_R` of where it lands is poisoned,
  not only the body the projectile point intersected. The roof rule is unchanged and
  applies per victim, not per drop.
- `TOXIC_DROP_EVERY` 0.4 → 0.15.
- `TOXIC_POISON_DPS` 2.0 → 6.0.

## Notes

- Nothing about the mechanism changes: drop, projectile, roof check, reset-not-stack,
  green bar, damage through `apply_damage_log`. This is the arithmetic.
- The arithmetic that made it dead: one drop per 0.4 s, uniform across a 1536 px map,
  against a 20 px-wide player — a full 8 s shower had a small chance of a single hit for
  6 total damage. **Compute the expected hits per shower before and after and put both
  numbers in the journal.** A change of this kind without its own before-number is a
  guess.
- 53 drops a shower is still not a load concern, but it is 2.6× the projectiles. Check
  the projectile cap and the snapshot cost at `SNAPSHOT_HZ` rather than assuming.
- The splash is a radius test around the landing point, and the *existing* blast helper
  already does radius tests with the roof/LOS shape. **Share the function** rather than
  writing a second one.

## Tests

- A player standing under open sky for a full `TOXIC_DURATION` shower **loses health** —
  driven by the real scheduler on a fixed seed, not by a hand-placed drop. Control: a
  player under solid rock in the same run loses none.
- A drop landing `TOXIC_SPLASH_R − 1` from a player poisons them; at `+1` it does not.
- One hit costs `TOXIC_POISON_DPS × TOXIC_POISON_DURATION`, pinned at both ends.
- Re-hit still resets rather than stacks — assert the total.
- Drops per shower equal `TOXIC_DURATION / TOXIC_DROP_EVERY`, pinned.
- **Pixel acceptance**: the health bar is green during the shower and not green before.
