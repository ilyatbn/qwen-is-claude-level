# T19.13 — Flames on screen (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: T19.12 · Size: ~200 lines

## Deliverable

- A `flame` `ProjectileKind`, drawn per object: a flickering warm blob, additive, with a
  short trail while it is moving and none once it rests.
- Each live flame contributes a light through the existing hazard path, so a fire lights
  the ground around it at night.
- The burning-ground disc draw and its `LavaBurn`-style effect strings go, along with
  whatever in `ordnanceFx` keyed off them.
- **A new e2e check, `fire-visible`**, standalone, on a real server: throw a molotov, and
  the frame after impact holds **many separate bright sources spread along the ground** —
  counted — against a control frame from before the throw.

## Notes

- Counting sources, not brightness, is the assertion that distinguishes this from the
  design it replaces: one disc is bright too. Cluster the lit pixels and count clusters,
  and say in the header why a single-number brightness check would have passed for the
  old behaviour.
- The flicker must be **client-local and deterministic per flame id** — a random flicker
  re-rolled each frame makes every pixel check a coin flip, and a gate that fails on a
  coin flip gates nothing (`CLAUDE.md`).
- `FLAME_MAX_LIVE` flames each with a light is the worst case for the lightmap. Run
  `perf` with a full field and report the frame time; if it does not hold 60 fps that is a
  number for the journal, not a silent cap change.
- A flame at rest still burns. It must not fade out as it settles, or the picture will say
  the fire is over while the damage continues — the exact class of divergence M19 exists
  to fix.

## Tests

- Unit: a flame's look is the flame look; a resting flame draws no trail; the flicker for
  a given id and time is the same value twice.
- Unit: `KIND_BY_WEAPON_KEY` resolves the flame weapon key, and the pinned registry test
  still passes.
- e2e `fire-visible`: cluster count after a molotov is ≥ half `MOLOTOV_FLAMES`; the
  control frame has none; a control patch away from the impact is unchanged.
- e2e: the lit region is **wider** than a single flame's radius — the spread is the point.
- `perf` with a full flame field reports its frame time.
