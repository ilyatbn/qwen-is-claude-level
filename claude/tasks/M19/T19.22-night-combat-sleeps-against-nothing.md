# T19.22 — `night-combat` waits against nothing, seven times (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small

Split out of the retired suite-context hypothesis. **Do not treat this and T19.23 as one
problem** — they share a symptom, not a cause.

## What was seen
`night-combat` goes red inside a full suite and green standalone. Profiled: **7 bare
`waitForTimeout` calls (500, 400, 200, 60, 1000, 400, 400 ms) against 2 condition polls.**
Bare sleeps against nothing are the undiluted form of "a short wall-clock window that a busy
machine can miss".

> **CENSUS REFRESHED 2026-09-06 — the shape holds, the numbers do not.** At HEAD the file is
> 186 lines with **10 `waitForTimeout` against 3 `waitForFunction`**, not 7 against 2. Recount
> before you start rather than quoting the numbers above; they date from M19 and the file has
> been edited since. The conclusion is unchanged and if anything stronger — bare sleeps still
> outnumber condition polls better than three to one.

## What is ruled out
Not a shared cause with the other three ex-family members: `hud-timer` was a broken metric,
`m10-checkpoint` was continuous weather carves plus a read-order artefact, and
`bullets-visible` has almost no waits at all (T19.23).

## Deliverable
Replace the seven fixed sleeps with **condition polls on the effect each one is waiting
for**. Either the reds stop — in which case the sleeps were the cause — or they do not, and
the sleeps are exonerated, which is worth knowing. Both outcomes are a result; say which.
