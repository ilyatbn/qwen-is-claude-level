# T19.09 — The teleport charge is 1.5 seconds (v7)

Milestone: M19 · Depends on: nothing · Size: one constant, and the fixtures that pin it

## Read first
- `docs/75-amendments-v7.md` §F8
- `crates/game-core/src/world/teleport.rs`

## Deliverable

`TELEPORT_CHARGE` 2.0 → 1.5. `TELEPORT_COOLDOWN`, `TELEPORT_PADS` and
`TELEPORT_ARM_DISTANCE` are unchanged.

## Notes

- This is a one-line change with a fixture problem attached: **a wait hardcoded against a
  tunable is a test that expires** (`CLAUDE.md`), and the pad ring's client animation,
  the `teleport` e2e check and any `sleep` around it are the places that will have
  hardcoded 2 s. Grep for the number in both languages, including the e2e scripts.
- The charge ring is drawn from the same constant. If the client has its own copy, that
  copy is the bug, not the animation.

## Tests

- A player standing on a pad teleports after `TELEPORT_CHARGE` and not before —
  pinned to the constant at both ends.
- Stepping off before the charge completes cancels it and the charge resets.
- The e2e `teleport` check's waits are expressed in terms of the constant, not a literal.

## Done when

```sh
cargo test -p game-core --lib teleport && node scripts/e2e.mjs teleport
```
