## Deliverable

Two-client checks fail intermittently at **both** the pre-bullet commit and HEAD, in the
same mode, with nothing to do with any M19 change:

- **`in battle (phase warmup, 0 players)`** — the client reads the roster while it is
  still empty. A join that has not arrived is being read as a join that returned nothing.
- **`vite did not report a port within 90 s`** under sustained browser load.

Find the actual cause of the first one and fix it. It is a **race, not a slow path**, so
the repair is a wait on the condition — a roster that has the players in it — and not a
larger number. `CLAUDE.md`: a wait hardcoded against a tunable is a test that expires.

The second may be the box rather than the code. Measure it before deciding: if vite is
genuinely slower under load, the check needs to stop competing with the suite, not get a
longer deadline.

## Notes

  flip through at least three sessions and has twice been blamed on the feature under

- Interleave anything you measure across commits (D-65). A block of A then a block of B

- One open number to close while you are here: interleaved HEAD runs produced two

## Tests

- The repaired check passes **10 consecutive runs**. Not 9. A flake that survives at 1 in
  10 is the same flake.
- The roster wait fails **loudly** when the roster genuinely never fills — a wait that
  times out silently into "0 players" is the bug being fixed, wearing a different hat.
- Whatever you key the wait to is asserted against the constant that governs it, not a
  literal.
