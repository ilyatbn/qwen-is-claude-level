# T21.05 — Low gravity (v9)

Milestone: M21 · Depends on: **T21.04** (the settings pattern), interacts with **T20.11** ·
Size: ~200 lines

Asked for: *"gravity: standard, low, none. Low gravity mode makes everything a bit slower
(including projectiles), it also makes you jump higher and drop slower. Fall damage is much
lower here."*

**This task is the `standard | low` half only.** Zero-g is T21.06 and T21.07 — it is not a
third value of one setting, it is a different game, and pretending otherwise is how it lands
half-built.

## Read first
- `crates/game-core/src/constants.rs:92-93` — `GRAVITY`, `MAX_FALL_SPEED`
- `crates/game-core/src/physics/resolve.rs:178-196` — `apply_gravity`
- `crates/game-core/src/weapons/projectile.rs` — `gravity_scale` per projectile
- `crates/game-core/tests/balance.rs` — **everything in it moves**

## ⚠ "Everything a bit slower, including projectiles" is not one number

`GRAVITY` is a constant read in several places, and projectiles already carry their own
`gravity_scale` (bullets are 0.0 — §F1 makes them fly flat). So:

- A **global** gravity multiplier changes jumps, falls and lobbed weapons, and does **nothing**
  to bullets, whose scale is zero. That is probably right — a bullet is not slowed by less
  gravity — but *"everything a bit slower including projectiles"* reads as though it should be.
- **"Slower" and "less gravity" are different axes.** Less gravity makes a lobbed grenade
  travel *further*, not slower. If the brief wants the whole match to feel slower, that is a
  time-scale change, which is a much larger and more dangerous thing than a gravity constant.

**The code answers this, so no ruling is owed.** Gravity *magnitude* is **not injectable** —
`apply_gravity` reads the `GRAVITY` constant directly and only the **scale** is a parameter.
So:

- **A gravity scale** is tractable: five `integrate` call sites (one non-literal) plus
  `projectile.rs`'s `gravity_scale`. No constant moves, no compile-time assert fires, the
  traversal consts stay put, **and golden hashes do not move.**
- **Lowering the `GRAVITY` constant** changes `JUMP_HEIGHT`/`JUMP_REACH` at compile time,
  which changes the map generator's verdict — see below — and trips a const assert at zero.
- **A time scale** would need `dt` threaded through every system and would change the sim rate
  itself. Nothing in the tree is shaped for it.

**So "everything slower" can only mean a gravity scale.** Lobbed things arc further and hang
longer, which reads as slower without touching a clock. Build that.

## Deliverable

- A `standard | low` setting on T21.04's path — bound, four-place exposure, replay tag, gate,
  panel row, and **in the header**, since it is simulation state that must survive a restart.
- One named multiplier in `constants.rs`. Not a second `GRAVITY`.
- **Fall damage "much lower"** — and note it is *already* lower for free, because impact speed
  falls out of gravity: `v = √(2gh)`, so half gravity is ×0.71 impact at the same height. Ask
  whether an additional multiplier is wanted **on top** of that, or whether the free reduction
  is the feature. If an extra one is wanted it is a second constant, and T20.11 owns the
  formula it multiplies.

## Why the constant is off limits, stated once

`JUMP_HEIGHT` and `JUMP_REACH` are **compile-time consts** derived from `JUMP_VELOCITY`,
`WALK_SPEED` and `GRAVITY`, and they drive the generator's traversability check. Changing the
constant therefore changes **which maps the generator accepts** — see T21.07 for the exact
mechanism. A runtime scale leaves them alone, so the generator keeps standard-gravity
reachability while the player moves under low gravity. **That asymmetry is the trade, and it
is the right one:** the alternative regenerates every map in the game.

## Two copies of the projectile gravity term — a scale applied to one misses every live shot

`projectile.rs::integrate` is the extracted function (used by two call sites and the tests),
but **the hot loop inlines `p.vel.y += GRAVITY * w.gravity_scale * dt;` and bypasses it.** A
gravity scale applied only to the extracted version would silently miss **every projectile in
flight**. That is the same-expression-in-two-places shape, with the second copy already
written.

## Notes

- **Determinism.** `GRAVITY` is read inside the pure simulation, so a per-room multiplier must
  reach `apply_input`'s path identically on client and server, or prediction desyncs — the same
  problem T21.02 and T21.03 have, with the same answer.
- **Every balance number moves.** `balance.rs:874 the_shipping_configuration_produces_a_fight`
  and the whole balance harness measure a fight under standard gravity. Bots jetpack when a
  target is `JETPACK_RISE` above them and then fall; halve gravity and their reach, their
  self-damage and their encounter rate all change. **Measure on 8 seeds and report**, the way
  §C20's repeal did.
- Interacts with **T21.02** (×3 jump height on top of low gravity) and **T21.03** (constant
  flight makes gravity irrelevant). State the combinations; do not discover them.

## Tests at risk

- `traversal.rs`'s `"220 px is beyond jump reach {JUMP_REACH}"` assertion.
- `movement.rs`'s air-control tests and `jetpack.rs`'s gravity-scale tests.
- `crates/game-core/tests/golden_hashes.txt` — **only** if you touch the constant, which this
  task forbids.

## Tests

- Jump height and fall speed scale with the multiplier, pinned to it, with a standard-gravity
  control.
- A lobbed weapon's range changes and a **bullet's does not** — the `gravity_scale == 0.0`
  boundary asserted on both sides.
- Fall damage is lower, and the test says whether that is the free reduction or an extra
  multiplier.
- Prediction agrees with the server under low gravity.
- The setting survives a round restart.
