# T23.34 — The ice world: a third look with its own cold (parking lot)

Parked by the owner, 2026-10-02 · **After T23.31** (the look registry) and **T23.33** (a look choosing its disasters,
`docs/78` §A8) · References: `mapideas/ice_biome/ice_biome_1.png` (the world), `mapideas/ice_biome/extreme_ice.jpg`
(the extreme frost — diamond dust)

> *"parking lot, ice "biome" (like volcanic and normal)*
> *- map is semi transparent ice. background is an ice world.*
> *- disasters: hale storm (like meteor shower but ice shards), extreme frost (patch of extreme cold moving through the
>   map randomly, penetrates shields. looks like diamond dust)*
> *- frost: like radiation in space, healthbar goes blue, you lose health due to cold if no shield. happens during the
>   "night phase". if you can make the camera (edges of the players view) appear like they are frosted, could be cool.*
> *- flamethrower becomes ice-flame thrower. (basically make anything fire related like particles of ice like the
>   extreme cold)"*

## Do
1. **`WorldLook::Ice`** in the open list (T23.31), picked at random per map with the others.
2. **Look**: terrain as **semi-transparent ice** — translucent blue, light passing through edges, frosted/snow caps
   on top surfaces, icicles under overhangs (render-only; collision unchanged). Background: an ice world — towering
   ice spires, snowy mountain ranges, valley mist, a huge pale planet and a small moon in a pale blue sky
   (`ice_biome_1.png`); night end of the blend a deep cold blue.
3. **Hail storm** (replaces meteor shower on ice): meteor mechanics with **ice shards** — falling crystalline shards
   (decide at the code whether they carve like meteors or shatter on impact; state it).
4. **Extreme frost** (replaces heavy fog on ice): a patch of extreme cold drifting randomly across the map, damaging
   whoever is inside **through shields**; drawn as sparkling **diamond dust** (`extreme_ice.jpg`).
5. **Frost at night**: during the night phase, cold works like space radiation — unshielded players lose health over
   time, the health bar turns **blue** while freezing, and the screen edges frost over (a vignette of frost growing
   in from the view's edges).
6. **Ice-flamethrower**: on ice maps everything fire-related renders as ice — the flamethrower's jet becomes freezing
   particles like the extreme frost, burning patches become frost patches. Decide at the code whether this is purely
   visual (same burn mechanics, ice look) or a different status; the owner's words suggest the look.
## Tests
Per §A8: the scheduler rolls hail/extreme frost on ice and never meteors/fog; frost damage only at night and only
unshielded (control: shielded); extreme frost damages a shielded player (control: outside the patch); pixels for the
ice terrain, the frosted edges and the ice flame (layer-hidden controls). GPU screenshots of each.
