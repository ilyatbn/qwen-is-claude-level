# T21.41 — Rewrite toxic rain (parked)

Parked by the owner on 2026-09-15: *"Disable toxic rain completely for now its not working properly.
Add a parking lot item to rewrite it."* T21.39 switches it off; this is the rewrite.

## What was wrong, as reported and measured

- *"ive never seen any actual damage from toxic rains"* (T21.25): damage was real — ~76 hp to a player
  in the open in `toxic-rain-game` — but a player could not tell they were being poisoned.
- *"it literally rains from the whole screen when the clouds are below me"* (T21.31): the picture was
  a screen-space sheet. T21.31 redrew it as one streak per real server drop plus a green cloud deck
  at `SKY_MARGIN`, but open issues remained: the green screen tint shows inside caves, and a green
  drop can pass in front of a lower white cloud.
- The owner's verdict after playing T21.31's version: not working properly.

## Before designing

- Play it with the owner, or read his next report — "not working properly" is not yet a specification.
- Inventory everything toxic rain touches: `effects/toxic.rs`, the scheduler weight, the poison status
  (`TOXIC_POISON_*`, `PlayerState::poisoned`), the HUD poison tint (`bars-math.ts::healthBar`), the
  cloud deck and streaks in `weather.ts`, and the checks T21.39 disabled.
- Decide one source of truth for **where** it rains, shared by damage and picture, so they cannot
  disagree (T20.05's and T21.25's lesson).
