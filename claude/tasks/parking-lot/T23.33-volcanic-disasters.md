# T23.33 — Volcanic disasters: ash clouds and lava bursts (parking lot)

Parked by the owner, 2026-10-02 · **After T23.31** (the volcanic world must exist) · Spec: `docs/78` §A8

> *"parking lot, volcanic map disasters:*
> *- "heavy fog" is off. should be a new disaster called "volcanic ash" instead which spreads for a few seconds like a
>   passing dark cloud that makes it almost impossible to see, but it doesnt fill the whole screen, more like a passing
>   cloud.*
> *- "meteor shower" is off. instead there's a "lava burst" - same exact mechanic like meteor shower but make it lava
>   rocks (grey rocks with a red glow inside/outside) from the sky instead. lava rocks dont explode, they hit the ground
>   and spread fire around them.*
> *- if possible to animate the background volcano to burst with smoke/lava and then make it shake for a couple
>   seconds before it hits it would be even cooler."*

## Do
1. **The look picks the hazard set** (server side: the look is chosen from the seed there — `docs/78` §A8). On a
   Volcanic map the effect scheduler never rolls heavy fog or meteor showers; it rolls volcanic ash and lava bursts.
   Classic unchanged. (Map shapes still apply: islands have no sky-fall disaster at all; multilevel lava bursts hit the
   top level only, like meteors.)
2. **Volcanic ash** — a dark, drifting cloud a few seconds across the map: a bounded moving volume (not the whole
   screen) inside which vision drops to almost nothing (the vision/FoV rule, so it is fair: hidden players stay hidden
   — `docs/14` §5 and the seeing rule from T23.10B). Telegraphed like other effects; drawn as dense dark ash with
   embers, in F's style.
3. **Lava burst** — meteor shower's mechanic (telegraph, spawn band, fall, timing) reused, but each rock is a lava
   rock (grey with a red glow inside/out, a light source while falling) that **does not explode or carve**: on impact
   it spreads **fire** around it (the existing fire/burn hazard — molotov-like patch, sized from constants). Bots dodge
   them like meteors (T23.26C's dodge).
4. **The volcano warns** (cosmetic, client): during the telegraph the background volcano rumbles — the camera shakes
   gently for a couple of seconds, then the volcano erupts smoke and lava in the background just before the first
   rocks fall.
## Tests
Scheduler: over many seeds a Volcanic map never schedules fog/meteors and does schedule ash/bursts; Classic unchanged
(golden). Lava rock: no carve, a fire patch at impact, damage by burn. Ash: a player inside is not rendered to an
outside viewer and sees almost nothing (pixels, with a control outside the cloud). GPU screenshots of an ash cloud
passing and of a burst with the volcano erupting.
