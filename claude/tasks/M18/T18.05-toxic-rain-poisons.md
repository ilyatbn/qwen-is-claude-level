# T18.05 — Toxic rain poisons on hit (v6)

Milestone: M18 · Depends on: nothing · Size: ~250 lines

## Read first
- `docs/74-amendments-v6.md` §E13, §E15
- `crates/game-core/src/effects/meteor.rs` (the shape this copies)

## Deliverable

**Puddles are removed.** A toxic drop is a projectile, and what it hits it poisons.

- Hitting a player: poison for `TOXIC_POISON_DURATION` at `TOXIC_POISON_DPS`. A second
  hit **resets the timer**; it does not stack.
- **A roof protects you** — a drop must not reach a player with solid terrain above.
  The meteor shower has no such check either; **both get it**.
- Hitting terrain: a small carve, `TOXIC_DROP_CARVE_R`, the size a bullet makes.
- **The health bar goes green** for the duration, through the existing `healthBar` colour
  path.

`PlayerState` has no per-player status field today. Add one.

## Notes

- `TOXIC_PUDDLE_EVERY`, `TOXIC_PUDDLE_RADIUS`, `TOXIC_PUDDLE_LIFE` and `TOXIC_DPS` are
  retired. Delete them; do not leave constants nothing reads.
- Damage still routes through `apply_damage_log`, which returns early during `Warmup` —
  that gate stays, and it is worth knowing it is why "toxic rain does nothing" is
  reportable at all.
- The scheduler, the telegraph and the rain visuals are unchanged. This is the drop and
  what it does on contact.
- The poison timer is per-player state, so it must be deterministic and must survive a
  snapshot round trip if the client is to colour the bar. Decide whether it goes on the
  wire or is derived, and say which.
- The roof check is one piece of logic used twice. Share the function (`CLAUDE.md`).

## Tests

- A drop that hits a player poisons them; a control player it misses is unpoisoned.
- Total damage over the duration equals `DPS × DURATION`, pinned to the constants.
- A second hit resets the timer rather than stacking — assert the total, which is the
  only thing that distinguishes reset from stack.
- A player under solid terrain is not hit. Control: the same player with open sky is.
  **The same pair of tests for the meteor shower.**
- A drop carves terrain, and the crater is bullet-sized, not meteor-sized — pinned to
  constants at both ends.
- **Pixel acceptance**: the health bar is green while poisoned and not green before,
  with a control frame.
- `grep` proves no puddle code or constant survives.
