# T18.04 — Rocks and bushes are bigger, and sit on the ground (v6)

Milestone: M18 · Depends on: T16.02 · Size: ~200 lines

## Read first
- `docs/74-amendments-v6.md` §E12, §E15
- `docs/73-amendments-v5.md` §D4, §D5

## Deliverable

- `OBJECT_TARGET_PLAYER_H_ROCK` 1.5 → 2.25, `_BUSH` 1.0 → 1.5. Crystals and ruins
  unchanged.
- **Anchor on the sprite's own footprint**, not on a player-sized box: a site is usable
  only if the terrain under the object's base is solid across enough of its width, and
  the object is seated so its base meets that terrain.

## Notes

- **The float is measured** (§E12): `surface_anchor` picks one column and `is_standable`
  tests a `HALF_W`-wide player box at that column only. A sprite far wider than a player,
  on a peak or a slope, has its centre supported and its outer base columns over air —
  and a 50% bigger sprite makes it worse.
- Partial burial into a slope is correct and wanted. Hanging in air is not. Say which
  your rule permits.
- **The sweep decides the counts.** Bigger objects eat more `OBJECT_PIXEL_BUDGET`, so
  expect fewer per map. Re-run the 999-seed sweep and the golden table. If traversability
  degrades that is a finding, not a threshold to lower (`docs/73` §D5, and D-19/D-22 in
  `tasks/DECISIONS.md` are what that looked like last time).
- Regenerate `masks.bin` and the atlas; `masks.bin` is a build input for the Docker
  images (D-42).

## Tests

- An object's base row is supported across the required fraction of its width, on every
  placement, across seeds and scales — with the count reported.
- Falsify: restore the centre-column-only test and the assertion goes red.
- The golden table moves on all rows; the 999-seed sweep passes with its numbers reported.
- **Pixel acceptance**: a rock renders against ground with no gap under it, control
  region of ground beside it.
- Sizes: a mean rock measures the new target height in mask pixels, pinned to the
  constant.
