# T20.05 — There are two toxic rains and they do not know about each other (v8)

Milestone: M20 · Depends on: nothing · Size: unknown — **settle the spec question first**

Reported: *"is it me or are there 2 toxic rains now? One that is just visual, the other one
is the refactored one that works as projectiles?"* **Confirmed. There are two, and both run
at once.**

## Read first
- `docs/72-amendments-v4.md:166-168` (§C6) **and** `:509-521` (§C21) — read both, they
  disagree
- `crates/game-core/src/effects/toxic.rs:110-140`
- `client/src/render/weather.ts:40-174`, `client/src/render/weather-math.ts:53-98`

## What is there

**Rain A — real, server-side, projectiles.** `ToxicRain::tick` spawns `WEAPON_TOXIC_DROP`
projectiles every `TOXIC_DROP_EVERY` (0.15 s → **54 per shower**). They ride the normal
`projectile_spawn`/`projectile_move` path and are drawn by the ordnance layer as
`{r: 3, colour: 0x9bf05a, trail: 8}`. **These are the drops that carve and poison.**

**Rain B — cosmetic, client-only.** `WeatherLayer` owns a `RainField` of **260
screen-space drops**, seeded 4242, `setScrollFactor(0)`, additive, plus a full-screen green
vignette. Pure client simulation; it knows nothing about the server's projectiles. Switched
on by a boolean: `worldView.ts:259` `setToxic(weather.toxicActive)`.

Both run in both scenes — `GameScene.ts:1417` (A) and `:1442` (B); `SandboxScene.ts:1024`
(A) and `:1052-1059` (B).

**So a player sees a heavy 260-drop sheet and takes damage from an unrelated sparse set of
54.** The visual neither reflects nor is constrained by the real drops.

## The spec question — settle it before writing code

This is **not** a builder's oversight. It is two amendments in one document pulling opposite
ways:

- `docs/72` **§C6** (`:166-168`): droplets *"are a particle emitter, not per-drop
  entities"* — which is what T13.04 built, and it is rain B.
- `docs/72` **§C21** (`:509-521`): a drop becomes a projectile *specifically so that* *"it
  makes the rain visible, because drops are drawn by the same layer as every other
  projectile"* — which claims the visual job §C6 gave the emitter.

**Neither retires the other**, and neither `docs/75` §F6 nor T19.06 mentions the emitter at
all. **Report this to the coordinator and get a ruling before deleting anything** — a
builder choosing between two live spec clauses is exactly what `CLAUDE.md` forbids.

## RULING — take the shape that violates neither clause

**2026-09-04.** Both clauses stay satisfied by **keeping the emitter and driving it from the
real drops**: §C6 gets its particle emitter, §C21 gets a rain whose visible density is the
rain that actually falls on you. **Do not delete rain B**, and do not leave it running on a
seed of its own.

If that turns out to be impossible without a rule change, **stop and report** rather than
choosing between the two clauses.

**Three constraints on the ruling, added after the sweep — read them before you design.**

**(a) The mapping is not linear, and the naive reading produces seven streaks.** The
Deliverable's "54 drops" is the **cumulative** count for a whole shower
(`TOXIC_DURATION / TOXIC_DROP_EVERY`); rain B's 260 is a **live** on-screen count. The
comparable figure is how many real drops are alive at once — `constants.rs:650-653` says the
drop speed gives *"roughly a second of visible descent on a medium map"* against a 0.15 s
cadence, so about **7**. Against 260 that is a factor of ~37, not ~5. (SUSPECTED: it rests
on that doc comment, not a measurement — measure it.) So **each real drop must dress N
emitter particles**, or the rain becomes a drizzle. Choose N and say why.

**(b) Density only — do not draw the emitter *at* the drops.** `RainField` is
`setScrollFactor(0)` screen-space; the real drops are world-space projectiles. Driving a
scalar is the ruling. Positioning particles on projectiles means abandoning
`setScrollFactor(0)` and is a rewrite, not this task.

**(c) Put the derivation inside `WeatherLayer` and update BOTH call sites** — see the
blocker below. `setToxic(active: boolean)` (`weather.ts:87`) must carry density instead of a
boolean; that signature change is the whole cost. The data is already in scope:
`OrdnanceState.projectiles` is a map the client maintains (`ordnance-state.ts:283-284`) and
`KIND_BY_WEAPON_KEY.toxic_drop = 'drop'` (`:148`), so a live count is one `filter`.

## BLOCKER — the gating check runs the scene that skips the obvious fix site

`SandboxScene.ts:1035` calls `this.world.update(this.world.rig.center)` with **no `dt` and
no `weather`**, so `worldView.update`'s entire `if (dt > 0 && weather)` block is skipped —
the sandbox pokes `setToxic(...)` and `.update(...)` by hand at `:1052-1059` instead.

**And `weather-visible.mjs` is a sandbox check** (`regenerate('4242','medium')` at `:17`,
`forceWeather` at `:132`) — it is the **first name in the Done-when**. So a coder who
implements the derivation inside `worldView.update`, the natural site and the one this
ruling points at, gets **no observable change in the gating check**, and the sandbox keeps
rain B on seed 4242 forever. **Green Done-when, unfixed game.**

That is why constraint (c) exists: the derivation goes **inside `WeatherLayer`**, fed by a
count that **both** call sites pass — `worldView.ts:258` **and** `SandboxScene.ts:1052-1059`.
The sandbox has real drops to count: `:1023` syncs `this.core.liveProjectiles()`.

## Deliverable

One rain, or two that agree. The likely shapes:
- **Keep A only** — the honest one, but 54 drops over a 1536 px map is a visibly thinner
  downpour than players see now. Say so with a screenshot rather than discovering it later.
- **Keep B as a dressing driven by A** — the emitter's density derived from the real drop
  rate rather than from 260 and a seed of its own.

## Tests at risk — read these before touching rain B

- **`weather-visible.mjs:293` gets *more* fragile under this ruling, not less.**
  `rainDrops` (`weather.ts:93-94`) is `intensity > 0.02 ? drops.length : 0`. Once
  `drops.length` tracks ~7 real drops instead of a constant 260, it is genuinely 0 at shower
  start and again after the last drop lands while `intensity` still fades — and the check
  samples at one moment. A count with real gaps replaces a constant.
- `weather-visible.mjs:293` throws hard on `rainDrops === 0`; `:303` measures a sky pixel
  delta that the 54 real drops alone may not clear; `:310` asserts `toxicIntensity` decays.
  **All three measure rain B only.**
- `weather-math.test.ts:12-60` — five `RainField` unit tests.
- `m5-weather.mjs` measures **rain A only** (terrain removed by `TOXIC_DROP_CARVE_R`) and is
  unaffected either way.
