## What is there

**Rain A — real, server-side, projectiles.** `ToxicRain::tick` spawns `WEAPON_TOXIC_DROP`
projectiles every `TOXIC_DROP_EVERY` (0.15 s → **54 per shower**). They ride the normal
`projectile_spawn`/`projectile_move` path and are drawn by the ordnance layer as
`{r: 3, colour: 0x9bf05a, trail: 8}`. **These are the drops that carve and poison.**

**Rain B — cosmetic, client-only.** `WeatherLayer` owns a `RainField` of **260
screen-space drops**, seeded 4242, `setScrollFactor(0)`, additive, plus a full-screen green
vignette. Pure client simulation; it knows nothing about the server's projectiles. Switched
on by a boolean: `worldView.ts:259` `setToxic(weather.toxicActive)`.

Both run in both scenes — `GameScene.ts:1417` (A) and `:1442` (B); `SandboxScene.ts:1024`
(A) and `:1052-1059` (B).

**So a player sees a heavy 260-drop sheet and takes damage from an unrelated sparse set of
54.** The visual neither reflects nor is constrained by the real drops.

## The spec question — settle it before writing code

This is **not** a builder's oversight. It is two amendments in one document pulling opposite
ways:

- `docs/72` **§C6** (`:166-168`): droplets *"are a particle emitter, not per-drop
  entities"* — which is what T13.04 built, and it is rain B.
- `docs/72` **§C21** (`:509-521`): a drop becomes a projectile *specifically so that* *"it
  makes the rain visible, because drops are drawn by the same layer as every other
  projectile"* — which claims the visual job §C6 gave the emitter.

**Neither retires the other**, and neither `docs/75` §F6 nor T19.06 mentions the emitter at
all. **Report this to the coordinator and get a ruling before deleting anything** — a
builder choosing between two live spec clauses is exactly what `CLAUDE.md` forbids.

## RULING — take the shape that violates neither clause

**2026-09-04.** Both clauses stay satisfied by **keeping the emitter and driving it from the
real drops**: §C6 gets its particle emitter, §C21 gets a rain whose visible density is the
rain that actually falls on you. **Do not delete rain B**, and do not leave it running on a
seed of its own.

If that turns out to be impossible without a rule change, **stop and report** rather than
choosing between the two clauses.

## Deliverable

One rain, or two that agree. The likely shapes:
- **Keep A only** — the honest one, but 54 drops over a 1536 px map is a visibly thinner
  downpour than players see now. Say so with a screenshot rather than discovering it later.
- **Keep B as a dressing driven by A** — the emitter's density derived from the real drop
  rate rather than from 260 and a seed of its own.
