# T20.10 — Animals on the ground (v8)

Milestone: M20 · Depends on: nothing · Size: **large** — a new entity across nine files

Wanted: *"add some random animals on the ground. Jumping spiders (they don't attack you),
and idk something else. Players killing these can drop health or batteries."*

**No doc governs ground animals** — `grep -i "animal|spider|wildlife|creature"` over `docs/`
and `tasks/` returns nothing. Birds are `docs/72` §C16. This task is self-contained; flag
to the coordinator that an amendment would be the durable home for it.

## Read first — birds are the template, and they are complete
- `crates/game-core/src/world/birds.rs` — the whole entity in one file
- `crates/game-core/src/world/mod.rs:492,556` — `hit_targets` / `targets`
- `crates/game-core/src/world/mod.rs:1872` — `resolve_bird_kills`, the loot path
- `crates/game-core/src/world/mod.rs:5936` — `mod birds_in_a_round`, the test template
- `scripts/checks/birds.mjs` — the browser-check template

## What birds already give you, free

- **Damage from every weapon, generically.** `HitId` (`explode.rs:53`) is
  `Player | Bird`; `hit_targets` builds one `Vec<HitTarget>` so blast/ray/swing/mine/lava
  all hit birds without knowing they exist. **A third variant is a compiler-enforced sweep
  of ~11 call sites.**
- **Loot on death.** `resolve_bird_kills` maps kind → `MEDKIT` / `BATTERY_PACK`, calls
  `make_room()` **before** `spawn` (because `cull` only enforces `len <= MAX`), and emits
  `ItemSpawn`. Exactly the path you need.
- **Wire**: three JSON events, `Scope::Everyone`; `BirdMove` emitted only at `SNAPSHOT_HZ`,
  not every tick. `killed: bool` on despawn separates shot-down from flew-off.
- **Client**: mirror + a diffed layer + Phaser-free maths reading `C()` so drawn size cannot
  drift from the hit box.
- **Test seam**: `place_for_test` puts a known animal in a known place — **essential**,
  since natural spawn takes most of a minute.

## Where the template does not carry

**Birds have no terrain collision** — `pos.y` is a sampled sine, not integrated. A ground
animal that walks or jumps needs `Body::sized` + `integrate(map, &mut body, 1.0, dt)`.
Non-player precedents: `tombstones.rs:125`, `placed.rs:134`, `items/world.rs:191`.

Depth: birds are `DEPTH.birds = -19`, **behind** terrain. A ground animal wants
`DEPTH.decorations` (10) or `DEPTH.actors` (30) — in front.

## Deliverable

- Two kinds, one of them jumping spiders. **They never attack** — no damage path to players
  at all, not a damage path with a zero.
- Killing one drops health or a battery, through `resolve_bird_kills`' shape.
- Its own RNG substream (`substream(seed, "...")`), its own constants block with relationship
  guards like `constants.rs:1591`.

## Notes

- `world/mod.rs:3330 every_field_of_world_has_been_considered_for_the_state_hash`
  **destructures `World` exhaustively — adding a field is a compile error until it is
  considered.** That is by design. Birds are hashed (count + every position) because they
  drop items; animals must be too, for the same reason.
- `client/src/net/subscription.test.ts` parses `GameScene`'s source and asserts every mirrored
  event is subscribed. It caught the bird omission before the browser did — you get that
  coverage free, but **both** places must be updated.
- **A new RNG substream or an unhashed timer breaks `replay_run.rs` and `checksum.rs`.**
- `balance.rs:874 the_shipping_configuration_produces_a_fight` will move: new corpses and
  drops change bot encounter numbers.

## Tests

- Follow `birds_in_a_round`: plant one, shoot it, assert **exactly one** drop and that it
  lands.
- A spider never damages a player — with the control that it can itself be killed, or
  "never attacks" is satisfied by an animal that does not exist.
- **Rendered pixels** for anything visible, with a control region and control frame.
- Determinism: a fixed seed gives the same animals across runs.
