## Where a landing is detected today: nowhere

`integrate` is the whole per-tick step, and **the impact speed is destroyed inside `move_y`
before it returns** (`resolve.rs:98-102`: `body.vel.y = 0.0` then `grounded = true`).
`was_grounded` is a **local**, not returned and not stored, and `integrate` returns `()`.

That is textbook `CLAUDE.md`: *"Return what the caller needs. An API that makes the caller
preserve state it is about to destroy will be called wrong."*

## ⚠ The two landing paths have OPPOSITE velocity semantics — the naive detector is inverted

`move_y` (`resolve.rs:98-100`) sets `vel.y = 0.0` **and then** `grounded = true`.
`ground_snap` (`:110-122`) sets `grounded = true` **without touching `vel.y`**.

So at the end of the tick:
- a **real landing** — the one that must deal damage — has `vel.y == 0`;
- a **downhill snap** — the one that must not — has `vel.y` = one tick of gravity, small but
  **nonzero**.

A coder who detects "grounded went true this tick" and reads `vel.y` gets **zero damage on
every real landing and nonzero damage on every downhill step.** Exactly inverted. The naive
instrument does not merely leak into `ground_snap` — it reads *only* `ground_snap`. This is
the day the task will cost if it is not read first.

## The landing-sound bug is not a separate job — it falls out of this one

`GameScene.ts:1145-1150` scales the landing sound by `Math.abs(body.vy) / MAX_FALL_SPEED`,
and `body.vy` comes from `player_state()` **after `integrate` zeroed it** — so `force` is a
constant and the comment describes something that cannot happen. Duplicated at
`SandboxScene.ts:264`.

**The impact speed `integrate` must start returning for fall damage is exactly the number
the sound needs.** Do not book it separately; fix both, and say you did.

## RULING — reuse `was_knocked` unchanged. My earlier arithmetic was wrong.

An earlier draft said `KNOCKBACK_FIRE_GRACE`'s 0.6 s *"cannot cover a fall"* and offered
three options on top of that. **The premise is false.** From the constants
(`GRAVITY = 1400`, `KNOCKBACK_MAX = 320`):

- time to apex `320/1400` = **0.229 s**, apex height `320²/(2·1400)` = **36.6 px**;
- fall back to launch height, another 0.229 s, arriving at 320 px/s;
- **round trip 0.457 s** — comfortably inside the 0.6 s grace, with ~60 px of extra drop
  still covered at t = 0.6 s.

My 289 px / 0.643 s figure is arithmetically right but describes a **free fall from rest**,
which nothing knocked into. It is not the rocket-jump case. So the doc comment
(*"long enough to cover the arc of a rocket-jump"*) **is true**, and `was_knocked` works as
it stands.

**The real decision is narrower: should a rocket-jump that also drops you off a ledge be
exempt from the ledge?** The knockback gave you 36 px; the cliff gave you the other 250.
**Ruling: no.** Reuse `was_knocked` unchanged, and a player blasted off a cliff takes the
cliff's damage. It is coherent, explainable, and free.

**And the earlier cost estimate was backwards.** `knocked_until` is **already** a stored
field (`state.rs:99`), **already** hashed (`world/mod.rs:3075`), and **already** in the
destructure. Reusing it costs nothing; a second `fall_exempt_until` is the expensive option
and *"derive, do not add a fourth flag"* points the same way.

**Note this makes T20.11 `was_knocked`'s first reader.** `world/mod.rs:1774-1777` records
that §F4's repeal left it with none — a thirteenth built-and-wired-to-nothing mechanism.
Good news here: no existing behaviour to preserve, the field is maintained by all four
throwing paths, and the hash already covers it.

`JETPACK_GRAVITY_SCALE = 0.35` already caps a descent while Space is held, so "hold jump to
land softly" is free counterplay worth stating.
