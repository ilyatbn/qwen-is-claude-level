## Where a landing is detected today: nowhere

`integrate` is the whole per-tick step, and **the impact speed is destroyed inside `move_y`
before it returns** (`resolve.rs:98-102`: `body.vel.y = 0.0` then `grounded = true`).
`was_grounded` is a **local**, not returned and not stored, and `integrate` returns `()`.

That is textbook `CLAUDE.md`: *"Return what the caller needs. An API that makes the caller
preserve state it is about to destroy will be called wrong."*

There is a **second** landing path — `ground_snap` (`resolve.rs:111-122`) sets
`grounded = true` **without** zeroing `vel.y`, for walking downhill within `STEP_DOWN` (8 px).
**Fall damage must not fire there.**

## A bug to fix or book while you are here

`GameScene.ts:1145-1150` scales the landing sound by `Math.abs(body.vy) / MAX_FALL_SPEED`,
but `body.vy` comes from the wasm `player_state()` — **already zeroed by `move_y` on the
landing tick**. So `force` is almost certainly a constant `0.25` and the comment describes
something that cannot happen. Duplicated at `SandboxScene.ts:264`.

## Deliverable

- `integrate` (or a new `integrate_reporting`) **returns the landing impact**; `apply_input`
  forwards it; `World::apply_inputs` turns it into a damage-log entry.
- **Fall damage must not go inside `apply_input`.** Its doc comment says purity is *the
  property that makes prediction correct*, and `prediction.ts` replays it dozens of times
  per frame. Health is not predicted client-side — it arrives in the binary snapshot — so a
  server-only health effect does not desync.
- Go through `apply_damage_log`, which is the single warmup gate and buys the `Damage`
  event and attribution.

## The two decisions this task must settle explicitly

1. **What `DamageSource`/`DeathCause` is a fall?** There is no natural variant. It is
   closest in shape to `step_void`, which bypasses the funnel and lets `resolve_deaths` name
   the cause. A fifth `DeathCause` changes the kill feed and scoring (`docs/21` §6).
2. **The knockback exemption, and `was_knocked` will not work as it stands.** A rocket-jump
   imparts 320 px/s upward; a fall from 289 px reaches `MAX_FALL_SPEED = 900`. But
   `KNOCKBACK_FIRE_GRACE = 0.6` is documented as *"deliberately short: long enough to cover
   the arc of a rocket-jump"* — for the **fire** gate, which fires *within* the arc. A fall
   lasts well past it, so by touchdown the stamp has expired. The honest options:
   - a **separate** `fall_exempt_until` on a longer clock (must be hashed, §A34, and added
     to the destructuring test);
   - exempt while the jetpack is active — **the opposite of the feature's intent**;
   - no exemption, and rocket-jump self-damage is accepted — say so out loud.

   Note `JETPACK_GRAVITY_SCALE = 0.35` already caps a descent while Space is held, so
   "hold jump to land softly" is free counterplay worth stating.
