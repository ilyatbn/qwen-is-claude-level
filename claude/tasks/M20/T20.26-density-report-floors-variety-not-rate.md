# T20.26 — `density_report` floors variety, and the thing that moves is rate (v8)

Milestone: M20 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small, **but decide the claim before the number**

**Found by T20.16**, checking the premise that justified it: *"planting `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL`
14 → 42 passes all 1296 gate tests, because every gate assertion is pinned to the constant.
The ignored measurements are the only thing that catches balance and density drift."*

**The first half is true. The second half is not.** With that plant in place, **all six**
`#[ignore]`d tests in `balance.rs` pass, and so do all three gate-resident ones. Nothing in
this repository catches it.

## Measured

`ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` 14.0 → 42.0 in `constants.rs`, `--release`, 8 seeds, both runs on the
same idle box:

```
=== density_report, UNPLANTED ===
   Small:  distinct 14.1/19 (74%)  spawns 35.5  peak live 19/40  despawns  82  1st weapon 13s
   Medium: distinct 12.6/19 (66%)  spawns 37.1  peak live 20/40  despawns 121  1st weapon 13s
   Large:  distinct 14.0/19 (74%)  spawns 43.5  peak live 27/40  despawns 160  1st weapon 13s

=== density_report, PLANTED 14 -> 42 ===
   Small:  distinct 11.1/19 (59%)  spawns 24.8  peak live 11/40  despawns  57  1st weapon 26s
   Medium: distinct 11.6/19 (61%)  spawns 26.1  peak live 16/40  despawns  89  1st weapon 31s
   Large:  distinct 13.1/19 (69%)  spawns 31.4  peak live 20/40  despawns 126  1st weapon 40s
```

`test result: ok` both times.

## Why it passes

`density_report` asserts three things, and the plant moves none of them past their bound:

- `mean_distinct >= floor` — **variety**, and variety is the statistic that barely moves. The
  drawable pool is 19 types; 26 spawns still covers 11.6 of them, so tripling the interval
  costs **8 %** of distinct types on Medium.
- `peak < MAX_WORLD_ITEMS` — an upper bound. A *slower* spawn rate moves it the safe way, so
  this assertion cannot fail in this direction by construction.
- `mean_spawn > 10.0` — the vacuity control, satisfied by 26.1.

The statistics that actually moved are printed and not asserted: **spawns 37.1 → 26.1
(−30 %)** and **first weapon 13 s → 31 s (2.4×)**.

**"Density is not weight" is the distinction the file's own comment draws** — and it then
floors the variety half while the rate half is the one `ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` controls.

## What to decide, before picking a number

The cheap fix — add `mean_spawn >= <measured>` — is available and is probably right, but it is
a claim nobody has made yet, so make it deliberately:

1. **Is the player-facing claim about rate or about wait?** "First weapon at 13 s" moved by
   2.4× and is the number a player experiences. A floor on time-to-first-weapon is closer to
   the thing that matters than a floor on spawns-per-round, and it is already computed.
2. **Whatever is chosen, it must be per scale.** Large spawns 43.5 against Small's 35.5
   unplanted; one pooled floor would be slack for Small and tight for Large.
3. **Record the margin at the assertion**, the way `balance.rs`'s `SEED_LOS_FLOOR` and
   `POOLED_DAMAGE_FLOOR` now do, and let `the_balance_floors_record_their_basis` cover the new
   constant — it walks a list of names and adding one is a one-line change.

## Falsify the fix with the same plant

`ITEM_SPAWN_INTERVAL` 14 → 42 must turn the new assertion red, and the unplanted value must
leave it green with the margin printed. **A floor that the plant does not trip has not fixed
anything**, and this task exists because the current one does not.

## Notes

- Do **not** widen `the_shipping_configuration_produces_a_fight` to cover this. It measures
  whether players meet, and the plant correctly leaves it green (worst-seed sight 63.3 %,
  pooled damage 2676). Mixing the two claims into one test is how a control ends up varying
  two axes at once — which is what T20.16 was cleaning up.
- `scripts/ignored.sh` classifies `density_report` as a **guard**. On this evidence it is a
  guard for variety only; the classification is not wrong, but the manifest comment is worth
  a second look once the claim is decided.
