## Correcting the brief: it already drains

**The premise "no battery cost" is wrong.** `tick_stats` (`state.rs:296-309`) already
subtracts `SHIELD_DRAIN = 2.0`/s while the shield is up and kills it at zero battery. A full
100 battery buys 50 s, so the 20 s `SHIELD_DURATION` is simply what ends first.

**And the shape you want already exists.** `apply_damage` (`state.rs:326-341`) already
special-cases energy weapons against the shield: pierce at `LASER_SHIELD_MULT` **plus** a
`LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN` charge **per hit**. A per-hit multiplier with a per-hit energy cost is
therefore a *generalisation of code already there*, not a new mechanism.

**`apply_damage` is the one damage path.** `World::apply_damage_log` is the single warmup
gate and calls it, and the wasm client mirror holds a real `PlayerState` and damages through
the same method. **One edit reaches server and client prediction.**

## Deliverable

- The generator is **held, not used**. `use_item`'s `Shield` arm must stop consuming — or
  refuse with `UseError::WrongKind` the way weapons do. `ItemKind::Shield { duration }` loses
  its meaning.
- **Damage × 0.75 per hit, costing 1 energy per hit**, only while a generator is held and
  the battery can pay.
- `shield_until` is **deleted, not supplemented**. `shield_active(now)` becomes derived —
  "holds a generator and has ≥ 1 energy". **Derive, do not add a fourth flag**; it needs the
  inventory, so it takes `&Inventory` or moves to a method that has both.
- Decide what the **laser pierce** means now. Today it is a different multiplier *and* a
  bigger drain, both keyed off `shield_active`. Two rules meeting is a decision, not a
  detail.

**T20.07 deletes `flashlight_on`, hashed at `:3049`, for the same reason.** Whichever of the

## A production consumer the earlier draft missed

`bots/mod.rs:975` — `if threatened && me.shield_until.is_none_or(|t| t <= world.round_time)`
— is the bot's "should I pop a shield" gate, which then scans for an `ItemKind::Shield` slot
to select. **Under the new design there is no popping**, so this branch is not merely broken
by the deletion, it is *meaningless*: a bot that "uses" a held generator does nothing.
Decide what a bot does with a passive generator and **say so**; there is bot work here and
the earlier draft listed none.

## The derived boolean has two affordability thresholds, and bit 3 will lie

`shield_active` becomes "holds a generator and has ≥ 1 energy". But `apply_damage`
(`state.rs:327-342`) charges `LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN = 8.0` on the energy branch, against the
new normal cost of 1. **A player with 4 energy reads as shielded** — bit 3 says so, the
client draws the bubble — and then a laser hit finds the battery cannot pay. One boolean,
two thresholds.

That is the *"decide what the laser pierce means"* bullet made concrete. However it is
settled — charge `min(battery, cost)` and pierce proportionally, take the higher threshold,
or drop `LASER_BATTERY_DRAIN` to 1 — **the boolean and the charge must agree or the wire
lies.** Note `apply_damage:333-335` also writes `self.shield_until = None`: a second
deletion site.

## The wire does not need to change — but its readers do

Bit 3 already packs `shield_active(now)`, a **derived boolean**. Under the new rule it means
"carrying a charged generator", still derivable server-side. What breaks is everything
treating it as a countdown:

- `client/src/ui/bars-math.ts:133-154` — **`shieldRing` should be deleted, not adapted.**
  `byTime = duration - (now - startedAt)`, and `startedAt`, `now` and `duration` all vanish
  with the timer. `battery`/`drain` survive as inputs but **change kind**: `battery / drain`
  is *seconds* under a per-second drain, while the new cost is *per hit*, so what remains is
  `floor(battery / cost)` — **a count of absorptions, with no denominator.** `shieldRing`
  returns a 0..1 fraction that `bars.ts:145-147` renders as `width: ${shield * 100}%` — a
  fraction of a full scale that no longer exists. If the HUD should show remaining
  absorptions that is a **pip count**, a different widget, and a decision to state rather
  than adapt into. With it go `GameScene.ts:153-157` (which exists *only* to supply
  `startedAt`), `:1086-1088`, `:1771-1778`.
- `playerView.ts:181-190,237` — `shieldBubble.setVisible(flags.shield)` would now be up
  **permanently** while carrying a charged generator. That is a visual design decision;
  **make it explicitly** rather than shipping a bubble nobody can turn off.
