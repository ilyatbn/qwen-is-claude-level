# T20.18 — Seven copies of `connect` (v8)

Milestone: M20 · Depends on: nothing · Size: small

**Booked from T20.10's handoff**, which named `tests/common` as the durable fix and correctly
declined to widen into it mid-task.

## What was seen

Seven `game-server` integration tests carry their own copy of a socket `connect` helper.
T20.10 needed `emit_when_ready` retry behaviour in one of them and had to choose between
fixing one copy or seven.

## Deliverable — and it is three duplicated fixtures, not one

One shared module in `crates/game-server/tests/common/`. **Share the function, not the guard**
— the point is that the next fix lands once.

**`connect` is not the only one, and consolidating it alone leaves the other two to drift:**

- **`connect`** — seven copies.
- **`test_config()`** — **eight** copies, counted directly rather than from a filtered
  listing: `checksum.rs`, `in_progress.rs`, `integration.rs`, `join.rs`, `lobby.rs`,
  `room.rs`, `room_logging.rs`, `rooms.rs`. **`room.rs` is where T20.20's `fixed_seed: None`
  lives**, so that file is not optional.
  *(An earlier draft said five. The count came from a `grep | head -12` read as the whole
  set — `ls | tail` in other clothes. Verify with `grep -rln "fn test_config" crates/game-server/ | wc -l`,
  not with a filtered listing.)*
- **`BUDGET_MS`** — three copies, and **each is exactly a shipped constant** (10_000 against
  `LOBBY_BOT_TIMEOUT`/`WARMUP_SECONDS`; 30_000 against `READY_TIMEOUT_SECS`/`ROOM_EMPTY_TTL`).
  See T20.20. **Do not consolidate these to a shared literal — pin them to something that is
  not a state-transition boundary**, or you will have centralised the bug.

## Notes

- T20.10's `emit_when_ready` **retries any `Err`**, not the `IllegalActionBeforeOpen` it was
  diagnosed for. It weakens nothing (`wait_for("welcome")` keeps its own deadline, and it
  panics with the original error after `EMIT_READY_WINDOW`), but a genuinely refused emit now
  costs 5 s before it says so. **Narrow it while consolidating**, or state why not.
- Check the seven copies actually agree before merging them. If two have drifted, the
  difference is a finding — say which behaviour won and why.

## Tests

- The seven suites still pass, and a deliberate break in the shared helper reds **all** of
  them — otherwise "shared" is a claim rather than a fact.
