## Deliverable

```rust
/// Per-player jump bookkeeping. Lives on PlayerState (T4.12); defined here
/// because this is the only file that reads or writes it.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug, Default)]
pub struct JumpState { pub buffered_ticks: u32 }

/// Try to jump this tick. Returns true if a jump was launched.
pub fn try_jump(
    body: &mut Body,
    jump_state: &mut JumpState,
    jump_pressed: bool,
    dir: f32,
) -> bool;
```

Rules:

- A jump launches when the body is grounded **or** within coyote time, and either
  `jump_pressed` this tick or a buffered press is still live.
- On launch: `vel.y = -JUMP_VELOCITY`. If `dir != 0`, additionally
  `vel.x += dir * JUMP_H_BOOST`.
- `grounded = false`, `airborne_ticks` pushed past coyote time so the same press
  cannot launch twice.
- A press that cannot launch (airborne, out of coyote time) sets
  `buffered_ticks = JUMP_BUFFER * SIM_HZ`, decremented each tick.

## Notes

The brief's three requirements map onto this directly:

- *standing jump goes straight up* — `dir == 0` adds no horizontal boost, and
  `vel.x` is already ~0 from friction;
- *jumping while moving carries you* — `JUMP_H_BOOST` adds **on top of** current
  walking speed, it does not replace it;
- *direction changeable mid-air* — nothing here restricts that; air control in
  T2.07 stays live for the whole flight.

Pushing `airborne_ticks` past the coyote window on launch is essential. Without it,
a held jump re-launches on the next tick because the body is still nominally within
coyote time, and the player rockets upward.

Coyote time and the jump buffer are small and unglamorous but they are most of what
separates movement that feels responsive from movement that feels like it is
ignoring you. Do not skip them.

Do not handle the jetpack here at all. The Space-is-both-buttons disambiguation is
entirely T2.09's problem, and `try_jump` is called first in that ordering.

## Tests

- Grounded, jump pressed: `vel.y == -JUMP_VELOCITY`, returns true.
- Airborne past coyote time, jump pressed: returns false, `vel.y` unchanged.
- Within coyote time (`airborne_ticks = 5`): jump succeeds.
- Just past coyote time (`airborne_ticks = 7`): jump fails.
- Standing jump (`dir = 0`): `vel.x` unchanged.
- Running jump (`dir = 1`, `vel.x = WALK_SPEED`): `vel.x` becomes
  `WALK_SPEED + JUMP_H_BOOST`.
- **Apex height**: integrating a jump with gravity, the peak is within 2 px of
  `JUMP_VELOCITY² / (2 * GRAVITY)` ≈ 66 px.
- **A standing jump lands within 2 px of where it started**, horizontally.
- A running jump travels measurably further horizontally than a standing one.
- Holding jump for 60 ticks from the ground launches **exactly once**.
- Jump buffer: pressed 5 ticks before landing → launches on the landing tick.
- Jump buffer: pressed 15 ticks before landing (past `JUMP_BUFFER`) → does not
  launch.
- The buffer expires and does not launch a jump much later.
