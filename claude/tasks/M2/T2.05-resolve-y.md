## Deliverable

```rust
/// Move vertically by dy, resolving collisions. Sets `grounded` on a downward hit.
/// Returns true if blocked.
pub fn move_y(map: &Map, body: &mut Body, dy: f32) -> bool;

/// After both axes: if the body was grounded and is now falling off a slope,
/// snap it back down up to STEP_DOWN px. Keeps downhill walking smooth.
pub fn ground_snap(map: &Map, body: &mut Body, was_grounded: bool);

/// Gravity integration with terminal velocity.
pub fn apply_gravity(body: &mut Body, gravity_scale: f32, dt: f32);

/// The full per-tick movement step: gravity, X, Y, snap, grounded bookkeeping.
pub fn integrate(map: &Map, body: &mut Body, gravity_scale: f32, dt: f32);
```

## Notes

`move_y` per sub-step: move, and if overlapping, undo, zero `vel.y`, and — if the
movement was downward — set `grounded = true`. Upward hits are ceilings: zero
`vel.y` and nothing else.

`ground_snap` runs **after both axes**, and only when all of:
- `was_grounded` was true at the start of the tick,
- the body currently overlaps nothing,
- `vel.y >= 0` (not rising).

Then `ground_probe(.., STEP_DOWN)`; if it finds ground, move down by that amount and
keep `grounded = true`. Without this, every downhill step becomes a brief fall, and
walking down a slope produces a visible stutter and a flickering `grounded` flag
that breaks jump input.

**Grounded bookkeeping**, in `integrate`, in this order:
1. record `was_grounded`;
2. set `grounded = false` (it must be re-established this tick, not inherited);
3. `apply_gravity`;
4. `move_x` then `move_y` — X first, so a body sliding into a slope climbs it before
   gravity pulls it into the face;
5. `ground_snap(.., was_grounded)`;
6. if `grounded` then `airborne_ticks = 0` else `airborne_ticks += 1`.

Clearing `grounded` before resolving (step 2) is what makes walking off a ledge
register immediately, which is what makes coyote time meaningful.

Gravity is clamped to `MAX_FALL_SPEED` on the way down only — upward velocity is not
clamped, so a strong knockback still launches properly.

## Tests

- A body resting on flat ground stays at the same position for 600 ticks, with
  `grounded` true throughout and no sinking or jitter. Assert the position is
  **bit-identical** to the start, not merely close — a drift of 0.001 px per tick
  is a real bug that this catches and an approximate assertion hides.
- A body dropped from 200 px above lands on the floor and stops, `grounded` true.
- A body jumping into a ceiling has `vel.y` zeroed and does not embed.
- Terminal velocity: after 5 s of free fall, `|vel.y| == MAX_FALL_SPEED`.
- Walking down a 30° slope: `grounded` is true on **every** tick of the descent.
  This is the ground-snap test and it is the one that matters most.
- Walking off a ledge: `grounded` goes false on the first tick past the edge, and
  `airborne_ticks` starts counting.
- Walking down a step taller than `STEP_DOWN`: the body correctly becomes airborne
  rather than snapping down.
- `integrate` with `gravity_scale = 0` leaves `vel.y` unchanged.
- No tunnelling: a body at `vel.y = 10 * MAX_FALL_SPEED` is stopped by a 1 px floor.
- A body in a 1-px-tall gap does not oscillate between grounded and airborne across
  600 ticks.
