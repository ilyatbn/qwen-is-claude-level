## Deliverable

```rust
/// Apply directional thrust for one tick. Only called when state.active.
pub fn apply_thrust(body: &mut Body, input: &Input, dt: f32);

/// Gravity multiplier for this tick: JETPACK_GRAVITY_SCALE while thrusting,
/// 1.0 otherwise. Fed to `integrate` (T2.05).
pub fn gravity_scale(state: &JetpackState) -> f32;
```

Thrust:

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| `W` (UP) | `vel.y -= JETPACK_THRUST_UP * dt` (2200) |
| `S` (DOWN) | `vel.y += JETPACK_THRUST_DOWN * dt` (900) |
| `A` (LEFT) | `vel.x -= JETPACK_THRUST_SIDE * dt` (1100) |
| `D` (RIGHT) | `vel.x += JETPACK_THRUST_SIDE * dt` (1100) |

Then clamp **each axis independently** to `±JETPACK_MAX_SPEED` (260).

## Notes

This gives the free WASD flight the brief asks for. Note that `A`/`D` provide
thrust here in addition to the air control from T2.07 — that is intentional and is
what makes flight feel meaningfully different from falling. The per-axis clamp keeps
the combination from compounding into something uncontrollable.

Clamp **per axis**, not by vector magnitude. A magnitude clamp makes diagonal flight
slower on each axis than straight flight, which reads as the controls fighting you.

`JETPACK_GRAVITY_SCALE` (0.35) rather than 0 is deliberate: holding Space with no
WASD gives a slow controlled descent rather than a dead hover. That is the useful
default state, and it means running out of fuel is a gradual loss of lift instead of
a sudden drop.

Do not apply thrust when `state.active` is false — the caller (T2.11) gates this,
but a `debug_assert!` here documents the contract.

The clamp applies only while thrusting. Velocity from a jump, a fall or an
explosion knockback is untouched, which is what lets a rocket-jump launch you far
faster than the jetpack ever could.

## Tests

- `W` alone: `vel.y` decreases, converging to exactly `-JETPACK_MAX_SPEED`.
- `S` alone: `vel.y` increases, converging to `+JETPACK_MAX_SPEED`.
- `A` and `D` alone: `vel.x` converges to `∓JETPACK_MAX_SPEED`.
- `W` and `D` together: **both** axes reach the full clamp (this is the test that
  catches a magnitude clamp).
- `A` and `D` together cancel: `vel.x` unchanged.
- `W` and `S` together cancel on the y axis apart from the asymmetric thrust
  values — assert the net is `(THRUST_DOWN − THRUST_UP) * dt`.
- No input while active: velocity is unchanged by `apply_thrust` (gravity still acts
  via `gravity_scale`).
- Upward thrust exceeds gravity: from rest, holding `W` produces net upward motion
  when integrated with `gravity_scale`.
- `gravity_scale` returns `JETPACK_GRAVITY_SCALE` when active and 1.0 when not.
- **Hover**: holding Space with no WASD for 60 ticks produces a slow descent, not a
  hover and not a fall at full gravity — assert the fall distance is between 10 %
  and 50 % of an unpowered fall over the same time.
- A body at `vel.y = -800` from a rocket jump is **not** clamped down to 260 by
  starting the jetpack — only thrust-driven velocity is clamped. (If your
  implementation clamps unconditionally, this test will fail; the doc's intent is
  the clamp bounds the thrust, so clamp only when the axis was thrust this tick, or
  clamp toward the limit without reducing an already-larger opposing velocity.
  Pick one, document it in a comment, and make the test match.)
