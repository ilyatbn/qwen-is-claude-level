## Deliverable

```rust
/// Split a displacement into steps of at most MAX_SUBSTEP_PX, capped at
/// MAX_SUBSTEPS. Returns (step_count, per_step_delta).
pub fn substeps(delta: Vec2) -> (u32, Vec2);

/// Move the body horizontally by dx, resolving collisions. Handles step-up.
/// Returns true if the body was blocked (velocity was zeroed).
pub fn move_x(map: &Map, body: &mut Body, dx: f32) -> bool;
```

Per sub-step:
1. Move by `dx / steps`.
2. If `aabb_overlaps_solid`:
   a. `step_up_clearance(.., STEP_UP)` — if some lift clears it, apply the lift and
      accept the horizontal move (a slope or small ledge was climbed);
   b. otherwise undo the horizontal move, set `vel.x = 0`, return blocked.

## Notes

**The sub-step cap is a correctness guarantee, not an optimisation.** No step ever
exceeds 1 px, so a body can never skip over a 1-px wall regardless of speed or tick
rate. `MAX_SUBSTEPS` (64) bounds the worst case; a body moving faster than 64 px per
tick will move slower than requested rather than tunnel. That trade is correct —
capping speed is recoverable, tunnelling is not.

Compute `substeps` from the **total** displacement once, then loop. Do not
recompute per step.

Stop the loop early when blocked. Continuing to push after a wall wastes work and,
worse, can climb a wall one sub-step at a time if step-up is retried each iteration.

Step-up applies **only** during horizontal movement, and only when the body is
moving into something. It must not fire while airborne against a wall — that would
let players climb sheer surfaces by holding a direction. Gate it on
`body.grounded || body.in_coyote_time()`.

Do not touch `vel.y` here. Y is entirely T2.05's job.

## Tests

- `substeps(Vec2::new(0.5, 0))` returns 1 step.
- `substeps(Vec2::new(10.0, 0))` returns 10 steps of 1 px.
- `substeps(Vec2::new(1000.0, 0))` is capped at `MAX_SUBSTEPS`.
- `substeps(Vec2::zero())` returns at least 1 step and does not divide by zero.
- Moving right in open air moves exactly `dx` and returns false.
- Moving right into a 20 px wall stops the body against it, zeroes `vel.x`, and
  returns true.
- The stopped position is within 1 px of the wall — not 5 px short, and not
  embedded.
- Moving into a 4 px step climbs it: final `pos.y` is 4 px higher, `vel.x` is
  unchanged, returns false.
- Moving into a 7 px step does not climb it and returns true.
- **Step-up does not fire while airborne**: the same 4 px step, with
  `grounded = false` and `airborne_ticks` past coyote time, blocks instead of
  climbing.
- **No tunnelling**: a body at `dx = 500` moving into a 1 px wall is stopped by it.
  Test both directions.
- Moving left mirrors every case above.
- A body already embedded in rock does not move further into it.
