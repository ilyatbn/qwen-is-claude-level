## Notes

Space is both jump and jetpack, so the engagement rules are the whole point of this
task. Implement the doc's table **exactly**:

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Space pressed while grounded or in coyote time | Jump (T2.08 already handled it). Jetpack does **not** engage. |
| Space still held `JETPACK_HOLD_DELAY` after that jump, fuel ≥ `JETPACK_MIN_FUEL_TO_ENGAGE` | Engage |
| Space pressed while airborne and out of coyote time | Engage immediately, if fuel allows |
| Space released | Disengage instantly |
| Fuel reaches 0 | Disengage, `locked_out = true` until fuel ≥ `JETPACK_MIN_FUEL_TO_ENGAGE` |

`ticks_since_jump` is reset to 0 by `jumped_this_tick` and incremented otherwise;
the hold-delay rule fires when it exceeds `JETPACK_HOLD_DELAY * SIM_HZ` (11 ticks).

Fuel:
```
active:  fuel -= JETPACK_DRAIN * dt          // 1.0/s
idle:    if idle_ticks > JETPACK_REFILL_DELAY * SIM_HZ:
             fuel += JETPACK_REFILL * dt     // 0.5/s
```
Clamped to `[0, JETPACK_MAX_FUEL]`. This yields the brief's rule exactly: 5 s of
flight, and 2 s of refill for every 1 s used, so a full burn recovers in 10 s.

Refill runs whether grounded or airborne — there is no landing requirement.

`locked_out` prevents the stutter where a player at 0.01 fuel gets a single tick of
thrust per frame. It clears only once fuel climbs back to
`JETPACK_MIN_FUEL_TO_ENGAGE`.

This function decides `active` and manages fuel. It applies **no** thrust — that is
T2.10. Keeping the decision separate from the force is what makes both testable.

## Tests

- A press while grounded does not engage the jetpack on that tick.
- Holding through a jump engages after exactly 11 ticks (`JETPACK_HOLD_DELAY`), not
  before.
- A press while airborne (outside coyote time) engages on that tick.
- Releasing Space disengages on that tick.
- **Fuel drain**: 5.0 s of continuous thrust takes fuel from 5.0 to exactly 0.0.
- Fuel never goes below 0.
- **Refill rate**: 10.0 s idle after a full burn restores fuel to exactly 5.0.
- Refill does not start during the first `JETPACK_REFILL_DELAY` (0.5 s) of idling.
- Fuel never exceeds `JETPACK_MAX_FUEL`.
- Running dry sets `locked_out` and disengages.
- While `locked_out`, holding Space does not engage until fuel reaches
  `JETPACK_MIN_FUEL_TO_ENGAGE` (0.3).
- A full burn followed by a full refill returns fuel to exactly its starting value
  (no accumulated float drift over 900 ticks).
- Jetpack does not engage with `fuel == 0` under any input combination.
